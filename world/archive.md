# The Archive

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/archivist.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Archivist, a hooded figure in an indigo robe with pale eyes, arms raised over its lectern, its tome open and a spell circle in its hand" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The Archivist, writing a spell in the air.</span>

A buried library of magic, deep under the Overworld. Its doors are sealed by elements and open only to spells, its
halls are kept by Runebound, and at its heart the **Archivist** waits over its lectern. Past the Archivist is a vault
of Tier IV runes. It's usually the first dungeon you'll clear, and the one the 8th Heart Circle asks for.

## Finding it

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/archive.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A ring of broken deepslate-brick pillars in a birch forest clearing, two topped with amethyst clusters, around a stairway going down" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The way into an Archive: broken pillars around a stairway going down.</span>

- **Where:** under most land biomes: forests of every kind, taigas (snowy ones too), jungles, savannas, badlands,
  plains, sunflower plains, snowy plains, deserts, meadows, cherry groves and groves. Never under oceans, rivers,
  swamps or beaches.
- **How often:** roughly one in every square 704 blocks across, where the biome allows.
- **What to look for:** a ring of broken pillars of deepslate brick, 2 to 5 blocks tall, some crowned with an amethyst
  cluster, around the mouth of a stairway. Over the stairway stands an arch of polished deepslate with a crying
  obsidian keystone and a soul lantern hanging from it.
- **Torn Pages point the way.** Reading a Torn Page in the Overworld gives you a riddle, and in its margin a sketch of
  the way to the nearest Archive: *"In the margin, a map: an Archive lies about 350 blocks to the northeast."* The
  distance is rounded to 50 blocks. (A page only reads if it has a riddle you haven't seen; once you know them all,
  pages stay blank and keep.) See [Secret Spells]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}).

Finding one earns the advancement *The Buried Library*.

## The layout

Down 24 steps of deepslate brick (open to the sky at the top, then roofed and lit by soul lanterns), you come out
about 24 blocks below the surface. From there the Archive runs in a straight line:

| Room | What's in it | Guards | Door out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The stairway** | 24 steps down | none | open |
| **The Hall of Shelves** | A library about 23 by 17 blocks: bookshelves and chiseled bookshelves along both walls, pillars with soul lanterns, four reading tables with lit candles, a lectern, five hanging lanterns, a purple carpet down the middle, and **a chest** in the far corner | 3 Runebound: a skeleton, a witch and a pillager | Rune Seal door of **Frost and Storm** |
| **The Hall of Braziers** | A blackstone hall with four unlit campfires on pedestals and amethyst clusters along the walls. A fire spell landing by a campfire lights it | 2 Runebound: a vindicator **Adept** and a skeleton | Rune Seal door of **Fire and Wind** |
| **The corridor** | A short passage | none | open |
| **The Arena** | A domed circle about 25 blocks across, a magic circle inlaid in its floor (a ring of amethyst, a ring of gilded blackstone and a hexagram of crying obsidian), eight pillars each with a light at its heart, and the **Archive Lectern** in the middle | **The Archivist** | Side door of **Arcane and Life** to the vault |
| **The Vault** | **Two chests** and a **Wellstone** | none | |

The guards carry spells from the usual Runebound lists (see [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }})). On
top of them, skeletons, zombies and witches spawn in the Archive's dark, and 35% of those are Runebound too.

The Archive is old and not quite asleep: faint shafts of light fall from its lamps with dust drifting through them,
the circle in the arena floor glows softly (brighter while the Archivist is abroad), and now and then a page turns
among the shelves or something whispers in the dark.

## Rune Seal doors

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/seal-door.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A door of twenty Rune Seals in a deepslate frame, checkered with frost snowflakes and storm lightning bolts" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The Archive's first door: Frost and Storm seals, checkered.</span>

Every door in the Archive and the three dimension dungeons is a **Rune Seal door**: a block of seals 5 wide and 4 high,
checkered with two elements, each seal carved with its element's glyph.

- **Seals can't be broken**, by tools or blasts. Only spells open them.
- **Strike the door with a spell of one of its elements**, and every seal of that element in the door lights up with
  a chime (they glow brighter, too).
- **Any effect of that element counts**, harmful or helpful. The effect has to land within a block of a seal of its
  element, so aim at the door itself.
- **Only players' spells** open doors; a Runebound's stray spell never will.
- **A lit element stays lit for 10 seconds**, then goes dark again.
- **Light every element of the door while the first is still lit** and the door dissolves, from the middle out, in a
  ring of light.
- Everyone within 24 blocks earns the **Sealbreaker** feat the first time (250 mana toward the next Heart Circle), and
  the advancement *Sealbreaker*. An opened door stays open.

The easy way is **one spell with both elements**: `Bolt · Chill · Shock` lights Frost and Storm in the same moment and
opens the Archive's first door in one cast. Otherwise, cast one element and then the other within 10 seconds.

You don't need strong runes. The cheapest Tier I effect of each element does the job, and any Cord can cast Tier I:

| Element | A cheap effect that opens its seals |
|---|---|
| Fire | Ember (Tier I) |
| Frost | Chill (Tier I) |
| Storm | Shock (Tier I) |
| Wind | Push (Tier I) |
| Earth | Pelt (Tier I) |
| Life | Nourish or Heal (Tier I), Venom (Tier II) |
| Void | Hex or Blind (Tier I) |
| Arcane | Harm (Tier I) |
| Time | Countdown (Tier I) |
| Blood | Rend (Tier I) |

Recipes are on [Rune Recipes]({{ '/items/rune-recipes/' | relative_url }}). The doors in all four dungeons:

| Dungeon | First door | Second door | Vault door |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Archive | Frost and Storm | Fire and Wind | Arcane and Life |
| The Ember Sanctum | Fire and Earth | Void and Storm | Frost and Fire |
| The Astral Observatory | Void and Arcane | Time and Wind | Void and Life |
| The Drowned Scriptorium | Frost and Storm | Life and Wind | Storm and Arcane |

## The Archivist

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/archive-arena.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Archive's domed arena, its floor inlaid with a glowing magic circle, the Archivist rising over the lectern at its centre under a purple boss bar" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The Archivist rises. Its boss bar names every spell before it's cast.</span>

The keeper of the Archive: a tall hooded figure in an indigo robe trimmed with gold, hovering just above the floor,
its face an empty dark with two pale eyes. An open tome floats before it, turning a page now and then, and three loose
pages circle it.

### Waking it

The Archivist rises from the Archive Lectern the first time a player comes within **12 blocks** of it. Everyone within
48 blocks reads: *"Pages stir. The Archivist rises from its lectern."* It rises only once. The lectern glows and
sheds enchanting glyphs while it waits, and goes dim once the Archivist is awake. Its first spell comes about 3
seconds after it rises, and it starts blinking after about 10.

### What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health** | 400 |
| **Armour** | 8 |
| **Knockback** | Barely moves (80% resistance) |
| **Immune to** | Fire (fire spells' burning damage does nothing; a blast such as Explode still lands), falls, and other monsters |
| **Can't be hurt** | While it rewrites its Cord (2.5 seconds, twice a fight) |
| **Boss bar** | Purple, darkens the sky, and names the spell it's casting: *The Archivist · casting Shock Rain* |
| **Spell power** | 10% more than a Runebound's: 66% of normal on Easy, 88% on Normal, 110% on Hard |

### How it fights

- **It floats and keeps its distance.** If you're more than 16 blocks away it drifts toward you. Every 8 to 14 seconds
  it may **blink**: always if you're within 6 blocks, otherwise half the time. It reappears about 10 blocks from you,
  roughly on the side it was already on, with a purple flash and a small circle where it lands.
- **It never leaves its arena.** If it ends up more than 22 blocks from its lectern, it blinks back.
- **It needs to see you to start a spell.** Then it raises its arms, its tome lifts and riffles and its writing
  blazes, the spell's circle opens in its hand, and **1.4 seconds** later the spell goes off.
- **Spells in turn.** In each phase it casts its spells in a fixed order, round and round, 2.75 to 4.25 seconds apart
  (2 to 3.5 in the last phase).

### Its phases and spells

It changes phase at two thirds and one third of its health (267 and 133).

| Phase | Boss bar says | Runes | What it does (at normal strength, before its power) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** (400 to 267) | Splitting Frost Bolt | Bolt · Frost · Split | Three bolts; each hit does 5 freeze damage, freezes and slows (Slowness III for 4 seconds) |
| | Shock Rain | Rain · Shock | 5 strikes from the sky around you over 2 seconds, 4 lightning damage each, arcing to one more |
| | Arcane Orb | Orb · Harm | A slow orb drifting 20 blocks forward, striking everything within 2 blocks once a second for 7 |
| **2** (267 to 133) | Volleying Fire Crescent | Crescent · Fire · Volley | Three crescent slashes in quick succession, each 5 fire damage and burning |
| | Wide Venom Zone | Zone · Venom · Widen | A wide field of poison at your feet for 6 seconds (Poison II and 2 damage, every second) |
| | Greater Arcane Blitz | Blitz · Harm · Amplify | It flashes up to 8 blocks forward, striking everything it passes for 7, and half as much again |
| | Splitting Chill Mine | Mine · Chill · Split | Three hidden mines that go off when you step near: slowness and a little damage |
| **3** (133 to 0) | Arcane-Chill Domain | Domain · Harm · Chill | A 9-block domain round itself for 6 seconds: everything inside is struck every second and slowed |
| | Dismantle Barrage | Barrage · Dismantle | 8 blows in a second, each three slashes straight through armour |
| | Sonic Boom Beam | Beam · Sonic Boom | A beam doing 16 damage that ignores armour |
| | Frost-Shock Bolt | Bolt · Frost · Frost · Shock | A bolt of two frosts and a shock |
| | **Sunfall** | a secret spell | One time in four, instead of its next spell (see below) |

#### Sunfall

In its last phase, one time in four the Archivist casts **Sunfall**, one of the ten
[secret spells]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}), instead of its next spell. A wide orange circle marks
the ground around you, the boss bar says *casting Sunfall*, and the circle in its hand spells Sunfall out rune by rune,
for anyone who can read circles. When it goes off, **everyone within 40 blocks learns Sunfall's riddle** in their
Grimoire.

Sunfall weighs far more than a Shield can hold, so a Shield won't stop it unless you
[parry]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}) it at the last moment. Get out of the orange circle.

### Rewriting its Cord

At two thirds and one third of its health the Archivist **rewrites its Cord**: pages tear loose from its tome in a
burst, it throws its arms wide with the book open over its head and the pages whirling far out, and for **2.5 seconds
it can't be hurt**. The boss bar says *rewriting its Cord*, and everyone within 48 blocks reads:

- the first time: *"The Archivist tears out a page and rewrites its Cord."*
- the second time: *"The Archivist burns its last pages. Its Cord blazes with new runes."*

Each time it calls up **two Runebound** from the stacks, about 5 blocks away: a skeleton and a pillager, carrying spells
from their usual lists. The second time they're **Adepts**. Then it starts its new phase's spells from the first.

{: .note }
Unlike the three dimension bosses, the Archivist has no floor on a phase. One huge blow can carry it from the first
phase straight past a third of its health: it then rewrites once and goes straight to its last phase.

### Strategy

- **Leave fire at home.** It's immune to burning. Frost, storm, void, arcane and the rest all work, and blasts like
  Explode still land.
- **Fight at range.** It blinks away whenever you close in, so bolts, beams and lances are worth more than Touch or
  Burst. Its own best spells (Sonic Boom Beam, Dismantle Barrage, the Blitz) want you close or in a line.
- **Watch the boss bar.** It names every spell 1.4 seconds before it lands. Step out of a Zone, sidestep a bolt, don't
  stand where a Rain or Mine is marked.
- **Break line of sight.** It can't start a spell without seeing you. The eight pillars round the arena are cover.
- **Shield up** against its bolts and orbs. See [Shields and Parrying]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}).
- **Don't waste a big spell** while it's rewriting: it can't be hurt for those 2.5 seconds. Use them to kill the two
  Runebound it calls, before they add their spells to its own.
- **Answer its Domain.** In the last phase its Domain fills 9 blocks round it for 6 seconds. Step out, or open a
  Domain of your own and break its one (a monster's Domain is strong, so bring a strong one). See
  [Playing Together]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}).
- **Get clear of Sunfall's circle**, or parry it.

### When it falls

Slain, the Archivist sinks into its robe with a flash of gold. It drops, where it dies:

| Drop | |
|---|---|
| **A Tier IV rune** | One its killer doesn't know yet (any, if they know them all): Domain, Sonic Boom, Wither, Dragon Breath, Summon, Starfall, Hollow, Infinity, Reversal, Stasis or Rewind |
| **2 Torn Pages** | |
| **3 Mana Crystals** | |
| **A greater staff** | 50% chance: a Greater Staff of any of the ten elements. See [Casting Gear]({{ '/gear/' | relative_url }}) |
| **200 experience** | |

And for the players around it:

- **The Last Page**: everyone within 64 blocks earns this feat, worth **2,000 mana** toward the next Heart Circle. It's
  also the feat the **8th Heart Circle** asks for. See [Heart Circles]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}).
- **The 7th Heart Circle's boss breakthrough**, for everyone nearby: *"A boss has fallen. Your heart can now break
  through to the 7th Circle."*
- The advancement *The Last Page* (500 experience and 3 Mana Crystals).

Tier IV runes need an Echo Cord to cast.

## The chests

### The Hall of Shelves chest

| Pool | What |
|---|---|
| **2 to 3 runes** | Each one is about 3 in 4 a Tier II rune, 1 in 4 a Tier III rune, and about 1 in 64 a rune of the world that Archive libraries hold: Echolocate, Infest, Fangs, Treasure Sense or If Wounded |
| **1 to 2 of** | A Torn Page (the likeliest), 2 to 5 Blank Runes, 1 to 3 books, 3 to 9 Lapis Lazuli, a Mana Crystal, or 2 to 6 amethyst shards |
| **Sometimes** | 20% chance of the Tome of the Fifth Page or one of the four foci (Haste, Thrift, the Deep Well, Echoes). See [Casting Gear]({{ '/gear/' | relative_url }}) |

### The Vault

Past the Arcane and Life door off the arena: two chests with a Wellstone between them. Each chest holds:

| Pool | What |
|---|---|
| **1 Tier IV rune** | One of Domain, Sonic Boom, Wither, Dragon Breath, Summon, Starfall, Hollow, Infinity, Reversal, Stasis or Rewind |
| **1 to 2 Tier III runes** | Any craftable Tier III rune |
| **2 to 3 of** | 1 to 2 Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, 2 to 6 gold ingots, 1 to 3 diamonds, or an echo shard |
| **Sometimes** | 45% chance of casting gear: the Tome of the Fifth Page, one of the four foci, or a greater staff of any element |

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wellstone.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A Wellstone, a dark block banded with violet light, awake on a ley line with circles turning on the ground around it" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A Wellstone, awake on a ley line.</span>

**The Wellstone** is yours to take: mine it with a pickaxe. Set on a ley line it wakes and quickens everyone's mana
near it. See [Ley Lines and the Wellstone]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}).

A server can make runes, Torn Pages, Mana Crystals and gear more or less common in chests.
