# The Margin Between Places

An original three-page Archive lesson attributed to **Ilyra Venn, Third Copyist**. This is newly authored lore, not a claim that the existing Archive already contained this text. Its locations use the established shelves, arena, lectern and Archivist. The contradiction explains a mechanical limit: the focus is a separate origin, but it cannot carry a cast through a broken sightline.

## Page one: the record

The Archive's register says its last copyists stood inside the arena's circle. The Archivist copied every word. The margin survived because nobody called it a word.

Ilyra Venn  
Third Copyist

## Page two: the copyist

I was not inside the circle. I left my mark there and stepped between the shelves. The spell departed from the mark. When a fallen book hid it, the line closed. The register calls this cowardice. I call it a second place to stand.

## Page three: the exercise

Thread Relay + Harm, Frost or Shock. Cast sets a focus within 8 blocks. Release the key, move, aim; Cast again within 4s. Cost: 24 + effect, once; no refund. Rest: 8s. Keep both sightlines clear.

Cord > Grimoire > Relay: controls and practice.

## Delivery and memory

The authoritative text is generated by `tools/generate_relay_lesson.py`. The physical item is a written book with three pages, an author and a distinct item model. It carries no entitlement. Server-validated access to a visible, nearby, spent and quiet Archive Lectern, with the player's existing Archivist feat and active VIII, grants a permanent verified copying receipt. Copying does not teach Relay or complete its practice. That receipt permits an ordered three-page reading now or later through the Grimoire; the final page teaches Relay while active VIII and the victory are still present.

The Grimoire stores separate permanent verified-copy, learned-study, practice and physical-book-copy records using its existing saved, private, death-copied attachment. Reading sessions are separate, transient, bound to the exact connected player body, level and nonce, and discarded on interruption. Once copying is verified, the source lectern and Archivist no longer participate in reading admission. Ordinary missing-boss rearm and respawn remain unchanged, including for legacy lecterns without the later slain flag. The copied receipt survives save/load, death and reconnect; a fresh reading starts at page one. The final action must follow all three pages. No packet accepts a learned bit or practice-completion claim.

The practical screen adds the current bindings, full costs, interruption rules and optional Dummy exercise. It can scroll at small GUI sizes and supports the mouse wheel and arrow/Page Up/Page Down keys. The book uses action names instead of fixed key letters, so rebinding cannot make its printed instructions wrong.

Learning and practice grant no XP, condensed mana or affinity. The lesson does not alter existing Archive rewards. Inventory space is optional for reading and learning, and the complete three pages remain available in the Grimoire after the physical book is lost.

## Acceptance status

The candidate requires the combined compile and focused rule checks, followed by native survival/persistence, forged-packet, full-inventory, copied-but-unfinished reconnect, unchanged boss rearm, repeated/interrupted reading and GUI-scale acceptance. Documentation and source review alone are not native validation.
