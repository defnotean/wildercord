# The Fusion Altar

Once you know a rune, every further copy used to be useless. The Fusion Altar turns spare runes
into stronger ones, new ones, or a whole spell in a single rune.

**Craft it** from 4 Amethyst Blocks, 4 Deepslate Tiles and a Lodestone (tiles in the corners,
amethyst on the sides, the lodestone in the middle). Right-click it to open it. Put runes in the
three sockets round the middle, and a catalyst in the middle socket: the altar works out which
fusion you mean, shows what it will make and what it costs, and the button does it. Fusions are
paid in XP levels (free in creative). The result waits in the result socket; take it out before the
next fusion.

## Upgrade: three of the same rune

Three copies of one rune, at the same rank and with nothing in the middle, make that rune's next
rank.

| Rank | Power | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| I | as crafted | |
| II | +25% | 2 XP levels |
| III | +50% | 5 XP levels (three rank II runes) |

- Right-click a ranked rune to learn it. That rune is then stronger **in every spell it's
  threaded in**, at the same mana cost. Ranked runes shimmer, and their name says their rank
  ("Fire Rune II").
- Only effects with power have ranks (Fire, Harm, Heal, Shock...). For effects measured in levels,
  like Swift's Speed or Haste, and for mining tiers, rank III counts as one Amplify.
- The Cord screen shows your rank in each rune's tooltip, and the readout names it: "A bolt: Fire II
  (+25% power)".

## Combine: two effects, an amethyst shard

Two effects of the right elements, with an amethyst shard in the middle, make a new fused effect
for 3 XP levels. Any effect of an element counts: Ember works as well as Fire.

| Elements | Makes | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Fire + Wind | **Firestorm** | Sets targets alight for 6 s and deals 4 damage; the fire leaps to every enemy within 2 blocks |
| Fire + Frost | **Steam** | 4 damage and Blindness for 3 s |
| Fire + Earth | **Magma** | The ground under the target burns for 4 s: 2 damage a second to enemies standing on it |
| Storm + Wind | **Tempest** | A lightning strike for 8 damage and a gale that hurls targets away |
| Storm + Fire | **Plasma** | 9 damage, half of it straight through armour |
| Storm + Frost | **Hail** | Three hailstones of 2 damage, and Slowness II for 4 s |
| Frost + Earth | **Glacier** | Frozen in place for 2 s (1 s on players) |
| Life + Void | **Lifesteal** | 5 damage, and you heal for what it dealt |
| Wind + Void | **Warp** | You and the target swap places; an enemy is left with Slowness II for 2 s |
| Life + Earth | **Bloom** | Regeneration II for 6 s to allies, and plants grow around them |
| Life + Storm | **Surge** | Speed I and Strength I for 8 s |
| Arcane + Void | **Nullify** | Strips an enemy's good effects, or an ally's bad effects |

Fused runes are Tier III (an Amethyst Cord or better) and can't be crafted or found. They take
modifiers like any effect, and can themselves be ranked up. The first time you make each one it goes
into your Grimoire (with a little condensed mana); the Grimoire lists the ones you haven't found as
`??? + ???` with a hint of one element.

## Tie a Knot: a Blank Rune and string

Put a Blank Rune in a socket and string in the middle, and the panel lists your spells. Pick one and
it's tied into a **Knot**: one rune holding the whole spell.

- It costs 1 XP level per rune inside (a Knot's runes included), at least 2.
- A Knot takes **one socket** and costs **10% less mana** than the runes inside it.
- Knots can hold Knots, 2 deep. A Knot inside a Knot is discounted twice (19% in all).
- **Anyone can learn a Knot**, even without knowing the runes inside: give your signature spell to a
  friend. Its tooltip shows the whole spell, rune by rune, and it keeps the name you gave the spell.
- A Knot is sealed: a modifier inside changes only the Knot's own runes, and one outside can't reach
  in. Its tier is the highest tier inside, so it needs a Cord that could hold every rune in it.
- A spell with Imbue can't be tied, and Knots can't be passives.
- Your learned Knots are in the Codex under **All**; thread them like any rune. The readout
  explains the spell as if it were written out, and adds a line for the Knot.

## Feats

- **Honed**: rank up a rune.
- **Fusion**: fuse two effects into a new one.
- **Knotted**: tie a spell into a Knot.
