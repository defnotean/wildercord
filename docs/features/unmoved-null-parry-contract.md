## Unmoved: immediate paid defence, delayed offence

Preserve successful-admission defensive onset. The original-body Unmoved ward is granted once during finalized paid admission, including the original EMPTY route. Its deadline remains acceptance time plus eighty ticks with the existing expiry convention; release at tick six cannot refresh it.

The transaction must:

1. Consume a real guard/string proof, retain its original owner lifetime and reserve any clash normally.
2. Install the pending token and shared recovery before callback-capable payment.
3. Commit price/rest once and lower only the original earned guard.
4. After callback-bearing eligibility checks, recheck the exact pending token, original admission, guard receipt and original owner. Do not capture a new owner lifetime after a world-changing callback.
5. Finalize a one-use internal defence grant, then recheck identity after any callback-capable defensive mutation before broadcasting or scheduling surviving offence.

Cancellation during payment/validation leaves the ordinary paid rest/recovery but grants no ward. Cancellation after finalization stops offence while retaining the already-paid defence, original deadline and rest/recovery. No refund, fresh deadline or second payment is allowed. Zero-price modifiers still have one logical admission and one rest/grant. An already-paid cancellation is terminally accepted, preventing a later clash-forfeit path from charging again.

Clash reservation grants nothing. A valid win admits and pays once; loss, stale transport or invalid final admission follows the existing forfeiture rules without defence. Duplicate or wrong-key resumes cannot grant or perform again.

The offensive release retains one 1.0× weapon hit, the existing outward impulse 1.6 with vertical 0.4 and existing player/boss limits. The victim hold still waits at least ten ticks for ground/water, times out at forty, and requests twenty ticks of hold with existing PvP limits. That released landing watcher is independent of the player's sixteen-tick recovery.

### Overlapping protection

`ArtWards.harden` is also used by Unmoving Mountain's twenty-five-tick refresh. The new design uses independently owned leases and one effective, nonstacking knockback modifier. A shorter refresh cannot shorten Unmoved's eighty-tick lease; expiry/removal of either source cannot remove the other's valid protection. Every affected caller must receive independent review.

The ward/knockback lease is exact-body and cannot rebind after death, respawn, disconnect, replacement or world retirement. Stale cleanup cannot remove a newer lease or replacement body's modifier.

Vanilla Resistance is an already-applied effect and merges with potion/Dominion protection. Grant ordinary Resistance I for eighty ticks once, preserve native merging/duration, and never blanket-remove Resistance during lease cleanup. Its state is reported separately: independent or merged protection can legitimately remain after the Unmoved lease ends. Do not reapply an old grant to a replacement body.

## Target acquisition: preserve current rules

Current `ArtKit.attacker(..., 4)` priority is:

1. STRUCK: the live, harmable server-observed struck body. There is no explicit distance, cone or LOS cap.
2. CAUGHT: the live, harmable caught attacker, within six blocks inclusive. No LOS/cone test.
3. NEAREST: the nearest harmable body in the existing four-block, 120-degree cone, retaining its body-width and vertical allowances.
4. EMPTY.

Capture the selected primary and route at admission. Do not silently retarget at release. Revalidate each original route without importing a six-block blanket cap or LOS requirement. Intentionally distant STRUCK selection must be documented and tested.

`ArtKit.harmable` currently adds LOS when a committed aim exists. This migration must avoid that incidental behavior change consistently in selection, hit/control helpers and Null's collateral query. Use narrow per-art policy, never a global LOS relaxation. The existing four authored earned counters keep their current contracts.

## Null Parry: independent radial release

No ward or damage occurs at admission. On a still-valid owner release:

- Query the original six nearest eligible bodies within radius three plus body half-width, vertical range −1/+2.5. Exclude the primary only after that query; do not refill its slot.
- Preserve pulse-before-primary order and no-LOS collateral: 0.25× weapon damage and the original 0.5/0.15 shove.
- If the captured original primary remains valid, apply its 1.0× hit and the original surviving-target effects: SHADOWED for eighty ticks, hold for eight, silence for sixty. Player silence stays capped at thirty with one-hundred-tick repeat rest; bosses retain interruption without silence.
- EMPTY admission still pulses. Loss of a selected primary also leaves the independent pulse intact while only that primary strike and primary-dependent hooks whiff. There is no replacement primary.
- Owner interruption, death or invalid-world release cancels all unreleased offence. Revalidate current party/eligibility for each actual pulse victim.

Keep generic completion for an actually performed valid area release distinct from missing-primary hit hooks. Preserve ordinary passives, rune/resonance processing, shared damage budgets and existing post-hit predicates. Applied status effects retain their own ordinary lifetimes; cosmetic callbacks never acquire damage authority.
