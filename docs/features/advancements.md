# Advancements

Wildercord has its own advancement tab, set on dark indigo stone with faint rune script. It leads
a new player from their first Blank Rune to the 8th Heart Circle and the bosses of the four
dungeons: 74 advancements below the root. Everything here is generated by
`tools/generate_assets.py` (the `ADVANCEMENTS` list); don't edit the JSON by hand.

Frames: **task** unless marked *goal* or **challenge**. *Hidden* ones stay out of sight until earned.
XP is the experience reward. "Blank Runes" is a stack of 8, "a Mana Crystal" is 1, "Mana Crystals" is 3.

## The tree

**Wildercord** (root): craft a Blank Rune, or wear a Cord.

### Cords (from the root)
| Advancement | Needs | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Tied On | Wear a Twine Cord | 10 XP |
| Copper Wire | Wear a Copper Cord | 20 XP |
| Singing Stone (*goal*) | Wear an Amethyst Cord | 50 XP |
| Echoes of the Deep (*goal*) | Wear an Echo Cord | 100 XP, Blank Runes |

A better Cord also counts for the ones before it.

### Heart Circles (from First Words)
Heartbeat (1st Circle, 20 XP), Deeper Wells (2nd, 30), Mana Skin (3rd, 40), Turning as One (4th, 50),
Flow (*goal*, 5th, 75), Brighter Still (*goal*, 6th, 100 XP and Blank Runes), Overflow (*goal*, 7th,
150 XP and a Mana Crystal), **Archmage** (**challenge**, 8th, 500 XP and Mana Crystals).
Off the 1st Circle: Awakening (innate rune, feat, 25 XP), then Mirrorfrost (*hidden*, feat, 50 XP).

### Shields and imbuing (from First Words)
Spellguard (your Shield stops a spell, feat, 20 XP), then Shieldbreaker (feat, 25 XP) and Parry
(raise a Shield at the last moment and turn a spell back on its caster, feat, 25 XP); Imbuer
(imbue an item or block, feat, 20 XP) then Tripwire (one of your glyphs goes off, 25 XP).

## How they're granted

The mod registers six criteria (`advancement/WildercordTriggers.java`):

| Criterion | Conditions | Met when |
|---|---|---|
| `wildercord:feat` | `feat` | the Grimoire holds `feat:<id>` |
| `wildercord:grimoire` | `entry`, or `prefix` + `count`, or `prefix` + `all` | the Grimoire holds that entry, that many with the prefix, or all of them (bar the innate-only and multiplayer-only feats) |
| `wildercord:heart_circle` | `level` | that many circles formed, or more |
| `wildercord:runes_known` | `count`, or `all` | that many runes known (Knots and runes of add-ons that aren't loaded don't count, as for the Heart Circles), or every rune you can find or make and your own innate one |
| `wildercord:cord` | `tier` | wearing that Cord or a better one |
| `wildercord:moment` | `moment` | just happened: `cast`, `long_cast`, `passive`, `glyph`, `runebound_adept` |

All but `moment` look at what the player already has, so firing them again is always safe.
`Advancements.sync` fires them all when a player joins and after a data pack reload, which is how
players who got somewhere before the tree existed catch up. They're also fired straight away from
`Grimoire.unlock` (feats, reactions, secrets, riddles), `HeartCircles.form` (and `/wildercord
circles`), and `Spellbooks.set`/`setCord` (runes learned, Cords worn). "The Buried Library" uses
vanilla's `minecraft:location` with the `wildercord:archive` structure, and the root also takes
vanilla's `inventory_changed` for a Blank Rune.

A feat with no advancement does nothing. To give a new feat one, add a line to `ADVANCEMENTS` in
`tools/generate_assets.py`, e.g.

```python
feat_adv("parry", "shields/spellguard", rune("deflect"), xp=25)
```

The title and description default to the feat's name and text in `Feats.java`. `AdvancementTreeTest`
fails the build while any feat lacks an advancement.
