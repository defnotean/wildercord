# Parrying and wild magic

## Parrying with a Shield

A Shield raised at the last moment doesn't just stop a spell: it **parries** it.

- **The timing.** Cast a Shield no more than **7 ticks (about a third of a second)** before a harmful
  spell reaches you, or while a spell is already flying at you and close (within the 7 blocks where
  a Shield's circles would appear). The same goes for an ally you raise a Shield on with Burst or
  Nova. A Shield renewed by a passive never parries.
- **Weight doesn't matter.** A parry turns any spell, even one costlier than the Shield that would
  otherwise shatter it (the Archivist's Sunfall included). The Shield is spent either way.
- **Flying spells turn back.** Bolts, Arcs, Sparks, Wisps, Comets, Clusters and their shards turn
  round where they meet the circle and fly back at whoever cast them, **25% faster** (a lobbed Arc
  flies straight), steering after them. They're yours now: they hit the original caster with the
  spell's own effects and power, and can be parried back again.
- **Everything else is negated and answered.** A beam, a blast, a Zone's pulse or anything that
  lands at once is stopped at you, and a **counter-burst** of your Shield's light strikes its caster
  (if they're within 32 blocks): half of what the spell was worth, 4 + 0.4 per mana it cost, at most
  12 damage, scaled by the caster's own power (and by PvP scaling between players).
- **Feedback.** The circles hold and flush gold, rings of light race out across them, a bright chime
  rings, your view kicks, and **"Parried!"** shows above the hotbar. The caster is told their spell
  was parried. Your first parry is the **Parry** feat.
- It works against Runebound, the Archivist and other players alike.
- **Only a spell arriving is parried.** Damage that lingers on after a spell landed (a Soulfire or
  Blood Moss burn ticking on, a Sandstorm's or Eclipse's pulses, a Cinderheart's heat) meets a
  Shield like any other spell, blocked or breaking through by weight, but is never parried: a Shield
  timed to a burn's tick doesn't turn it.

## Wild magic on an overcast

When you overcast (cast again within 2 s without the mana, cracking a Heart Circle to pay), the
spell may **surge**: **20%** at least, plus 15 points for every full mana bar the spell went past
the mana you had, up to **50%**. A surge announces itself with a swirl of its colour around you and
a coloured line above the hotbar. Your first surge is the **Wild Magic** feat.

| Outcome | Weight | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Twice | 10 | The spell goes off, and again 0.4 s later. |
| Element | 9 | Every harmful effect swaps to one of a random other element (nearest tier). |
| Grand | 8 | Every radius in the spell is widened twice (+125%); a spell with no radius comes out 50% stronger. |
| Butterflies | 8 | Nothing is cast: a harmless shower of butterflies of light and fireworks. |
| Heal All | 7 | Nothing is cast: everything within 8 blocks, friend and foe, heals 6. |
| Blink | 7 | You're thrown 3-6 blocks onto safe, solid ground, then the spell goes off. |
| Wisps | 7 | The spell goes off, with four violet wisps (Harm at half power) chasing whatever's near. |
| Levitate | 7 | The spell goes off; everything within 6 blocks floats up for 1.2 s (players get Slow Falling for 6 s). |
| Stray | 6 | The spell goes off at a random creature within 16 blocks you can see, whoever it is. |
| Slow Time | 6 | The spell goes off; everything within 8 blocks, you included, is slowed (Slowness III) for 2 s. |
| Backfire | 6 | Nothing is cast, and you take up to 4 damage (never below half a heart). |
| Free Recast | 7 | The spell goes off, and your next cast within 3 s costs no mana and ignores its cooldown. |
| Ward | 6 | The spell goes off, and a 16-mana Shield settles on you for 10 s (unless a stronger Shield is already up, which stays). |

Secret spells never get the outcomes that rewrite runes (Element, Grand). Wild magic never changes a
block itself (the spell's own effects still ask permission for every block, as always) and never
kills its caster.
