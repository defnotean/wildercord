# Reweave: Ebb Ledger

This is one Circle XII capability in the development source. Its precursor mechanism passed natively on public `6c750309`. The complete ordinary-player diagnostic passes on [`3f8b54e1`](https://github.com/defnotean/wildercord/actions/runs/37524600196) in 56.35 seconds: an eligible fixture character actually studies the three Grimoire pages, equips through Codex clicks, then places and rewrites through rebound keys. This does not claim a naturally played boss victory or a full-release result.

## Learn and equip

Circle XII retains its existing 300,000 lifetime mana, 20 Runebound and permanent Low Tide breakthrough. Open your Cord, then Grimoire → Master studies → Ebb Ledger. Low Tide holders from older saves can retrieve it directly; another Tide Scribe victory or an intact altar is unnecessary. Read three ordered pages and choose Learn Reweave with an active Twelfth Circle. Five minutes is the reading-session timeout, not a mandatory wait.

Thread exactly Reweave then Harm in an ordinary Echo Cord slot. The copied lesson and learned shape persist through death and reconnect. A physical book is lore, never authority to grant the study. A quiet, defeated Tide core offers an optional original book and one replacement; full inventory and lost books do not remove the Grimoire lesson. Importing a spell code cannot grant learning.

## Cast and rewrite

Use the existing rebindable Cast control, or a configured direct-slot control. A fresh press at visible floor within eight blocks pays once and places a radius-two disc. Release the key, aim, and press again to rewrite it into one fixed horizontal seven-by-1.25-block lane from its original center.

The cost is 32 shape + 8 Harm = 40 total base mana before normal discounts. Rest is exactly eight seconds, shared across every Reweave row. The four half-Harm beats stay at 0.4, 1.4, 2.4 and 3.4 seconds; the field expires at four seconds. The rewrite's eight-tick warning is silent: due beats are consumed, never replayed. A useful rewrite must be requested by age 60 ticks. An invalid or blocked rewrite preserves the current field.

Sneak with a fresh Cast press on the Reweave row explicitly cancels without refund or rest reset. Menus, lost focus, changing rows, damage, gear and ordinary control effects preserve released beats. Returning from a menu requires a real key release before another action. New conversion still requires active XII, Low Tide, the study, an Echo Cord and the original exact row. Other committed actions may prevent that request.

The original body owns the field: death, replacement, disconnection, dimension departure, spectator mode, shutdown or a reversed clock retires it. Actual spectator entry is latched at the mode transition, including admission callbacks; it cannot be restored by returning in the same tick. A restarted server retains the saved rest and study, never the old field.

## Counterplay and limits

Both the center and each victim must be visible from the caster within 16 blocks; the center must also see the victim. The lane has fixed aim and its full narrow corridor must be clear. Loaded cells, world borders and the original known ward-room identity remain mandatory. Unknown structure references refuse admission without loading them. Temporary cover, range loss or unresolved wards consume due beats; only later original beats can resume.

Targets use their feet within the footprint and up to two blocks above it. No terrain, residue or Rune-Seal writes are authorized. All beats and any admitted immediate Harm hooks share the original paid allowances. Conversion keeps captured Heart power, gear, mastery and payment; ordinary rune rank, affinity, reactions and environmental impact modifiers stay live as they do for Harm. Delayed effects cannot reopen the paid beat window.

No extra effects, modifiers, links, Knots, woven runes, passives, scrolls, imbues, copies, free recasts or overcasting. This is a field rewrite, not another Tidewrit water-wall variant.

The HUD shows remaining time and beats, the active bound control, and whether a rewrite is still useful. The original disc, silent warning and fixed lane are visible to nearby observers with bounded essential geometry. Optional practice records a real rewritten-lane wound on a Training Dummy outside the old disc; it grants no extra resource or unlock.
