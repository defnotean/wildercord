# Spell presentation coverage by stage

## Current development contract

`FlightBodies` explicitly admits 170 distinct effect identities across six families:
Fire 29, Frost 32, Storm 20, Wind 27, Earth 33 and Life 29. These counts include
the six field signatures in this development batch. They describe preparation
and travelling projectile coverage, not completed ability lifecycles.

The current runtime contains 265 effect runes. Coverage for the remaining effect
identities must be implemented and tested before their legacy travelling layer
is replaced. A mixed group with an uncovered identity retains its fallback.

Bloodboil has a Fire preparation recipe and a travelling recipe case, but it is
not admitted by the canonical Fire moving-body contract. Its Blood-family
delivery and ingredients still need review; it is not counted as a 171st fully
covered identity. Likewise, isolated Void and Life outcome drafts are outside
the build and contribute no accepted coverage.

## Acceptance is separate for each stage

| Stage | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation | Exact runtime roster, distinct authored recipes, rear circle placement, visible front materials, bounded emission, Full and Minimal quality |
| Moving projectile | Actual paid casts, ingredient preservation, directional movement, retirement and uncovered-mix fallback |
| Impact | Actual successful gameplay change and its authored material response; a generic hit event cannot prove success |
| Persistent outcome | Actual ward, field, status, construct or summon lifetime; renewal, refusal, cancellation and recipient synchronization |
| Sound | Distinct authored cue and outcome, decoded quality checks, actual event routing and owner-recipient checks |
| Alternate delivery | Each applicable beam, rain, Self, trap, summon and other delivery acts at its real location and communicates counters |

Life 29 preparation and travelling projectile acceptance is recorded in
`life-choreography.md`. Its individually authored impact, persistent outcome
and sound work remains separate. The six field signatures have narrower
gameplay and presentation acceptance in `../field-signature-fusions.md`.
Neither establishes complete coverage for all 265 effect lifecycles.

The sustained mixed-material benchmark is a separate local measurement, not
proof of every effect's performance, multiplayer correctness or ecosystem cost.
See `../living-world-performance.md` for its workload and limits.
