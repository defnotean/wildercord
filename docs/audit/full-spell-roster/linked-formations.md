# Linked formation origins

The engine emits an isolated formation after admitting a linked segment through its existing
alive/segment-budget checks. This includes scheduled Delay/Pulse/Echo execution, successful conditions
and impact continuations entering `runSegment`. Failed conditions and cancelled casts remain absent.
The ordinary initial sender still owns the complete rear glyph; continuation packets contain no glyph.

Self, Orbit and Trail follow the caster because those deliveries do. Other non-caster triggers carry
a fixed position and normalized direction. Ground-centered modes use the actual server aim/ground
resolver; Rain is twelve blocks above that point. Fixed preparations do not follow subsequent caster
turning. Domain's floor rings and linked Rain/Zone/Ring preparation use the fixed anchor.

The packet codec validates finite vectors and direction magnitude, alongside existing list/string,
scale, mode and range bounds. Its wire format changes: use this same build on clients and server.
Effects precede modifier details in the compact visual list to avoid losing effect identity behind
a long run of modifiers. Existing event/emission caps still apply; this is not a measured speedup.

No gameplay delay, price, damage or delivery was added. These two-beat client preparations accompany
the engine's existing release; they do not yet provide a complete pre-release launch animation for
every continuation. The existing impact/release presentation remains in place.

## Native verification scope

`GroupFormationTest` retains its ten ordinary placement cases and paid mixed Bolt/Self cast. Added:

- A paid Survival Delay/Self/Fireward cast: no premature assembly, then a caster-attached continuation
  with no second glyph.
- A real paid Bolt/Ember/On Hit/Burst/Fire cast against a creature: the projectile's impact creates
  the linked Burst at the foe.
- Nine controlled trigger fixtures through the actual admitted segment path: Burst, Ring, Domain,
  Zone, Rain, Bolt, Self, Orbit and Trail. Decoded payload/client canvas positions agree with the
  server resolver or trigger; fixed direction is preserved.
- Successful and refused If Sneaking gates.

The trigger fixtures are constructed segments, not nine full paid casts. Native scroll use,
stored/reflected initial casts, secret-specific staging, every remaining link, all terrain/turning
edge cases, shaders, real multiplayer and sustained profiling remain separate work.

Actual-game captures and the tested JAR are in `artifacts/review/linked-formations`. The full
spell/Aura identity and living-world goal remain active; no content-count target is advanced here.
