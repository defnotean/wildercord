## Verification scope

StormFormationTest exercises all twenty through native encoded formation packets in Full and Minimal settings, finite/evolving/nonduplicate recipes, ten mixed-material recipes, rear glyph and front material positions, and actual framebuffer differences. Ripple rejects electrical material in its recipe. Paid Survival Bolt/Shock and Self/Surge exercise production staging; Surge must grant Speed and Strength after release.

Fixtures use Bolt as a controlled carrier. This is preparation coverage, not twenty complete effect executions or full launch/travel/impact coverage. Existing gameplay, costs, sounds and aftermath remain unchanged. Full delivery/link pairings, remote multiplayer, shaders and sustained profiling remain open.
