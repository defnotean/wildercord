# Backdraft and Rooted Parry: earned counter source candidate

This change gives two existing Third Arts their own committed Classic and articulated body/hand timelines. It adds no ability, teacher reward, lesson entitlement or equipped slot. Existing Ember/Verdant method access and Edge progression remain authoritative. Style playback IDs 20 and 21 append to the existing shared IDs 0–2 and style IDs 3–19; Master Forms uses a separate protocol. Source coverage is 19 of 50 committed style forms, with 31 remaining. Nine of those style forms have articulated clips (shared clips are counted separately).

## Gameplay contract

- Backdraft: four-tick windup, fourteen-tick exposed recovery. Its existing 0.75-weapon plus caught-damage bonus (bonus capped at one weapon), 60-tick fire, 0.7 knockback and 4.5-block/75-degree half-damage cone remain unchanged.
- Rooted Parry: six-tick windup, sixteen-tick exposed recovery. Its existing 0.95-weapon primary, 30-tick root, 2.5-block nearby 0.3-weapon thorn pulse and slow remain unchanged. Self-healing remains clamp(0.75 × caught damage, 3, 6), through the existing mending cap.
- Both retain Edge, the first ordinary attack within sixteen ticks of an actual perfect Aura Guard, including while sneaking, eight base Aura and eighty-tick art rest. Existing modifiers and player/control caps still apply.
- The actual guard earns immutable owner/body/world/weapon/attacker/damage evidence before reflection or stagger callbacks. The ledger snapshots it with the actual stroke, including the attack-before-Punch path. New guards cannot replace a previous stroke's catch. Claimed COUNTER bits or bare generic cues cannot create earned evidence.
- The request consumes its original one-use ledger suffix. A refused readiness/resource/condition check leaves its guard and the existing ledger/refusal semantics intact. A fully admitted form pays and begins rest once, then lowers its original guard. Cancellation during the payment callback still lowers that paid guard and retains exposed recovery. Guard admission is not globally exempted.
- The original struck, caught, nearest or explicitly empty route is selected before clash entry. Paid windup captures facing; the selected body stays the originally selected body. Accepted primary bodies must be loaded, in the same world, currently hostile and visible, and within 6.5 blocks for Backdraft or 6 blocks for Rooted Parry. This intentionally hardens the previously unbounded struck route and adds explicit LOS/loaded-body checks.
- Active release revalidates the exact body and UUID, world, current relationship, LOS and finite radius. Losing a selected target is a paid whiff with no retarget, splash or heal. An explicitly accepted empty route keeps its original untargeted cone/pulse/heal and never gains a new primary.
- Immediate counter consequences recheck the original owner and selected target after damage callbacks. Resonance, etched-rune effects, completion hooks and discovery also respect the active callback boundary. Successfully released effects are not rolled back by a later ordinary interruption or weapon change. Nested rune effects retain their ordinary released lifetime after the synchronous counter frame; cosmetic timers never apply damage or healing.

## Clash audit and deliberate correction

The existing clash rhythm has beats at ticks 10, 19 and 28, closes its last enlarged window at 33, settles for four ticks, and grants at most six network ticks: serverLength() is exactly 43. Clashes.tick forcibly resolves at that age and terminates earlier for dead/removed/world-invalid sides or changed party/trial admission. There is no indefinite hold contract.

The old Held record stored only player/art/marks. Winning release queried AuraGuard.caught again, but that mutable cache expires at COUNTER_TICKS 16 + SEEN_SLACK 20 = 36 ticks. Consequently a normal 43-tick winning clash lost its original caught bonus/attacker and could instead use a newer guard or target. The correction intentionally preserves the original earned receipt through only the specific finite clash which reserved it. It never extends the sixteen-tick earning window: the real stroke must already have earned COUNTER inside that window, and the ordinary last-stroke request slack remains ten ticks. One-use reservation keys reject replay, unrelated/replaced holds, clock reversal and release after age 43. Payment stays at resolution: losers forfeit, and winners pay only their single resulting committed attempt or existing eligibility forfeit.
