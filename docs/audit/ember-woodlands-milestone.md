# Ember woodland creature and fern milestone (2026-10-04)

## Playable scope

Cinder Bailiffs are original broad four-footed territorial creatures with a ceramic breastplate, brush tail, sculpted feet and three independently hinged/slatted back vents. Their 128-square authored skin, six physical voices and actual rig are registered. A reached mature cooled fern requires40 uninterrupted browse ticks; the meal consumes one growth stage, preserves the root and grants a saved1200-tick meal rest and160-tick shelter rest. Competing animals cannot consume the same maturity twice. Saved deadlines survive a whole-world close/reopen.

The24-tick warning commits to an original direction. Three vent releases at0/6/12 ticks form an18-tick physical fan, four health per unarmored receiver once across the action,40-tick recovery and100-tick attack rest. It creates no terrain fire or burn damage. The raw living pool stops at13; saturation refuses all harm, and at most12 receiver UUIDs are retained. Source removal, world departure and action changes stop further callback-driven harm. Existing neutral animals survive Peaceful with aggression cancelled; natural Peaceful admissions refuse.

Cinder Ferns have three growth stages and distinct cooled/hot states. Ordinary fern, clay and charcoal craft a root in the inventory grid, making the loop accessible in older worlds. Actual client planting consumes that earned root. Mature cooled harvesting returns one replantable item and resets the retained root to AGE0. Ordinary harvesting works with spell terrain edits disabled while building/claim rights still apply.

Real paid Grow, Fire, Tidebreath, Frost and Freeze routes interact with the fern or a real warning. One changed fern cell is shared by a payment's linked deliveries and Echo. Cooling a Bailiff requires a real admitted warning/cold outcome. Exact mana and native cooldown checks pass with actual regeneration disabled. Per-species natural switches, mature habitat predicate and manual-spawn compatibility pass; natural distribution is not established by those predicates.
