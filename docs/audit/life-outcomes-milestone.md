## What changed

- Real health, absorption, food, durability, condition removal, changed terrain, ward admission, actual trigger and expiry observations own each response. A paid cast that changes nothing does not manufacture a successful response.
- Tissue, sap, seed coats, thorns, leaf shutters, equipment stitches, roots, berries and spores follow the actual body, slot or changed cell. Surface normals orient the authored recipes; root material stands outside opaque wall faces.
- All thirty identities are covered, including the original twenty-eight Life effects, Ashen Mercy and Pulse Ferry. The latter two retain their existing sole casting/landing voices. Fifty-six new cue/outcome sound identities and112 generated variants belong to the other twenty-eight.
- Whole groups with declared authored outcome coverage suppress the old generic impact/SELF overlays. Mixed, unknown and uncovered groups keep their fallback. Requested rear casting glyphs remain.
- Source-aware client delivery chooses Your spells or Other spells using the actual caster UUID. Minimal retains smaller authored ingredients; finite per-client decoration and queue budgets, loaded chunks, dimension, range, freshness and eye/camera clearance constrain rendering.
- Owner maps and voices prune removed bodies, old worlds and server shutdown state. Quiet passive renewals remain quiet.
- Spectators no longer consume RuneBolt, scheduled line, Touch, Chorus aim or imbued aim delivery before an eligible target. Paid Bolt/Spark/Ray/Touch regressions exercise the collision fix; actual two-singer Chorus and charged-imbuing interactions remain separate acceptance.
- Fortune displays a lucky strike only after actual final admitted health loss. A denied final gate emits no successful lucky response. Its real hostile-kill experience branch observes the actual dead victim and reward, replacing the old green flourish.
- The web/GitBook Life field chapter and its labelled journal illustration explain reading actual outcomes. The native Grimoire has learned-only material notes and a direct Magic visuals link with a return to the same journal. Native UI visual acceptance is recorded separately below.
- The strict Void alternate-flight camera follows the actual production body while retaining its original pixel thresholds. This corrects a fixture framing failure; it is not a claim that the full Linux descriptor is green.
