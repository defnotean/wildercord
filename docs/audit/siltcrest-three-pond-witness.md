# Siltcrest positive ecology witness: fixed three-pond cohort

## Why the fixture changed

The original single 4×4 pond admitted a genuine coil without guaranteeing a catch. Two unmodified native runs illustrate the difference. Run 37508370261, job 112422739613, reached coil at 58 and killed its initially healthy Cod at 73. The quarry remained horizontally still and drifted 0.075 vertically; native damage was 4, the exact bird owned the final damage, the eligible pool became 2, and appetite became 6073. Run 37514244725, job 112442954113, reached the same initial bird position and coil timing, but correctly refused two opportunities before any damage. At 73 the quarry had moved 0.749268 from the locked center and was 1.922991 from the bird. At 511 its movement was 0.558725, while its distance to the bird was 2.204831. The original gates are 0.6 and 1.8 respectively.

These observations establish ordinary prey-path variance. They do not establish a production hunting defect. The passing run is historical evidence for the original habitat and lifecycle; it does not verify the new cohort fixture.

## Fixed scope and unchanged gameplay

SiltcrestEcologyTest now supplies exactly three simultaneous copies of the original sixteen-cell 4×4 source-water pond and clay floor. Their x offsets are -20, 0, +20. Each has one original bittern and three full-health ordinary factory-created Cod. There are no sequential world retries, target overrides, AI/physics changes, fish relocation after admission, health changes, or RNG/seed selection. The central Creative observer stays in place during hunting; only camera look angles change.

The initial admission wait retains its 500-tick cap. The earliest actual native coil starts one shared 500-tick server deadline and one shared 500-advance harness allowance. Later cohorts, refusals, captures, and successful candidates cannot reset either. Native render requests are immediate; asynchronous GPU readback/file futures are drained through the same budgeted hunt loop after catch/preen evidence. All-refusal is still a test failure. Three habitats provide three fixed opportunities, not a per-pond success guarantee or a mathematical guarantee that some fish will be caught.

Actual observation checks account for the production raw prey query, whose source bounding box is inflated by 6, and native schooling queries inflated by 8. Every living fish pair across ponds stays outside the schooling bounds, every bird's raw prey query contains only its own fish, and living prey remains physically within its supplied pond. Loaded/registered entity checks are accompanied by consecutive per-server-tick entity tick-count checks. The observer begins within 32 blocks of all pond water; it is not moved for candidate captures.

## Evidence that can pass

Passive native boundary receipts identify suite, cohort, source UUID and entity ID, world, commitment epoch, admitted quarry, locked position, and actual tick. All three receipt streams are checked before selecting any winner. Every observed hit requires the original four damage against the exact admitted full-health fish, successful native return, the same final damage object, and the same responsible/direct source UUID. The source remains grounded, healthy and fixed through the commitment; sight and both original reach predicates remain required for every hit. Exact eligible population 3→2 and appetite=strikeTick+6000 are required.

A healthy, undamaged final refusal can qualify as a non-success only when the actual quarry exceeds a native distance predicate or loses line of sight. It must retain the native 200-tick failed-attempt rest and exact cancellation. Missing or malformed receipts, foreign final ownership, unexplained damage/death among any of the nine fish, observer errors, or omitted records fail the whole cohort even if another bird succeeds. This failure is latched. A proven corpse may undergo normal removal and forty-tick last-damage expiry; a new observed damage object or revival still fails.

The chosen successful source is fixed once. Candidate coil frames are named by cohort and exact epoch; only that chosen source's already captured winning epoch is referenced by the acceptance receipt. Native client pose and animation thresholds are checked in the render callback. Its own strike and preen frames must complete. No other commitment may remain unresolved when the shared hunt observation closes and daylight is introduced. The same original source then travels to a real dry covered bank within the original 420-tick window. Full save/reopen must restore that exact UUID, appetite deadline, shelter deadline and refuge position with grounded shelter still valid. The separate SiltcrestDodgePreservationTest is unchanged.

## Local validation and remaining evidence

Focused Java 25 compilation of all 20 Siltcrest gametest sources passes against the bundled Minecraft 26.3/Fabric-transformed dependencies and previously compiled mod classes. SiltcrestPresentationProbeChecks, SiltcrestPresentationWitnessChecks and SiltcrestEcologyWitnessChecks pass. The new controls cover simultaneous scope selection, all-refusal failure, valid-first/malformed-second rejection, foreign direct/responsible/final damage owners, missing damage calls, exact population counts, unobserved fish injury/death, normal corpse cleanup, unresolved-cohort closure, and both shared budget limits. These controls classify supplied receipts; their synthetic successful cases are not native gameplay proof.

The revised cohort has not yet run in a native client at this local checkpoint. Native execution must confirm actual captures, each actor's residency, selected lifecycle persistence and repeatability. No measured pass probability or universal wetland behavior is claimed. A native failure must retain all three outcomes and be diagnosed; increasing the cohort count, replacing failed worlds, or extending the shared allowance is outside this fixture design.
