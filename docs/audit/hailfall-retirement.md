# Hailfall retirement boundaries

The native released-owner matrix retains its real Punch admission, release +3 mutation,
release +5 first-stone observation, full-cloud wait, and actual reconnect check. Its ordinary
Husk fixtures now opt out of the normal Runebound admission roll individually and verify
200 health, 200 maximum health, and no Runebound attachment after insertion in either world.

The October 6 native `d3612e11` failure reached the destination victim's compound untouched
assertion in the DEATH case, after the original-world victim passed. The log did not include
the destination's health, effects or identity. A Runebound admission can change fixture
health, but the historical failing operand and cause remain unproven.

## Hailfall-specific callback continuation

Each stone now rechecks its original released owner before each snapshot victim and after
the native strike. Removed, dead or foreign-world victims receive no further stone damage
or Hailfall chill. An original owner that dies, changes world or disconnects retires its
cloud, including during a synchronous damage callback; an ordinary weapon or method change
and physical interruption preserve a live owner's independently released effect.

`HailfallDamageCallbacks`, called by the existing native released-owner suite, admits the
real art through native Punch observations. It mutates owners and both current and later
snapshot victims from the first stone's actual damage callback. Control cases preserve real
damage, slowness and frost. Its original-owner transitions include native death, respawn,
dimension change and a same-call round trip without polling the old binding in between.

## Separate shared-strike audit item

`AuraCombat.projected` and `ArtKit.Hits.raw` can finish a common admitted strike's passives,
stance and hit bookkeeping after a synchronous damage callback. Their retirement guard is
scoped to an active `ArtFields` pulse; it does not cover every released art. In particular,
the common Rime passive adds slowness independently of Hailfall's own chill/frozen step.
This remains a separate shared-strike audit item, outside this Hailfall continuation fix.

The callback probes therefore make a legitimate post-release change to Stone at +3 and
assert that released ownership remains valid before the +4 damage callback. This isolates
Hailfall's own extra chill and later victims. It does not prove that all common hit-side
effects stop after retirement. The original Rime lifecycle matrix remains unchanged.

Native execution is a required acceptance gate. A successful standalone compile does not
establish that the native callback or reconnect assertions passed.
