# WilderCord progress inventory — October 13, 2026

This report covers work since baseline `0a183589` on `defnotean/world-depth-audit`.
The full audit and dated evidence are in [the world-depth ledger](2026-10-13-world-depth.md).
Development is continuing in [draft PR #2](https://github.com/defnotean/wildercord/pull/2).
Content described as implemented is present in source; it has not been published
as a new release or installed on the live Minecraft server.

## Audit and plan

- Inventoried the main/client Java, content registrations, structures, recipes,
  loot, advancements, journal, quest state, networking, packaging and test setup.
  The starting mod contained roughly 300,000 production Java lines, 48 sites,
  sixteen Master schools, 341 registered client suites and no registered custom biome.
- Reviewed critical integration paths and separated existing features from missing
  content. This is a structural/content audit, not a claim to have manually played
  every encounter or read every line.
- Wrote a prioritized roadmap covering polish, four regional biomes, four proposed
  regional bosses, buildings, residents, investigations, legends, progression,
  accessibility, world compatibility, performance and cooperative verification.
- Created the active development goal, evidence ledger, development branch and
  draft PR. Commits are pushed as coherent checkpoints.

## New playable content

### Luminous Reedlands

- Implemented the mod's first custom Overworld biome with real climate placement,
  swamp-compatible surfaces, distinctive water/grass/foliage colours and wet banks.
- Connected existing wetland fauna, Moonreed pollination and smokehouse placement.
- Added bounded natural Moonreed placement while preserving habitat checks and
  occupied blocks. A fixed 49-chunk native census found six naturally placed reeds.
- Added automatic, persistent owner-synced field-note discovery and an illustrated guide.
- Verified saved biome identity and plants after reopening. Population density,
  broad multi-seed balance and placement frequency remain open measurements.

### Reedkeeper Landing and residents

- Added a raised survey house with mud-brick walls, prismarine roof, public shelter,
  workbench, seat, bed, fishing chest and a functional open Moonreed garden.
- Added a persistent apprentice fisher using ordinary villager trading.
- Added People discovery and four remembered conversations, with a per-player
  pause between lines so repeated interaction does not spam every story at once.
- Authored the four-page First Crossing book connecting Iona's established survey
  history, the Tideward and the Ferryman legend.
- Verified all four building orientations, clipped chunk replay, entrances,
  garden habitat, merchant interactions, resident UUID and saved conversation state.
- This is a Reedkeeper resident implementation. The proposed named Iona/Tamsin
  NPCs and repair service are still future work.

### Drowned Road investigation

- Implemented three ordered chapters: The Drowned Road, The Second Count and
  A Light for the Last Boat.
- Connected the Reedkeeper account to a new three-page boathouse passenger ledger,
  an expanded lighthouse log and the existing working lighthouse lever/lamp.
- Added real structure discoveries for all ten water sites, including the two new sites.
- Earlier visits count when their chapter becomes available. Older structures
  retain their existing furnishings and remain eligible for progress.
- Rewards total 90 XP, five Blank Runes and one Aura Shard per player, once.
- Verified real interaction packets, a dark lamp, a copied/unregistered structure,
  an unrelated lamp, spectator refusal, owner UI, exact rewards and save/reopen.

### Scout's Last Cairn investigation and Sleeping Blade history

- Added three connected chapters after The Scout's Wall: The Scout's Last Cairn,
  The Road Before Roads and An Oath at Rest.
- Connected watchtower orders, standing-stone history and an authentic Sleeping
  Blade stone. Authored two additional book pages to support the route.
- Added prerequisite ordering, earlier-visit recovery and one-time rewards.
- Made the Last Oath available to an explorer without drawing or claiming the
  sword. A copied stone cannot award that history.
- Verified actual site discovery, block interaction, book delivery, interrupted
  draws and existing blade ownership/guard behavior.

### Reed-Crowned Ferryman and the Last Crossing

- Implemented an original regional boss with an articulated reed raft, woven
  mantle, uneven crown, long oar, suspended anchor and emissive lantern.
- Added three distinct attacks: a locked oar sweep, a marked anchor circle and
  alternating wake lanes with a continuous dry safe crossing.
- Added visible ground geometry that remains readable with Minimal particles,
  explicit warnings/recoveries and a half-health pattern change.
- Added the 35-by-35 Last Crossing basin: four shallow pools, broad dry paths,
  four shore exits, lantern posts, bell shelter, instruction book and supply loot.
- Added an authentic Crossing Bell that starts the optional fight. Copied bells
  cannot summon it; repeat clicks cannot duplicate its saved guardian.
- Implemented target-loss cancellation, finite abandonment, reset after reopening,
  saved completion and replay protection for the bell and containers.
- Eligible surviving contributors receive 80 XP, a field note and a named compass
  pointing back to that exact crossing. Completed bells cannot repeat the reward.
- Verified real survival interaction, every attack and dodge, phase transition,
  departure, four orientations, chunk replay, two reopens and exact rewards.
- Verified the actual structure locator can admit the basin on unmodified
  Reedlands terrain and its foundation reaches solid support. Random-spread
  frequency and a real cooperative client tour remain unfinished.
