# 0.12 "Tempering"

Players had outgrown the world. Spells hit for roughly ten to twenty times what vanilla weapons do, a stacked guard gave
about thirteen times a player's effective health, monsters never changed, and a heart could be condensed by casting into
the air. Circles and aura stages came quickly and felt empty when they did. 0.12 answers all of that in one release.

Decisions (2026-10-09):

- About **100 hours of play to circle 20**: circle 5 in about 6 hours, circle 10 in about 25, Sovereign aura in about 45.
- **Full re-temper of existing saves**: circles and aura stages are measured again under the new thresholds, and a save
  can drop circles. Condensed mana and aura experience are kept.
- One release, not split.

## 1. Power

| What | Before | 0.12 | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spell damage allowance per hit | 96 | 40 | `SpellDamageAllowance.MAX_HIT` |
| Spell damage allowance per cast | 512 | 256 | `SpellDamageAllowance.MAX_TOTAL` |
| Allowance per point of mana | 2 + 12 flat | 1.2 + 4 flat | `PER_MANA`, `FLAT` |
| Multi-target allowance | full on every target | the k-th target past 3 gets 3/k | `FULL_TARGETS` |
| Bonus multiplier on a creature | uncapped stack | at most 3x | `SpellDefenceRules.CREATURE_MAX_BONUS` |
| Spell power per circle | 3% | 1.5% | `Circles.POWER_PER_CIRCLE` |
| Overflow power | 1.3 | 1.15 | `Circles.OVERFLOW_POWER` |
| Aura armour share | 25% | 15% | `AuraRules.ARMOUR_SHARE` |
| Lasting Resistance on a player | any level | at most II | `DefenceCaps.RESISTANCE_CAP` (`ResistanceCapMixin`) |

A Resistance grant of 10 ticks or less (a dodge, like Time Skip) keeps its level. Boss and Master spells skip the
player's spell guard, so they have to be survived with skill. A duelist's held guard takes only 25% off a spell from in
front and never staggers the caster (`AuraWorldRules.spellThroughGuard`).

**Spellbane (0.12.1).** Sword Masters erase every spell, whatever its side or shape. Hostile bolts unravel within 4
blocks closing in from any side (`MastersRules.erasesBolt`, `RuneBolt.spellbane`), unless a guarding Master with Aura
turns one back. While a spell applies (`SwordMaster.underSpell`), the Master refuses magic damage, relayed and lingering
spell damage, effects, heals, knockback and motion, teleports, fire and freezing, interrupts and cast-locks, and
`Targets.canHarm` skips it. Only blade and aura reach a Master.

## 2. The grind

**Heart Circles** (`Circles.CONDENSE`): every threshold is about 2.8 times higher, from 1,000 condensed mana for circle 1
up to 3,420,000 for circle 20.

How condensing works now (`CondenseRules`):

- Only a tenth of the mana spent condenses at once.
- The rest waits for 200 ticks (capped at 600 mana). It condenses only if one of the caster's spells hurts a hostile
  creature in that time. Casting into the air or at a dummy barely builds a heart.
- Spell kills count toward breakthroughs. Past 12 kills in a minute, each further kill counts only 15% of the time, so
  farms mostly don't count.
- A boss's breakthrough (the 7th circle) goes only to players who dealt at least 2% of its health.

**Aura** (`AuraRules.THRESHOLD`): stage thresholds are tripled, to 450, 1,800, 5,400 and 13,500. The "moment"
multiplier (low health, a crowd, a boss, a dungeon) is capped at 1.5x.

**Re-temper** (`Retemper`, `VERSION = 1`): the first time a player joins on 0.12, their heart keeps the most circles,
up to what it had, that its condensed mana pays for. Their aura keeps the highest stage, up to what it had, that its
experience reaches, and never drops below Glow. The player is told what changed.

## 3. Breakthroughs

- **Tribulations** (`TribulationRules`, `Tribulation`): the 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th circles must be won.
  - When the meditation completes, waves of tempered Runebound come for the caster: 3 waves at the 5th circle, up to 6
    at the 20th. The last wave brings a Herald.
  - The caster must stay within 24 blocks. Each wave has 75 seconds.
  - Dying, fleeing or running out of time loses the tribulation. The condensed mana is kept, and it can be faced again
    after 5 minutes.
  - A won tribulation leaves runes (2 per tier, rarer from the 10th circle on), Mana Crystals (1 per tier) and
    experience.
- **Tribulation Scars** (`TribulationScars`): each tribulation circle held gives one more heart of maximum health for
  good.
- **The breakthrough screen** (`BreakthroughScreen`, `BreakthroughPayload`): after the title card, a page shows what the
  circle added (mana, regeneration, spell power), what it opens, any vow to choose and any tribulation loot. It also warns
  when the next circle is a tribulation.
- **Announcements**: a tribulation circle, the Archmage's circle, and aura Edge and above are announced to every player.

## 4. Enemies

**Tempering** (`TemperingRules`, `Tempering`): each hostile creature is tempered when it first appears near players.

- **Threat** is circles plus 3 per aura stage, from the strongest player within 64 blocks, up to a maximum of 32.
- **Health**: +10% per threat point, at most 4x.
- **Damage**: +5% per threat point, at most 2.5x. It is applied where the blow lands on a player (`TemperedBlowMixin`), so
  arrows, blasts and spells grow too.
- The tempering is saved in the creature's tags, so loading a chunk again changes nothing.

**Elites**: there are none below threat 4. From there the chance is 2% plus 0.6% per threat point, at most 20%. An elite
has 1.5x health, a coloured name and extra experience. The five kinds:

| Elite | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Swift | A third faster. |
| Ironhide | 10 armour and 4 toughness. |
| Vampiric | Heals half the damage it deals. |
| Brutal | 1.5x damage, and it throws you. |
| Splitting | Breaks into 2 lesser copies, with half their kind's health, when it dies. |

**Bosses**:

- A boss grows by 50% of its health for each extra challenger within 48 blocks, up to 6 challengers.
- A boss without phases of its own enrages once at half health: 25% faster, 40% harder hits, and an absorption shield
  worth 15% of its health. Everyone nearby is told.

**Spawns** (`MonsterRules`): Wildercord's monsters spawn at double their old weights and mostly come in packs.
Gloomstalkers also spawn in snowy taigas and groves.

## 5. Variants and packs

`MonsterVariantRules` covers Bramblewalkers and Gloomstalkers.

| Variant | Where it's born | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frost | Base temperature 0.15 or lower, or anywhere it snows | +25% health. Its blows slow (3 s) and chill (5 s). |
| Ash | Base temperature 1.9 or higher | +20% damage, fireproof. Its blows burn (3 s). |

- **Packs**: a Gloomstalker that finds prey calls its kin within 16 blocks onto it.
- **Alpha**: the first Gloomstalker of a group is its Alpha half the time. An Alpha has +60% health, +30% damage, is 20%
  bigger, and its call reaches 28 blocks.
- Names follow the variant, for example "Alpha Ash Gloomstalker".

## 6. Cooking

`CookingRules`, `Meals`, `CampPotBlock`:

- **Camp Pot**: craft it, set it over a lit campfire, fire, magma or lava, and right-click with a bowl. It cooks from your
  pack: the meal you picked, or the richest one you can make. Sneak-click with an empty hand to pick the next meal, and
  click with an empty hand to read the cookbook.
- **21 meals**, which stack to 16. They feed well and carry buffs that keep a fighter going, never raw damage. The meals
  run from Hearty Stew and Forager Soup to the Archmage's Feast.
- **New effects**:
  - Nourished: +20 max mana per level.
  - Focused: 8% off every spell's price per level, counting at most 2 levels.
  - Meals also give Clarity (mana regeneration) and vanilla buffs.

## 7. Mounts and creatures

- **Ridgeback Stag** (`RidgebackStag`, `MountContent`): the mod's first mount.
  - Found on plains, meadows and savannas.
  - Tamed and ridden like a horse, but sturdier and quicker: 24–32 health, 0.25–0.33 speed and 0.65–0.9 jump.
  - Two tame stags fed golden food raise a calf.
- **Black bobcat** (`BlackBobcat`, `BobcatContent`): a rare melanistic wildcat of dark forests and old taigas, as big
  as a polar bear.
  - Fish tames it one time in three.
  - A tame bobcat follows, sits, joins fights, heals on fish and breeds.
  - It has a config toggle: `creatures.black_bobcat`.
- **Name tags** (`NameTags`): a tag now always names a Wildercord creature, even one that answers every right click
  itself. The name stays visible and the creature never despawns.

## 8. The Wayfarer Inn

`town/`: `BountyRules`, `Town`, `BountyBoardBlock`, `WayfarerKeeper`, `RidgebackDeedItem`.

**The bounty board**:

- It offers one bounty a day per traveller, the same for a given traveller, day and board.
- A bounty is 3 to 8 kills of one creature: Wildercord's monsters or the common vanilla ones.
- Kills count only within 256 blocks of the board, in its dimension.
- It pays up to 8 emeralds and 5 to 10 reputation.
- Click the board to read the offer, click again within 10 seconds to take it, and sneak-click to give it up.

**Standing**: each player's standing (`town_standing`, kept through death) moves through Stranger, Known (15
reputation), Friend (40) and Honoured (80).

**Keepers**: they stay home, can't be hurt, never despawn, and sell more as your standing grows.

| Keeper | Sells |
| --- | --- |
| Inn Cook | Buys crops, and sells the Camp Pot and meals in tiers. |
| Stablemaster | Leads, hay and saddles. Then golden carrots and name tags. At Friend, the Ridgeback Deed (20 emeralds) and iron horse armour. At Honoured, diamond horse armour and golden apples. |
| Master's Emissary | Blank runes and Mana Crystals. Then aura shards, then experience bottles. At Honoured, one technique scroll a day for 24 emeralds and 4 aura shards. |

**Ridgeback Deed**: using it leads a tame, saddled Ridgeback Stag of yours up beside you.

Only inns generated on 0.12 have the board and keepers. Older inns stay as they were (until 0.13, which retrofits them).
