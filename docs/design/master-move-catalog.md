Status (2026-10-06): the current catalog contains nine authored attack IDs, including Kiln Ring after its separate native acceptance. This isolated patch connects bounded ordinary-pattern selection to the existing pure planner. The live adapter itself is a source candidate independently source-reviewed and pending fresh integrated native gates; it adds zero attacks. Historical foundation/candidate sections below describe the earlier gates, not current delivery claims.

These rules are not difficulty knobs for this lane.

Offensive enrollment is narrower than ownership: `participant`, `canHarmParticipant`, `projected`, and `mayCut` currently allow enrolled, living, Survival/Adventure server players in this dimension and arena. Owned summons/projectiles resolve their enrolled owner for incoming harm/control through `MasterVictories.owner` and `acceptsHarmFrom`; they are not automatically outgoing targets. The adapter must preserve both boundaries. Future owned-entity targeting needs a separately reviewed guard change.

Ordinary attacks lock aim six ticks before release; Cinder Wake and the movement signatures accept fixed geometry before their first warning. Pursuit uses a server-authored charge aged at least six ticks and never reads private input, a future action, or a client's requested move. Its same-charge token is checked through impact. Movement uses native collision and safe supported paths; displacement, missed ticks, invalid roster or lifecycle changes cancel. Cover can shorten a warned lane but its removal cannot expand accepted reach. Existing `projected`, spell defenses, guard/parry callbacks, `MasterHitReceipt`, `CastHitRules`, and `Statuses.interrupt` own resolved damage and interruption immunity.

Presentation currently syncs integer attack ID, start tick, and aim pitch. IDs derive from enum ordinal + 1 and are duplicated explicitly in `MasterAnimationRules`. The rigid body/weapon sampler supports all eight; the articulated master sampler supports IDs 1, 7, and 8, with explicit rigid fallback elsewhere. `MasterRenderer` reads accepted server state and clears vanilla swings on cancellation. A catalog cannot quietly replace this with client move choice or equate a renderer stub with authored choreography.

## Distinct-attack acceptance bar and honest counts

A new authored attack needs all four: (1) a meaningful mechanical decision different from existing attacks, expressed in spatial geometry, movement, timing structure, conditional resolution, or resource interaction; (2) a recognizably different warning that tells the player what will happen; (3) a demonstrable counterplay choice and a real punish window; (4) original body and weapon choreography that agrees with the hit and warning. Review includes the strongest prior lookalike and explains the meaningful difference. Recoloring, mirroring, speed/range/damage tuning, extra party lanes, a renamed move, a school skin, or sequence permutations do not pass by themselves.

Track separate fields/counts: unique authored attack IDs; certified authored attacks; variants of those IDs; finite authored transition edges; observed combinations. An attack may have multiple hits. A combination is an ordered use of existing attacks with complete recovery, not a new attack ID. The current census of nine named actions is not retrospective certification of every evidence requirement below.

Scope ambiguity: “hundreds” could mean across the encounter roster, per school, or per individual boss. The foundation supports growth but does not resolve that ambition by inflating counts. An illustrative staged program across the existing three schools is 8 existing → 11 after the first pack → 20 → 32 → 50. Each stage is conditional on fresh mechanics and evidence. Going to 101 would require 32 certified signature attacks per school plus five shared; 200 requires 65 per school plus five shared. That is 70 available per school, not 200 per school. Reaching 100 available per school with five shared requires 290 unique attacks across the roster; 200 per school requires 590. These are arithmetic scope estimates, not delivery commitments. Do not add schools or inflate stats to reach a number.

No finite, eligibility-constrained catalog can guarantee never repeating through an arbitrarily long fight. Promise bounded anti-repeat preferences and varied legal sequencing, while retaining lawful fallback and visible recovery.

## Later bounded style graph

The graph chooses the next fully warned action only when `SwordMaster` is free after its entire recovery and any due guard. It never owns or alters in-flight sub-beats. Each school's immutable graph has at most 16 nodes and 64 edges in v1; expansion requires an explicit bound change. Each node references a reviewed action or a neutral state, each edge has a stable ID, bounded positive integer weight (1–100), and a closed enum condition. No arbitrary expression, reflection, command, callback name, class loading, entity selector, or effect program can be supplied by data.

Use a maximum of two action nodes per phrase for Ember and Gale and one for Stone initially, retaining current guard cadence. Every edge crosses the source action's full recovery. Ending a phrase returns to neutral; a cycle in the graph cannot reset depth. A school signature's internal two-beat choreography counts as one action but still owns its complete recovery. Breathing, guard, obstruction, interruption, loss of target, or a changed valid roster end the phrase. Reevaluate eligibility after every recovery; never reserve or execute an unconditionally chained next hit. A graph suggestion is expendable if target, resources or terrain changed.

| Ember | Neutral → broad pressure (Sweep) or marked commitment (Cinder Wake); after full recovery → narrow extension (Thrust) or space (Crescent); then neutral/guard | Shape pressure creates a positioning question. | Gale | Neutral → spacing (Crescent) or lateral commitment (Reprise when its existing cadence permits); after full recovery → point pressure (Thrust) or close pressure (Sweep); then neutral/guard | The side and landing are fixed before movement, and the reply keeps its own tell. These are graph intentions, not enabled sequences. Live-cast pursuit and nearby Break Cast remain observable, eligibility-checked openings selected at the existing priority only from free neutral state. Specials retain sequence cadence and cooldowns; a graph edge does not grant permission. A future first pack adds only reviewed nodes and edges.

For seeded variation, create one encounter seed when the roster locks, retain it for the encounter, and record it with catalog/graph version in test receipts. Use a specified, tested integer mixing function over seed, school, successful decision ordinal and sorted edge IDs; do not use shared world RNG, wall time, collection insertion order, or runtime-dependent object hashes. Derive one bounded weighted choice over canonical eligible edges. A declined runtime admission does not advance the ordinal, consume Aura, change cooldowns or append history. A successfully started action advances once even if later interrupted; this prevents repeatedly retrying an interrupted move without counting it. Native replay must record the accepted observation/eligibility snapshots as well as the seed: a seed alone cannot reproduce a player's actions or terrain.

History is an immutable ring of the last four successfully started authored IDs, independent of variant. Exclude the immediately preceding ID if another legal candidate exists; downweight other recent IDs. Do not fabricate an ineligible candidate to avoid a repeat. If nothing remains, return neutral/approach/guard/breathe under existing rules; if the only lawful attack remains the same after neutral, repetition is allowed and recorded. No redraw loop, unlimited history, graph search, or lookahead into inputs.

## Runtime safety contract before activation

Keep one server-owned accepted attack at a time. Validate free state, current roster, finite sufficient Aura, all cooldowns, graph depth and executor-specific terrain/line of sight before paying once and accepting fixed geometry. Repeat lawful-target/ownership and lifecycle checks at every impact and at movement/effect ticks. Retain attack-instance hit ledgers, callback reentrancy protection, interruption immunity and existing damage pathways. Do not shorten recovery after parry, miss, cancel, dodge, graph transition, phase change or spell response; existing move-specific cancellation contracts remain the minimum baseline.

The catalog describes an allowlisted execution family; code owns actual geometry, safe-motion checks, timing, damage routing, bounded particle/target/hit/effect counts and cancellation. V1 contains only existing executors. Future families require code review and explicit caps, with no terrain editing, homing after lock, speed override, invulnerability, dynamic effect execution or client-authoritative hit. A warning must bound actual harm. Changed cover may reduce harm but never expand a captured warning. Missed scheduled beats expire harmlessly rather than appearing late.

## Implemented unconnected graph/planner foundation

`MasterMoveGraph` validates one school and one versioned graph ID, exactly one neutral node, at most 16 total nodes, at most 64 edges, and integer weights 1–100. All action references resolve to the unchanged legacy catalog and must belong to that school. Node IDs and edge IDs are bounded ASCII keys. Duplicate identities/routes, unknown endpoints, unreachable nodes, and malformed/null inputs fail construction. A neutral-only empty graph is valid and always yields neutral. Collections are copied and immutable; nodes and edges are ordered lexicographically by stable ID.

Edges enter actions only. Neutral is an explicit outcome, so an action-to-neutral graph cycle cannot reset phrase depth and continue without returning control. The closed conditions are `ELIGIBLE` and `NEUTRAL_ONLY`; the latter can only leave the neutral node. Break Cast and Pursuit Break require neutral-only edges. No production school graph is supplied: test graphs exercise the machinery without making an authoring or balance decision.

`MasterMovePlanner.State` holds the encounter seed, successful-start ordinal, node, phrase depth and an immutable oldest-to-newest list of the last four authored IDs. It rejects negative/overflowing bounds, impossible history lengths, unknown IDs, school-incompatible history, and node/depth/history mismatches. Ember/Gale have at most two accepted actions in a phrase; Stone has one. Self-loops and larger cycles retain those caps. A saturated ordinal fails rather than wrapping.

`Eligibility` freezes the caller-approved move-ID set and a finite Aura snapshot in [0, 100]. The caller must already have checked cooldowns, lawful roster, free state, current priorities, terrain and all other live conditions. An omitted ID remains ineligible even when funds suffice. The planner additionally filters by the existing cost through `LegacyMasterMoves`; it does not reserve/debit Aura, own cooldown clocks, or replace the exhaustion/guard budget checks. Known school-legal IDs absent from this graph are harmless: the graph cannot invent an edge to them. NaN, infinity, negative/over-cap resources, unknown IDs and cross-school eligibility are rejected.

Selection scans at most 64 edges, keeping only edges from the current node whose target is eligible and affordable. If a different legal authored ID exists, every edge to the immediately preceding authored ID is excluded, including another node referring to that ID. Remaining IDs present in the four-entry history receive `max(1, weight / 2)`; this penalty applies once, using integer division. If repetition is the only lawful choice it remains legal. Empty candidates or the school depth cap produce neutral, with no new decision or history entry. Ending a phrase retains the seed, ordinal and history.

The replay algorithm is explicit Java 64-bit wraparound arithmetic. Start with the SplitMix64 finalizer of `seed XOR (0x9e3779b97f4a7c15 * (school + 1)) XOR (0xd1b54a32d192ed03 * successfulDecisions)`. For each remaining edge in canonical ASCII ID order, fold every ID character and a NUL separator with `(hash XOR character) * 0x100000001b3`; finalize again and take unsigned remainder by the positive total effective weight (at most 6,400). Walk canonical candidates once to resolve that ticket. No shared RNG, object hash, wall clock, floating random draw or unbounded rejection loop participates. The graph ID/version and the complete accepted eligibility snapshots belong in future replay receipts; changing graph contents, weights or observations may change results.

A proposal contains its starting state, optional edge/move choice, and tentative next state. `resolve(current, proposal, DECLINED)` returns the exact unchanged current state; neither planning nor declining consumes a random decision. `STARTED` advances once after the executor reports successful admission. `STARTED_INTERRUPTED` advances once and ends the phrase; a later interruption uses `endPhrase` on the already accepted state. Reapplying a proposal against the advanced state, using another graph's proposal, or calling an attack outcome on a neutral proposal fails closed. `NEUTRAL` is valid only for a neutral proposal. These are pure state values, not an exactly-once world transaction; a future server adapter must store the resolved state once and prevent duplicate execution callbacks. Calling `endPhrase` is an explicit executor boundary, never permission to skip recovery.

Authored attack count remains **eight**. A graph node, repeated use, edge, school-specific variant, or ordered path through those attacks is not a new authored attack. This slice provides bounded combination machinery; it makes no claim of hundreds of distinct moves or certified native combat behavior.

A signature admission terminates an ordinary phrase and enters the same accepted history. The server supplies candidates after native checks: Sweep requires distance at most 4; Thrust/Crescent at most 6; all require ordinary height, line of sight, finite Aura sufficient for the existing attack-plus-guard budget, free combat state and the same valid target/roster. A second check runs immediately before payment and also rejects a newly legal pursuit, Reprise or Kiln opening. The graph cannot request a new executor or signature. It does not relocate entities, modify ownership, touch a cooldown, choose client input or resolve damage. Existing native projectile/melee collision, roster, Aura Guard, absorption, hit receipts and interruption paths execute the selected attack.

One encounter seed derives from the native master/challenger UUIDs at roster lock using specified XOR/rotations; selection consumes no world RNG or wall time. The four-ID history includes every successfully admitted authored action, including the fixed opener and higher-priority attacks. Successful-start ordinal advances at the existing payment/start boundary, once; failed/stale proposals do not mutate state or pay. Guard, breathing, obstruction, dodge, interruption, target/valid-roster changes and terminal cleanup end the phrase without erasing seed/history. A neutral proposal is accepted once; the existing eligible ordinary fallback remains available, with no recursive redraw.

There are nine bounded weight profiles (three preferred ordinary moves for each of three schools), each with four nodes. ### Explicit ordinary cancellation/release change

This adapter intentionally fixes an existing ordinary-action timing gap. Previously `beginAttack` did not reserve Sweep/Thrust/Crescent recovery at admission; `cancelAttack` cleared an early ordinary warning, and `interruptWindup` only enforced `max(previousRecovery, interruptTick + 20)`. Thus an interrupted 22 + 24 Thrust could be retried earlier than its promised full window. An ordinary `tickAttack` callback delivered after `attackAt` could also resolve a stale attack. These are deliberate behavior changes, separate from weighted selection:

- Every paid Sweep/Thrust/Crescent now reserves `attackAt + recovery`, including the fixed opener, ranged response and lawful fallback. Early interruption, NoAI and lifecycle cancellation retain this full window without refund or another history entry.
- A late ordinary release callback expires harmlessly, clears the presentation/phrase and preserves at least its full recovery after that callback. It cannot deliver a late hit.
- Ordinary release is consumed before native damage callbacks. Re-entering AI at the release tick cannot repeat the strike; cancellation in one callback stops later roster hits and cannot overwrite the reserved recovery. Melee iteration snapshots the current player list, while every target still passes the native live participant/cover check.
- Cinder Wake, pursuit, Reprise, Fracture, Kiln and Break Cast retain their prior timing/cancellation handlers. Health, armour, speed, tell durations, Aura costs and regeneration are unchanged.
