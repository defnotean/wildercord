This separate GameTest experiment evaluates the revised contract: one quarter-turn of a qualifying native horizontal impulse, with its magnitude and native vertical physics preserved. It promises no distance. A wall may clip all useful travel after the rotation. That paid blocked outcome is conditional policy; it does not satisfy the earlier first-successful-step transaction.

The approved stationary-owner negative control remains unchanged on its original branch (d6b6266e, 88f81745). Its native execution is still pending at this writing. This comparison adds a read-only horizontal-collision field to its reusable observer; it does not change that control's assertions.

It requires the original hit to start grounded and still be grounded after the whole native attack returns, the complete captured state to remain unchanged, and the existing strict receipt to qualify.

The existing receipt still requires an actual accepted nonlethal health/absorption wound from the exact real hostile frontal melee source and exactly one associated positive default native impulse. The helper grants no additional provenance and does not broaden production form cancellation.
