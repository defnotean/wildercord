# Excise: one Circle XVI capability

This slice adds one original capability and one authored three-page lesson, not completion of the wider magic update. It starts from `77a63016806971668eeace916bdc45cc6d33a0e0`.

## Player contract

Saved Heartwood makes Rootbound retrievable through Grimoire > Master studies, including older characters whose Root Guardian altar no longer exists. The server validates one live ordered three-page reading of **The Root That Outlived Its Gardener**. Reading teaches Excise only while Circle XVI is active. The optional bound book has one original and one replacement entitlement; a full inventory retains those entitlements and never prevents reading. Study, learned rune, shared rest and the remaining absolute recovery deadline persist through saved attachments. Recovery never renews on respawn, reconnect or load; it expires against the same authoritative overworld clock. Invalid, expired, backwards-clock and overflowing deadlines cannot create a permanent lock. No pending focus, target identity or presentation is restored. Pending holds cancel at SERVER_STOPPING before player save.

An ordinary Echo Cord row must contain exactly Beam + Excise, with both runes learned. Hold the actual rebound Cast key at one visible hostile native Zone knot within twelve blocks. Acceptance locks that exact emitter. Payment is 36 base mana with Heart/mastery cost discounts once, a 240-tick rest shared across slots, a 16-tick held commitment, and 12-tick recovery after either success or cancellation. There is no overcast, retarget, second payment or refund. Row/Cord/selection, body/world, eligibility, sight, ward, menu/focus, damage, or more than one block of displacement invalidates the commitment.

## Exact native ownership

`CastEngine.deliver` retains its original per-center Zone loop, pulse scheduling, `Cast.pulse`, budget, hit, damage and secondary-effect paths. A small optional `NativeZoneEmitters.Emitter` belongs to one loop center. Only its `excised` bit suppresses future callbacks for that center. No `Cast.cancel`, shared lifetime or budget mutation is used. Split siblings, previously applied venom/fire and earlier secondary magic retain their original ownership, budgets and expiry. Registry removal only removes targetability and does not stop an uncut original emitter.

Only harmful nonpassive native Zones without an anchored link are registered. Registry entries and per-viewer snapshots are bounded (512 total, 64 visible). Each scheduled callback consumes its own remaining-pulse counter before running the original pulse, so a cosmetic tail cannot be mistaken for future work. Expiry, owner/field lifetime and server stop prune the index. Player emitter ownership is checked against its captured original connected body; nonplayer owners remain original live entities in the same resident level. Every acceptance and completion rechecks both directions of current Targets hostility, current party/PvP rules, visibility/range and resident resolved ward identity. No Excise read asks an unloaded chunk or structure start to load.

## Admission and progression

`ActionAdmission` covers synchronous callbacks. A short Excise-only admitting scope plus held/recovery ownership rejects other fresh paid actions before their payment or cancellation points. This includes ordinary spells, charge, Relay/Reweave, Aura technique/form/string entrypoints, Unity, ordinary scroll consumption/inscription, fresh imbued-item release and new imbued bow charges. Refused secondary physical bindings cannot release the accepted binding. Existing paid delayed Imbue/glyph/projectile continuations remain on their old execution paths.

The legacy `Mastery.onCast` does not itself grant XP. It creates future-hit accounting, performs biome/structure circumstance reads, presents rank/sigil, applies Second Wind mana restoration, cosmetic traits and spoken names. Excise deliberately avoids that path: its sole field cut invokes no combat hit/effect proc, refund, copy, residue or location query. A verified successful cut calls a narrow utility-earning method using the existing mastery rate, cap, repetition and practice accounting. Cancellation earns no utility mastery. Normal paid condensation/element affinity, rune-reading and wildlife cast records remain. Existing built-in AFTER_CAST listeners record rune understanding/wildlife activity; callbacks run inside admission and are followed by lifetime/eligibility checks.
