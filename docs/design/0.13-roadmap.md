## Bounty variety

`BountyRules.Kind`, `BountyHunts`, `Town.Active.kind`. Each standing opens more kinds; hunts stay the most common offer
(weights 6, 3, 2, 1 for hunt, gather, elite, dungeon).

| Kind | Opens at | Asks | Pays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hunt | Stranger | 3 to 8 of one creature within 256 blocks of the board | up to 8 emeralds, 5 to 10 reputation |
| Gathering | Known | 4 to 32 of one item, taken from the pack at the board | 4 to 7 emeralds, 6 to 9 reputation |
| Great | Known | Once a week, in place of the day's bounty: 12 to 20 of one creature | 24 emeralds, 2 Mana Crystals, 20 reputation |
| Named elite | Friend | One champion set loose 48 to 96 blocks away | 14 emeralds, 12 reputation |
| Dungeon | Honoured | Any dungeon's guardian, anywhere | 20 emeralds, 15 reputation |

A named elite (`Tempering.champion`) is tempered to the hunter (threat 6 at the least), is an elite of a random kind (never
Splitting), has three times an elite's health, keeps its name shown and never despawns. It carries
`wildercord.bounty_mark.<uuid>`, so only the hunter who took the bounty can claim it.

## Older inns

`WayfarerInnPiece.retrofit`, called from the travel sites' two-second check of where each player stands. When an inn's
board spot is still air, the board goes up and the cook, emissary and stablemaster move in. An inn that has its board is
never touched, so a keeper who is killed by command is not brought back.

## Inn raids

`InnRaidRules`, `InnRaid`, `Town.Standing.lastRaidDay`. The travel sites' two-second check gives a player inside an inn a
1 in 200 chance each beat, at night, not on Peaceful, when their standing is Known or better and they haven't been raided in
3 days. The raid is keyed by the inn's middle, so an inn has one raid at a time, and everyone within 40 blocks is a
defender (each is marked raided).

| Standing | Waves | Bandits per wave | Pays each defender |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Known | 2 | 4, 5 | 10 emeralds, 4 reputation |
| Friend | 3 | 5, 6, 7 | 13 emeralds, 6 reputation |
| Honoured | 4 | 6 to 9 | 16 emeralds, 8 reputation |

Bandits are pillagers and vindicators, spawned 22 to 30 blocks out from one side and tempered as they load. The last wave
adds a Bandit Captain (`Tempering.champion`, 2.5x, threat 6 at the least). Each wave has 90 seconds, with 5 seconds of
breath between. With no defender left in range, or a wave out of time, the raid ends and its bandits vanish, with no
penalty. Nothing is saved, so a restart ends a raid.

## Escort bounties

`BountyRules.Kind.ESCORT` (opens at Known, weight 2), `BountyEscorts`, `Town.Active.destX/destZ`. The offer names a
traveller and a road of 160 to 240 blocks, and pays 6 emeralds plus 1 for every 40 blocks, and 10 reputation. Taking it
leashes a tame, chested llama with 40 health to the player, tagged `wildercord.escort.<uuid>`, and picks the end at a
random bearing from the board. Every second, the road check finds the llama within 64 blocks of its leader:

- Within 8 blocks of the end, it arrives. The llama goes with its traveller, and the bounty is turned in there (it counts
  as the day's bounty).
- Past 32 blocks from both the board and the end, there's a 1 in 45 chance of an ambush: 2 or 3 zombies, husks,
  pillagers or vindicators, 14 to 20 blocks off, set on the llama.
- Every 10 seconds, the overlay gives the distance and bearing to the end.

If the llama dies, its leader's escort is lost.

## Wandering caravans

`town/CaravanRules` (pure) and `Caravans`. Every `CHECK_TICKS` (8 minutes), with `CHANCE` 0.3 and the
`spawn_wandering_traders` rule on, a random overworld player gets a caravan: a `WayfarerKeeper` with the new `CARAVANEER`
role, 24 to 40 blocks off on the surface (dry ground, entity-ticking, no collision; ten tries), with two `TRADER_LLAMA`s
leashed to it. None camps within 256 blocks of another caravaneer. The caravaneer's despawn delay is `STAY_TICKS` (24000)
and, unlike the inn keepers', isn't reset on load, so it leaves after a day and its llamas with it. Its stock is built once per
traveller for its stay, from their standing: `runes(tier)` runes (2, 3, 3, 4), each tier 3 or 4 by `runeTier` (tier 4 below
0.15 + 0.15 × the standing's ordinal), one use each, from `EventRules.rewardRune("caravan", …)`; mount gear (the Ridgeback
Deed from Friend); exotic ingredients; and it buys leather.

## Inn rooms

`town/InnRoomRules` (pure), `InnRooms` and `RoomKeyItem` (`wildercord:room_key`). The Inn Cook sells the key at `price(tier)`
(6 down to 3). Used within 16 blocks of a non-caravan keeper, it sets the `inn_room` attachment (`until`, `bed` as a
`GlobalPos`) to `extend(until, now)` (3 more days, adding to a running stay) and a forced respawn at the player's block.
Sneak-clicking an inn keeper opens the `inn_chest` attachment (27 slots, kept through death) while the room is let. Every
10 seconds an ended stay is cleared, and its forced respawn too if it still points at the room's bed.

## Hamlets

`town/HamletRules` (pure), `Hamlets` and `HamletTradeMixin` (on `Villager.rewardTradeXp`). A hamlet is the closest `MEETING`
POI within 64 blocks that is still a bell with 3 or more villagers within 32 blocks of it. Its name is `HamletRules.name` from
the bell's packed position. The `hamlet_standings` attachment (kept through death) maps `dimension|pos` to a `Standing`
(reputation, day, earned today). A villager trade and an `Enemy` killed by the player inside a hamlet each earn 1, capped at
`DAILY` (8) per hamlet per day. The tiers are `BountyRules.Tier`. Every 5 seconds a player inside a hamlet is greeted on the
way in and gets 30 seconds of Hero of the Village at `favour(tier)` (none, then I to III). It never lowers a stronger Hero
effect, such as a raid's.

## Heart Paths

`spell/HeartPaths` (pure), `cast/HeartPathCommands` (`/path`), and the `heart_path` attachment (an int: 0 is none, then 1 to 3).
The Tenth Circle offers the three paths as clickable chat when it forms, and meditation reminds you once per session after any
open vows. A path's numbers are a `CircleVows.Effect` added in `Heart.vowEffect`, so mana, regeneration and spell bonuses
pick it up wherever vows already apply. Like a vow, a path is silent below an active Circle X.

| Path | Numbers | Signature | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Storm | power x1.08, cooldown x0.95, max mana -20 | Overflow at 75% mana | `Heart.overflowing` (spell caster, Relay, Reweave) |
| Well | max mana +40, regen +1 | meditation regen bonus x2 | `Mana.of` |
| Ward | cost x0.94, duration x1.15 | Mana Skin share 0.35 (instead of 0.2) | `ManaSkinRules.recovery(..., share)` |

Choosing is free. Leaving costs 10 levels (`HeartPaths.LEAVE_LEVELS`) and works even while the circle is cracked.

## Ascension

`spell/AscensionRules` (pure), `HeartCircles.ascend`, and the `ascension` attachment (0 to 10, clamped on read).
`Circles.MAX` stays 20. Nothing that sizes arrays or colours by circle changes. Ascension needs total condensed mana
`condenseNeeded(20) + 400,000·r + 40,000·r²`, from 3,860,000 for I to 11,420,000 for X. The heart tick forms it like a circle
(`FORMING` map, so a hit breaks it, and twice as fast on a ley line) once all twenty circles are active.
Each rank is a `CircleVows.Effect(5, 0.1, 1.01)` per rank, added in `Heart.vowEffect`. The Cord screen's Heart page shows the
rank and the progress to the next one.

## Rival tribulations

`TribulationRules.rival(n)` is true for tribulation circles from `RIVAL_FROM` (10). There, `Tribulation` spawns the last wave's
leader as a Runebound zombie dressed by `mirror`. It gets the player's head (`DataComponents.PROFILE`), copies of their chest,
legs, feet and main hand (an iron sword if empty), and zero drop chances. Its health is `shadowHealth = max(40, playerMax × tier)`
through the tribulation health modifier, its speed is 0.3 and it calls no reinforcements. It casts the player's selected spell
if `SpellCompiler` compiles it, else a random Runebound spell. It carries both `HERALD_TAG` and `SHADOW_TAG` and is tracked like
any wave monster, so the wave ends when it falls.

## Master Gauntlet

`aura/world/GauntletRules` (pure) and `MasterGauntlet`. `/master gauntlet` opens once `MasterVictoryRules.ALL` is cleared. A run
walks a shuffled order of the sixteen schools. Each Master comes from `SwordMaster.gauntletTrial`, which is `spawn` with enrolment
and `begins` pulled in to 60 ticks. `SwordMaster.die` reports the credited players to `MasterGauntlet.felled`. The run's
10-tick loop then heals a quarter, waits `BREATH_TICKS` (100) and sends the next Master. A dead or missing challenger, or a
Master removed without falling, ends the run. The best time is the `gauntlet_best` attachment (seconds). The first clear gives
`MasterGauntlet.blade` (netherite, the school's `master_blade` model, epic, Sharpness V and Unbreaking III) and unlocks
`aura:gauntlet` for the `aura/gauntlet` advancement.

## Mage-hunters

`monster/MageHunterRules` (pure), `MageHunter` and `MageHunters`. `MageHunter extends Vindicator` and is `RuneboundKin` with no
spells, so it never rolls Runebound. Each tick it calls `RuneBolt.unravel(this)` on every bolt within `NULL_RANGE` (6). That
fizzles any bolt another caster sent. `hurtServer` halves spell harm, meaning a cast being applied, `Dungeons.spellLanding()`,
or magic damage types. `doHurtTarget` takes `DRAIN` (15) mana through `Spellbooks.setMana`. `MageHunters` checks every 2
minutes at night in the overworld. Each non-creative player with `Heart.circles >= 5` has a 0.15 chance of being hunted.
`band(circles, roll)` sends one hunter, or two when the roll is under `(circles - 5) / 10`, 24 to 32 blocks away. No band
comes while a hunter is within 64 blocks. The client uses `VindicatorRenderer` with the skin from `tools/magic_art.py`, and
the hat layer shown as a hood. Loot is in `entities/mage_hunter`: 1 to 3 emeralds, and a Mana Crystal 25% of the time with a
player kill.

## Ritual spells

`dev.wildercord.ritual`: `RitualRules` (pure), `Rituals` and `RitualTabletItem`. The tablet keeps its ritual in the
`wildercord:ritual` component. Sneak-use cycles it, and holding use channels it for the ritual's time (BOW animation).
On completion, `Rituals.perform` checks again, works the ritual, takes the reagent from the leader and splits the cost
across the circle (`RitualRules.split`: even shares, short casters give everything, the rest cover the gap).

| Ritual | Circle | Mana | Channel | Reagent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bounty | 2 | 60 | 80 | Bone Block |
| Clear Skies | 4 | 90 | 100 | Sunflower |
| Call Storm | 6 | 120 | 120 | Storm Feather |
| Sanctuary | 8 | 160 | 140 | Mana Crystal |
| Dawn | 10 | 240 | 200 | Glowstone |

Weather goes through `WeatherData` setters, and Dawn through `ServerClockManager.moveToTimeMarker(defaultClock, DAY)`.
Sanctuary wards live in memory only (lost on restart, cleared on server stop) and every half second clear targets on
players and push monsters outwards. Bosses are left alone.

## Flawed and corrupted runes

`RuneTwistRules` (pure) holds the twists and numbers. A found rune item carries its twist in the `wildercord:twist`
component. Learning it writes `RUNE_TWISTS` (rune id to twist id, synced, kept through death), so the twist follows the
rune into every spell, like rune ranks. `RuneTwists` reads it.

- Power: `Effects.applyEffect` multiplies by `RuneTwists.power`. `Effects.twisted` runs before the rune lands: volatile
  fizzles (15%), bloodletting (2 health) and hungering (4 exhaustion) fire once a cast via `cast.once`, searing ignites
  the helped list for 60 ticks.
- Cost: `Heart.secretCost` multiplies by `RuneTwists.costFactor` (0.85 per distinct flawed rune, Knots opened, floor
  0.5), so every price path (casting, charging, scrolls, the client readouts) sees it.
- Loot: `TwistedRunes` registers `wildercord:twisted_rune` and adds a 12% pool (scaled by `runeLootChance`) to eight
  vanilla chests. The rune is a Tier I-III effect rune, never innate, Excise or a Grimoire lesson rune. The twist roll
  is flawed half the time, else one of the four corruptions, evenly.
- `RuneItem.use`: a twisted copy learns and twists; a plain copy used while sneaking on a twisted rune untwists it.

## Spell-duel arena

`dev.wildercord.duel`: `ArenaRules` (pure), `ArenaLadder` (overworld saved data), `Arena` and `ArenaStoneBlock`. Using
the stone records the caster as waiting there (in memory, a minute). A second caster's use, with the first still within
reach, calls `Duels.startBout` on `Arena.TERMS` (16-block radius round the stone, the duel countdown, three minutes,
killing blow only, not into the duel record) with an `Arena.Bout` watcher that shows the count, the ring and the result.

On a decisive ending or a draw, `Arena.record` moves both ratings by Elo (K 32) and counts the win and loss. An
interrupted bout records nothing. Seasons are 28 days of overworld game time. A standing from an older season is
carried over lazily (`Standing.in`): halfway back to 1000 for each season missed, wins and losses cleared. Ranks:
Apprentice, Adept 1100, Duellist 1200, Archmage 1350, Grandmaster 1500.
