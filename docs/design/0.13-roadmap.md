## Bounty variety

`BountyRules.Kind`, `BountyHunts`, `Town.Active.kind`. Each standing opens more kinds; hunts stay the most common offer
(weights 6, 3, 2, 1 for hunt, gather, elite, dungeon).

| Kind | Opens at | Asks | Pays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hunt | Stranger | 3 to 8 of one creature within 256 blocks of the board | up to 8 emeralds, 5 to 10 reputation |
| Gathering | Known | 4 to 32 of one item, taken from the pack at the board | 4 to 7 emeralds, 6 to 9 reputation |
| Great | Known | Once a week, in place of the day's bounty: 12 to 20 of one creature | 24 emeralds, 2 Mana Crystals, 20 reputation |
| Named elite | Friend | One champion set loose 48 to 96 blocks away | 14 emeralds, 12 reputation |
| Dungeon | Honoured | Any dungeon's guardian, anywhere | 20 emeralds, 15 reputation |

A named elite (`Tempering.champion`) is tempered to the hunter (threat 6 at the least), is an elite of a random kind (never
Splitting), has three times an elite's health, keeps its name shown and never despawns. It carries
`wildercord.bounty_mark.<uuid>`, so only the hunter who took the bounty can claim it.

## Older inns

`WayfarerInnPiece.retrofit`, called from the travel sites' two-second check of where each player stands. When an inn's
board spot is still air, the board goes up and the cook, emissary and stablemaster move in. An inn that has its board is
never touched, so a keeper who is killed by command is not brought back.
