# Wildercord 0.9.0-alpha

**Minecraft Java 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25**

The wilds come alive and the sword finds its path. Install the ordinary `wildercord-0.9.0-alpha+mc26.3.jar` on the server and each client, with Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer. A 0.9.0 server needs 0.9.0 players: this release adds creatures, items, sounds and synced data. Back up your worlds before updating.

## Monsters of the wilds

- **Six magical monsters**, each with its own model, glow and voice, a tell before its big attack and a way to beat it: the root-lashing **Bramblewalker**, the near-invisible **Gloomstalker**, the lightning-calling **Thunderwing Harpy**, the curling **Geode Crawler**, the bubble-lobbing **Bog Witch-Frog** and the spell-drinking **Mana Ooze**.
- They spawn where they belong (never on Peaceful), can roll Runebound, and their magic meets your spell defences like any spell.

## Magical wildlife

- **Six enchanted animals**: swarms of **Glimmerwings**, the shy **Lumen Stag** that sheds crystal antlers by moonlight, the garden-shelled **Mossback Tortoise**, the tameable **Cinderfox**, the gliding **Skyray** and the frost-printed **Rimehare**. Each leaves something worth a little.
- **The field guide**: creatures you've seen up close fill a new section of your Grimoire.

## Aura: the swordsman's path

- **Ten breathing methods**, one per element, found in vaults and libraries or taught by duelists, each with its own colour and passive.
- **Five stages**, each earned by a breakthrough: **Glow** (coated blows, aura sense), **Flow** (wide cuts, Aura Guard with a perfect-guard counter), **Edge** (a crystal blade and the Aura Slash), **Form** (Aura Step, aura armour, Intent) and **Sovereign** (Dominion).
- **The spellblade**: cast while sneaking with your blade and the spell rides your next slash. Elemental strikes leave marks a mage's spells can set off.
- **The world of aura**: wandering **duelists** who teach their method when beaten, **fallen knights** in old places, **aura-forged weapons** and the **Breath Sash**, and slashes that clash in mid-air.
- One Aura key (Z by default): tap to slash, sneak to guard, double-tap to step, hold for Dominion.

## Fixes

- Blooms and fallen stars take grass blocks again (26.3 took grass out of the dirt tag).
- Meditation no longer fills your own view: the circle, rising light and formation rings stay small or show only to others.
- Sneaking while you trace a sigil no longer counts as meditating.

Every new server setting has its own section of `config/wildercord.json` (`monsters`, `creatures`, `aura`, `aura_world`); older files gain them at their defaults.
