# Wildercord 0.16.0-alpha — Sovereigns & Seas

**Minecraft Java 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25**

Install `wildercord-0.16.0-alpha+mc26.3.jar` on the server and every client, with Fabric API
0.161.0+26.3 and Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer. This update adds entities, structures, items,
blocks, NPCs and packets, so **0.15.x clients must update before joining**. Keep only one Wildercord
JAR in `mods`. The `-sources` JAR is for development.

**Back up your worlds first.**

## Highlights

- **Oathhold Sovereign Citadel City, 750+ Citizens & Watch Stockade Law**: discover **Oathhold**, a
  walled `2,048×2,048` capital city featuring Outer Walls & Moat, four Imperial Causeways, the
  Tournament Colosseum, Imperial Harbor with Sailing Galleons, King's Square, High Sovereign Abbey,
  Grand Market Hall, Copper Guildhall, and the Upper Acropolis crowned by the Great Hall and Grand
  Cathedral. Over `750` citizens spanning `16` professions trade and issue `30` rotating
  **Adventurer Guild Rank (`F–S`)** requests, while neutral **Oathbound High Knights** patrol the
  streets; breaking city houses or detonating TNT in front of guards triggers prosecution,
  temporary **Watch Stockade** imprisonment (`15–45s`), and visible NPC rebuilding of every damaged
  block before release.
- **Four Oathbound Champion Knights**: duel or face the articulated 3D **Oathbound Bastion**,
  **Oathbound Blade-Champion**, **Oathbound Halberdier**, and **Oathbound Banner-Marshal** with
  shield bashes, two-handed ripostes, sky-vault dives, and rallying banners.
- **Deep Ocean Expansion & 1:1 High-Voxel Polish**: explore five underwater landmarks
  (**Coral-Dome Sanctuary**, **Sunken Sovereign Galleon**, **Kelp-Spire Watchtower**,
  **Hydrothermal Vent Forge**, and **Abyssal Trench Citadel**), meet **Tidefolk Brine-Merchants**,
  tame the **Pearl-Pup** sea otter and mountable **Lumi-Seahorse**, listen to **Singing
  Moon-Whales** for ambergris, craft the 3D **Abyssal Diving Suit**, **Tide-Cleaver Harpoon**,
  **Leviathan Glaive**, **Tidecaller Conch**, and **Siren-Pearl Lantern** at the **Trench Anvil**,
  and battle **Barnacle-Hulk Dredgers**, **Siren-Veil Jellies**, **Coral-Spine Makos**,
  **Dread-Nautilus Sentinels**, and the 14-segment serpent boss **Thalassor, Abyssal Sovereign**.
- **Forest White Rabbit & Hollow Tree Portals**: track the pocket-watch **White Rabbit** through
  forest biomes to an enchanted **Hollow Ancient Tree** doorway that warps you randomly to an
  underground crystal cave, the **Wonderland Root-Vault** treasure room, or an all-bosses gauntlet.
- **Cinnamon Party Licks, Grand Bow & Flying Chest Harness**: Cinnamon now rushes or flies to
  lick her owner and injured party members below `65%` health (`6` HP / `10` HP grown +
  `Regeneration I`), wears a craftable **Grand Bow** for permanent `3×` size and amplified combat &
  magic stats, and equips a craftable **Flying Chest Harness** enabling 4-rider party flight,
  54-slot shared side chests, passive elemental bolts, and `2–3` second hit pauses for balance.
- **Enduring Bosses, Arena Respawns, Clean Fire FX & Structure Non-Overlap**: raised boss health
  pools above vanilla's `1,024` HP ceiling with hold caps and breakout immunity, enabled timed boss
  respawning inside lairs and arenas (`LairRespawns`), replaced view-blocking vanilla fire overlays
  on burning custom mobs with low perimeter embers (`CustomMobFireFx`), and added a global
  `StructureClearance` spacing guard across all `89` structure sets (`96`-block Oathhold buffer)
  so buildings never spawn inside each other.

The full list, with every fix, is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Testing and known limits

This build was tested on the Windows test machine: unit and tooling tests, generated asset and
guide validation, packaging audit across `.jar` and `.mrpack` profiles, the native `release-smoke`
suite, plus multi-angle in-game verification and live screenshots across Oathhold, the Oathbound
Knights, Cinnamon companions, the White Rabbit portals, and the Deep Ocean expansion. Those suites
cover the main journeys, not every feature. This is an alpha: report problems on the issue
tracker.
