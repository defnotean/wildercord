# Wildercord 0.15.0-alpha — Shrine & Sakura

**Minecraft Java 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25**

Install `wildercord-0.15.0-alpha+mc26.3.jar` on the server and every client, with Fabric API
0.161.0+26.3 and Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer. This update adds entities, structures, items,
runes and packets, so **0.14.x clients must update before joining**. Keep only one Wildercord
JAR in `mods`. The `-sources` JAR is for development.

**Back up your worlds first.**

## Highlights

- **Four Shrine Cats (`Yuki`, `Odie`, `Freya`, `Kuro`), Cat Sanctuary & Lantern Bathhouse**: conquer
  the four-story Japanese pagoda dungeon guarded by 36 **Karakuri Sentinels** and **Paper Lantern
  Spirits**, Floor Progression Seals, and Guardian Shrine Aegis (`85%` damage reduction while
  floor sentries stand) to earn sanctuary buffs and tame all four immortal companion cats with fish
  or a **Suzu Cat-Bell Charm**; clear the steaming **Lantern Bathhouse** (`Fire + Wind` Rune Seal
  vault).
- **Four Sakura Villages & six kimono NPCs**: explore **Sakura Village**, **Sakura Canal Harbor**,
  **Sakura Onsen Village**, and **Sakura Castle-Town**, and trade emeralds with **Tea Masters**,
  **Shrine Maidens**, **Katana Swordsmiths**, **Lantern Artisans**, **Onsen Hostesses**, and
  **Koi Canal Fishermen**.
- **New runes & crafting variants**: craft **Suzu Chime** and **Ofuda Ward**, bounce **Ricochet**
  up to 5 times solo (2 with Split), and craft **Blank Runes** and shaped/shapeless recipes with
  cobbled deepslate, stone, wood, sandstone, glass, and wool variants.
- **Mojito's Fetchblade, 3D weapons & boss rewards**: Mojito drops the Epic **Fetchblade**
  greatsword; all 5 forged/boss weapons and 20 elemental staffs use stepped 3D held models with
  polearm guard and cleave on **Skyrend Glaive**; bosses and Sword Masters award scaled Aura
  Shards, Mana Crystals, pages, and runes.
- **Co-op, dungeon wards & polish**: cooperative Reed-Crowned Ferryman phase-two paired anchors and
  shared shard payouts, sealed dungeon wards across all 32 warded structures, Rootcoil Serpent and
  Archivist anti-cheese fixes, dry interior tabletop teacups across the sanctuary and villages,
  party offline leader succession, escort companion payouts, and peaceful iron golems around tamed
  Coffer Mimics.

The full list, with every fix, is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Testing and known limits

This build was tested on the Windows test machine: the unit and tooling tests, generated asset and
guide validation, the native `release-smoke` suite, `shrine-sanctuary` and `area-mounts-taming`
suites, and multi-angle in-game screenshot verification covering the sanctuary floors, villages,
custom villager rigs and Shrine Cats. Those suites cover the main journeys, not every feature.
This is an alpha: report problems on the issue tracker.
