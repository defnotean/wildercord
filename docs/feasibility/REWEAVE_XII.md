# Reweave XII: isolated mechanism proof

This slice is not a player capability or a finished lesson. `ReweaveFields` is package-private; its entrypoint and tick registration are called only by the native feasibility test. The rune definition is deliberately absent from `Runes`, and nothing is registered in the ordinary cast, packet, editor, item, passive or storage paths. No teaching or presentation acceptance is claimed. Do not wire this into production without the policy decisions and integration work below.

## Existing entitlement

Circle XII already costs 300,000 lifetime condensed mana and requires 20 Runebound plus the permanent `feat:tide_scribe` (Low Tide) record. A future retrievable Tide Scribe “Ebb Ledger” lesson must accept that existing record; another boss kill must not be required. An item/book cannot supply the record or completed study. The prototype requires active Circle XII, Low Tide, a separate server study record, an Echo Cord and the exact learned Reweave/Harm row. Its native fixture explicitly declares those records; this is not evidence of a real kill, retrieval or completed lesson.

## Mechanism and invariants

One real survival payment creates a disc of radius 2 at a visible floor hit within 8 blocks. Its four half-Harm beats are fixed at ages 8, 28, 48 and 68, and it expires at age 80. The immutable schedule consumes late and warning beats without replay. Only a remaining beat at or after the complete 8-tick warning permits conversion. An accepted rewrite fixes a horizontal 7 × 1.25 lane from the immutable center towards the server-observed aim. Targets are admitted by their feet within the footprint and up to 2 blocks above it; large bodies do not enlarge the area.

The conversion keeps the same Cast identity, payment object, captured Heart/gear/mastery, original center, absolute expiry, damage allowance and aggregate entity budget. It never calls Cast.pulse/again, repays, recounts progression, renews cooldown, adds beats, or refunds consumed beats. Target relationships, entity roster identity, world, loaded cells, current visibility and original-body lifetime are rechecked by guarded admission before Harm mutations. Input and pulse callbacks run under ActionAdmission; reentry cannot create a second payment or convert during an admitted beat. A rejected/blocked/useless conversion leaves the exact paid state intact.

The retained original body is permanently retired on death, replacement, disconnect or dimension departure, including a same-callback return. Both the Cast lifetime and entity admission are open only during the synchronous paid beat; the separate field schedule stays alive between beats. The impact lifetime also rechecks the current geometry and original hit-point read halo, including when a depleted budget produces an empty target list. Thus delayed inherited world-quirk echoes, including empty-target callbacks, cannot enter Effects.apply or its world-read hooks outside a paid beat. Harm’s read-only Rune-Seal/shimmer halo is checked for loaded cells and known resident structure references before invoking those existing hooks. Owner and footprint resident-reference checks also precede Mastery’s existing structure lookup, with another check after payment callbacks. Ward admission uses the existing resident-only MovementWard query: the captured room identity set must be known and equal at each checked cell, and an unresolved structure-start reference refuses admission. It does not call DungeonWards.warded or StructureManager. The native regression injects an unloaded start reference into an already FULL footprint chunk and checks placement refusal before payment, conversion refusal without field loss, due-beat suppression, later recovery on the original schedule, and that even STRUCTURE_STARTS was not loaded. No prototype path deliberately requests a chunk load. Queries check a bounded conservative volume before clipping/querying, and refuse excess entity density. `blockAdmission(false)` denies terrain, residue and Rune-Seal writes in the inherited Harm hooks. The proof's conservative rectangular chunk/ward check can refuse more than the exact diagonal lane requires; this is safe but needs playability review.

## Recommended contract for approval

- Exact active Reweave + Harm only; no links, modifiers, composite runes, storage, passives, copies, free recasts or overcast.
- Proposed price: shape 32 + Harm 8 = 40 base mana, normal existing cost discounts, paid once at placement. Conversion is included. Shared 160-tick rest starts at payment and never renews.
- Proposed controls: one fresh cast edge places; after key-up, the next fresh edge on the original row requests conversion. Invalid aim does nothing to the field. A further conversion is refused. Explicit cancel closes the field with no refund or rest reset. One field per original body; replacement is not supported in this proof.
- Proposed cancellation policy: ordinary damage, control, slot/loadout changes and other casts leave an already released field alive. Control/eligibility/loadout failures prevent issuing a new conversion. Death, disconnect, replacement and any dimension departure permanently retire it. This is an option to approve, not a settled change to the earlier cancellation proposal.
- Proposed ward/cover policy: owner, entire conservative footprint and every ray must stay in loaded cells within the world border and the original known resident ward-room identity set. Owner-to-center and both owner/center-to-victim LOS are required. Temporary obstruction suppresses and consumes due beats without refund; reopening may allow only later original beats. A lane needs all three corridor rails clear before conversion.
- A published version should persist shape rest independently of the original body. The feasibility map is transient and does not establish reconnect/restart cooldown behavior.

## Live Harm semantics, unchanged

The release captures Heart power, gear and mastery through the existing Cast path; conversion never refreshes those. Ordinary Harm still reads rune rank, player affinity, world quirks, target state/reactions, defensive rules and other contextual modifiers at each impact. The native A/B case changes Harm rank I → III after release with and without conversion, also changes Heart/cord and takes ordinary damage, and compares corresponding beat damage. This explicitly distinguishes “conversion does not resnapshot” from “every impact statistic is frozen.” A global freeze would be a separate design/API change, not part of this slice.

## Verification boundaries

Minecraft-free tests cover exact grammar/entitlement, all 101 conversion-age boundaries, warning beat consumption, fixed expiry, no same-tick replay, late-clock consumption and geometry boundaries. The native suite exercises actual paid placement, immutable conversion, blocked/late refusal, callback reentry/retirement, original-body same-callback dimension return, shared one-entity budget, Rune-Seal refusal the live-rank A/B comparison, unresolved structure-start refusal, and a real deterministic Harm ECHO whose original target is removed before the delayed callback. Fixture configuration and injected references restore in server-thread finally cleanup before the world closes. It intentionally does not synthesize a Cast to stand in for the paid entrypoint.

The native entrypoint is `dev.wildercord.cast.ReweaveFeasibilityTest`. It is registered at the end of the full descriptor and in the fixed two-class `progression-feasibility` diagnostic, after the existing full-roster entries. It is absent from the 38-class Masters, six-class articulated and 46-case paired release rosters. This registration does not register an ordinary RuneDef, lesson or input path. Local Loom/native execution is blocked in this environment. Independent javac/JUnit checks, if run, establish compilation and pure rules only. Until a native diagnostic passes, none of the in-world assertions are claimed as observed results.

Before full implementation: approve price, controls, cancellation and wards; implement authenticated retrievable Ebb Ledger teaching without re-kill; add protected exact-row integration across compiler/editor/storage/copies/passives; persist rest; implement visible disc/lane/warning/lore and rebound-key accessibility; verify native party/pet and pre-/post-damage callback mutation cases, unloaded-edge behavior, and final multiplayer defenses. The package-private proof intentionally cannot grant teaching or ordinary input acceptance.
