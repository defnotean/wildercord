# Chorus casting

When allied casters cast the **same shape** at the **same target** at the **same moment**, they sing a **chorus**:
the last voice's spell goes off much stronger, and everyone's colours braid together over the target.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-chorus.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Three burning zombies on the grass, ringed by golden circles of light where several casters' spells braided together" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A chorus of fire. The circles of every voice turn together at the target.</span>

## What makes a chorus

Two or three casters sing together when **all** of these are true:

| Rule | Exactly |
|---|---|
| **The same shape** | Each spell's first shape is the same: Bolt with Bolt, Burst with Burst, Beam with Beam. |
| **The same kind of spell** | Both harmful, both helpful, or both neither. A spell that both harms and heals always sings alone. |
| **At the same moment** | Each voice comes **within a second** of the voice before it. |
| **Close together** | Each caster stands **within 12 blocks** of the caster before them. |
| **At the same target** | Aimed at the **same creature**, or at points **within 5 blocks** of each other. |
| **Allies** | Every singer is allied with every other (see below). |
| **Different casters** | You can't sing with yourself, and one caster counts once in a chorus. |

### Aiming

- For a shape that goes somewhere (a Bolt, a Beam, a Cone...), your aim is the creature or block you're looking at, up
  to 24 blocks away.
- For shapes centred on you (**Burst, Nova, Ring, Domain, Orbit** and **Trail**), your aim is where you stand. So two
  casters' Bursts sing together when they stand within 5 blocks of each other.
- A voice aimed **at another singer** isn't singing with them.
- **Self** spells never sing. They only ever touch their caster.

### Who counts as an ally

Two casters are allies for a chorus if they're on the **same team**, or if **neither could harm the other** anyway
(with PvP off, for example, any two players qualify). Two players who could fight each other never sing together, and
two players duelling each other never do.

## What a chorus does

The **last voice's** spell is the chorus. It's cast bigger for every voice that joined before it:

| Voices | Power | Shapes with a size |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (alone) | normal | normal |
| 2 | **+50%** | one Widen bigger |
| 3 | **+100%** (twice as strong) | two Widens bigger |
| more | no more than three | no more than three |

- **"Shapes with a size"** are the ones Widen works on: Burst, Zone, Rain, Domain and the like.
- **The chorus keeps its caster's own strengths**: their Heart Circles, charge and [casting gear]({{ '/gear/' | relative_url }})
  still count, and the chorus bonus multiplies on top.
- **The earlier voices' spells go on untouched.** Nobody can cut your spell short or take its kill by casting after you.
  The chorus only makes the last spell bigger.
- **Everyone pays for their own spell.** A chorus costs nothing extra and refunds nothing.

## What you see

- **Chorus!** appears above everyone's hotbar.
- A ray of each singer's colour runs from their hand to the target, a double helix of two colours winds along the last
  voice's line, and the singers' circles turn against each other on the ground at the target, with a bell-like chime.
- Every singer earns the **Chorus** feat in the [Grimoire]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}), and the
  *Chorus* advancement.

## Tips

- **Count it in.** Agree on a shape, then cast on a count of three: every voice has a second after the one before.
- **The last voice matters most.** Whoever casts last gets the boost, so let the player with the strongest spell (or
  the best staff for its element) go last.
- **Bursts are easiest.** Centred shapes only need you standing within 5 blocks of each other.
- **Mix elements for Unison.** A chorus is about the same shape; hitting the same foe with **different elements**
  within a second is a separate bonus, [Unison]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#unison). Unison makes
  the second hit land 50% harder.
