# Cord enchantments
{: .no_toc }

Cords take **seven enchantments** of their own: three for your **mana** and four for your **spells**.
They only work on the Cord you're wearing, and they only go on Cords.

1. TOC
{:toc}

## At a glance

| Enchantment | Levels | Per level | At max level | How common |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reservoir** | I-III | +25 max mana | +75 | common |
| **Wellspring** | I-III | +25% mana regeneration | +75% | common |
| **Siphon** | I-II | +2 mana back for each creature your spells hit (up to 16 a cast) | +4 a creature | rarest |
| **Potency** | I-III | Spells hit 8% harder | +24% | uncommon |
| **Celerity** | I-III | Spell cooldowns 8% shorter | 24% shorter | uncommon |
| **Thrift** | I-III | Spells and passives cost 7% less mana | 21% less | uncommon |
| **Persistence** | I-II | Spell effects last 20% longer | 40% longer | uncommon |

Any combination can go on one Cord: none of them rule each other out.

## Getting them

- **Enchanting table.** Cords can be enchanted at the table like tools. Books enchanted at the table
  can come out with these too.
- **Anvil.** Put an enchanted book on your Cord, or combine two Cords, the usual way.
- **Librarian villagers** can offer them as enchanted books.
- **Loot.** Enchanted books found in chests can carry them.

Reservoir and Wellspring are the most common and the cheapest on an anvil. Potency, Celerity, Thrift and
Persistence turn up about half as often, and Siphon is the rarest of all.

{: .note }
Upgrading a Cord at a crafting table **keeps its enchantments**: the new Cord comes out with everything the
old one had (its enchantments, its name and its anvil history). Your spells, runes, mana and circles are
saved on you and carry over too, so nothing spent on an early Cord is lost.

## Mana enchantments

### Reservoir

**+25 max mana per level** (+75 at Reservoir III). Shown in the mana badge as *"+50 from Reservoir II"*.
See [Mana]({{ '/progression/mana/' | relative_url }}).

### Wellspring

**+25% mana regeneration per level** (+75% at Wellspring III). It adds to the other regeneration
boosts (meditating, Clarity, ley lines): see [Mana]({{ '/progression/mana/#regeneration' | relative_url }}).

### Siphon

Each creature your spells hit gives you back **2 mana per level** (4 at Siphon II), up to **16 mana
from one cast**. A creature counts if your spell hits it and it's one you could harm (or it dies to
the hit).

- One cast shares one limit of 16, even if it echoes, pulses or goes off again (a wild surge's second go, a mana
  storm's echo, Twin Star's second cast), and even when it grows into a chorus.
- **Imbued items and glyphs never Siphon**: their mana was paid when you imbued them.

A Burst that hits eight enemies with Siphon II gives back the full 16.

## Spell enchantments

### Potency

**Spells hit 8% harder per level** (+24% at Potency III). It strengthens everything a spell does with
its power: damage, healing, pushes. It stacks with your Heart Circles' +3% each: Potency III with all
eight circles makes spells about 54% stronger (1.24 × 1.24).

### Celerity

**Spell cooldowns 8% shorter per level** (24% shorter at Celerity III). It stacks with the 5th Circle's
**Flow** (15% shorter): together, cooldowns are about 35% shorter (0.76 × 0.85). No cooldown ever goes
below a quarter of a second.

### Thrift

**Spells and passives cost 7% less mana per level** (21% less at Thrift III). It stacks with the 8th
Circle's **Archmage** (15% less): together, spells cost about 33% less (0.79 × 0.85). Passive upkeep
is lowered the same way.

Prices round up to whole mana, but a discount always saves at least 1 mana, even on a cheap spell.
Thrift never makes a [Shield]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}) weaker: Shields are weighed
at a spell's full price.

### Persistence

**Spell effects last 20% longer per level** (40% longer at Persistence II): buffs, slows, burns, a
Shield's 30 seconds, anything with a duration.

## Everything together

The HUD and the Cord screen always show a spell's cost and cooldown after all your discounts: Thrift,
Celerity, the Heart's perks, staffs and foci. Hover the mana badge to see what your Reservoir,
Wellspring and Siphon add.

| Stat | Cord enchantment | Heart Circles | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max mana | Reservoir (+25/level) | +15 per circle | Mana Crystals, Focus of the Deep Well |
| Regeneration | Wellspring (+25%/level) | +0.5/s per circle | Meditating, Clarity, ley lines, Wellstones, storms, familiars |
| Power | Potency (+8%/level) | +3% per circle; Overflow +30% at full mana | Charging, rhythm, leaning, staffs, rune ranks |
| Cooldown | Celerity (-8%/level) | Flow -15% | Rapid, Vow |
| Cost | Thrift (-7%/level) | Archmage -15% | Staffs, Focus of Thrift, mana storms, Frugal |
| Duration | Persistence (+20%/level) | | Extend |
| Mana back | Siphon (+2/creature/level) | Forming a circle refills you | Soulfire, Manatide, Devour, Potions of Mana |
