# Make magic your own

**Minecraft Java 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25**

Wear a Cord, thread runes from left to right, and cast the whole sequence with one key. Build a cheap knockback bolt, a mixed-element beam, a healing circle, a temporary bridge, or a spell that changes when it lands. Or pick up a blade, learn a breathing method and walk the swordsman's path of aura.

## The current update: Masters of Tomorrow

**16 Sword Masters**, one for each breathing method, now wait to test you. Each Master fight has its own techniques to read and answer. Read [What's New]({{ '/whats-new/' | relative_url }}) for everything in the update, and [Sword Masters]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}) to prepare.

To call a Master, first reach **Aura Form** or **Heart Circle VIII**. Then either:

- **sneak and use a wandering Duelist**, then do it again within 10 seconds to accept its offer, or
- type `/master challenge <name>`, for example `/master challenge ember` or `/master challenge venom`.

Masters don't come on Peaceful. Friends nearby can join your trial with `/master join`.

## A spell you can read

`Bolt · Fire · Split · On Hit · Burst · Explode`

Three fire bolts. Wherever one hits, a burst carries Explode. The Cord screen explains each rune, warns about modifiers that do nothing, and shows the real mana cost and cooldown before you cast.

Your large casting circle forms **behind your shoulders**. A smaller focus ahead of you shows the delivery: a beam gathers, a bolt forms and flies, rain opens overhead. Linked circles open where their trigger happens.

## Keep experimenting

A wider spell may be weaker per target. A defensive focus may lower your damage. A temporary bridge expires. Bring more than one kind of spell, and try new ideas in the practice arena before you take them into a dungeon.

Wildercord is still in development. Back up your world before you update, and read [What's New]({{ '/whats-new/' | relative_url }}) first.
