# Runic Hearth

## What it is

A **Runic Hearth** is a block you charge by casting spells at it. Spend the charge on small boons for the players
around it, or start a **Threefold Ritual** with friends.

## How to get it

{% include recipe.html id="runic_hearth" alt="Crafting grid: top row Gold Ingot · Book · Gold Ingot; middle row Stone Bricks · Mana Crystal · Stone Bricks; bottom row Stone Bricks · Stone Bricks · Stone Bricks" %}

Five Stone Bricks, two Gold Ingots, a Book and a Mana Crystal. The **first player to use it becomes its owner**. Only
the owner can change its project.

## How to use it

Use one of these items on the hearth to pick its project. The item is not used up. Changing project clears the charge.

| Use | Project | Charge it with | What a charge does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amethyst Shard | Reading Lantern | Fire or Arcane spells | Night Vision for 60 seconds to players nearby |
| Wheat | Bloom Planter | Life spells | Grows one young crop within 2 blocks by one stage. Never harvests. |
| Feather | Ward Chime | Wind spells | Slow Falling for 5 seconds to nearby players falling more than 3 blocks |
| Ender Pearl | Threefold Ritual | Three different elements | See below |

- Cast at the hearth from within **24 blocks**. Your spell still costs its mana.
- A hearth holds up to **3 charges**. It takes one charge a second at most, and spends one every 5 seconds.
- A fused effect counts for each element inside it.
- Boons go to players within 8 blocks.
- The owner and their [party]({{ '/social/parties/' | relative_url }}) or team can charge it and get
  its boons.
- It only works while the owner is online in the same dimension.
- Use a **Book** on it to open your notebook. Use it with an **empty hand** to see its project and charge.

### Threefold Ritual

1. Set the project to Threefold Ritual (Ender Pearl).
2. Use a **Mana Crystal** on it to begin. The crystal is used up, except in Creative.
3. Cast **three different elements** at the hearth.

The ritual finishes **20 seconds** after it starts, or **10 seconds** with two or more contributors. Contributors who
are still nearby get **Regeneration for 10 seconds**, and the owner gets a **Torn Page**. Then the ritual rests for
**60 seconds**.

## Tips and counterplay

- Bring friends: two contributors halve the ritual time.
- An unfinished ritual waits for its owner to come back. Nothing happens while they're offline.
- Breaking the hearth loses its charge and ritual progress.
