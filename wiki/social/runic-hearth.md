# Runic Hearth: projects and shared rituals

Craft a hearth from five Stone Bricks, two Gold Ingots, a Book and a Mana Crystal. The first player using it becomes its owner. Configuration belongs to the owner; players on the owner's Minecraft scoreboard team can contribute spells. The block stores its owner, project, charge and ritual progress. It works only in loaded chunks with its owner online in that dimension and eligible players nearby.

Use one of these items to configure a project; the selection item is not consumed. Reconfiguration clears stored charge.

| Selection | Project | Spell charge | Utility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amethyst Shard | Reading Lantern | fire or arcane | Spend one charge to grant nearby eligible readers 60 seconds of night vision |
| Wheat | Bloom Planter | life | Spend one charge to advance one immature crop within a small managed garden; never harvests it |
| Feather | Ward Chime | wind | Spend one charge to give falling eligible players five seconds of slow falling |
| Ender Pearl | Threefold Ritual | three different elements | Cooperative or solo research ceremony |

Projects store at most three charges. Utility charges have a one-second intake limit and a five-second consumption interval. Casting at the hearth consumes the accepted effect as project fuel; it still costs the spell's mana.

## Ritual

Select Threefold Ritual, then use a Mana Crystal to begin; Survival consumes the crystal. Cast three different elements at the hearth. One contributing player completes after twenty seconds; two or more contributing teammates complete after ten. Contributors must still be nearby at completion. Named and woven fusions contribute their real ingredient elements: Firestorm supplies fire and wind. Utility projects also accept matching ingredients within a fusion.

Completion grants contributing players ten seconds of Regeneration and gives the owner one Torn Page. A saved sixty-second ritual cooldown prevents reconfiguration from resetting the cooldown. An unfinished ritual resumes when its owner and participants return; it does not produce rewards offline. Breaking the workstation clears its block entity, including its stored project charge and progress.

Use a Book on the hearth to read research and list your saved builds. Empty-hand use reports project and charge.
