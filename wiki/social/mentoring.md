# Mentoring

A swordsman learns from a master through a [lineage]({{ '/progression/lineage/' | relative_url }}). Mentoring is the mage's version: a seasoned mage takes a
newcomer under their wing and helps them through the first Heart Circles.

## Taking an apprentice

- A mage of the **10th circle** or past it can be a mentor.
- An apprentice has to be taken **before the 5th circle**, and can have only one mentor.
- A mentor takes at most **three** apprentices, and can't take any while they still have a mentor themselves.

Use `/mentor take <player>`. They get an offer they can accept with `/mentor accept` or by clicking it. The offer lasts a
minute. `/mentor` shows your mentor and your apprentices, and `/mentor end <name>` ends an apprenticeship from either side.
The bond is saved with the world, so it outlasts a restart.

## Learning

While you cast within **24 blocks** of your mentor, the mana you spend counts **25% more** toward your next
[Heart Circle]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}). That's the same pace a disciple learns near their master. It stacks with a
[coven]({{ '/social/guilds/' | relative_url }}).

Every circle you form pays your mentor in Mana Crystals: one for each of the first four circles, and two from the 5th on.
They have to be online to be paid.

## Graduating

When you form the **10th circle**, you graduate and the apprenticeship ends on its own. Only the early circles go faster,
so the long road to the 20th stays as long as it is.
