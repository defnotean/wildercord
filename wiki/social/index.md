# Friends and Rivals

Magic is better with other people. Trade runes with a villager, take on daily contracts, duel a friend without anyone
dying, sing one great spell together, share your best spells with a code, a scroll or a Knot, and teleport to each
other.

## The short version

- **Helpful spells only land on you, your own pets and your teammates.** To heal or shield a friend with your spells,
  be on the same team. See [friendly fire and PvP]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#friendly-fire-and-pvp).
- **Harmful spells never touch you, your pets or your team**, and only touch other players when PvP is on (or you're
  duelling them). By default, spell damage from one player to another is 60% of what it would be against a monster.
- **Share a spell** four ways: a spell code in chat, a [Spell Scroll]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#spell-scrolls)
  anyone can cast once, a [Knot]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}) anyone can learn, or the runes themselves.
- **Hit the same foe together** with different elements for a [Unison]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#unison)
  (+50%), or with the same kind of spell in the same shape for a [chorus]({{ '/social/chorus/' | relative_url }}) (up to
  twice as strong).
- **Settle it with a duel.** Nobody dies, and afterwards the harm you did each other is undone.
- **Get around together.** Set homes, visit friends with a teleport request, and track waypoints on your screen. See
  [Getting Around]({{ '/social/travel/' | relative_url }}).
