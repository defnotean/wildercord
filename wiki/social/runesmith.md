# The Runesmith

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/runesmith.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A villager in violet robes with gold trim and a hood, standing beside a wooden Scribing Desk on a deepslate base" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A Runesmith at their Scribing Desk</span>

## What it is

The **Runesmith** is a villager who deals in runes. They sell Blank Runes, runes up to Tier III, Mana Crystals and
Torn Pages, and they buy back or swap runes you already know.

## How to get it

Any villager without a job takes the Runesmith's job from a **Scribing Desk**, just as a librarian takes a lectern.

### The Scribing Desk

{% include recipe.html id="scribing_desk" alt="Crafting grid: top row Wooden Slab · Wooden Slab · Wooden Slab; middle row Blank Rune · Bookshelf · Blank Rune; bottom row empty · Wooden Slab · empty" %}

Any wooden slabs work, even mixed. The desk gives off a soft light and can burn. Right-click it to open your
[daily contracts]({{ '/social/contracts/' | relative_url }}).

## How to use it

### What a Runesmith sells

Each level offers **two random trades** from its list. Every rune on sale is random too. Prices below are starting
prices; they change with reputation and demand like any villager's.

| Level | They want | They give | Stock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1 Emerald | 4 Blank Runes | 12 |
| Novice | 4 Emeralds | a random Tier I rune | 4 |
| Novice | 12 Lapis Lazuli | 1 Emerald | 16 |
| Novice | 24 Paper | 1 Emerald | 16 |
| Apprentice | 5 Emeralds | a random Tier I rune | 4 |
| Apprentice | 9 Emeralds | a random Tier II rune | 3 |
| Apprentice | 8 Amethyst Shards | 1 Emerald | 12 |
| Apprentice | 1 Emerald | 12 Paper | 12 |
| Journeyman | 10 Emeralds | a random Tier II rune | 3 |
| Journeyman | 14 Emeralds and a Book | a Torn Page | 2 |
| Journeyman | 1 Emerald | 4 Ink Sacs | 12 |
| Expert | 22 Emeralds and 4 Amethyst Shards | a Mana Crystal | 3 |
| Expert | 12 Emeralds | a random Tier I or II rune | 3 |
| Expert | 16 Emeralds | a Torn Page | 2 |
| Master | 32 Emeralds and a Diamond | a random **Tier III** rune | 2 |
| Master | 20 Emeralds | a Mana Crystal | 3 |

A Runesmith never sells [innate]({{ '/runes/innate/' | relative_url }}), [fused]({{ '/runes/fused/' | relative_url }})
or [world]({{ '/runes/world/' | relative_url }}) runes.

A **wandering trader** sometimes sells a random Tier I or II rune for **8 Emeralds**, once.

### Buyback and swaps

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-runesmith-trades.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A Master Runesmith's trading screen: 20 emeralds for a Mana Crystal, and 32 emeralds and a diamond for a Tier III rune" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A Master Runesmith's trades. Buybacks and swaps appear below when you carry runes you know.</span>

Carry runes **you already know**, open the trades, and extra offers appear just for you.

**Buyback** pays emeralds for a known rune:

| Tier | Pays |
|---|---|
| I | 1 Emerald |
| II | 2 Emeralds |
| III | 5 Emeralds |
| IV | 10 Emeralds |

**Reroll** swaps **two known runes of one tier** for **one rune of that tier you don't know yet**. The trade shows the
rune you'll get, and it stays the same all day until you learn something new.

The limits:

- Up to 6 buyback offers show at once, highest tiers first.
- **8 buybacks a day**, across every Runesmith. Rerolls have no daily limit.
- Only plain rank I runes the Runesmith deals in. Not [ranked]({{ '/fusion-altar/ranks/' | relative_url }}) runes,
  Knots, innate, fused or world runes, and never **Lightning**.
- No experience for you or the Runesmith.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Learn first, then trade.** A rune only counts once you know it.
- **Reroll before you sell.** Two spare Tier III runes are 10 emeralds, or one new Tier III rune.
- **Keep spares for the altar.** See [Ranks]({{ '/fusion-altar/ranks/' | relative_url }}) and
  [Combining]({{ '/fusion-altar/combining/' | relative_url }}).
- Paper and ink sacs are what you need for [Spell Scrolls]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#spell-scrolls).
