# The Runesmith

The **Runesmith** is a villager who deals in runes. They sell Blank Runes, runes of every tier up to III, Mana
Crystals and Torn Pages, and they'll buy back runes you already know or swap them for ones you don't.

## Getting a Runesmith

Any villager without a job takes the Runesmith's job from a **Scribing Desk**, the same way a librarian takes a
lectern. Runesmiths wear violet robes with a gold band and a hood.

### The Scribing Desk

Crafted like a lectern, with Blank Runes:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden Slab | Wooden Slab | Wooden Slab |
| Blank Rune | Bookshelf | Blank Rune |
| | Wooden Slab | |

Any kind of wooden slab works (you can mix them). It makes one desk.

- It faces you when you place it, and gives off a soft light.
- Mine it with anything (an axe is quickest) and it drops itself. Like other wooden things, lava can set it alight.
- Right-clicking a desk opens your [daily contracts]({{ '/social/contracts/' | relative_url }}). You don't need a
  Runesmith for that: any desk will do.

## What a Runesmith sells

A Runesmith levels up from Novice to Master by trading, like any villager. Each level has a list of possible trades,
and every Runesmith gets **two of them at random** at each level (a Master gets both of the Master's). Every rune on the
shelf is also picked at random, so no two Runesmiths sell quite the same runes.

The prices below are the starting prices. Like any villager's, they drop as your reputation grows and rise with demand.

### Novice (level 1)

| They want | They give | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Emerald | 4 Blank Runes | 12 |
| 4 Emeralds | a random Tier I rune | 4 |
| 12 Lapis Lazuli | 1 Emerald | 16 |
| 24 Paper | 1 Emerald | 16 |

### Apprentice (level 2)

| They want | They give | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| 5 Emeralds | a random Tier I rune | 4 |
| 9 Emeralds | a random Tier II rune | 3 |
| 8 Amethyst Shards | 1 Emerald | 12 |
| 1 Emerald | 12 Paper (for [Spell Scrolls]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#spell-scrolls)) | 12 |

### Journeyman (level 3)

| They want | They give | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| 10 Emeralds | a random Tier II rune | 3 |
| 14 Emeralds and a Book | a Torn Page | 2 |
| 1 Emerald | 4 Ink Sacs (for Spell Scrolls) | 12 |

### Expert (level 4)

| They want | They give | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| 22 Emeralds and 4 Amethyst Shards | a Mana Crystal | 3 |
| 12 Emeralds | a random Tier I or Tier II rune | 3 |
| 16 Emeralds | a Torn Page | 2 |

### Master (level 5)

| They want | They give | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| 32 Emeralds and a Diamond | a random **Tier III** rune | 2 |
| 20 Emeralds | a Mana Crystal | 3 |

Stock refills when the Runesmith works at their desk, like any villager.

### Which runes they sell

A random rune can be any rune of that tier, each as likely as the next, except the special ones: a Runesmith never
sells an [innate]({{ '/runes/innate/' | relative_url }}) rune, a [fused]({{ '/runes/fused/' | relative_url }}) rune or
a [rune of the world]({{ '/runes/world/' | relative_url }}). Their shelf never goes above Tier III.

### The wandering trader

Now and then a wandering trader has a rune too: **8 Emeralds** for a random Tier I or Tier II rune, once.

## Buyback and swaps

When you open a Runesmith's trades, they look at the runes you're carrying that **you already know**, and add offers
made for you alone to the bottom of their list. Duplicates always have value.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-runesmith-trades.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A Master Runesmith's trading screen: 20 emeralds for a Mana Crystal, and 32 emeralds and a diamond for a Tier III rune" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A Master Runesmith's own two trades. Buybacks and swaps appear below them when you carry runes you know.</span>

### Buyback

The Runesmith buys any rune you already know for emeralds:

| Rune tier | Pays |
|---|---|
| Tier I | 1 Emerald |
| Tier II | 2 Emeralds |
| Tier III | 5 Emeralds |
| Tier IV | 10 Emeralds |

- Up to **six** buyback offers show at once, the highest tiers first.
- Reputation and demand never change a buyback price.

### Reroll

For each tier where you carry **two runes you know** (the same rune twice, or two different runes of that tier), the
Runesmith offers a swap: **those two runes for one rune of that tier you haven't learned yet**.

- The trade shows which rune you'll get before you take it.
- If you hold a rune twice, that pair is offered; otherwise it's your two most plentiful runes of the tier.
- The rune on offer stays the same all day for that Runesmith, until you learn something new.
- There's one reroll per tier at a time, and none for a tier where you already know every rune they deal in.
- It works for Tier IV too: two known Tier IV runes can become a Tier IV rune you don't have.

### The limits

These offers can't be farmed:

- **Only runes the Runesmith deals in** are taken: never an innate, fused or found-only rune, and never **Lightning**
  (a lightning rod in a thunderstorm turns a whole stack of Blank Runes into Lightning runes, so it isn't worth
  emeralds).
- **Only plain rank I runes pay.** A rune [ranked up]({{ '/fusion-altar/ranks/' | relative_url }}) at the Fusion Altar
  is refused. Knots are never taken either.
- **Only runes you already know** count, and only ones in your main inventory and hotbar.
- **8 buybacks a day** per player, across every Runesmith. Once they're used, the buyback offers close until the next
  day. Rerolls have no daily limit.
- **No experience.** Neither kind of trade gives you or the Runesmith any experience, so they don't level a Runesmith up.
- **They're yours alone.** Nobody else sees your offers, and they disappear when you close the trading screen.

## Tips

- **Learn first, then trade.** A rune only counts for buyback and rerolls once you know it. Right-click a new rune to
  learn it, then bring the spares.
- **Reroll before you sell.** Two spare Tier III runes are worth 10 emeralds, or one Tier III rune you don't have yet.
- **Keep the good spares for the altar.** Three copies of a rune make its next [rank]({{ '/fusion-altar/ranks/' | relative_url }}),
  and any two effects make a [fused rune]({{ '/fusion-altar/combining/' | relative_url }}).
- **Paper and ink.** An Apprentice sells paper and a Journeyman ink sacs: exactly what you need to inscribe
  [Spell Scrolls]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#spell-scrolls).
