# Daily contracts

Every day brings **three contracts**: small jobs done with magic, each with a reward. Read them, and hand in the
finished ones, at any **Scribing Desk**.

## The contract board

Right-click any [Scribing Desk]({{ '/social/runesmith/' | relative_url }}#the-scribing-desk) to open your board. You
don't need a Runesmith nearby: the desk alone is enough, and every desk shows the same board for you.

- **Three contracts**, each with what it asks, a progress bar (*2 / 30*) and its reward.
- **A countdown** to the next board: *New ones at dawn, in* however long is left. If the daylight cycle is switched off,
  it just says new ones come at the next dawn.
- **Handing in** happens by itself: every time you open the board, any finished contracts are handed in, their rewards
  go into your inventory (or drop at your feet if it's full), and the board lists what was *Handed in*.
- Finished contracts show *Done: hand it in at the desk*; handed-in ones show *Handed in*.

Your board is yours alone. Every player gets their own three contracts each day, and they stay the same all day
however often you look.

## Every contract

Each day's three are three **different** kinds, picked from these six. The numbers vary from day to day within the
ranges shown.

| Contract | Target | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Defeat (3 to 5) Runebound with (element) spells** | Fire, Frost, Storm, Wind, Earth or Arcane | **a random Tier II rune** |
| **Set off (2 to 4) (reaction) reactions** | Shatter, Conduct, Wildfire, Implode or Collapse | **6 Blank Runes** |
| **Cast 15, 20 or 25 spells on a ley line** | | **8 Emeralds** |
| **Imbue a weapon and use all its charges** | once | **1 Mana Crystal** |
| **Slay 10, 15 or 20 monsters with spells** | | **6 Emeralds** |
| **Cast 20, 25 or 30 (element) spells** | Fire, Frost, Storm, Wind, Earth or Arcane | **4 Blank Runes** |

A Tier II rune reward is picked at random from the runes a Runesmith deals in, and you're told which one you got.

## What counts

The contracts count what your magic actually does, so they can't be finished by casting at the air.

### Casting contracts (ley line and element casts)

- **A cast counts only once your spell hurts a real creature**: a monster, an animal or another player, not you. Casting
  at nothing, or at a [Training Dummy]({{ '/progression/training-dummy/' | relative_url }}), an armour stand or a
  mannequin, earns nothing.
- The spell has **five seconds** to land. A slow bolt still counts when it arrives.
- **One cast counts once**, however many creatures it hits.
- **Element casts:** a spell counts for each element its effects have. A spell with Fire and Frost effects counts
  toward a Fire contract and a Frost contract alike. See the [effects]({{ '/runes/effects/' | relative_url }}) for each
  rune's element.
- **Knots:** the effects inside a [Knot]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}) count toward element contracts
  just as if they were threaded one by one, as they do for leaning.
- **Ley line casts:** you must be standing on a [ley line]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}) as you cast.
- Only spells cast from your Cord count, not scrolls.

### Reaction contracts

- A reaction counts when you set it off and your spell lands on a real creature at the same moment. Setting one off on
  a Training Dummy earns nothing.
- Each reaction contract names one reaction. See [Reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}) for how
  to set off Shatter, Conduct, Wildfire, Implode and Collapse.

### Slaying contracts (monsters and Runebound)

- A kill is yours if the creature dies **within five seconds** of your spell hurting it. It doesn't have to be the
  spell that kills it: a sword blow or a fall right after your spell still counts.
- If someone else's spell hurts it after yours, the kill is theirs.
- **Monsters** means hostile creatures. Animals don't count.
- **Runebound** kills count toward the element of the spell that last hurt it. Runebound are monsters, so they count
  toward *Slay monsters with spells* too. See [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}).

### The imbue contract

Imbue a spell into a **weapon** (a sword, an axe, a bow, a crossbow, a trident, a mace or a spear) and use up **every
charge**. It counts when the last charge is spent. See [Imbuing]({{ '/spellcraft/imbuing/' | relative_url }}).

## Progress

- As a contract moves, its progress shows above your hotbar: *Cast 30 Storm spells: 12/30*.
- When one is finished you hear a chime and read *Contract complete: (contract). Hand it in at a Scribing Desk.*
- One cast or kill counts toward **every** contract it fits. A Frost spell cast on a ley line that kills a Runebound
  can move three contracts at once.

## New boards

- **A new board comes each dawn.** Unfinished contracts from yesterday are gone, handed in or not, so hand in finished
  ones before the sun comes up.
- **Turning time back doesn't bring a new board**, and can't make contracts pay twice. The board only changes when a
  later day comes.
