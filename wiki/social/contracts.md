# Daily contracts

<span class="caption">Today's contracts, with progress bars and rewards</span>

## What it is

Every day you get **three contracts**: small jobs done with magic, each with a reward. Every player has their own.

## How to get it

Right-click any [Scribing Desk]({{ '/social/runesmith/' | relative_url }}#the-scribing-desk) to open your board. You
don't need a Runesmith nearby. Every desk shows you the same board.

Each day's three are different kinds, picked from these six. The numbers vary within the ranges shown.

| Contract | Reward |
|---|---|
| **Defeat 3 to 5 Runebound** with Fire, Frost, Storm, Wind, Earth or Arcane spells | A random Tier II rune |
| **Set off 2 to 4** of one reaction (Shatter, Conduct, Wildfire, Implode, Collapse, Overload, Fracture, Blight, Unweave, Rupture or Elapse) | 6 Blank Runes |
| **Cast 15, 20 or 25 spells on a ley line** | 8 Emeralds |
| **Imbue a weapon and use all its charges** | 1 Mana Crystal |
| **Slay 10, 15 or 20 monsters with spells** | 6 Emeralds |
| **Cast 20, 25 or 30 spells** of Fire, Frost, Storm, Wind, Earth or Arcane | 4 Blank Runes |

## How to use it

- **Handing in is automatic.** Open the board and finished contracts are handed in; rewards go to your inventory, or
  drop at your feet if it's full.
- Progress shows above your hotbar as it moves. A chime tells you when a contract is done.
- One cast or kill counts toward **every** contract it fits.

### What counts

- **Casts** count only when your spell hurts a real creature within 5 seconds. Casting at the air, a
  [Training Dummy]({{ '/progression/training-dummy/' | relative_url }}), an armour stand or a wisp earns nothing. One
  cast counts once, however many it hits.
- **Element casts** count once for each element in the spell. Effects inside a
  [Knot]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}) count too.
- **Ley line casts** need you standing on a [ley line]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}).
- Only spells from your Cord count, not scrolls.
- **Reactions** count when you set one off on a real creature. See [Reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}).
- **Kills** are yours if the creature dies within 5 seconds of your spell hurting it, unless someone else's spell hurt
  it after yours. Monsters only, not animals. Runebound kills count toward the element of the spell that last hit it.
- **The imbue contract** needs a weapon (sword, axe, bow, crossbow, trident, mace or spear) with every charge used. See
  [Imbuing]({{ '/spellcraft/imbuing/' | relative_url }}).

## Tips and counterplay

- **A new board comes each dawn.** Unfinished contracts are gone.
- **Finished but not handed in?** It stays on the next board, reward and all, until you hand it in.
- Turning time back doesn't give a new board or pay twice.
- A Frost spell cast on a ley line that kills a [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}) can move three
  contracts at once.
