# Chorus casting

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-chorus.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Three burning zombies on the grass, ringed by golden circles of light where several casters' spells braided together" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A chorus of fire. The circles of every voice turn together at the target.</span>

## What it is

When allied casters cast the **same shape** at the **same target** at the **same moment**, they sing a **chorus**: the
last caster's spell goes off much stronger, and everyone's colours braid together over the target.

## How to use it

Two or three casters sing together when **all** of these are true:

| Rule | Exactly |
|---|---|
| **Same shape** | Each spell's first shape matches: Bolt with Bolt, Burst with Burst. |
| **Same kind of spell** | Both harmful, both helpful, or both neither. A spell that both harms and heals sings alone. |
| **Same moment** | Each voice comes **within 1 second** of the one before. |
| **Close together** | Each caster stands **within 12 blocks** of the one before. |
| **Same target** | The same creature, or a point **within 5 blocks** of where the last voice aimed. |
| **Allies** | Every singer is allied with every other. |
| **Different casters** | You can't sing with yourself. |

### Aiming

- For shapes that travel (Bolt, Beam, Cone and so on), your aim is what you look at, up to 24 blocks away.
- For shapes centred on you (**Burst, Nova, Ring, Domain, Orbit** and **Trail**), your aim is where you stand. Two
  Bursts sing together when the casters stand within 5 blocks of each other.
- Aiming **at another singer** doesn't count.
- **Self** and **Trigger** spells never sing.

### Who counts as an ally

Two casters are allies if they're in the same [party]({{ '/social/parties/' | relative_url }}) or
team, or if **neither could harm the other** (with PvP off, any two players qualify). Two players duelling each other
never sing together.

### What a chorus does

The **last voice's** spell is the chorus:

| Voices | Power | Size (shapes Widen works on) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (alone) | normal | normal |
| 2 | **+50%** | one Widen bigger |
| 3 | **+100%** | two Widens bigger |

- More than three voices adds nothing.
- The last caster's own Heart Circles, charge and [casting gear]({{ '/gear/' | relative_url }}) still count.
- The earlier spells go off as normal. Nobody can cut your spell short by casting after you.
- Everyone pays for their own spell. A chorus costs nothing extra.

You see **Chorus!** above your hotbar, rays of each singer's colour meet at the target, and every singer earns the
**Chorus** feat in the [Grimoire]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}).

## Tips and counterplay

- **Count it in.** Agree on a shape, then cast on a count of three.
- **The last voice matters most.** Let the player with the strongest spell cast last.
- **Bursts are easiest.** Just stand within 5 blocks of each other.
- **Mix elements for Unison.** Hitting the same foe with different elements within a second is a separate bonus:
  [Unison]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#unison).
