# Rime Master

## What it is

The Rime Master fights at a quick, even tempo, and its frost works under your feet. Its signature marks a cross of frost beneath you that freezes a moment later. It has 26 named [techniques]({{ '/masters/techniques/' | relative_url }}). It raises its guard after every third attack.

At the start of the trial it tells you its lesson: its frost marks a cross under you, so keep moving before it freezes.

## How to get it

Sneak and use a [wandering duelist]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}#wandering-duelist) who breathes Rime, or type `/master challenge rime`. You need Aura Form or Heart Circle VIII. See [Sword Masters]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}) for the full trial rules.

Rime students gather at the **Waterfall Shrine**, a hall it shares with Tide. Look for it in snowy plains, taiga, groves and slopes. To reach it, hop the ledges up to the cliff alcove. See [Masters sites]({{ '/world/masters-sites/' | relative_url }}).

## Rime Lattice

A frost trap that pulses three times. The banner reads "Step off the frost cross, or jump as it freezes."

- The Master uses it when you're 2 to 7 blocks away and about level with it. It stands still while it casts.
- Each pulse marks a plus shape of frost under every challenger: the spot you stand on and the four spots beside it.
- Each mark freezes **0.6 seconds** after it appears.
- Each freeze deals 12 damage before armor. One lattice can't deal you more than 30.

| Pulse | Freezes at |
|---|---|
| First | 1.0 s |
| Second | 1.8 s |
| Third | 2.6 s |

Answer each pulse in either of two ways:
- **Move:** step diagonally, or two spots in any direction, to leave the whole cross.
- **Jump:** be in the air when it freezes.

The only wrong answer is standing still. The Master is open for about **0.8 seconds** after the last freeze. It can't use the lattice again for 7 seconds.

## Other moves

- **Techniques:** chains of two to four strikes. Wind-ups take about 0.6 seconds, and the Master stays open for 0.7 to 0.8 seconds after each one. Its finisher, **Last Frost**, is four strikes. See [Reading a Master]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}#reading-a-master).
- **Pursuit dash:** if you hold a spell too long, it may dash up to 4.8 blocks at you, at most once every 7 seconds.
- **Crescent volley:** it throws flying blades if you stay more than 6 blocks away or well above or below it.

## Rewards for a clear

- Your first Rime clear teaches the **Ward** [technique part]({{ '/progression/techniques/' | relative_url }}).
- The Rime Master has no Master Form.

## The breathing method

The Master teaches Rime Breath, a frost method. Coated blows slow the target. Its arts unlock as your [Aura]({{ '/progression/aura/' | relative_url }}) grows:

| Stage | Art |
|---|---|
| Glow | Frostbite |
| Flow | Hailfall |
| Edge | Glacier Mirror (a counter) |
| Form | Skate |
| Sovereign | Winter's Hush |

Full details are on [Breathing methods]({{ '/progression/breathing-methods/' | relative_url }}#rime-breath).

## Tips and counterplay

- During the lattice, watch the ground, not the Master. Each new cross is drawn where you stand.
- A diagonal step is the easiest answer, and it keeps you close enough to strike back.
- Jumping works too. Time the jump to the freeze, not to the mark.
- The Rime Master guards after every third attack. Flank it or use an axe.
