# Verdant Master

## What it is

The Verdant Master fights at a quick, even tempo and tries to pin you in place. Its signature blooms a ring of petals around where you stand and roots anyone caught in it. It has 26 named [techniques]({{ '/masters/techniques/' | relative_url }}). It raises its guard after every third attack.

At the start of the trial it tells you its lesson: its bloom rings the ground you stand on, so leave it before it opens, or be rooted.

## How to get it

Sneak and use a [wandering duelist]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}#wandering-duelist) who breathes Verdant, or type `/master challenge verdant`. You need Aura Form or Heart Circle VIII. See [Sword Masters]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}) for the full trial rules.

Verdant students gather at the **Root Temple**, a hall it shares with Venom. Look for it in jungles and dark forests. To get in, beat the Runebound guard in the root pit. See [Masters sites]({{ '/world/masters-sites/' | relative_url }}).

## Verdant Bloom

A ring of petals that opens under you. The banner reads "Leave the petal ring, or jump as it blooms."

- The Master uses it when you're 2 to 7 blocks away. It stands still while it casts.
- It draws a ring with a 3-block radius around the spot you stood on when it began. The ring doesn't follow you.
- It blooms after **1.5 seconds** and deals 22 damage before armor.
- Anyone caught is **rooted**: Slowness IV for 2 seconds.

Answer it in either of two ways:
- **Move:** walk out of the ring before it blooms.
- **Jump:** be in the air when it blooms.

The Master is open for about **0.8 seconds** after the bloom. It can't use the bloom again for 7 seconds.

## Other moves

- **Techniques:** chains of two to four strikes. Wind-ups take about 0.6 seconds, and the Master stays open for 0.7 to 0.8 seconds after each one. Its finisher is **Last Bloom**. See [Reading a Master]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}#reading-a-master).
- **Pursuit dash:** if you hold a spell too long, it may dash up to 4.8 blocks at you, at most once every 7 seconds.
- **Crescent volley:** it throws flying blades if you stay more than 6 blocks away or well above or below it.

## Rewards for a clear

- Your first Verdant clear teaches the **Rally** [technique part]({{ '/progression/techniques/' | relative_url }}).
- The Verdant Master has no Master Form.

## The breathing method

The Master teaches Verdant Breath, a life method. Coated blows heal you a little. Its arts unlock as your [Aura]({{ '/progression/aura/' | relative_url }}) grows:

| Stage | Art |
|---|---|
| Glow | Thorn Lash |
| Flow | Blossom Fall |
| Edge | Rooted Parry (a counter) |
| Form | Wild Growth |
| Sovereign | Grove's Heart |

Full details are on [Breathing methods]({{ '/progression/breathing-methods/' | relative_url }}#verdant-breath).

## Tips and counterplay

- The ring stays where you were standing. A few steps in any direction get you out.
- Being rooted hurts more than the damage does. A rooted player is an easy target for the next technique.
- If you're short on room, jump as it blooms instead.
- The Verdant Master guards after every third attack. Flank it or use an axe.
