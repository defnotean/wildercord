# Dawn Master

## What it is

The Dawn Master fights with light. It holds its ground like the Stone Master: it braces after every attack and hits about 10% harder than most Masters. It moves at the Ember Master's pace: wind-ups of about 0.25 to 0.7 seconds, and an opening of about 0.75 seconds after a chain. It has 23 named [techniques]({{ '/masters/techniques/' | relative_url }}).

At the start of the trial it tells you its lesson: when its sun gathers, turn your eyes away, then dodge and counter.

## How to get it

No wandering duelist breathes Dawn. To call this Master, breathe Dawn yourself. Then sneak and use a [wandering duelist]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}#wandering-duelist) of any method except Starlit, Hourglass, Crimson, Tide, Iron or Dune, since those always call their own Master. You can also type `/master challenge dawn`. You need Aura Form or Heart Circle VIII. See [Sword Masters]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}) for the full trial rules.

To train for it, look for a **Star Terrace** in cherry groves or plains. Its reading room is sealed with arcane: hit every seal block with an arcane spell within 10 seconds. See [Masters' sites]({{ '/world/masters-sites/' | relative_url }}). Cherry groves also hide a **Dawn Pavilion** with Dawn manual pages. See [wild places]({{ '/world/wilds-sites/' | relative_url }}).

## Noon Glare

Noon Glare is one of the Master's named techniques: a rising cut, then a high sweep. The banner reads "Look away from the light!"

- A small sun gathers about 3 blocks above the Master.
- Each of the two strikes hits every challenger within **12 blocks** who is looking at the Master, no matter where they stand. A hit blinds you for 2 seconds and deals damage.
- The answer is to look away, more than about 60 degrees off the Master. Moving farther away doesn't help while you are within 12 blocks.
- Together the two strikes deal about 40 damage before armor. The second one lands at about **1 second**, and the Master is open for about **0.75 seconds** after.

It needs a clear line to you, so solid cover blocks it.

## Other moves

- **Techniques:** chains of two to four strikes. Read each strike's shape. See [Reading a Master]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}#reading-a-master).
- **Pursuit dash:** if you hold a spell too long, it may dash up to 3.6 blocks at you, at most once every 8 seconds.
- **Crescent volley:** flying blades if you stay far away or high up.

## Rewards for a clear

- Your first Dawn clear teaches the **Ward** [technique part]({{ '/progression/techniques/' | relative_url }}).
- The win goes into your [lore journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}). Type `/master victories` to see which Masters you have beaten.

## The Dawn breathing method

Dawn is an Arcane method. Its passive makes foes glow and burns the undead, and from Flow onward it also heals you a little. Its arts unlock as your aura grows:

| Art | Opens at | Input |
|---|---|---|
| First Light | Glow | swing, swing, low swing |
| Sunrise Arc | Flow | leaping swing, then low swing |
| Halo Guard | Edge | first swing after a perfect Aura Guard |
| Dawnbreak Rush | Form | first swing after an Aura Step |
| Noon Zenith | Sovereign | three full swings, then low swing |

See [Breathing Methods]({{ '/progression/breathing-methods/' | relative_url }}) for the full numbers. A Star Terrace's Dawn chest can hold Halo, Grace and Beacon Swell runes.

## Tips and counterplay

- When the sun gathers, turn your camera away and keep moving. Turn back once the light fades, then counter.
- Its guard comes up after every attack, so hits from the front are halved most of the time. Flank it, or bring an axe to break the guard.
- It hits harder than most Masters. Don't trade blows; wait for the opening.
- In a group, call out the glare. It hits everyone who is looking at the Master.
