# Monsters of the Wilds

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/monster-bramblewalker.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A Bramblewalker, a hunched walking thicket of bark and moss with thorned branches on its back and two green lights for eyes, standing in a forest clearing" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A Bramblewalker at the edge of the woods.</span>

Six magical monsters live out in the world beside the zombies and skeletons. Each has its own way of fighting, a
**tell** before its big attack (a sound and a pose you can learn), and a **way to beat it**. None of them appear on
Peaceful, and they're never common: a few in fifty of the monsters where they live. And in the deep old places,
strongholds, ancient cities, expeditions and dungeons, [fallen knights](#fallen-knight) walk.

| Monster | Where | Watch for | Beat it with | Drops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Bramblewalker](#bramblewalker) | Forests at night | It rears back, vines glowing: a lash is coming | Fire; step aside as it rears | Living Bramble |
| [Gloomstalker](#gloomstalker) | Dark forests at night, deep caves | Two violet eyes in the dark; they flare before it pounces | Light: torches, glowing, fire, storm, arcane or life spells | Shadow Pelt |
| [Thunderwing Harpy](#thunderwing-harpy) | Mountain peaks and windswept hills | A shriek with its wings flung wide; a ring of light in a storm | Dodge the dive and strike it while it's down; earth spells | Storm Feathers |
| [Geode Crawler](#geode-crawler) | Caves, mostly near amethyst geodes | It curls into a ball and rattles before it rolls | A mace, a pickaxe, a blast or a storm spell | Geode Grit, amethyst |
| [Bog Witch-Frog](#bog-witch-frog) | Swamps and mangrove swamps at night | Its throat swells and glows before it spits | Shoot the bubble down; a shield turns its tongue | Bog Gland |
| [Mana Ooze](#mana-ooze) | Deep caves, and caves under ley lines | It drinks your spells and swells | Blades, arrows and fire | Mana Gel |
| [Fallen Knight](#fallen-knight) | Strongholds, ancient cities, expeditions, old dungeons | It raises its blade high, and a line of light marks the ground | Step off the line; wait out its guard or break it with an axe | Manual Pages, Aura Shards |

Any of the six can also be [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}), carrying a spell that suits it, with
the nameplate, glow and drops that come with a Cord. Their spells, and their own magic (a harpy's lightning, a frog's
poison, an ooze's burst), land like any spell: armour, Warding, the Potion of Warding and the spellguard all count.

Every one has its own affinities, which your Grimoire's Bestiary fills in as you strike them (see
[Affinities]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }})). A server can make them rarer or commoner, or switch any of
them off.

## Bramblewalker
{: #bramblewalker }

A hunched thicket that walks: a trunk of bark under moss and leaves, thorned branches reaching up off its back like a
crown, long vine-wrapped arms hanging to its knees, and two pale green lights deep in its face.

- **Where:** the forest floor at night: forests, birch and flower forests, the dappled forest, and most of all dark
  forests and the pale garden.
- **How it fights:** it's slow and hits hard up close. Its real threat is the **vine lash**: it rears back, both arms
  raised and its vines glowing green, then whips a vine along the ground at where you stood a moment before. Caught,
  you're **rooted** where you stand for a breath (a second and a half on Normal, a little longer on Hard): you can turn
  and fight, but not walk away.
- **How to beat it:** step aside as it rears, and the lash cracks on empty ground. Better still, burn it: it's dry wood,
  so fire hurts it half again as much, and **set alight it panics and runs** until the flames go out. A Fire spell, a
  flint and steel or a Fire Aspect blade all work.
- **Affinities:** weak to fire; resists earth and life.
- **Drops:** 1 to 2 **Living Bramble** (and sticks). Throw a Living Bramble and it unfurls on whatever it hits,
  rooting it for two seconds. It also goes in a composter.

## Gloomstalker
{: #gloomstalker }

A lean shadow panther with a long tail that trails wisps of dark. In the dark it's all but invisible, a ripple where
the light bends, and only its two violet eyes hang in the gloom.

- **Where:** dark forests and the pale garden at night, and the deep caves below 0 anywhere in the Overworld.
- **How it fights:** it circles you at a pounce's length, hidden. Then it **crouches** (its eyes flare and it growls),
  **pounces**, and slinks away into the darkest place it can find to try again. A pounce that misses leaves it sprawled
  on the ground for a moment.
- **How to beat it:** bring light. It can't hide:
  - within a torch's glow, or in daylight;
  - when you're right next to it;
  - while it's **glowing** (a spectral arrow, an arcane familiar, a Star Glyph residue);
  - for **six seconds** after a spell of **fire, storm, arcane or life** lands on it (or a Light or Reveal), and for
    two seconds after any wound.

  Watch its eyes for the flare and step aside, then punish the sprawl. Listen for its purr: close enough to hear is
  close enough to worry.
- **Affinities:** weak to arcane; resists void.
- **Drops:** a **Shadow Pelt**. Brew it into an Awkward Potion for a **Potion of Invisibility**, no night vision needed.

## Thunderwing Harpy
{: #thunderwing-harpy }

A hunter of the high peaks: storm-blue feathers streaked with lightning-yellow, a hooked gold beak, a crest swept back
like a blade, and great wings.

- **Where:** the bare peaks and windswept hills, from about height 90 up, at night or in a thunderstorm.
- **How it fights:** it circles high over you, out of a blade's reach. Then it hangs in the air and **shrieks**, wings
  flung wide, and **dives** straight at where you are (and where you're heading). In a **thunderstorm** it also calls
  lightning: a yellow ring marks the ground under you, and a moment later the bolt falls there.
- **How to beat it:** dodge the dive. A dive that misses ploughs into the ground and leaves the harpy **stunned**, flat
  on the ground, for a few seconds: that's your moment. Step out of a lightning ring. Shoot it while it circles. Any
  **earth** spell drags it out of the sky and holds it on the ground for a few seconds.
- **Affinities:** weak to earth and frost; resists storm and wind.
- **Drops:** 1 to 3 **Storm Feathers** (and feathers). Use a Storm Feather and a gust lifts you a few blocks, then you
  drift gently down.

## Geode Crawler
{: #geode-crawler }

A low, broad beetle of the caves, its dark shell grown through with amethyst points, pincers at its mouth and six
arched legs.

- **Where:** caves below about height 50, most often near amethyst geodes.
- **How it fights:** it bites up close. Struck, it **curls into a ball** of crystal-studded shell: only a fifth of
  any blow gets through. Curled, it **rattles** (the tell), then **rolls** at you. A roll that hits a wall leaves it
  dazed, tipped on its back with its legs kicking.
- **How to beat it:** crystal cracks under the right blow. A **mace**, a **pickaxe**, an **explosion**, lightning or a
  **storm** spell lands harder than usual, even on a curled crawler, and breaks it open, dazed. Sidestep the roll and
  let it hit the wall.
- **Affinities:** weak to storm; resists earth and arcane.
- **Drops:** 1 to 2 **Geode Grit** (the earth [reagent]({{ '/world/residues/' | relative_url }})) and up to 2 amethyst
  shards.

## Bog Witch-Frog
{: #bog-witch-frog }

A great warty frog the size of a cow, mossy green and bruise-purple, with lamp-yellow eyes, a throat sac that glows a
sickly green, and a lily pad on its head for a hat (with a toadstool growing out of it).

- **Where:** swamps and mangrove swamps at night.
- **How it fights:**
  - its throat **swells and glows** (the tell), then it lobs a **bubble of bog poison** in a high, slow arc. Where it
    bursts it poisons and hurts everything close, and leaves a little poisoned mist;
  - up close it **opens its mouth wide**, then lashes its tongue at you to drag you in;
  - its tongue snatches chickens, rabbits, bats, silverfish, endermites and the smallest slimes, and it **swallows them
    whole**, healing a little. Its next bubble is fatter and its poison stronger.
- **How to beat it:** bubbles are slow: an arrow, a snowball or a blade pops one in the air, harmlessly. Raise a shield
  and its tongue can't drag you. Milk or a Cleanse clears its poison.
- **Affinities:** weak to frost; resists life.
- **Drops:** a **Bog Gland** (and slimeballs). Brew it into an Awkward Potion for **Water Breathing**, or into a Thick
  Potion (otherwise good for nothing) for **Leaping**.

## Mana Ooze
{: #mana-ooze }

A slime of clear, faintly pink jelly with a mote of raw mana turning at its heart. It **eats spells**.

- **Where:** the deep caves below 0, and caves below about height 40 that lie under a [ley line]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}).
- **How it fights:** it hops at you like a slime. Its touch hurts, and drinks a little of your mana. A spell's harm
  doesn't hurt it at all: it sinks in and **fills** it (its heart burns brighter, the jelly swells). Full, it **grows a
  size**, healed. Full at its biggest, it **bursts into two**, throwing raw mana at everyone close.
- **How to beat it:** don't feed it. Blades and arrows work as on any slime, and **fire** (a Fire spell, flames, lava)
  boils it: fire's harm is never drunk, and it takes half again as much. Unlike a slime, it doesn't split when it dies.
- **Affinities:** weak to fire.
- **Drops:** 1 to 2 **Mana Gel**. Eat one for 15 mana back at once.

## Fallen Knight
{: #fallen-knight }

An old suit of armour that a swordsman's aura still walks in, long after the swordsman. Its visor slit and the cracks in
its plate glow a dim, smoky version of the [breathing method]({{ '/progression/aura/' | relative_url }}) it once
breathed, and its blade carries a faint crystal edge of aura.

- **Where:** in the dark of the old places: strongholds, ancient cities, the [expeditions]({{ '/world/expeditions/' | relative_url }})
  and the spawner rooms of dungeons. Never more than a couple at once around you, and never on Peaceful. The deeper the
  place, the tougher the knight: an ancient city's or an expedition's hits harder and slashes more often than a
  dungeon's.
- **Its slash:** it raises its blade high and holds it, its visor flaring, with a rising hum. A moment before it swings, a
  line of light runs along the ground where the crescent will fly. **Step off the line.** Or meet it with your own
  [Aura Slash]({{ '/progression/aura/#edge' | relative_url }}) (the two clash and break), or turn it back with a
  [perfect guard]({{ '/progression/aura/#flow' | relative_url }}). After the swing it's open for a moment: strike then.
- **Its guard:** close in and it braces its blade across its body. A rushed swing into it is turned aside and leaves you
  staggered; the guard halves the rest. Wait it out (it can't strike while it guards), circle round and strike from the
  side or behind, or **break it with an axe**: it reels, open, taking more for a few seconds.
- **Its slash is magic:** your armour, Warding, the Potion of Warding and the spellguard all count against it.
- **Drops:** always a **Manual Page** of its method's manual (four pages of one method and a book bind into its
  Breathing Manual), and about one time in four an **Aura Shard**, which forges [aura weapons]({{ '/progression/aura/#aura-forged-gear' | relative_url }}).
