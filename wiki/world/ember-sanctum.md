# The Ember Sanctum

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/ember-sanctum.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Cinder Warden, a hulking figure of magma plates and chains, cracking open in a burst of fire and frost in its forge-lit arena" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A reaction cracks the Cinder Warden's armour open.</span>

A forge-temple cut into the Nether's caves, kept by the **Cinder Warden**: a hulking figure of iron plates, magma and
chains whose armour turns every blow and every plain spell. Only an **element reaction** breaks through. The Sanctum
is where you learn reactions for real.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dimension** | The Nether |
| **Biomes** | Nether wastes, basalt deltas and crimson forests |
| **How often** | Roughly one in every square 576 blocks across |
| **Doors** | Fire and Earth, then Void and Storm; the vault's is Frost and Fire |
| **Boss** | The Cinder Warden, 300 health |
| **Its trick** | Armour only a reaction breaks |
| **Boss's own rune** | [Cinderheart]({{ '/runes/world/#cinderheart' | relative_url }}) (Tier IV) |
| **Trophy** | The Cinder Heart |
| **Runes found only here** | [Cinderbrand]({{ '/runes/world/#cinderbrand' | relative_url }}), [Ashen Veil]({{ '/runes/world/#ashen_veil' | relative_url }}), [Kindled]({{ '/runes/world/#kindled' | relative_url }}) |

## Finding it

The Sanctum sits on a cave floor: the first solid ground with room to stand over it, going down from height 100 to
32 (above the lava sea). Where there's no cave floor there, it's carved straight into the rock at height 48, so you may
find it by tunnelling as well as by exploring.

Look for **the causeway**: a path of blackstone bricks running between two trenches of lava, with lit braziers on the
low walls either side and two great braziers on gilded blackstone at the gate. The whole Sanctum is about 42 blocks
wide and 80 deep.

A Sanctum never cuts through a Nether fortress or a bastion: it keeps a few chunks clear of them. In the basalt
deltas, the land's basalt columns and lava sheets don't grow into its halls (though a Sanctum generated by an older
version of Wildercord may still be choked with them).

### The guards

| Guard | Nameplate | Runes |
|---|---|---|
| Wither skeleton | Fire Bolt | Bolt · Fire |
| Wither skeleton | Fire Arc | Arc · Fire |
| Skeleton | Frost Bolt | Bolt · Frost |
| Piglin brute | **Adept** · Flashfire Cone | Cone · Flashfire |

The skeleton's Frost Bolt and a wither skeleton's Fire Bolt make a Shatter on you together, so deal with one before
the other catches you. More wither skeletons (and blazes and magma cubes) spawn inside the Sanctum, and 35% of the
wither skeletons are Runebound.

Mind the floor: the lava channel runs the length of the hall, and magma blocks burn your feet unless you sneak.

## The Cinder Warden

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/world-ember-warden.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Cinder Warden standing on the glowing forge-circle in the middle of its domed arena, a spell circle in its hand, lava pools near the walls" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The Cinder Warden on its forge-circle, a spell in its hand.</span>

### Waking it

The Warden climbs out of its forge-altar the first time a player comes within **12 blocks** of it: the altar spits
fire and lava, and everyone near reads *"The coals stir. The Cinder Warden climbs out of its forge."* Its first spell
comes about 3 seconds later.

### What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health** | 300 |
| **Size** | Nearly three blocks tall |
| **Armour** | 4, and its plates (below) |
| **Knockback** | None: it can't be moved |
| **Fire** | Lava and fire are its home: it never burns, and lava, fire and burning never hurt it (fire *spells* follow its armour rules below) |
| **Moves** | Walks toward you, slowly. It never strays more than 22 blocks from its altar (it's pulled back) |
| **Boss bar** | Red, darkens the sky, names the spell it's casting, and says *cracked open!* while its armour is open |
| **Spell power** | 10% more than a Runebound's: 66% of normal on Easy, 88% on Normal, 110% on Hard |
| **Affinities** | Weak to **frost** (+50%) and resists **fire** (half), on top of its armour; a reaction set off on it breaks through the resistance, so a Shatter still lands in full. In the Nether your fire burns 20% hotter and your frost 25% softer. See [Creature Affinities]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }}) |

### Its trick: armour only a reaction breaks

The Warden's plates turn almost everything:

| What hits it | What it takes |
|---|---|
| A sword, an axe, an arrow, a trident, a fist: anything that isn't a spell | **Nothing.** A dead clang and a spray of sparks |
| Fire, lava, burning | **Nothing** |
| A spell that isn't a reaction | **A tenth** of its damage |
| A spell that sets off a **reaction** on it (any of them: Shatter, Fracture, Blight, Rupture...) | **Full damage** (with the reaction's bonus), and its plates **crack open** |
| Any spell while its plates are cracked open | **Full damage** |

A crack stays open for **1.5 seconds**: magma bursts from between its plates and the boss bar says *cracked open!*.
Now and then a blow it turns tells you why: *"Its armour turns that aside. Only a reaction breaks through: freeze it,
then burn it"*.

It teaches reactions by using them. Its own spells set reactions up on **you** (a frost bolt, then a fire bolt; a
shove, then a comet of fire; a pull, then a blast), and the boss bar says which is coming. How each reaction works is
on [Reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}).

### Its fist

It has no ordinary melee attack. Instead, when you're within 4.5 blocks and it isn't casting, it winds up a blow (at
most once every 3.5 seconds): **a ring of fire glows on the floor round it, 3.8 blocks out, for 0.8 seconds**, with a
roar, and then its fist comes down. Everything in the ring takes **12 damage** on Hard (about 10 on Normal, 7 on
Easy), is set alight for 3 seconds and thrown back. It won't start a spell while it's winding up.

### Its spells

It casts each phase's spells in turn, 1.4 seconds after its circle opens, with a pause of 3 to 4.5 seconds between
them in the first phase, 2.5 to 4 in the second and 2 to 3.5 in the third. A *Delay* in a spell means the second half
leaves the Warden a second after the first.

| Phase | Boss bar says | Runes | What it does | The reaction it sets up on you |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** (300 to 200) | Frost Bolt › Fire Bolt | Bolt · Frost · Delay · Bolt · Fire | A frost bolt, then a fire bolt a second later | Shatter |
| | Fire Pillar | Pillar · Fire | A column of fire erupts under you, 1.5 blocks wide and 6 high | |
| | Ember Wave | Wave · Ember | A 3-wide wave of embers rolls 14 blocks along the ground | |
| **2** (200 to 100) | Dash Bolt › Fire Comet | Bolt · Dash · Delay · Comet · Fire | A bolt that shoves you hard, then a comet of fire | Wildfire |
| | Frost Ring › Fire Ring | Ring · Frost · Delay · Ring · Fire | A ring of frost out to 7 blocks, then a ring of fire | Shatter |
| | Fire Rain | Rain · Fire | 5 strikes of fire from the sky around you over 2 seconds | |
| | Flashfire Cone | Cone · Flashfire | A flash of heat in a 6-block cone in front of it | |
| **3** (100 to 0) | Drawing Bolt+ | Bolt · Pull · Delay · Comet · Explode | A bolt that pulls you in, then a comet that explodes | Implode |
| | Frost Bolt+ | Bolt · Frost · Delay · Bolt · Fire · Amplify | The Shatter pair, the fire half 50% stronger | Shatter |
| | Inferno Zone | Zone · Inferno | A 3-block field at your feet for 6 seconds; everything within 4 blocks of it burns | |
| | Frost Wave › Fire Wave | Wave · Frost · Delay · Wave · Fire | A wave of frost, then a wave of fire | Shatter |

### Between phases: stoking its forge

At two thirds and one third of its health (200 and 100) it stokes its forge. For **3 seconds** it can't be hurt: fire
climbs its chains, and just before the end it brings both fists down and **the heat throws everyone within 8 blocks
back**, for 5 damage on Hard (4 on Normal, 3 on Easy). The boss bar says *stoking its forge*, and you read:

- the first time: *"The Cinder Warden stokes its forge, and its keepers climb out of the fire."*
- the second time: *"The Cinder Warden's core roars white-hot. Its keepers return, stronger."*

Two Runebound **wither skeletons** climb out of the fire about 6 blocks from it: one with **Fire Bolt**, one with
**Frost Arc**. The second time they're Adepts. They vanish when the Warden falls.

No blow carries it past the start of its next phase: however big the hit, the damage stops there and the stoking
begins at once.

### Strategy

- **Frost into the crack.** It's weak to frost: while its plates are cracked open, a frost spell hits it 50% harder
  (a little less in the Nether's climate, still ahead). Plain fire does half of its tenth, but Shatter, Wildfire and
  Implode land in full: a reaction breaks through its resistance. Its keepers are wither skeletons, weak to life (the
  Fire Bolt one to frost too; the Frost Arc one resists frost). See [Creature Affinities]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }}).
- **Bring a reaction you can cast in one spell.** The easiest is the Warden's own: `Bolt · Frost · Delay · Bolt · Fire`.
  The frost half does a tenth; the fire half sets off **Shatter** for full damage and 60% more, and cracks it open.
  Some others that work on it:

  | Reaction | Set it up with | Then, within | Example spell |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Shatter** | Frost, Freeze or Chill (it freezes; a boss isn't held still, but the mark counts) | 4 seconds after Frost (2 after Chill) | `Bolt · Frost · Delay · Bolt · Fire` |
  | **Wildfire** | A wind effect that throws it: Push, Launch, Dash, Levitate (it won't budge, but the mark counts) | 2.5 seconds | `Bolt · Push · Delay · Bolt · Fire` |
  | **Implode** | Pull | 2.5 seconds, a blast: Explode or Meteor | `Bolt · Pull · Delay · Bolt · Explode` |
  | **Collapse** | Pull | 2.5 seconds, Repel | `Bolt · Pull · Delay · Bolt · Repel` |
  | **Conduct** | Soak it: Mire, Undertow or another soaking spell (there's no rain or water in the Nether) | while it's soaked, a storm effect | |
  | **Fracture** | Frost, Freeze or Chill, as for Shatter | 4 seconds after Frost (2 after Chill), an earth effect | `Bolt · Chill · Pelt` |
  | **Blight** | Hex or Blind | 8 seconds after Hex (5 after Blind), a life effect that hurts | `Bolt · Hex · Venom` |
  | **Rupture** | Rend, Bleed or Cleave | 4 seconds after Rend, a wind effect that hurts | `Bolt · Rend · Windcut` |
  | **Unweave** | Two marks at once: frost and a shove, say | the shorter of the two, an arcane effect | `Bolt · Chill · Push · Harm` |

  Overload and Elapse can't crack it with fire: nothing sets the Warden alight. Wither it, though, and time
  damage sets off Elapse.

- **Follow up inside the crack.** For 1.5 seconds after a reaction every spell does full damage. Anything that lands
  right after, a Rain strike, a second bolt, a friend's spell, hits in full.
- **Friends help.** A reaction anyone sets off on it cracks it for everyone.
- **Stay out of the ring.** When a ring of fire glows round its feet, you have 0.8 seconds to step 4 blocks away. It
  only winds up a blow when you're within 4.5 blocks, so a ranged fight never sees its fist.
- **Watch for its pairs.** When the boss bar says *Frost Bolt › Fire Bolt* or *Frost Ring › Fire Ring*, the second
  half is the dangerous one: dodge it, or thaw first. When it says *Drawing Bolt+*, get away from where the comet will
  land.
- **Fire Resistance helps.** A Fire Resistance potion turns aside the burning damage of its fire spells (not the blast
  of an Explode, nor its fist). So does the Cinder Heart, once you have one, for your next visit.
- **Kill the keepers.** Its two wither skeletons carry Fire Bolt and Frost Arc: a Shatter on you, from two sides.
- **Mind the lava.** Its fist and the stoking both throw you back, and four lava pools wait near the arena's edge.
- **Don't bother with blades or arrows.** They do nothing at all.

### When it falls

It dies slowly, over 3 seconds, sinking into smoke and cooling lava. Then:

- **Everyone who fought it** and is still within 96 blocks of the altar gets a **Tier IV rune** they don't know yet,
  straight into their pack.
- **Its killer** gets, the same way:

  | Loot | |
  |---|---|
  | **The Cinder Heart** | Its trophy. Hold it up: fire can't touch you for 3 minutes (then it rests for 5) |
  | **[Cinderheart]({{ '/runes/world/#cinderheart' | relative_url }})** | Its own Tier IV rune: your heart burns for 12 seconds, Strength II, fire can't hurt you, and every enemy within 4 blocks takes 3 fire damage a second |
  | **3 Mana Crystals** | |
  | **A Torn Page** | |
  | **A fire or earth rune** | One Tier II or III rune from the hall chest's list |
  | **A rune of the Sanctum** | 50% chance: Cinderbrand (most often), Ashen Veil or Kindled |

- **220 experience** on the altar.
- Anything that doesn't fit in a pack, or has nobody to take it, is left on the altar, glowing, safe from fire, lava
  and blasts.
- **Tempered**: everyone within 64 blocks earns this feat (*"Broke the Cinder Warden's armour with reactions and brought
  it down"*), worth **1,500 mana** toward the next Heart Circle, and the advancement *Tempered*.
- It counts as a boss for the **7th Heart Circle** for everyone within 96 blocks.

The altar stays quiet after that.

## The Vault

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/world-ember-vault.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Ember Sanctum's vault: two chests either side of a brazier burning on gilded blackstone" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The vault, behind the Frost and Fire door.</span>

Behind the Frost and Fire door off the arena. Each of its **two chests** holds:

| Pool | What |
|---|---|
| **1 Tier IV rune** | One of Domain, Sonic Boom, Wither, Dragon Breath, Summon, Starfall, Hollow, Infinity, Reversal, Stasis or Rewind |
| **1 rune of the Sanctum** | [Cinderbrand]({{ '/runes/world/#cinderbrand' | relative_url }}) (5 in 9), [Ashen Veil]({{ '/runes/world/#ashen_veil' | relative_url }}) (2 in 9) or [Kindled]({{ '/runes/world/#kindled' | relative_url }}) (2 in 9) |
| **Sometimes another** | 35% chance of a second rune of the Sanctum |
| **2 to 4 of** | 1 to 2 Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, 2 to 6 gold ingots, 2 to 6 blaze powder, 1 to 3 diamonds, 1 to 4 magma cream, an echo shard, or netherite scrap |

The runes of the Sanctum:

- **Cinderbrand** (Tier II fire): brands each target for 3 fire damage, and for 6 seconds your fire spells burn it 50%
  hotter.
- **Ashen Veil** (Tier III fire): wreathes the target in ash for 10 seconds: fire can't hurt it, and whatever strikes it
  up close is set alight.
- **Kindled** (Tier III modifier): +20% power, and the effect sets what it hits alight for 4 seconds.
