# Cinder Bailiffs and Ferns

## What it is

A **Cinder Bailiff** is a broad, four-footed woodland animal with a fired-clay breastplate, a brush tail and three back
vents. It feeds on **Cinder Ferns** and guards them. Come too close and it warns you, then blows a short fan of ash.

![A Cinder Bailiff browsing beside a cooled fern]({{ "/assets/ember/bailiff_browse.png" | relative_url }})

## How to get it

**Finding ferns.** Cinder Fern patches grow on dry ground in newly generated **forests** and **wooded badlands**.

**Making a fern.** Craft **fern + clay ball + charcoal** (shapeless) for a Cinder Fern root. Plant it on grass, dirt,
coarse dirt, rooted dirt or podzol, out of water.

**Attracting a Bailiff.** Bailiffs spawn naturally only near a **mature, cooled** fern, and no more than two in one
area. Planting a fern doesn't guarantee one will come. They don't spawn on Peaceful. A Bailiff drops nothing special
when killed.

## How to use it

### Growing ferns

![The six fern stages, young to mature, cooled and hot]({{ "/assets/ember/fern_states.png" | relative_url }})

A fern grows young, then prepared, then mature. A cooled fern grows slowly on its own in good light. Hot fronds don't
grow until rain or a spell cools them.

Spells that hit the fern (for example with **Touch**):

| Spell effect | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Grow** | A cooled fern grows one stage |
| **Fire** | Heats the fronds and grows one stage |
| **Tidebreath**, **Frost** or **Freeze** | Cools the fronds |

One cast changes one fern.

**Harvesting:** use a **mature, cooled** fern with your hand to take one root. The plant stays and goes back to young.
Breaking it just gives you the root back.

### Feeding

A Bailiff browses a mature, cooled fern for **2 seconds**, then rests beside it for up to 8 seconds. The fern drops back
a stage. It waits a minute before eating again. Once it has fed, it guards that fern.

### Its ash fan

![A Cinder Bailiff warning, its three vents raised]({{ "/assets/ember/bailiff_warning.png" | relative_url }})

| Phase | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Warning** (1.2 seconds) | It braces, vents rise, clay fragments mark three lanes | Step out of the lanes, back off, or answer it |
| **Fan** (0.9 seconds) | Three vents fire one after another | Its aim is locked; move out of the lanes |
| **Recovery** (2 seconds) | Vents settle, ash falls | Reposition |

The three lanes point left, straight ahead and right, reaching 4 blocks. You can only be hit once per fan, for **4
damage**. It doesn't set anything on fire.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Answer the warning:** a melee hit, or a **Frost** or **Freeze** spell that chills it, interrupts the warning. It
  doesn't stop a fan that has already started.
- **Sidestep:** once it warns, it won't turn to follow you.
- **Tidebreath** won't calm a wild Bailiff, but it does cool a fern.
- Back off or put a wall between you and it, and it goes back to feeding.
- Server owners can switch natural Bailiff spawns off. Existing ferns stay.
