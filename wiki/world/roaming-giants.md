# Roaming Giants

Now and then, out in the overworld, the ground shakes and a giant wakes. It's one of the wilds' own monsters grown
three times over, and which one depends on the land it wakes in.

| Giant | Wakes in | What it is | Its edge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frost Matriarch | Snowy and cold land | A Frost Gloomstalker, the Alpha of every pack in the snow | **Swift**: a third faster |
| Ash Colossus | Deserts, badlands, savannas and other hot land | An Ash Bramblewalker | **Brutal**: half again the damage, and it throws you |
| Elder Bramblewalker | Anywhere between | A Bramblewalker, old as the woods | **Ironhide**: heavy armour |

## Where and when

About every ten minutes there is a one in four chance that a giant wakes 48 to 96 blocks from someone out in the
overworld. That player is told which way it lies. No giant wakes within 192 blocks of another, and none wake on
Peaceful.

## Fighting one

- **Huge and hard to shift.** It's three times its kind's size and shrugs off most knockback.
- **Tough.** It's tempered to whoever it woke near, the same way a champion is, but at least to threat 6 and four times as
  tough. It keeps its name over its head and never despawns.
- **A bar of its own.** Everyone within 48 blocks sees its health on a boss bar.
- **Its stomp.** About every five seconds in a fight it stamps the ground. Everything within 6 blocks of its feet that
  isn't a monster takes up to 10 damage, half that at the edge, and is thrown up and away. Keep your distance, or time
  your swings between stomps.
- **It roams.** With nothing to fight, every half a minute or so it sets off somewhere 24 to 48 blocks away, so it may
  not stay where you were told it was.

## Giant's Heart

A giant drops a **Giant's Heart** when it falls, and one time in four it drops two. Everyone near is told it has fallen.
The heart is a rare ingredient for the finest meals.
