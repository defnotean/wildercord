# Residues and reagents
{: .no_toc }

Big magic leaves a mark. Cast something strong and the ground where it lands remembers it for a while: storm-glass
where lightning struck, frost that won't melt, ash that stays warm, strange flowers where healing was poured out, a
scar of darkness that slowly closes. These marks are **residues**. Each one fades on its own, and each gives a
**reagent** when you harvest it, a little piece of that magic you can use at the Fusion Altar and elsewhere.

## What leaves a residue

- **A strong spell.** A spell that costs **30 mana or more** (its full price, before any discount) leaves a residue of
  its element where it lands, once each cast. A spell of 30 leaves one block; one of 45 leaves two; one of 60 three; up
  to **five blocks** for the very costliest, scattered a block or two round where it landed. Stronger spells' residues
  last longer too, up to half again.
- **An overcast.** Any spell you pay for by cracking a Heart Circle leaves one, however cheap it was, and a little
  bigger than usual.
- **A boss's spell.** The Riftcaller and the dungeons' masters leave them too, where their magic lands (never inside a
  dungeon's warded rooms).
- **An element reaction, now and then.** About one reaction in three (Shatter, Wildfire, Conduct, Overload, Implode,
  Collapse, Fracture, Blight, Unweave, Rupture, Elapse and Blackspark) leaves a small residue of its element on the spot.

The element is the element of the spell's effect: a spell with Fire leaves ash, one with Frost everfrost. A rune with no
element (a pure shape or helper) leaves nothing. One caster leaves at most one residue every **five seconds**, so a
long fight leaves marks, not a carpet.

## The ten residues

| Residue | Element | Looks like | What it does | Lasts | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Smouldering Ash** | Fire | A thin layer of grey ash with embers glowing in it, sparks lifting off | Still warm: nothing standing in it freezes. Glows softly (light 6) | Half a day | Cinder Ash |
| **Everfrost** | Frost | Frozen ground with frost ferns and glints, in place of the ground it took | Slick as ice. Never melts on its own | About a day | Everfrost Shard |
| **Fulgurite** | Storm | A branching cluster of dark storm-glass with crackling gold tips | Whatever brushes past it gets a jolt: Speed I for 3 seconds | A day and a half | Fulgurite Shard |
| **Lingering Eddy** | Wind | A little whirl of air going round and round | Lifts whatever steps into it gently up, and breaks a fall | Five minutes | Bottled Gale |
| **Riven Stone** | Earth | Cracked stone with amber light in its seams, in place of the ground it took | Breathes out dust and rumbles under your feet | About a day | Geode Grit |
| **Wildbloom** | Life | A strange glowing pink flower | Bees visit it like any flower. Now and then it seeds a seedling nearby (only a couple of generations, never outliving the first) | About a day | Wildbloom Petal |
| **Void Scar** | Void | A black rift with a glowing violet rim, in place of the ground it took | Hums, draws loose items and experience in from 5 blocks, and animals keep away from it. It narrows as it closes | Five minutes | Hollow Dust |
| **Star Glyph** | Arcane | A glyph of starlight burned into the ground | Stand on it and anything invisible within 8 blocks glows. Glows softly (light 7) | Half a day | Star Dust |
| **Stilled Sand** | Time | A low mound of golden sand with grains hanging in the air | Time runs thick: whatever walks on it is slowed, and items dropped on it stop ageing (they never vanish) | Half a day | Hourglass Sand |
| **Bloodmoss** | Blood | A patch of dark crimson moss that weeps | Feeds nether wart growing beside it. Animals step round it | Half a day | Sanguine Bead |

A residue that took the place of the ground (everfrost, riven stone, a void scar) gives that ground back when it fades
or when you harvest it. One that lay on the ground simply goes.

## Residues never grief

- They only ever take **natural ground** (grass, dirt, sand, gravel, clay, stone, deepslate, snow, netherrack, end stone
  and the like) or the open air just above it. Never planks, cobblestone, bricks, glass, smooth stone or anything else
  players build with, never a chest or any block that holds something, and never another spell's passing block.
- They ask the same permission any spell's block change asks. Where your spells may not change blocks (a claim that
  isn't yours, spawn protection, a server that turned spells' block changes off), they leave no residue either.
- They keep out of a dungeon's warded rooms.
- There are only so many: at most 12 in a chunk, 6 a few blocks around one spot, and 64 from any one caster's magic.
- They fade on their own, on time, even across a server restart. One in ground nobody is near waits until someone
  comes back before it fades.
- Other spells leave residues alone, and pistons can't move them.

Server owners can switch residues off, or make spells cost more or less before they leave one.

## Harvesting

Break a residue to harvest it: it drops its reagent (some give one or two). Any tool works; the right one is quicker
(a pickaxe for everfrost, fulgurite and riven stone, a shovel for ash, sand and a void scar, a hoe for bloodmoss).

A **Lingering Eddy** is only air: breaking it just disperses it. Use a **glass bottle** on it instead to bottle it
as a Bottled Gale.

The first residue you harvest earns the **Lasting Mark** feat.

## Reagents

Every reagent has a small use of its own. The bigger use is at the Fusion Altar (below).

| Reagent | Use it |
|---|---|
| **Cinder Ash** | Burns in a furnace: four items, a quarter faster than usual |
| **Everfrost Shard** | Use it on still water to freeze it into ice, or on still lava to cool it into obsidian |
| **Fulgurite Shard** | Use it on weathered copper to scrape off a stage, as an axe would |
| **Bottled Gale** | Drink it: you fall slowly for 30 seconds. The bottle comes back |
| **Geode Grit** | Use it: the ore within 8 blocks of you glows through the rock for 10 seconds |
| **Wildbloom Petal** | Use it on a plant: it grows, as bone meal makes it |
| **Hollow Dust** | Use it: every loose item and experience orb within 10 blocks comes to you |
| **Star Dust** | Use it on a block: a mote of starlight hangs beside it, lighting the spot for five minutes |
| **Hourglass Sand** | Use it on a lit furnace that's smelting: it jumps ten seconds ahead. On a young animal: it grows two minutes older |
| **Sanguine Bead** | Use it on nether wart: it grows a stage |

## Reagents at the Fusion Altar

When two effects fuse (with an amethyst shard) or weave (with an amethyst block) at the
[Fusion Altar]({{ '/fusion-altar/' | relative_url }}), lay a reagent in the **third rune socket**. Each reagent
changes the result in one way of its element's, and is used up with the fusion:

| Reagent | At the altar |
|---|---|
| **Cinder Ash** | **Tempered**: the result keeps the *higher* of the two runes' ranks, not the lower |
| **Everfrost Shard** | **Stilled**: half the XP levels (rounded up). A big weave of eight effects costs 11 instead of 21 |
| **Fulgurite Shard** | **Charged**: a rank I result comes out at rank II |
| **Bottled Gale** | **Unbound**: two runes with a signature fusion of their own make their elements' fusion instead (Chill and Shock make Hail, not Frostwire) |
| **Geode Grit** | **Grounded**: the amethyst (shard or block) stays on the altar |
| **Wildbloom Petal** | **Bountiful**: two of the result |
| **Hollow Dust** | **Hollowed**: the lower-tier of the two runes stays on the altar (adding a rune to a weave, the rune stays) |
| **Star Dust** | **Exalted**: the result comes out one rank higher (up to III), for 3 more XP levels |
| **Hourglass Sand** | **Familiar**: a named fusion you've made before costs no XP |
| **Sanguine Bead** | **Bloodbound**: up to 6 of the XP levels are paid in health instead, half a heart each. Never your last heart |

The altar's panel says what the reagent will do ("With Everfrost Shard: half the XP"). One that would change nothing
(Fulgurite on a result that's already rank II, a Bottled Gale on a pair with no signature, Hourglass Sand on a fusion
you've never made) is refused, and the panel says why, so a reagent is never wasted. One reagent at a time.

## Tips

- **Cast big where you want the marks.** A Domain of fire in your garden is ash for the furnace; a frost Burst on a
  path leaves a slide. A healer's strong Self heal leaves wildblooms at their feet.
- **A void scar is a magnet.** Fight beside one and the drops come to you, but it only lasts a few minutes.
- **Hourglass Sand under a farm's drop.** Items dropped on stilled sand never vanish, while the sand lasts.
- **Save Everfrost Shards for weaves.** Halving 21 levels is the biggest saving any reagent makes.
