# Residues and reagents
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What it is

Strong magic leaves a mark on the ground where it lands. Lightning leaves storm-glass, and frost leaves ice that won't melt. These marks are **residues**. Each one fades on its own. Harvest it first and you get a **reagent**.

## How to get it

These leave a residue of their element:

- **A strong spell.** The spell must cost 30 mana or more at full price.
  - A 30-mana spell leaves 1 block of residue, a 45-mana spell 2, and a 60-mana spell 3. The biggest spells leave up to 5.
  - Stronger spells also leave residue that lasts longer, up to half again.
- **An overcast.** Any spell you pay for by cracking a Heart Circle leaves one, even a cheap spell.
- **Boss spells.** These count too, but never inside a dungeon's warded rooms.
- **Element reactions.** About one reaction in three leaves a small residue.

A rune with no element leaves nothing. Each caster leaves at most one residue every 5 seconds.

### The ten residues

| Residue | Element | What it does | Lasts | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smouldering Ash | Fire | Warm (nothing in it freezes), glows softly | Half a day | Cinder Ash |
| Everfrost | Frost | Slippery like ice, never melts | 1 day | Everfrost Shard |
| Fulgurite | Storm | Brush past it for Speed I, 3 s | 1.5 days | Fulgurite Shard |
| Lingering Eddy | Wind | Lifts you gently, breaks falls | 5 min | Bottled Gale |
| Riven Stone | Earth | Rumbles and puffs dust | 1 day | Geode Grit |
| Wildbloom | Life | Glowing flower bees visit; seeds a few more | 1 day | Wildbloom Petal |
| Void Scar | Void | Pulls in items and XP from 5 blocks; animals avoid it | 5 min | Hollow Dust |
| Star Glyph | Arcane | Stand on it: invisible things within 8 blocks glow | Half a day | Star Dust |
| Stilled Sand | Time | Slows walkers; dropped items never despawn | Half a day | Hourglass Sand |
| Bloodmoss | Blood | Feeds nearby nether wart; animals avoid it | Half a day | Sanguine Bead |

Times are Minecraft days. Everfrost, Riven Stone and a Void Scar replace the ground they land on. They give it back when they fade or when you harvest them.

### Residues never grief

- They only land on natural ground such as grass, dirt, sand, stone or netherrack. They never land on built blocks or containers.
- Claims, spawn protection and server settings block them, just as they block spell block changes.
- There is a cap on how many can exist:
  - 12 per chunk
  - 6 in a small area
  - 64 per caster
- They fade on time, even across restarts.

Server owners can turn residues off or change the mana threshold.

## How to use it

Break a residue to harvest its reagent. Any tool works, but the right one is faster:
- pickaxe: Everfrost, Fulgurite, Riven Stone
- shovel: Ash, Sand, Void Scar
- hoe: Bloodmoss

A **Lingering Eddy** is only air. Use a **glass bottle** on it to collect a Bottled Gale.

Your first harvest earns the **Lasting Mark** feat.

### Reagent uses

| Reagent | On its own | At the [Fusion Altar]({{ '/fusion-altar/' | relative_url }}) (third rune socket) |
|---|---|---|
| Cinder Ash | Furnace fuel: smelts 4 items, a quarter faster | **Tempered:** keeps the higher rune rank |
| Everfrost Shard | Freezes still water to ice, still lava to obsidian | **Stilled:** half the XP levels, rounded up |
| Fulgurite Shard | Scrapes one weathering stage off copper | **Charged:** a rank I result becomes rank II |
| Bottled Gale | Drink: Slow Falling for 30 s, bottle returned | **Unbound:** signature pairs make the plain element fusion instead |
| Geode Grit | Ores within 8 blocks glow for 10 s | **Grounded:** the amethyst stays on the altar |
| Wildbloom Petal | Works like bone meal | **Bountiful:** two of the result |
| Hollow Dust | Pulls items and XP orbs within 10 blocks to you | **Hollowed:** the lower-tier rune stays on the altar |
| Star Dust | Places a starlight mote that lights a spot for 5 min | **Exalted:** one rank higher (up to III), +3 XP levels |
| Hourglass Sand | Skips a smelting furnace 10 s ahead; ages a baby animal 2 min | **Familiar:** a fusion you've made before costs no XP |
| Sanguine Bead | Grows nether wart one stage | **Bloodbound:** pay up to 6 levels in health, half a heart each, never your last heart |

The altar shows what a reagent will do. If a reagent would change nothing, the altar refuses it, so you never waste one. You can use one reagent at a time.

## Tips and counterplay

- Cast big spells where you want the marks. Fire in your garden gives furnace ash. Frost on a path gives you a slide.
- Fight next to a Void Scar and the drops come to you.
- Put Stilled Sand under a farm's drop point so items never despawn while it lasts.
- Save Everfrost Shards for large weaves. Halving a big XP cost is the biggest saving any reagent gives.
