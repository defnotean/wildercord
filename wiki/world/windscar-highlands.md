# Windscar Highlands

Gold-green grass follows the dry shelves between the **Windscar Highlands'**
exposed stone ridges. Sparse trees stand above slate-blue pools. The Marchkeepers
say the wind never took a road; it only found the paths people had stopped tending.

Look in dry Overworld hill country. Windscar occupies part of the windswept hills'
climate, with the same broad terrain transitions into neighbouring country. It
appears in newly generated chunks; saved land keeps its existing biome. Entering
the region adds a permanent field note to the **Places** tab of your
[lore journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}).

## Read the slope

The heights have exposed rock, grassy shelves and steep drops. Follow the ground
around a ridge before committing to a descent. A **Draft Kite** gives eight seconds
of Slow Falling and consumes one Windreed tassel from your inventory. Its twenty-second
rest means it cannot carry you down an unlimited series of cliffs. It does not lift
you, and it needs fuel before you step off.

**Windreed** grows in small patches on dirt and grass. Use a mature plant to collect
two tassels while leaving the root in place. Open sky and daylight let it regrow;
Life magic can advance the existing plant by one stage. Take the tassels, leave
the root, and the next walker can use the same patch.

At height **200 or higher**, Windscar also holds **Summit Wind**. Meditate with a
Blank Rune using the ordinary [attunement ritual]({{ '/world/runes-of-the-world/' | relative_url }}).
It shares the daily rest of the other mountain habitats; lower shelves do not
meet its height requirement.

Windreed, string and sweet berries make a **Windreed Braid**. Its thirty seconds of
Downwind let you approach highland animals more quietly, at a ten-percent movement
penalty. Sprinting gives you away. It will not cancel an attack already underway.

## Animals of the heights

| Look for | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Stonehorn** | A rare rock-shouldered grazer on open ground above sea level. Carry wheat and give it room. A calm animal can shed a useful plate without being killed. Its warning stamp precedes a straight charge. |
| **Galeclaw** | A rare feathered ridge runner. Offer raw rabbit or chicken before provoking it to gather a plume. If it crouches and marks a landing, move clear before the leap. |
| **Tussock Pika** | Small foragers among the grassy shelves. Watch where they pause before walking into the same patch. |
| **Skyray** | An exceptionally rare sight overhead. Its ordinary highland rarity still applies; a visit is not a promise of a sighting. |

The [Highland Beasts guide]({{ '/progression/aura-beasts/' | relative_url }}) explains
feeding, warnings, shelter, the Ridge Whistle and the recipes for travel gear.
Windscar gives these animals another habitat; ordinary spawning limits and server
settings still apply.

## Shelters on the ridge

**Highland Cairn Lodges** stand on dry stone shelves across the Windscar Highlands,
windswept hills and meadows. Look for a carved north wind-break wall, a spruce-and-stone
shelter and a tall stone cairn on the eastern point carrying a shuttered redstone
lamp and lever. In the lee of the north wall grows an open-sky terrace of six
living **Windreed** plants. Inside are a hearth, a bed, a cartography table, travel
supplies and a lectern holding **The Paired Cairns**, a four-page account of how the
Marchkeepers became ridge guides.

A **Highland Cairn Keeper** lives at the lodge. Use the resident to open ordinary
cartographer trades. Crouch and use them to hear their stories, leaving a short
pause between lines. Meeting the keeper adds them to the **People** tab of your
[lore journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}), and each story
remains under **Talk**. The keeper explains the leeward Windreed terrace, the
paired cairns and the high meridian survey ring. Returning as your investigation
progresses unlocks guidance on the watchtowers, the cairn lamp, and the optional
survey ring encounter.

Finding a lodge earns **Keeper of the Cairns**. Lodges need level shelf ground, so
not every ridge will carry one.

## Follow the Paired Cairns

Speaking with a Highland Cairn Keeper through their fourth story opens **The Paired
Cairns** in the lore journal. Visit a road watchtower or a ring of standing stones
to complete **The Ridge Watch**, connecting the hill road-markers to the high
shelves. The final chapter, **A Lamp on the Ridge**, asks you to flip the lever on
a Cairn Lodge's eastern cairn lamp and stand within six blocks of the lit beacon.
A cairn lamp another traveller already lit also counts.

The three chapters pay 90 XP, five Blank Runes and one Aura Shard in total, once
per player, with no combat or Aura stage required.

## Old routes and the High Meridian

Higher on the dry shelves stands the **Highland Survey Ring**, a stone meridian
court with four compass causeways and a northern shelter holding a **Survey
Astrolabe** and **The Glass Meridian**. Waking the astrolabe in Survival challenges
the [Glass Cartographer]({{ '/world/glass-cartographer/' | relative_url }}). The
encounter is optional and separate from the peaceful Paired Cairns journal route.

[Watchtowers and standing stones]({{ '/world/travel-sites/' | relative_url }}) can
appear here. Towers give a place to read the surrounding country, while standing
stones preserve clues to older routes. They also belong to both the Scout's and the
Cairn Keeper's investigations in the lore journal.

The [Wind Gate]({{ '/world/masters-sites/' | relative_url }}) can stand on suitable
highland ground. Its climbing challenge belongs to the Gale and Thunder schools.
These places need suitable terrain and are scattered across the world; every
ridge will not carry a landmark.

The region itself has no Heart Circle or Aura requirement. Ordinary monsters
still appear after dark. Keep a lit route back to camp and save a little Windreed
for the journey home.
