# Runebound

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/runebound.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A Runebound zombie at night, glowing orange rune marks on its body" class="shot">

## What it is

Runebound are monsters that carry their own Cord. They cast a real spell, built from the same runes and circles as yours. They are the first magic you meet in the world, and an early source of runes.

## How to get it

These monsters can be Runebound:

- **Zombies** of all kinds: husks, drowned and zombie villagers too
- **Skeletons** of all kinds: strays, bogged and wither skeletons too
- **Witches**
- **Pillagers and vindicators**
- every one of the [monsters of the wilds]({{ '/world/monsters/' | relative_url }}), each with spells that suit it

Each monster gets one roll the first time it appears:

- **Out in the world:** about 2%. The chance rises with local difficulty.
- **Inside an Archive or a dungeon:** 35%.
- **Never** on Peaceful, and never on a monster with a name tag.
- **One in six** Runebound is an **Adept**.

Servers can change these chances. If a Runebound turns into another mob that can carry a Cord, it stays Runebound. A drowned zombie is one example.

## How to use it

### Read it before you fight

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/world-runebound-nameplates.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Runebound nameplates: a zombie with Venom Touch and an Adept vindicator with Greater Arcane Blitz" class="shot">

- **The nameplate is its spell.** It shows rune icons and the spell name, in the element's colour. An Adept's name starts with *Adept ·*.
- **Rune marks** glow on its body in the spell's colour. Adepts glow brighter.
- **The warning.** Before every cast, it stops and faces its target. It holds open its spell's [magic circle]({{ '/spellcraft/magic-circles/' | relative_url }}) for **1.1 seconds**.
  - Its nameplate turns white, like *» Frost Bolt «*.
  - Zone, Rain, Mine and Domain spells also mark the ground where they will land.

### Their spells

Each monster picks one spell from its list. Adepts get a stronger version: **Split** (three copies) if the shape allows, otherwise **Amplify**.

| Monster | Spells |
|---|---|
| Skeletons | Frost Bolt, Shock Bolt, Fire Arc, Arcane Beam, Venom Bolt |
| Witches | Venom Zone, Chill Mine, Arcane Orb, Shock Rain, Blind Bolt |
| Pillagers, vindicators | Arcane Crescent, Arcane Blitz, Fire Cone, Arcane Barrage, Fire Bolt |
| Zombies | Gale-Arcane Burst, Venom Touch, Frost Wave, Shock Ring, Swift & Empower |

A zombie with *Swift & Empower* casts it on itself, then charges.

### How they cast

- It casts only at the target it is hunting, and only when it can see it.
- It waits until you are in range:

  | Shape | Range |
  |---|---|
  | Touch | 3.5 blocks |
  | Burst, Ring, Barrage | 5 |
  | Cone | 6 |
  | Blitz | 8 |
  | Self, Wave | 12 |
  | Crescent | 16 |
  | Zone, Rain, Mine | 18 |
  | Bolt, Arc, Beam, Orb | 22 |

- Its first cast comes 2 to 4 seconds after it appears. After that it casts every 3.5 to 6 seconds.
- Spell power is 60% on Easy, 80% on Normal and 100% on Hard. Adepts get 15% more.
- It hits players, pets, golems and its target. It never hits other monsters. It never changes blocks.

### Adepts and drops

Drops only count when you land the killing blow, whether by hit, arrow or spell.

| | Runebound | Adept |
|---|---|---|
| Extra health | +60% | +120% |
| Rune from its Cord | 35% | 60% |
| Torn Page ([Secret Spells]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }})) | 6% | 20% |
| [Rune of the world]({{ '/world/runes-of-the-world/' | relative_url }}): Vinelash, Undertow, Blazecall, Portalfall or Warcry | never | 8% |
| Extra experience | 10 | 20 |

### Progress

- Your first kill earns the **Runebreaker** feat and advancement. Killing an Adept earns *Adept's End*.
- The 6th [Heart Circle]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}) needs 8 Runebound kills.
- The Runesmith sometimes posts Runebound [contracts]({{ '/social/contracts/' | relative_url }}).

### Where you meet them

- anywhere their monster types spawn
- inside the Archive and dungeons
- as helpers that bosses call up
- guarding fallen stars and in rift sieges (see [World Events]({{ '/world/world-events/' | relative_url }}))

## Tips and counterplay

- **They resist their own element.** Your spells of that element do half damage to them. Pick another element.
  - Most Runebound are undead, so Life magic hits them harder. See [Creature Affinities]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }}).
- **Break line of sight** while the nameplate is lit. If you are out of sight when the warning ends, the cast fails.
- **Raise a [Shield]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}).** Raise it right at the end and you throw the spell back.
- **Hunt Adepts.** They give the best loot in the open world.
