# The Drowned Scriptorium

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/drowned-scriptorium.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Lightning arcing across the flooded pit of the Drowned Scriptorium, striking a creature wading in it, with sea-lantern pedestals standing out of the water" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A storm spell in the flood shocks everything wading in it.</span>

A library on the floor of the deep ocean, dry inside by an old trick: you come in from below, through a pool in the
floor, and water never rises through a hole in a floor. Its keeper is the **Tide Scribe**, a drowned sorcerer trailing
tendrils of ink, who floods its pit and drains it again, over and over. Water conducts: storm magic in the flood shocks
everything wading in it, the Scribe hardest of all.

## Finding it

It stands on the sea floor of a deep ocean, on foundations of prismarine. Look (or swim) for **the path**: prismarine
bricks with a dark centre line running over the sea floor, kelp and glowing sea pickles either side, and pillars of
prismarine brick topped with sea lanterns flanking the way. Behind it rises a **glass dome** over the arena. The whole
Scriptorium is about 42 blocks wide and 81 deep.

You'll be under water on the way in, so bring a way to breathe: the Tidebreath rune, a potion of Water Breathing or a
Respiration helmet.

## The Tide Scribe

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/world-tide-pit.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Scriptorium's arena from the balcony with the tide out: a dry pit with rings of brick and sea lanterns round the core, six pedestals, and a glass dome above" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The pit with the tide out. The six pedestals and the balcony stay dry when it floods.</span>

### Waking it

It rises out of a pool of ink over its core the first time a player comes within **14 blocks**: *"Ink blooms in the
water. The Tide Scribe rises from its core."* Its first spell comes about 3 seconds later, and its first flood about 5.

### What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health** | 340 |
| **Armour** | 6 |
| **Knockback** | None: it can't be moved, and water doesn't push it |
| **Breathes** | Under water, of course |
| **Immune to** | Falls and other monsters |
| **Moves** | It floats. With the pit dry it drifts low and slow in a circle 4.5 blocks from the core; flooded, it swims wide, quick circles 6.5 blocks out, down in the water. It never strays more than 16 blocks from the core |
| **Boss bar** | Blue, darkens the sky. Between spells it tells you the tide: *the tide is rising*, *the arena is flooded*, *the tide is going out*, or *stranded in the ice!* |
| **Spell power** | 10% more than a Runebound's: 66% of normal on Easy, 88% on Normal, 110% on Hard |
| **Affinities** | Weak to **life** (+50%: it's a drowned sorcerer), resists **frost** (half, though frost still freezes its flood and strands it). Its real weakness is still a storm through its own flood. See [Creature Affinities]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }}) |

### Its trick: the tide

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/world-tide-flood.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Tide Scribe swimming in its flooded pit, the water two blocks deep, the six pedestals standing dry above it" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The tide is in.</span>

The pit (everything within 12 blocks of the core) floods **two blocks deep** and drains again, over and over:

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dry** | 12 seconds | 10 seconds | 8 seconds |
| **Rising** | 2 seconds | 2 seconds | 2 seconds |
| **Flooded** | 15 seconds | 13 seconds | 11 seconds |
| **Ebbing** | 1.5 seconds | 1.5 seconds | 1.5 seconds |

The water rises a ring at a time from the core outward, with a rush and a warning to everyone near: *"The tide is
rising: get up out of the water, or freeze it"*. It ebbs from the rim inward, and **every ebb clears all the water and
ice** from the pit. The balcony and the tops of the six pedestals stay dry. The pit counts as flooded from halfway
through the rise to halfway through the ebb.

**Water conducts.** While the pit is flooded:

- **Storm in the flood shocks everything wading in it.** A storm spell that lands in the water (on it, or just over
  it, or striking something standing in it) sends a shock through the whole pit: **everyone in the water takes 5
  damage times the spell's power**, you included if you're wading. The Scribe takes **five times that**, straight
  through its water armour, and **staggers for 2 seconds**: it can't cast, breaks off any spell it was telegraphing,
  and takes 50% more from other hits. Each spell shocks the pit at most once a second (a Zone sitting in the water
  shocks once a second, not every moment). *"The water carries your storm to everything in it (you too, if you're
  wading)"*.
- **Frost freezes the top of the flood.** A frost spell that lands in the water turns the top layer round it into
  **ice you can stand on**: a disc about 5 blocks across. If the Scribe is swimming within 3.5 blocks of where it
  landed, the ice closes round it and it's **stranded** for **3.5 seconds**: held still, it can't cast (any spell it
  was telegraphing is broken off) and it takes **50% more damage**. Everyone near reads *"The ice closes round the Tide
  Scribe. It's stranded!"* Once it breaks free, frost can't strand it again for 5 seconds.
- **The water wraps it.** While it swims in the flood it takes **half damage** from anything but a shock through the
  water. With the pit dry, it's only a sorcerer: full damage from everything.

And the water works both ways: **its own storm spells shock you** through the flood just the same, if you're in it.

Every storm spell does more to you if you're wet: in water, in rain, or soaked by a bubble, it sets off **Conduct** on
you (+50%, arcing to two more). It telegraphs each spell for 1.4 seconds, with a pause of 3 to 4.5 seconds between
them in the first phase, 2.5 to 4 in the second and 2 to 3.5 in the third.

### Between phases: calling the tide

At two thirds and one third of its health it **calls the tide**: for **3 seconds** it can't be hurt while a whirlpool of
ink and bubbles turns round it, and if the pit was dry, the tide starts rising at once. The boss bar says *calling the
tide*, and you read:

- the first time: *"The Tide Scribe calls the tide, and the drowned answer."*
- the second time: *"The Tide Scribe writes the deep into the room."*

Two Runebound **drowned** rise in the pit about 9 blocks from the core: one with **Shock Bolt** and one with **Frost
Touch**. The second time they're Adepts. They vanish when the Scribe falls.

No blow carries it past the start of its next phase.

### Strategy

- **Life magic when it's dry.** Between floods it's only a sorcerer, and a drowned one: life magic (Vinelash,
  Moonpetal, Venom's hit) hits it 50% harder. Frost's own damage does half to it: freeze the water with it, don't
  count on the hit. See [Creature Affinities]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }}).
- **Get out of the water when the tide comes in.** Stand on the balcony or on one of the six pedestals. Up there, its
  shocks through the flood can't reach you, and you can shock the water freely.
- **Then shock the flood.** Any storm spell landing in the water, or hitting the Scribe while it swims, does five times
  the shock to it and staggers it. Even `Bolt · Shock` at normal strength takes 25 off it through the water. Stack
  your other damage into the 2-second stagger (+50%).
- **Freeze it where it swims.** Frost landing within 3.5 blocks of it strands it for 3.5 seconds: it can't cast and
  takes 50% more. The ice is also a floor you can stand on, but the next ebb takes it away.
- **Never cast storm while you're wading.** The shock goes through you too. Freeze yourself a floor first, or climb out.
- **Hit it hard while the pit is dry.** Then it takes full damage from everything. While it swims in the flood,
  everything but a shock is halved.
- **Watch the boss bar for the tide.** *The tide is rising* means you have about 2 seconds to get up and out.
- **Kill the drowned it calls up.** The Shock Bolt drowned's storm conducts through the flood to you if you're in it.
- **Beware of being soaked.** After a Bubble Bolt, its storm spells set off Conduct on you, and in the water you're wet
  anyway.

### When it falls

It dies slowly, over 3 seconds, trailing ink and bubbles, and the tide goes out for good: the pit drains at once.
Then:

- **Everyone who fought it** and is still within 96 blocks of the core gets a **Tier IV rune** they don't know yet,
  straight into their pack.
- **Its killer** gets, the same way:

  | Loot | |
  |---|---|
  | **The Drowned Quill** | Its trophy. Hold it up: breathe and swim like the drowned for 3 minutes (Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace; then it rests for 5) |
  | **[Tidewrit]({{ '/runes/world/#tidewrit' | relative_url }})** | Its own Tier IV rune: a 7-wide wall of water rolls from you through the point, 10 damage to everything in it, sweeping it 8 blocks on, soaked |
  | **3 Mana Crystals** | |
  | **A Torn Page** | |
  | **A storm or frost rune** | One Tier II or III rune from the hall chest's list |
  | **A rune of the Scriptorium** | 50% chance: If Wet (most often) or Drowning Word |

- **220 experience** on the core.
- Anything that doesn't fit, or has nobody to take it, is left on the core, glowing and safe.
- **Low Tide**: everyone within 64 blocks earns this feat (*"Turned the Tide Scribe's own flood against it and brought
  it down"*), worth **1,500 mana** toward the next Heart Circle, and the advancement *Low Tide*.
- It counts as a boss for the **7th Heart Circle** for everyone within 96 blocks.

## The Vault

Behind the Storm and Arcane door off the balcony. Each of its **two chests** holds:

| Pool | What |
|---|---|
| **1 Tier IV rune** | One of Domain, Sonic Boom, Wither, Dragon Breath, Summon, Starfall, Hollow, Infinity, Reversal, Stasis or Rewind |
| **1 rune of the Scriptorium** | [If Wet]({{ '/runes/world/#if_wet' | relative_url }}) (5 in 7) or [Drowning Word]({{ '/runes/world/#drowning_word' | relative_url }}) (2 in 7) |
| **Sometimes another** | 35% chance of a second rune of the Scriptorium |
| **2 to 4 of** | 1 to 2 Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, 2 to 6 prismarine crystals, 2 to 6 gold ingots, 2 to 5 ink sacs, 1 to 3 diamonds, 1 to 2 nautilus shells, an echo shard, or a heart of the sea |

The runes of the Scriptorium:

- **If Wet** (Tier II link): the rest of the spell fires only if you're in water or rain.
- **Drowning Word** (Tier III frost): for 5 seconds the target's lungs fill with water (it loses its air and takes 2
  damage a second), and it's soaked.
