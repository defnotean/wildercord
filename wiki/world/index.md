# The World

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/world-tide-flood.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Tide Scribe, a drowned sorcerer, swimming in its flooded pit under a glass dome, with sea-lantern pedestals standing out of the water" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The Tide Scribe's pit in the Drowned Scriptorium, with the tide in.</span>

Magic in Wildercord reaches the whole world. Monsters cast spells, old halls hide doors only spells can open, every
dimension has a dungeon boss that fights like a caster, and your spells change the ground they land on. This section
is your map.

## Where to go, roughly in order

You can do most of these in any order. Early rows need only a Twine Cord and a few runes. Later rows want a strong
Cord, good armour and a plan.

| | What | Where | What it gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Runebound monsters]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}) | Anywhere monsters spawn | A rune from its Cord, sometimes a Torn Page |
| 2 | [Monsters of the wilds]({{ '/world/monsters/' | relative_url }}) | Forests, peaks, swamps and caves | Brewing drops and small magics |
| 3 | [Creatures]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}) | Most Overworld biomes | Ingredients, a tame fox, Mana Crystals |
| 4 | [World magic]({{ '/world/world-magic/' | relative_url }}), [residues]({{ '/world/residues/' | relative_url }}) and [places of power]({{ '/world/places-of-power/' | relative_url }}) | Wherever your spells land | Frozen bridges, reagents, stronger spells at the right place and time |
| 5 | [World events]({{ '/world/world-events/' | relative_url }}) | Near you, now and then | Mana storms, fallen stars and rift sieges, with runes and Mana Crystals |
| 6 | [Runes of the world]({{ '/world/runes-of-the-world/' | relative_url }}) | Structure chests, biomes and fishing | Runes nobody can craft |
| 7 | [Expeditions]({{ '/world/expeditions/' | relative_url }}) | Deserts, forests, meadows and windswept hills | Relics |
| 8 | [Root Guardian and Storm Conductor]({{ '/world/root-and-storm-bosses/' | relative_url }}) | Swamps and peaks | Boss loot |
| 9 | [The Archive]({{ '/world/archive/' | relative_url }}) | Under the Overworld | Tier IV runes, casting gear |
| 10 | [The Ember Sanctum]({{ '/world/ember-sanctum/' | relative_url }}) | The Nether | The Cinder Heart trophy |
| 11 | [The Drowned Scriptorium]({{ '/world/drowned-scriptorium/' | relative_url }}) | The deep ocean floor | The Drowned Quill trophy |
| 12 | [The Astral Observatory]({{ '/world/astral-observatory/' | relative_url }}) | The End's outer islands | The Astral Lens trophy |

## 48 places to explore

Six families of smaller sites dot the world, eight in each family. Most are peaceful; a few hide a guard, a seal or a
small puzzle in front of their best chest. Walking into one earns its advancement, and many add an entry to your
[lore journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}).

They only appear in **newly generated chunks**. Land you explored before the update is never changed, and the old-world
upgrade tool doesn't add them either, so head out past the edge of your map to find them.

| Family | Where | The eight sites |
|---|---|---|
| [Farmstead Sites]({{ '/world/farm-sites/' | relative_url }}) | Plains, forests, meadows, savannas, taigas | Windmill farmstead, herbalist's cottage, beekeeper's apiary, walled orchard, shepherd's hut, sunken mushroom ring, granary barn, scarecrow field |
| [Master halls and shrines]({{ '/world/masters-sites/' | relative_url }}) | Badlands, windswept hills, peaks, snow, jungles, desert, cherry groves, deep caves | Forge Dojo, Wind Gate, Quarry Hall, Waterfall Shrine, Root Temple, Sundial Court, Star Terrace, Resonance Chamber |
| [Mines, Forges and Crystals]({{ '/world/mine-sites/' | relative_url }}) | Hills, meadows, underground, badlands, the Nether | Hillside Mine, Mountain Forge Hall, Crystal Survey Lab, Collapsed Delve, Basalt Foundry, Deepslate Vault, Prospector's Camp, Miner's Rest |
| [Roadside Sites]({{ '/world/travel-sites/' | relative_url }}) | Flat ground across the Overworld | Wayfarer Inn, Watchtower, Broken Bridge, Rune Library, Standing Stones, Caravan Camp, Cartographer's Hut, Vow Circle |
| [Water Sites]({{ '/world/water-sites/' | relative_url }}) | Rivers, swamps, coasts, warm seas, frozen water | Stilt Smokehouse, Lighthouse, Watermill, Ice Fishing Camp, Pier and Boathouse, Sunken Shrine, Net-Weavers' Huts, Tidepool Grotto |
| [Wild Places]({{ '/world/wilds-sites/' | relative_url }}) | Jungle, desert, snow, badlands, cherry groves, deep dark, the Nether, the End | Venom Ziggurat, Dune Temple, Rime Monastery, Iron Gatehouse, Dawn Pavilion, Echo Post, Ember Outpost, Void Lantern |

## Sword Masters

Sword Masters don't live in any building. A wandering duelist calls one to you for a trial. See
[Sword Masters]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}).

The eight [Master halls]({{ '/world/masters-sites/' | relative_url }}) are where their schools train. Each hall serves
two related schools and links to the pages for both, such as [Ember]({{ '/masters/ember/' | relative_url }}) and
[Crimson]({{ '/masters/crimson/' | relative_url }}) for the Forge Dojo. The lectern in every hall explains how to call
that school's Master.

A server owner can also add a plain **Wayfarer Training Pavilion** to an old world. See
[Pavilion Upgrade for Old Worlds]({{ '/world/pavilion-upgrade/' | relative_url }}).

## The four dungeons

| | The Archive | The Ember Sanctum | The Astral Observatory | The Drowned Scriptorium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Overworld, underground | Nether | End outer islands | Deep ocean floor |
| **About one per** | 704 blocks | 576 blocks | 512 blocks | 640 blocks |
| **First door** | Frost + Storm | Fire + Earth | Void + Arcane | Frost + Storm |
| **Second door** | Fire + Wind | Void + Storm | Time + Wind | Life + Wind |
| **Vault door** | Arcane + Life | Frost + Fire | Void + Life | Storm + Arcane |
| **Boss** | The Archivist (400 health) | The Cinder Warden (300) | The Star-Eater (320) | The Tide Scribe (340) |
| **Its trick** | Blinks and rewrites its spells | Only reactions hurt it | Its shield turns spells back | Floods its pit |
| **Trophy** | none | Cinder Heart | Astral Lens | Drowned Quill |

## How every dungeon works

Each one has the same shape:

1. A way in you can spot from a distance.
2. A **Rune Seal door**. Hit it with a spell of each of its two elements to open it. See
   [Rune Seal doors]({{ '/world/archive/' | relative_url }}#rune-seal-doors).
3. A hall of Runebound guards with a chest.
4. A second seal door, then the boss arena.
5. A vault behind a third seal door, with two chests.

About a third of the zombies, skeletons, witches and illagers that spawn inside are Runebound.

### Warded arenas
{: #warded-arenas }

The arena and the vault are warded. You can't break, blast or push their walls, floors or domes, so the only way in is
through the dungeon. The rest of the dungeon isn't warded. Blocks you place inside a ward can still be broken, and
Creative players aren't stopped.

The ward also stills the wind. [Soar]({{ '/runes/effects/wind/' | relative_url }}#soar) won't lift you inside an arena
or vault, and fliers are set down gently. The fight is on foot.

### The bosses

- **They wake once**, when a player comes near, and never again after they fall.
- **They stay in their arenas.** You can't lead them through portals or carry them off.
- **Every spell is announced** on the boss bar before it lands.
- **Three phases**, changing at two thirds and one third of health. During a change the boss can't be hurt, and it
  calls up two Runebound. No single blow skips a phase.
- **Monsters and falls can't hurt them.** Freeze, Levitate and Gravity Well don't hold or move them.
- **Rewards:** everyone within 96 blocks when it dies gets the 7th
  [Heart Circle]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}) boss credit. Everyone within 64 blocks earns its
  Grimoire feat.
- **The boss bar** shows within 48 blocks.

For the three dimension bosses, each player who hurt it and is still nearby gets a Tier IV rune they don't know yet.
The killer also gets the trophy and the rest of the loot.

### Trophies

A trophy is kept, not spent. Use it for its gift, then it rests for 5 minutes. Fire and lava don't destroy it.

| Trophy | From | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Cinder Heart** | The Cinder Warden | Fire Resistance, 3 minutes |
| **Astral Lens** | The Star-Eater | Night Vision for 3 minutes, Slow Falling for 1 minute |
| **Drowned Quill** | The Tide Scribe | Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace, 3 minutes |

### What to bring

- A spell of every element the doors ask for. A cheap Tier I effect is enough.
- A [Shield]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}) spell.
- Two elements that [react]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}). The Cinder Warden needs them.
- Spare [mana]({{ '/progression/mana/' | relative_url }}) and Blank Runes.
- For the Drowned Scriptorium, a way to breathe under water.
