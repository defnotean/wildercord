# Luminous Reedlands

Blue-green pools, pale grass and low swamp trees mark the **Luminous Reedlands**.
At night, the banks carry Moonreed buds and the passing lights of Glimmerwings.
The Tideward call them the lamps of the first crossing: leave a flower standing
for the next traveller.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/luminous-reedlands-day.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Naturally generated Moonreed colonies beside blue-green water and pale banks in the Luminous Reedlands" class="shot">

## Finding the region

Look in the wettest parts of ordinary Overworld swamp country. Reedlands share
the low ground and shallow pools of swamps, with cooler water colours and softer
green banks. They generate in new chunks; land already saved keeps its biome.
Mangrove swamps remain a separate habitat.

Entering the region adds a permanent field note to the **Places** tab of your
[lore journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}). Returning does
not duplicate it. You can explore the region at any Heart Circle or Aura stage.

## A living bank

| Look for | What to do |
|---|---|
| [Lantern Newts]({{ '/world/luminous-wetlands/' | relative_url }}) | Offer seagrass to a calm, wet newt for a Dusk Pearl. Its lanterns brighten at night. |
| [Moonreed]({{ '/world/moonreed-gardens/' | relative_url }}) | Leave a bud beside open water for a Glimmerwing to pollinate. Harvest the flower without uprooting the bank. |
| [Glimmerwings]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}#glimmerwing) | Watch their evening visits. Marshlights attract them, so a small garden can become a gathering place. |
| [Reedback Crabs]({{ '/world/reedback-crabs/' | relative_url }}) | Watch the shallows and reed cover; their existing feeding and shelter rules still apply. |
| [Siltcrest Bitterns]({{ '/world/siltcrest-bittern/' | relative_url }}) | Give a hunting bird room. A sheltered bank lets it return to cover after feeding. |
| [Mossback Tortoises]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}#mossback-tortoise) | These carry the same swamp garden as their neighbours in ordinary swamps. |

Moonreed grows in small colonies on eligible banks. A patch needs empty space,
suitable ground, water close by and an open sky; trees and dry ridges can leave
long gaps between flowers. New colonies do not replace an existing plant or
drain a pool.

## A light at the landing

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/reedkeeper-landing.png' | relative_url }}" alt="A green-roofed Tideward survey house and Moonreed garden on stilts beside a wooded Reedlands bank" class="shot">

**Tideward Reedkeeper Landings** stand on stilts over shallow pools. Look for two
lantern posts and a spruce survey house, with a small open garden beside the deck.
Inside are a bed, a map table, fishing supplies and a lectern holding **The First
Crossing**. Its four pages tell how the Tideward passed a lamp from boat to boat
during a flood, and why their last boatman counted everyone twice.

A **Tideward Reedkeeper** lives here. Use the resident to open ordinary fishing
trades. Crouch and use them to hear their stories, allowing a short pause between
lines. The first meeting adds the keeper to the journal's **People** tab, and each
heard story stays under **Talk**, including after you leave the world.
The keeper explains the garden, the first crossing and the Reed-Crowned Ferryman.
The resident lives as a villager and needs protection from the usual dangers.

The garden is planted with living buds beside water and beneath an open sky. It
can host the same evening pollination as a bank you plant yourself. Keep its roots
and water intact if you want it to flower again.

Finding the landing earns **Keeper of the Crossing**. Landings need suitable low
ground and shallow water, so they will not appear beside every pool.

## Follow the Drowned Road

Meeting a Reedkeeper opens **The Drowned Road** in the journal. Listen through the
Ferryman's account, then follow the coast to a pier boathouse. Its *Second Count*
ledger connects the passenger counts to the lighthouse keeper's duty. The last
chapter asks you to climb a lighthouse and tend its lamp with the lever beside it.
A lamp another visitor already lit also counts when you stand beside it.

The three chapters pay 90 XP, five Blank Runes and one Aura Shard altogether,
once per player. Earlier visits still count, and no combat or Aura rank is needed.
See the [Lore Journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}) for the
individual clues and rewards.

## Smoke above the water

An older basin, **The Last Crossing**, can also stand among the pools. Its four dry
paths meet at a mooring, with an instruction book beside the entrance bell.
Deliberately ringing that bell challenges the
[Reed-Crowned Ferryman]({{ '/world/reed-crowned-ferryman/' | relative_url }}).
The encounter is optional and is separate from the Drowned Road journal route.

[Stilt Smokehouses]({{ '/world/water-sites/' | relative_url }}) can stand over
shallow water here. Their tall signal smoke is easier to see than the deck between
the trees. Inside are fishing supplies and shelter for a pause along the bank.
They need suitable water and ground, so every stretch of Reedlands will not have one.

The region has ordinary night-time monsters. Lantern Newts and Glimmerwings make
the water beautiful, but they do not protect a camp. Place your own lights and
leave a clear route back to the deck.
