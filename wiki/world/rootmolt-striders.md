# Rootmolt Striders

## What it is

A wide cave creature with six legs, bark plates and shovel arms. It eats mature Glowcaps, then guards that garden. Before it strikes, it warns you with one straight physical rake.

## How to get it

Rootmolts appear in lush caves and dripstone caves. They need all of these:

- below Y 48
- a dark spot with no sky above
- dry footing
- a mature Glowcap nearby (fresh buds don't count)

A few Rootmolts close together stop new ones from arriving. Peaceful stops them completely.

They drop no items or experience. A meal resets the Glowcap to a bud and gives you nothing. That makes them rivals for your [Glowcap nursery]({{ '/world/glowcap-nurseries/' | relative_url }}), not a loot source.

## How to use it

![Top-view warning and sidestep diagram]({{ '/assets/images/rootmolt-rake-illustration.svg' | relative_url }})

| Tell | What to do |
|---|---|
| Shovel arms rise and dirt marks a straight line | Step sideways. The line doesn't turn to follow you |
| The rake hits you | It may hold you with a short Root Tether |
| You're slowed by Root Tether | You can still attack. Hit it, cleanse the effect or get out of its sight |
| Arms lower | Leave the garden before the next warning |

A real hit on the Rootmolt breaks its hold.

## Tips and counterplay

- React to the warning. Don't stand in the line and wait.
- [Rootbound Greaves]({{ '/world/belowkeeper-drainhouses/' | relative_url }}) let you resist one hold.
  - Crouch still on dry ground for 1.5 seconds to ready them.
  - This costs 8 extra wear and starts a 10-second rest.
- A nearby [Sporeback Snail]({{ '/world/sporeback-snails/' | relative_url }}) tucks into its shell when a Rootmolt warns. A narrow, sheltered nursery can let snails in and keep Rootmolts out.
- The Field Guide lists Rootmolts under Wildlife.
- Watch one eat from within 6 blocks, then counter or dodge its rake. Each counts as a field note for the nursery investigation.
- Server owners: the `rootmolt_strider` wildlife setting turns them off.
