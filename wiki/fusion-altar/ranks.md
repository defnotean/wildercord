# Ranking runes up

Every rune you learn starts at **rank I**, the rune as crafted. At the [Fusion Altar]({{ '/fusion-altar/' | relative_url }})
three copies of a rune make the next rank, and a higher rank hits harder at the same mana cost, in every spell the
rune is threaded in.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-tooltip-rank.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The tooltip of a Fire Rune II: Effect, Tier II, Fire, and a gold line reading Rank II: +25% power, at the same mana" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A ranked rune shimmers, and its name and tooltip give its rank</span>

## What a rank does

| Rank | Power | Mana | Tooltip line |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | as crafted | as crafted | |
| II | **+25%** | the same | Rank II: +25% power, at the same mana |
| III | **+50%** | the same | Rank III: +50% power, at the same mana |

- **Power** is whatever the effect's strength is: damage, healing, force and blasts, the same things Amplify
  strengthens.
- **The cost never changes.** A rank III Fire costs exactly what a rank I Fire costs.
- **Ranks stack with everything else.** A rank II Fire with an Amplify is 1.5 times 1.25: +88% power. Heart Circles,
  enchantments, charging and casting gear all multiply on top.
- **Effects measured in levels**, like Swift's Speed or Haste, and **mining tiers** work differently: rank II adds
  nothing there, and rank III counts as one Amplify (one level, or one mining tier, higher). Any damage or healing
  the same rune does still gets the full rank bonus.
- **Everywhere at once.** Your rank is part of what you know, not part of a spell. Raise Fire to rank II and every
  spell and passive with Fire in it is stronger, including spells in [Knots]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}).

The Cord screen shows your ranks. A rune's tooltip in the Codex gives its name with its rank ("Fire II") and the rank
line, and the readout names the rank with the power it adds, for example *Fire II (+25% power)*.

## Making a higher rank

1. Put three copies of the same rune, **all at the same rank**, in the altar's three rune sockets.
2. Leave the middle socket **empty**.
3. The panel reads **Upgrade** with the rank it will make. Press **Fuse**.

| To make | Put in | XP levels | Rank I copies needed in all |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank II | 3 rank I runes | 2 | 3 |
| Rank III | 3 rank II runes | 5 | 9 |

Going from nothing to rank III takes **nine** copies of the rune and **11 XP levels**: three rank-ups to rank II
(2 levels each) and one to rank III (5 levels). You can't mix ranks: a rank I and two rank II copies won't fuse.

Three is the top. A rank III rune can't go any higher.

## Learning a ranked rune

A ranked rune shimmers like an enchanted item and is named with its rank: **Fire Rune II**, **Fire Rune III**.
Right-click it to learn it.

- **If you already know the rune** at a lower rank, it goes up: *Fire is now rank II, in every spell it's threaded in*.
- **If you don't know the rune yet**, you learn it straight away at that rank.
- **A rank never goes down.** You know each rune at one rank. A copy at your rank or lower can't be learned (the game
  says you already know it) and isn't used up, so you can keep it for fusing or trading.

Ranks belong to the caster. Whoever casts a spell casts each rune at the rank *they* know it: a ranked rune you give
a friend upgrades *their* rune once they learn it. Monsters always cast at rank I.

## Which runes can rank

Only **effects with power** have ranks. That means:

- **Yes:** effects that deal damage, heal, push, blast or otherwise have a strength. That includes crafted effects,
  runes of the world, Tier IV effects and most fused runes.
- **No:** shapes, modifiers, links, Knots, and effects with no strength to raise. The altar refuses them with
  *(Rune) has no power to rank up*.
- **No:** [innate runes]({{ '/runes/innate/' | relative_url }}). They grow with your Heart Circles instead.

**The quick way to tell:** if **Amplify** is among the modifiers that work on a rune (every rune's entry on the
[Runes]({{ '/runes/' | relative_url }}) pages lists them), that rune has ranks.

Of the 55 [fused runes]({{ '/runes/fused/' | relative_url }}), 49 can be ranked. The six that can't are Glacier,
Nullify, Second Wind, Soulbond, Timesteal and Warp.

## Ranks and the rest of the game

| | What happens to ranks |
|---|---|
| **Combining** | A fused rune keeps the **lower** of the two ranks you put in. Two rank III effects make a rank III fused rune; a rank III and a rank I make a rank I. A fused rune that can't be ranked always comes out plain. See [Combining]({{ '/fusion-altar/combining/' | relative_url }}#keeping-ranks). |
| **Knots** | A Knot holds runes, not ranks. Whoever casts it uses their own ranks for the runes inside. |
| **The Runesmith** | Only plain rank I runes pay for a buyback or a reroll. A ranked rune is refused. See [The Runesmith]({{ '/social/runesmith/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Heart Circles** | Breakthroughs that ask you to know a number of runes count runes, not ranks. |

## Where to get copies

- **Craft them.** Every Tier I to III rune has a recipe (see [Rune Recipes]({{ '/items/rune-recipes/' | relative_url }})),
  and a Blank Rune recipe makes four blanks at a time.
- **Buy them** from a [Runesmith]({{ '/social/runesmith/' | relative_url }}), or earn a Tier II rune from a
  [daily contract]({{ '/social/contracts/' | relative_url }}).
- **Find them** in chests and on monsters. Tier IV runes and runes of the world can only be found, so rank III of one
  of those is a real prize.
