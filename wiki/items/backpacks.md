# Backpacks

## What it is

A **backpack** is extra room you carry. What's in it stays in it wherever the backpack goes: in your hand, on your
back, in a chest, on the ground, or dropped when you die.

| Backpack | Holds | Made from |
|---|---|---|
| **Backpack** | 2 rows, 18 slots | Leather, String and a Chest |
| **Reinforced Backpack** | 3 rows, 27 slots | A Backpack, a Chest, Iron and Leather |
| **Runewoven Backpack** | 4 rows, 36 slots | A Reinforced Backpack, a Mana Crystal, an Echo Shard, Amethyst and Blank Runes |

Backpacks don't stack. The tooltip shows how full it is and the first few things inside.

### Runewoven Backpack

{% include recipe.html id="runewoven_backpack" alt="Crafting grid, any layout: a Reinforced Backpack, a Mana Crystal, an Echo Shard, 4 Amethyst Shards and 2 Blank Runes" %}

A **Reinforced Backpack**, a [Mana Crystal]({{ '/items/' | relative_url }}#mana-crystal), an Echo Shard, 4 Amethyst
Shards and 2 [Blank Runes]({{ '/items/' | relative_url }}#blank-rune), anywhere in the grid. It **doesn't burn** in
lava or fire.

**Upgrading keeps everything:** the contents, in the same slots, plus the colour and name.

## How to use it

### Opening it

- **In your hand:** use it (right-click), from either hand.
- **Worn:** press `B` (**Open backpack** in Controls, under Wildercord). Works in the world and in your inventory.

It opens like a chest. `B`, `E` or `Esc` closes it. While it's open from your hotbar, its slot is locked so you can't
drop or swap it.

### The Backpack slot

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-backpack-slot.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The survival inventory with a tray on top holding a staff, a focus, the tome and, in the fourth slot, a backpack" class="shot">
<span class="caption">The Backpack slot, the fourth on the gear tray</span>

Your inventory has a **Backpack slot**, the fourth slot on the tray above it, after the
[gear slots]({{ '/gear/' | relative_url }}). **Shift-click** a backpack to put it there (and again to take it off).
A backpack in the slot is **worn**: `B` opens it, and everyone sees it on your back.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-backpack-worn.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A player seen from behind wearing a red Reinforced Backpack with a Fire Staff strapped across it" class="shot">
<span class="caption">A dyed Reinforced Backpack, with a staff strapped over it</span>

- A **staff** in your Staff slot is strapped across it.
- It sits over a chestplate or cape, and tucks between **elytra** wings while you glide.
- It's hidden while you're invisible. Others never see what's inside.

### What can't go inside

Nothing that holds items: no other backpack, no shulker box, no bundle, and no chest or block picked up with items
still in it. An empty chest or furnace is fine. A backpack can't go in a shulker box or bundle either, but it can go
in a chest, barrel or ender chest.

### Dyeing

Put a backpack in a crafting grid with one or more dyes, like leather armour. What's inside stays. Use it on a water
cauldron to wash the dye out.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Dropped** with `Q`, it keeps everything. Pick it up and it's all there.
- **Chests, hoppers and dispensers** move it whole.
- **When you die,** a backpack in your inventory or Backpack slot drops with everything in it (unless the server keeps
  inventory).
- **Burnt up or blown up,** a Backpack or Reinforced Backpack spills its contents on the ground, like a shulker box.
  Upgrade to Runewoven before trips to the Nether.
- **Logging out** with it open loses nothing.
