# Items and crafting

Every item and block Wildercord adds, apart from the runes themselves: what it's for, its recipe, and where to find
it. For the recipe of every rune, see **[Rune Recipes]({{ '/items/rune-recipes/' | relative_url }})**.

<span class="caption">The Wildercord tab in the creative menu</span>

Recipes below are for a crafting table. A grid shows a **shaped** recipe (the layout matters; an empty cell is an
empty slot). A list is a **shapeless** recipe (put the items anywhere in the grid). All of them show up in the recipe
book.

## Runes and blanks

### Blank Rune

The plain tablet every rune is made from.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| | Cobblestone | |
| Cobblestone | Lapis Lazuli | Cobblestone |
| | Cobblestone | |

4 Cobblestone around 1 Lapis Lazuli makes **4 Blank Runes**.

**Used for:**

- **Every rune recipe:** a Blank Rune plus a few items. See [Rune Recipes]({{ '/items/rune-recipes/' | relative_url }}).
  Once you've held a Blank Rune, all the rune recipes appear in your recipe book.
- **The Twine Cord** and the **Scribing Desk** (below).
- **Tying Knots** at the [Fusion Altar]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}).
- **Attunement:** some lands hold a rune of their own. Meditate there with a Blank Rune in hand and it becomes that
  rune. Its tooltip counts how many you've attuned. See [Runes of the World]({{ '/world/runes-of-the-world/' | relative_url }}).
- **Lightning runes:** drop Blank Runes on the ground within two and a half blocks of a **lightning rod** during a
  thunderstorm. When lightning strikes the rod, every blank lying there becomes a Lightning rune, a whole stack at a
  time. See the Lightning rune on [Storm effects]({{ '/runes/effects/storm/' | relative_url }}).

**Also from:** a Novice [Runesmith]({{ '/social/runesmith/' | relative_url }}) (4 for 1 emerald), reaction and
element-cast [contracts]({{ '/social/contracts/' | relative_url }}) (6 or 4), Archive library chests and the hall
chests of the other dungeons, a closed [rift siege]({{ '/world/world-events/' | relative_url }}), and some
[advancements]({{ '/progression/advancements/' | relative_url }}) (8 each).

### Runes

Each rune is its own item, named after its rune (*Fire Rune*). Hold one and right-click to learn it for good; after
that the item is a spare. Runes stack to 16.

- **Recipes:** [Rune Recipes]({{ '/items/rune-recipes/' | relative_url }}) lists every craftable rune.
- **Everything about them:** [Runes]({{ '/runes/' | relative_url }}).
- **Ranked runes** (*Fire Rune II*) shimmer and come from the [Fusion Altar]({{ '/fusion-altar/ranks/' | relative_url }}).
- **Spares** can be ranked up or combined at the Fusion Altar, or sold and swapped at a
  [Runesmith]({{ '/social/runesmith/' | relative_url }}).
- A rune from an add-on mod that's no longer installed shows as a **Silent Rune**. It stays safe and wakes up again
  when the add-on comes back.

### Knot

A whole spell tied into one rune at the [Fusion Altar]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}), from a Blank
Rune, a piece of String and one of your spells. Named after the spell (*Knot: Ember Fan*), it takes one socket, costs
10% less mana than the runes inside, and anyone can learn it with a right-click. Knots stack to 16.

## Cords

A Cord is worn in the **Cord slot above your off-hand** (open your inventory with E). Each Cord is crafted from the one
before it, so you build them up in order. Cords don't stack. See [Cords]({{ '/spellcraft/cords/' | relative_url }})
for everything about them.

| Cord | Sockets per spell | Spells | Runes up to | Mana | Regenerates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Twine Cord** | 3 | 1 | Tier I | 100 | 5 a second |
| **Copper Cord** | 5 | 2 | Tier II | 150 | 6 a second |
| **Amethyst Cord** | 8 | 3 | Tier III | 225 | 7 a second |
| **Echo Cord** | 12 | 4 | Tier IV | 300 | 8 a second |

### Twine Cord

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| String | | String |
| | String | |
| | Blank Rune | |

3 String and 1 Blank Rune. The first time you wear a Cord you learn Self, Bolt and Push, and spell 1 is ready to
cast. See [Getting Started]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}).

### Copper Cord

Shapeless: **Twine Cord**, **4 Copper Ingots**, **1 Amethyst Shard**.

### Amethyst Cord

Shapeless: **Copper Cord**, **4 Amethyst Shards**, **2 Gold Ingots**.

### Echo Cord

Shapeless: **Amethyst Cord**, **2 Echo Shards**, **1 Netherite Scrap**.

Cords can be enchanted with Wildercord's Cord enchantments at an enchanting table, an anvil or with books. See
[Enchantments]({{ '/progression/enchantments/' | relative_url }}).

### Mana Crystal

| | | |
|---|---|---|

**Use it:** right-click to absorb it: **+10 max mana, forever**. Up to **10** crystals count (+100 max mana); after
that the game tells you your mana can't grow further with crystals, and the crystal isn't used. See
[Mana]({{ '/progression/mana/' | relative_url }}).

**Used for:** every Tier III rune recipe (with a Diamond), the Wellstone, every staff and every focus.

**Also from:**

| Bastion treasure chests | 25% |
| Buried treasure | 20% |

| The Archivist, the Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater, the Tide Scribe | 3 each |
| The Riftcaller | 1 to 3 |
| A Fallen Star | 1 |

### Potions

Two potions, brewed in a brewing stand from an **Awkward Potion**:

| Potion | Brew | Effect | What it does | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Potion of Clarity** | Awkward Potion + **Amethyst Shard** | Clarity | Mana regenerates 50% faster | 3:00 |
| Potion of Clarity, longer | Potion of Clarity + **Redstone** | Clarity | Mana regenerates 50% faster | 8:00 |
| Potion of Clarity, stronger | Potion of Clarity + **Glowstone Dust** | Clarity II | Mana regenerates 100% faster | 1:30 |
| **Potion of Mana** | Awkward Potion + **Lapis Lazuli** | Mana | Restores **60 mana** at once | instant |
| Potion of Mana, stronger | Potion of Mana + **Glowstone Dust** | Mana II | Restores **120 mana** at once | instant |

- **Splash potions:** add **Gunpowder** to any of them. **Lingering potions:** add **Dragon's Breath** to a splash
  potion. You can also start from an Awkward splash or lingering potion.
- Lingering potions make **Arrows of Clarity** and **Arrows of Mana** in the usual way.
- The Potion of Mana can't be made to last longer: it's instant.

See [Mana]({{ '/progression/mana/' | relative_url }}) for how these fit with your other ways to regain mana.

## Paper magic

### Spell Scroll

One of your spells, written on paper, that **anyone** can cast **once**, with or without a Cord, whether or not they
know its runes.

- **Made:** on the Cord screen, press **Inscribe a scroll** with the spell selected. It takes a sheet of **Paper**, an
  **Ink Sac** (or Glow Ink Sac) and **twice the spell's mana**.
- **Named** after its spell (*Scroll of Splitting Frost Bolt*); it shimmers, and its tooltip shows the spell's
  readout and who inscribed it.
- **Used:** right-click to cast it; the scroll is used up.
- Scrolls stack to 16.

Everything about scrolls: [Spell Scrolls]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#spell-scrolls).

### Torn Page

A page torn from an old grimoire. **Right-click to read it:** you learn the riddle of a
[secret spell]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}) you haven't found yet, and the riddle goes into your
Grimoire. In the Overworld, the page's margin also sketches the way to the nearest **Archive**: *an Archive lies about
650 blocks to the northwest*.

If there's no riddle left you haven't found or been given, the page says so and isn't used up. Pages stack to 16.

**Found:**

| Where | Chance or amount |
|---|---|
| Stronghold library chests | 45% |
| Ancient city chests | 25% |
| Woodland mansion chests | 25% |
| Trial chamber vaults | 15% |
| Dungeon (monster spawner) chests | 12% |
| Desert pyramid chests | 12% |
| Archive library and vault chests, and the halls and vaults of the other dungeons | often |
| The Archivist | 2 |
| The Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater, the Tide Scribe | 1 each |
| A [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}) | 6% (a Runebound Adept: 20%) |
| A Runesmith | Journeyman: 14 emeralds and a Book. Expert: 16 emeralds. |

### Wellstone

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Polished Deepslate | Block of Amethyst | Polished Deepslate |
| Block of Amethyst | Mana Crystal | Block of Amethyst |
| Deepslate Tiles | Deepslate Tiles | Deepslate Tiles |

A mana well. Set it on a **ley line** in the Overworld and it **wakes**: circles turn on the ground around it, and

|---|---|---|

### Greater staffs

A **Greater Fire Staff** and so on, one for each element. Can't be crafted: they drop from the Archivist (50%, any
element), the Ender Dragon (50%: Void, Arcane or Time), the Wither (35%: Void or Blood), the Warden (35%: Earth or
Storm) and Elder Guardians (35%: Frost or Life), and are found in Archive vaults. Effects of its element +35% power.

### Tome of the Fifth Page

Off-hand: a fifth spell to thread and cast while you hold it. Can't be crafted: found in Archive vaults and libraries
and in stronghold libraries.

## Trophies

Each of the three dimension dungeons' bosses always drops its trophy. A trophy is kept, not spent: **hold it up**
(right-click) for a boon, then it rests for **5 minutes** before it works again. Trophies don't stack, and fire and
lava can't destroy them.

| Trophy | From | Hold it up for |
|---|---|---|
| **Cinder Heart** | The Cinder Warden, in the [Ember Sanctum]({{ '/world/ember-sanctum/' | relative_url }}) | Fire Resistance for 3 minutes |
| **Astral Lens** | The Star-Eater, in the [Astral Observatory]({{ '/world/astral-observatory/' | relative_url }}) | Night Vision for 3 minutes and Slow Falling for 1 minute |
| **Drowned Quill** | The Tide Scribe, in the [Drowned Scriptorium]({{ '/world/drowned-scriptorium/' | relative_url }}) | Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace for 3 minutes |

## Blocks found only in the world

These blocks are part of Wildercord's dungeons and events. They can't be broken or collected in survival. The first
three appear in the creative menu for builders.

| Block | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Rune Seal** | The Archive's doors | Each is carved with an element's glyph. Strike it with a spell of that element and every block of that element in the door lights up. Light every element in the door within ten seconds and the door dissolves. See [The Archive]({{ '/world/archive/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Archive Lectern** | The heart of the Archive | The Archivist rises from it the first time a player comes near, and it goes quiet after. |
| **Dungeon Altar** | The heart of each dimension dungeon's arena | The dungeon's boss rises from it the first time a player comes near: the Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater or the Tide Scribe. It goes quiet once the boss falls. |
| **Fallen Star** | Where a star comes down in a [starfall]({{ '/world/world-events/' | relative_url }}) | A glowing lump of starstone that can't be mined. Once its guards are beaten, use it to break it open for a rune and a Mana Crystal. It crumbles once looted, or fades after twenty minutes. It has no item at all. |
