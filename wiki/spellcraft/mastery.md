# Spell Mastery

## What it is

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/mastery-panel.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The mastery panel over the Cord screen: an Arcane Burst at Practised, its circle with a personal sigil at the centre, a bar at 100 of 350, four trait slots for ranks II to V with the first waiting, and three trait cards to choose from: Umbral Mark, Thrifty Weave and Soul Sip, with a Re-roll button" class="shot">

The spells you use grow with you. Each one climbs five ranks, takes a **trait** you choose at each rank after the
first, and carries your own **sigil** at the heart of its circle.

| Rank | Roughly | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| **Kindled** | Your first cast | Your sigil in its circle |
| **Practised** | Half an hour of real use | First trait, a fine ring round the circle |
| **Adept** | An hour or two | Second trait, deeper colour, its name spoken when cast, can be inscribed for a friend |
| **Master** | Three to four hours | Third trait, a second ticked ring |
| **Mythic** | Many hours | Fourth trait, a slow shimmer round the circle |

## How to get it

A spell is its **exact runes in order**. `Bolt · Fire` and `Fire · Bolt` are different spells. Change a spell and
you start a new record, but the old one is kept: change it back and its rank and traits return.

**Casting alone teaches nothing.** A spell grows from what it does:

- **Hitting foes.** More for a big share of their health, more for a kill. Runebound count half again, bosses
  double, players half.
- **Helping allies** who are hurt or fighting. Helping yourself counts only when you're in danger.
- **Moving or working the world** grows slowly, a little per useful cast.

What's around you matters:

- **Danger counts more**: low health, crowds, a boss, a dungeon. Up to three times as much in all.
- **Variety counts more**: a kind of foe the spell hasn't hit lately is worth a quarter more.
- **Farming counts less**: the same foe in the same place soon earns almost nothing. Bosses don't wear out.
- **Practice is capped**: dummies and the [practice arena]({{ '/progression/practice/' | relative_url }}) teach at
  half rate, and only partway to Practised.

Scrolls, imbued items, glyphs and passives don't grow mastery, and nothing grows in creative.

## How to use it

### Choosing traits

At Practised, Adept, Master and Mythic you're offered **three traits** and pick one. The choice opens next time
you open your Cord (`K`). The offer depends on the spell's runes and **how you've used it**: a fire spell cast
often in the rain may be offered **Undying Flame**. The same spell always gets the same offer.

Each rank allows **one change of mind for 3 XP levels**: **Re-roll** the offer, or **Unbind** a chosen trait and
pick again.

Traits are modest. Together they add at most **20%** damage (half that against players) and take at most **15%**
off price and cooldown.

"Cast often" means about one cast in seven in that situation. Soaked, Bleeding and Shadowed set up
[reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}).

### On the Cord screen

Each spell row has a **rank badge** and a progress bar. A gold dot means a trait is waiting. Click the badge or
press `Ctrl`+`M` for the mastery panel. The readout's price and cooldown already include your traits.

### Spoken names

[Rename]({{ '/spellcraft/cord-screen/' | relative_url }}#the-spell-tools) a spell and, once it's **Adept**,
everyone within 32 blocks sees its name when you cast it. Turn **Spell titles** off in the Magic visual settings
if you'd rather not see them.

### Sharing a mastered spell

Inscribe an **Adept** or better spell on a [scroll]({{ '/spellcraft/cord-screen/' | relative_url }}#the-spell-tools)
and it carries your traits and sigil.

- **Use it** to cast once, with those traits.
- **Sneak and use it** to study it. If you know its runes and your Cord can hold them, it's threaded onto an empty
  row at **Kindled**, with the traits **borrowed** and working at once. As it ranks up you can keep each borrowed
  trait or choose your own.
- A scroll never passes on experience, can't teach a spell you already have, and can't pass on borrowed traits.

## Tips and counterplay

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/mastery-circles.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The same spell's circle at Kindled, Adept and Mythic, gaining colour, rings and a shimmer" class="shot">

- **Read a rival's circle.** Everyone sees your sigil and rank rings, but not your traits.
- **Move around.** Fresh foes and fresh places grow a spell fastest.
- **Mastery stays.** It's kept through death, travels with [loadouts]({{ '/spellcraft/loadouts/' | relative_url }}),
  and survives tying the spell into a [Knot]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}). You remember your 48
  most recent spells, and any spell on your Cord.
- A server can turn mastery, traits, spoken names or inscribing off, or change how fast spells grow.
