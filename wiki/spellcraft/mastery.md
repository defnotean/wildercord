# Spell Mastery
{: .no_toc }

The spells you thread are yours, and the ones you use grow with you. Every spell keeps a record of how you have
used it: it rises through five ranks as you cast it where it matters, takes a **trait** of your choosing at each
rank, wears your own **sigil** at the heart of its circle, and its circle grows richer as it climbs. Name it, and
once it's Adept everyone nearby hears its name when you cast it.

## One spell, its exact runes

A spell is its exact runes, in order, as they fire. `Bolt · Fire` and `Fire · Bolt` are two different spells, and
so are `Burst · Harm` and `Burst · Harm · Widen`. Change a spell on the Cord screen and you're growing a new one,
from the start; but the old one isn't forgotten. Change it back and its rank, its traits and its sigil are all
still there.

- A [Knot]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}) is the spell tied inside it: tying a spell into a Knot keeps its mastery.
- [Quiet runes]({{ '/spellcraft/cords/' | relative_url }}#quiet-runes-and-a-smaller-cord) don't fire, so on a smaller Cord
  the spell that fires (and grows) is the one without them. Put the bigger Cord back on and the whole spell's record
  comes back with it.
- You remember the 48 spells you used most recently. A spell threaded on your Cord is never forgotten.
- Mastery is kept through death, and [loadouts]({{ '/spellcraft/loadouts/' | relative_url }}) carry it with them: load a
  loadout and each of its spells brings its own record.

## The five ranks

| Rank | About | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| **Kindled** | Your first cast | Your sigil at the centre of its circle |
| **Practised** | Half an hour or so of real use | Its first trait, and a fine ring around its circle |
| **Adept** | An hour or two | Its second trait; a deeper colour and a brighter sigil; its name spoken when you cast it; it can be inscribed for a friend |
| **Master** | About three to four hours | Its third trait, and a second ring with fine ticks |
| **Mythic** | Many hours of a spell you live by | Its fourth trait, and a slow shimmer running around its circle |

The times are for a spell you use as your main attack in ordinary play: fighting between exploring, mining and
building. A spell you only use now and then takes longer.

## How a spell grows

Casting alone teaches nothing. A spell grows from what it does:

- **Hitting real foes.** Every foe a cast strikes is worth something, more for the share of its health the hit
  takes, and more again for the blow that slays it. A [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}) is worth half again as
  much, a boss twice as much. Another player counts (with PvP on), but for half.
- **Helping allies who need it.** Healing someone who is hurt, by what it truly restores, and warding or
  quickening an ally who is hurt or in a fight. Healing or warding yourself counts only when you're in danger.
- **Moving you or working the world.** A spell that only blinks you about, mines or builds grows slowly, a little
  for each cast that does its job (twice that in danger).

What surrounds the cast counts too:

- **Danger is worth more.** Low on health, a crowd of foes around you, a boss in the fight, inside a dungeon: each
  makes a cast worth more, together up to three times as much.
- **Variety is worth more.** A kind of foe the spell hasn't struck lately is worth a quarter more.
- **The same thing in the same place is worth less and less.** Casting at the same kind of foe in the same spot
  over and over (a mob farm, say) soon earns almost nothing. Move on, or come back later: places are forgotten over
  time. Boss fights don't wear out like this.
- **Practice only teaches so much.** [Training dummies]({{ '/progression/training-dummy/' | relative_url }}) and the
  [practice arena]({{ '/progression/practice/' | relative_url }}) teach a spell at half the rate, and only up to a little over
  half the way to Practised. After that, it needs the real thing.
- **One cast can only earn so much**, however many foes it hits.

A spell read from a scroll, a spell let go from an imbued item or a glyph, and your passives don't grow anything, and
nothing grows in creative mode.

## Traits

At **Practised, Adept, Master and Mythic** your spell is offered **three traits**, and you choose one. A toast tells
you when a rank is reached; the next time you open your Cord (`K`), the choice opens by itself. It waits for as
long as you like: the rank is yours either way, but the trait only works once chosen.

### What you're offered

The offer depends on the spell:

- **What it's made of.** Its elements, its shapes, whether it harms or only helps. A fire spell may be offered
  Kindling; a healing spell, Mending Glow; a blink, Featherstep.
- **How you've used it.** Your spell counts where and how you cast it: in the rain, at night, underground, deep
  below, in water, in the Nether or the End, in the cold or the heat, at low health, beside allies, against the
  undead, spiders, bosses or crowds, in dungeons, in the air. Some traits are only offered to a spell used often in
  the right circumstance, and the more it was, the likelier. A fire spell cast a lot in the rain may be offered
  **Undying Flame** (its fire burns at full strength on the wet); one cast at night, **Nightshade**. The mastery panel
  shows what your spell has been used in most.

The same spell always gets the same offer for a rank, so there's no fishing for a better one by reopening the screen.

### Changing your mind

Each rank allows **one change of mind**, for **3 experience levels**:

- **Re-roll** the offer before you choose: three other traits instead.
- **Unbind** a trait you chose (the pencil beside it on the panel): it lets go, and you choose again from a fresh offer
  that leaves it out.

Once a rank has used its change, its trait is settled.

### What traits do

Traits are modest. None makes a spell another spell: a little cheaper, a little quicker, a small extra touch, a
look or a sound. However they add up:

- a spell's traits together add at most **a fifth** to a hit's damage, and against another player only half of what they
  would, within the limits that keep spells from killing players in one blow (see
  [Defending Against Magic]({{ '/progression/defence/' | relative_url }}));
- they take at most **15%** off its price and its cooldown;
- leaping hits leap once and never leap again from where they land.

## Your sigil

Every spell you cast carries a **sigil**: a small mark drawn from the spell's runes and from you alone. Nobody else's
version of the same spell has the same sigil, and yours never changes. It sits at the heart of the spell's
[magic circle]({{ '/spellcraft/magic-circles/' | relative_url }}), in place of the first rune's seal, wherever the circle
shows: behind your shoulders as you cast, in front of your hands as you charge, beside the Cord screen, and on the
scrolls you inscribe.

## How the circle grows

- **Practised:** a fine ring just outside the frame.
- **Adept:** a deeper, richer colour, and your sigil brighter, with a glow behind it.
- **Master:** a second ring outside the first, with fine ticks between them.
- **Mythic:** a slow shimmer of light running round the outer rings.

Everyone around you sees your circle as it is: they see your sigil and its rank, though not your traits or how far it
has grown. A few traits change how it looks or sounds too (Bellsong, Starlit Circle, Deep Hue, Ember Script,
Frostwork, Petalfall), for everyone.

## Spoken names

Give a spell a name (the **Rename** tool on the [Cord screen]({{ '/spellcraft/cord-screen/' | relative_url }}#rename)) and,
once it's **Adept**, everyone within about 32 blocks sees its name when you cast it: a brief title over your head that
drifts up and fades, or, when you're out of their view, a short line low on their screen. A spell with only its
automatic name stays quiet. Don't want to see them? Switch **Spell titles** off in the magic visual settings (the
Magic visual settings key, which you can bind in Controls). That only changes what you see.

## Inscribing a mastered spell

A spell of **Adept** rank or higher, [inscribed onto a scroll]({{ '/spellcraft/cord-screen/' | relative_url }}#inscribe-a-scroll)
(the same paper, ink and mana as any scroll), carries more than its runes: the **traits you chose** for it and your
**sigil**. Its tooltip shows the sigil, the rank and the traits.

- **Read it** (use it) and it casts once, as any scroll does, with those traits and your sigil on its circle. A
  scroll's cast teaches nobody anything.
- **Study it** (use it while sneaking) and, if you know all its runes and your Cord can hold them, the spell is
  threaded into an empty row of your Cord with its name, and you start your own record of it at **Kindled** with its
  traits **borrowed**. They work straight away. As your own spell reaches each rank, you can keep the borrowed trait
  for that rank or choose another; the panel marks borrowed traits in lavender.

What a scroll can't do:

- carry any experience: the reader starts at Kindled, and the inscriber loses nothing;
- teach a spell you already have your own way with (or one you've studied before): you'd stack traits on it;
- pass borrowed traits on: only the traits you earned are inscribed, and only from an Adept spell.

## On the Cord screen

- Each spell row has a **rank badge** at its right end, its rank's numeral in its colour, and a fine **bar** along the
  foot of the row toward the next rank. A gold dot glints on the badge while a trait is waiting.
- **Click the badge** (or press `Ctrl`+`M`) to open the **mastery panel**: the spell's circle with your sigil, its
  rank and how far it is to the next, its four trait slots (Practised to Mythic), the offer when a trait is waiting,
  re-roll and unbind, and what the spell has been used in most. Hover anything for what it means.
- The readout shows the spell's rank and progress and its traits, and its price and cooldown already include them.

See [The Cord Screen]({{ '/spellcraft/cord-screen/' | relative_url }}).

## On a server

Server owners can switch spell mastery off (records already earned are kept), switch traits off without losing
them, stop spoken names or inscribing, and make spells grow faster or slower. The Cord screen only shows ranks while
mastery is on.
