# Harmonies, quirks and reading runes
{: .no_toc }

Every world's magic is a little different. Somewhere in yours are spells that only your world answers, runes that
behave a little differently under its sky, and runes you'll have to learn to read before you understand them. None of
it is written down anywhere outside your world: not in the game's files, not on this wiki, not in a friend's world.
You find it by casting.

## Harmonies

A **harmony** is an exact sequence of three or four runes that your world, and only your world, answers. Cast it and
the spell you threaded goes off as usual, but the world adds a **twist** of its own: its rain lands as falling glass,
its fire mends your friends as it burns your foes, it bursts into birds of light, a pale horse rises under you, the
whole spell sounds a second time from where it landed... There are many twists, and each world holds about a dozen
harmonies, drawn when the world is made. Two worlds almost never share one.

### The rules

- **Exact order, nothing else.** As with a [secret spell]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}), the
  runes must be threaded in exactly the right order, with nothing before, between or after them.
- **Every harmony is an ordinary spell too.** Its runes always make a sensible spell, so a guess that's nearly right
  still casts something, and never tells you how close you were.
- **Runes you can get.** A harmony is made of runes up to Tier III that can be crafted, so an Amethyst Cord can hold
  any of them, and none costs more than an Amethyst Cord's caster can pay.
- **Never a secret spell, never a fusion.** No harmony is one of the ten secret spells, and none holds both runes of
  a [signature fusion]({{ '/fusion-altar/combining/' | relative_url }}#signature-fusions).
- **Nothing gives it away.** Until you've found it, the Cord screen, your spell panel and the spell wheel show it as the
  ordinary spell, and the cast that finds it costs just that.

### Finding one

The first time you cast a harmony:

- its **name** fills your screen (something like *"The Glasswind Rite"*), with *"A harmony of this world"* beneath it;
  a toast says *"A harmony answers"*, and light bursts out of the ground around you;
- it's written into your [Grimoire]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}) with its runes, its riddle and what
  its twist does, and it condenses **400 mana** toward your next Heart Circle;
- if nobody in your world had found it before, **everyone on the server is told**: *"Alex found a harmony of this
  world: the Glasswind Rite"*. Only its name is shared, never its runes: those are yours to keep, or to tell.

From then on, whenever you thread it, the Cord screen names it in its colour and says what its twist does.

### How they behave

- **A twist is a little extra, never a second spell.** It adds a few points of damage, some mending, a moment of
  protection or a set piece around you, scaled by your power like any spell. Its damage meets Shields, armour and the
  [spell defences]({{ '/progression/defence/' | relative_url }}) like any other, it never hurts you or your friends, and
  it never moves a boss.
- **They cost a little more once found:** 15% more mana than the ordinary spell, and a cooldown a fifth longer. The
  Cord screen, your spell panel and the spell wheel all show it.
- **Only from your Cord.** A harmony answers a spell cast from your Cord, not a scroll, a passive or an imbued item.

### Riddles

**Torn Pages** can carry a riddle of your own world's magic as well as the secret spells'. Reading one tells you a
harmony's **name** and its **riddle**, and puts both in your Grimoire until you solve it. As with the secret spells,
a riddle speaks of every rune in order, in a few words of verse: shapes by what they do, effects by name, modifiers by
what they do to the rune before them, and the twist by a line of its own.

### This world's harmonies

The Grimoire's **This world's harmonies** section lists every harmony you've found (with who found it first), the
riddles you've read, and the ones other players have found, by name and finder. The rest are counted: *"8 more that no
one has found"*. When a server keeps finds quiet, you see only your own.

## Quirks

Your world also bends a few runes its own way. A **quirk** is a small tweak to one rune, true in your world only, and
only in its own conditions: *"Here, Shock cracks harder in the rain"*, or *"Here, Veil lasts longer at night"*. A quirk
can make a rune a little stronger, make what it does last longer, or have it strike again, faintly, a moment later.
What counts is where the spell lands: under the open sky or deep underground, by day or by night, under a full or a
new moon, in the rain or the snow, high on a mountain, in the Nether or the End.

Nobody is told of the quirks. The first time one matters to one of your spells you'll see a soft glint where it
landed, hear a note, and read a line in chat (*"This world has a nature of its own..."*); a toast says *"This world's
nature"*, and the quirk goes into your Grimoire's **This world's quirks** section. Each quirk condenses 100 mana the
first time.

## Reading runes

A rune you've just learned isn't understood at once. Your Codex shows what it is (its name, family, tier, element and
cost) but not yet what it does in full:

| Stage | What the Codex shows | How it moves on |
|---|---|---|
| **Unread** | A short hint of what the rune is like, and a small **?** in its corner | Cast a spell that holds it |
| **Glimpsed** | Its full text, but every number is veiled, shimmering and unreadable, and a small dot in its corner | See it at work a few times: three casts that land on something, or five that don't |
| **Understood** | Its full text, numbers and all | |

You're told as each happens (*"You glimpse what Frost does"*, then *"You understand Frost now"*), and the Grimoire's
**Runes you're still reading** line lists the ones you're part way through. A rune item's tooltip goes by the same
rule, so a rune you haven't learned shows its hint too.

- **Nothing you already knew.** Every rune you knew before reading came to Wildercord, and the three you learn with your
  first Cord, are understood. Nobody has to learn their old runes again.
- **Fusions and weaves.** A rune made at the Fusion Altar is read like any other. A woven rune reads as far as the
  least read of the runes in it, and a Knot always shows the runes inside it.
- **Your innate rune** wakes unread too: it tells you what it is by being used.
- **Creative players** see every rune in full.
- **The full reference is here.** This wiki's [rune pages]({{ '/runes/' | relative_url }}) give every rune's numbers,
  for whenever you'd rather know.

## For server owners

A server can turn harmonies and quirks off, change how many harmonies a world holds, draw the world a fresh set (the
old ones, and who found them, are forgotten), keep finds out of chat, and switch reading runes off so every rune is
understood the moment it's learned. See the server settings in the [FAQ]({{ '/faq/' | relative_url }}#what-can-a-server-change).
