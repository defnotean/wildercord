# The living field journal

> **New in 0.10.0-alpha.** Actual-outcome gameplay and visual-detail routing have focused native acceptance; maintained and timed visual sequences remain under review. The plate below is an illustration, with native game captures to be added separately after review.

*A closed wound, a spent seed coat, a thorn that answers a blow. Life leaves a different mark for each kind of work.*

Watch the person, tool or plant the spell actually changes. Closing tissue belongs beside a mended wound. A cut straw bundle belongs over a harvested crop. The short material response helps you read the result while leaving the surrounding fight visible.

## Use the in-game journal

Open your Cord and select **Grimoire**. **Reading Life magic** shows field notes for Life runes you have actually learned. Hover a rune for its material clues and use; click its name to find it in the Codex. Unknown and undiscovered fused names stay hidden. **Magic visuals** opens your local settings; Done or Escape returns to the same journal position.

## Read the result

**A heal answers the health that actually returns.** A bright preparation or a spell reaching its target is not a receipt for all the healing written in the spell editor. The wound response follows the real change after the target's maximum health and other rules apply. Look at health, absorption or durability for the exact amount; the number of decorative pieces is not a numerical damage or healing meter.

**A cleansing response answers something that really left.** Spent husks represent removed harmful conditions, cleared fire or freezing, and cleared elemental marks. Casting Cleanse on a clean target does not manufacture a removal. Removing an ailment and healing a wound are different outcomes.

**Repair stitches follow the equipment slot.** Restore checks the held and worn slots. When an item really loses damage, its stitches appear beside that hand, head, chest, legs or feet. A slot inside its repair rest period produces no new repair stitches. Restore's body healing and fire clearing can still succeed separately.

**Growth belongs to a changed cell.** A young crop taking another stage, a new lichen block, or a vine gaining berries can answer at that cell. A mature crop, unsuitable plant, blocked placement or protected cell may produce no growth response. A successful cast does not promise that every cell in its area changed.

**A ward opening promises a limited opportunity.** It does not claim a future save or retaliation already happened. Read its admission, actual trigger and end as separate moments. A trigger response follows the real strike, save or projectile glance; expiration does not restore spent resources. Cancellation, death, removal or invalidation can end an owner quietly, without pretending it healed or saved someone.

These brief material outcomes follow your own-spell and other-spell visual settings using the actual caster as their source. Minimal keeps a smaller authored response with its supporting materials; crowded, distant or camera-close outcomes can still be omitted. The setting changes decoration, not the spell's result.

The material is a clue, not a guaranteed HUD: distant, crowded or camera-close decoration can be omitted. Check the real health bar, status, equipment, terrain and any refusal message before assuming an outcome.

## Mending: tissue, sap and spent husks

| Rune | Its own response | What it confirms and how to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Heal** | Two opposed tissue lips close toward a warm sap bead. | The recipient gained real health or additional absorption. At base power it requests eight health; repeats on the same target in one cast scale to 60%, then 40%. Unused healing can become a capped absorption shield. A full-health target already at its shield cap gives no new successful change. |
| **Regrowth** | Separate tissue bands open beside a small living stem. | A regeneration stage was actually admitted. Ordinary casting takes hold in three stages; these bands announce the accepted status, not every later vanilla regeneration tick. Overlapping regeneration can refuse a weaker stage. Give it time to work rather than treating every band as an instant heal. |
| **Cleanse** | Dry seed husks peel away beside a pale tissue seam. | Real harmful conditions, fire, freezing or elemental marks were removed. A clean target has no spent conditions to shed. Use it to interrupt a reaction setup as well as remove visible potion ailments. |
| **Nourish** | Two seed halves cup a food-colored sap drop. | A player's food/condition change or an eligible animal's healing or breeding admission actually occurred. Ordinary unsupported creatures are not fed by this spell. Food, animal healing and readiness to breed have their own real eligibility rules. |
| **Restore** | Short crossed repair stitches sit beside the actual repaired slot. | Body healing/fire clearing and item repairs are observed separately. At base power the repair requests 8% of maximum item durability, limited by the item's existing damage. Each recipient's slot has a one-minute repair rest; changing the item in that slot does not evade that rest. |
| **Remedy** | Removed husks give way to sap beads; a tissue patch follows actual healing. | Harmful conditions are converted into useful boons when admitted, with healing observed separately. Zombie-villager Weakness preparation is a different branch: its preparation effect does not mean the villager has already been cured. Bring the normal cure requirements. |

### A small field trial

Save **Self + Heal**, **Self + Cleanse** and **Self + Restore** separately. Heal while wounded, then compare with a full-health cast. Cleanse a real ailment, then try the clean target. For Restore, hold a damaged tool and watch the hand rather than the center of the screen; a second immediate cast should not repair that same slot again.

Shape, modifier and gear costs still contribute to the full price. An unsuitable paid target can produce no outcome without refunding the spell's mana.

## Gardens: changed cells leave their own signs

| Rune | Its own response | What it confirms and how to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Grow** | A branching vine spine rises from the changed cell and sheds a seed coat. | An eligible plant actually changed, or a young animal actually advanced its age. Aim at something with room to grow. Real cell feedback follows the plant writes; it does not promise an entire bonemeal area has succeeded. |
| **Harvest** | A cut straw bundle drops ripe seed pieces at the crop. | A mature eligible crop was actually harvested. Check its real drops and replanted crop. Immature and protected crops cannot be treated as a successful harvest merely because the spell passed over them. |
| **Glimmer** | Small pale lichen plates and a light spore sit at the edited block. | Legal glow lichen was placed. The result is a real block that can keep lighting the area after its brief decoration settles. Suitable support and room for the block matter. |
| **Ancient Seed** | A layered seed coat opens around a rising branched stem. | A flower placement or cell changed during one of the finite growth sweeps. Find young crops before using it. Existing mature crops and denied cells supply no invented growth progress. |
| **Glowvine** | A drooping vine carries a single berry weight. | A vine segment/head was placed or an existing berry state really changed. It needs suitable ceiling space. A missing ceiling keeps the failure branch rather than painting a successful vine over the air. |
| **Lifebloom** | Uneven living petals cup a sap drop, then answer actual heal pulses and burst recipients. | Immediate healing, a finite repeating bloom, or a real terminal ally heal was admitted. Refresh updates the existing bloom instead of starting another independent timer. Full-health pulses do not claim unused healing; the final burst is checked per actual ally. |
| **Bloom** | Three staggered opening petals; helped pollen recipients can receive a separate grain. | A direct ally regeneration, bounded pollen hop, or real plant change succeeded in its own branch. The receiving ally or changed cell gets the response. Enemies and unsuitable plants do not become successful recipients because they were nearby. |

*Garden note:* the changed crop itself is your lasting record. Use the short leaves and seed coats to find which cells answered, then inspect their growth stages. Respect the land's protections and leave room for the plant you want to create.

## Shelter: openings, triggers and the way out

| Rune | Its own response | What it confirms and how to answer it |
|---|---|---|
| **Bramble** | Unequal twig teeth open on the protected recipient; an actual retaliation has a separate thorn response at the attacker. | A thorn ward was admitted or a real hostile attack spent its retaliation. It has finite charges and refill timing. Strike from outside its close retaliation range, wait it out, or avoid feeding it ordinary blows. Thorns do not answer thorns in an endless exchange. |
| **Haven** | Sparse leaf shutters and a vine piece mark the living shelter and actual boundary responses. | The field exists; actual projectile glances and pushed foes have their own responses. This is no longer presented as a filled light dome. Hostile incoming projectiles can glance off; allied, outgoing and inside-fired shots are not a universal promise of reflection. It is temporary shelter, not invulnerability. |
| **Reversal** | A folded tissue-and-sap pocket opens when its actual death prevention triggers. | A valid save mark was admitted, or the real save fired. Deaths Door rest can refuse a new mark. Prepare it before danger; its opening does not mean a save already happened, and bypassing or ineligible damage is not promised to be caught. |
| **Soulbond** | Two endpoint knots of living fibre turn toward the real partner. | An eligible pair bonded, or a real transferred wound changed a recipient. The partner must be valid; a lone, distant or refused target cannot acquire a decorative substitute bond. Watch distance and the other partner's health. Shared wounds remain real danger, and a broken/expired bond is not a heal. |
| **Second Wind** | Two seed shell halves close around a small Time piece; a real save cracks the shell open. | A valid preparation or actual lethal save succeeded. At base power the save leaves four health, then gives the existing recovery/escape effects. Death cheated by this or another save rests for a minute. A refreshed mark does not give immunity or erase that lockout. |
| **Root Bulwark** | Root splinters climb each placed cell, with leaves and stone grit. | A real living wall cell was placed; successful restoration can shed a separate local response. Allies nearby receive the wall's existing brief regeneration. It is temporary and drops no free wood. Protection, space and existing terrain determine which cells can be placed. A foreign replacement is preserved rather than removed on expiry. |
| **Stitchtime** | Interrupted tissue stitches sit beside a small Time tooth. | A stitch ward, an actual recorded wound, or its real capped closing heal occurred. At base power it heals four initially, records four seconds of incoming wounds, and can return up to twelve at closure. A counted wound is not a claim of immediate healing. Refresh extends the existing bounded owner; it does not create a second closure timer. |

### Wards are different promises

Bramble answers an attacker. Haven changes the local approach. Soulbond shares a wound. Reversal and Second Wind prepare a limited rescue. Stitchtime closes a short account later. Their material responses distinguish those decisions; a leaf or seed opening should never be read as a permanent immunity icon.

Use ordinary defense, positioning and retreat alongside them. Enemies can wait out short fields, approach outside retaliation rules, break a link's eligibility, or exploit the time between admission and a real trigger. Successful saves still leave vulnerable health and their existing rest periods.

## Two cooperative signatures keep their own voices

### Ashen Mercy — scorched leaves become a living seam

Remove an ally's actual harmful conditions or fire to feed the existing capped conversion. Scorched leaf flakes show the removals; a tissue seam follows positive healing, with a small ember retaining its Fire ingredient. A clean target supplies no invented removal or converted healing.

Its original Ashen Mercy casting identity and **single authored landing voice remain**. The recipient's material response does not layer a second Life landing stinger over it.

**Field use:** keep it ready for an ally whose burning or harmful conditions give the conversion something real to remove. Compare the conditions and health afterward. See [Field Signatures]({{ '/fusion-altar/field-signatures/' | relative_url }}) for the exact Fusion Altar pairing and limits.

### Pulse Ferry — a parcel needs two wounded recipients

The parcel visits **two different** wounded eligible allies, on separate beats, within its four-block field. At most three health is offered to either recipient and six across the payment. Crouching refuses a visit; full health gives no unused recovery. Later eligibility, sight and living-owner checks still apply, so an intended second visit can fail.

The successful recipient receives a split leaf envelope, a sap tongue, positive-healing tissue and small Time teeth. The original travelling parcel, beat timing and **single authored landing voice remain**. A refused or prevented heal does not fabricate a positive-healing material response.

**Field use:** gather two wounded willing allies before casting, leave their routes clear and keep within the field. The two beats give both recipients a readable decision. See [Expedition Signatures]({{ '/fusion-altar/expedition-signatures/' | relative_url }}) for the exact altar recipe and consent rules.

## Carry this journal into play

Start with one useful job: heal a wounded friend, light a dark campsite with lichen, harvest a mature row, or prepare a finite ward before an expedition. Add delivery shapes only after you can recognize that job's response. Fused effects combine ingredients with their own purpose; a Time tooth beside living tissue belongs to its timing mechanic, and stone grit beside roots belongs to a real wall.

Use the [Life effect catalogue]({{ '/runes/effects/life/' | relative_url }}) for crafting and base costs, and the fused/world/innate pages for their acquisition rules. The full price and tier requirement belong to the spell editor and your casting setup. This chapter explains how to read a result, not a promise that every shape, target or repeated cast can perform the same work.
