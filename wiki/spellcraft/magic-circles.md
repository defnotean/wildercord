# Magic Circles

## What it is

Every spell draws its own magic circle, built from its runes. Learn to read it and you know what a spell does
before it lands: yours, another player's, or the one a Runebound is about to throw at you.

You'll see the same circle under a caster's feet, behind their shoulders while they charge, in a monster's hand,
on the floor of a Domain, in a Shield, on a glyph, and beside the Cord screen and Grimoire. A longer spell never
makes a bigger circle, just more points on its star.

## How to get it

Every spell has one. To learn the runes' looks, watch the circle beside the
[Cord screen]({{ '/spellcraft/cord-screen/' | relative_url }}): it redraws each time you change your spell.

## How to use it

### The parts, from outside in

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/spell-circle.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A charging magic circle: a heavy frame with rays, a band of script, a patterned band, a seven-pointed star with a coloured roundel on each point, inner rings and a seal in the middle" class="shot">

1. **Frame:** a heavy ring with a **ray** on each star point.
2. **Script band:** the spell's rune emblems, in order, written round and round.
3. **Pattern band:** the ring pattern of the spell's **first effect**, showing its element from far off.
4. **Star:** one point per rune. One to three runes get a six-pointed star, four runes an eight-pointed one.
5. **Roundels:** a small circle for each rune, in casting order clockwise from the top.
6. **Seal:** the emblem of the spell's first rune, usually its shape.

The circle takes the colour of the spell's first effect, or teal if it has none.

### Family and element

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/a-rune-rings.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Eight runes' rings with their emblems: one of each family on top, a Frost ring and three fused runes' braided rings below" class="shot">

| Family | Colour | Ring line | Emblem frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shape | Teal | Double line | Square |
| Effect | Its element's colour | Solid line | Circle |
| Modifier | Gold | Dashed line | Diamond |
| Link | Violet | Chain of ovals | Octagon |

| Element | Colour | Ring motifs | Emblem glyph |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | Orange | Flame teeth, embers | A flame peak |
| Frost | Pale blue | Crystals, snowflakes | A snowflake |
| Storm | Yellow | Lightning, forks | A lightning bolt |
| Wind | Pale green | Waves, swirls | Two wavy lines |
| Earth | Brown | Blocks, pillars | A downward wedge |
| Life | Green | Buds, leaves | A flower |
| Void | Violet | Crescents, eyes | A crescent |
| Arcane | Pink | Stars, crosses | A four-pointed star |
| Time | Pale gold | Hourglasses, dials | An hourglass |
| Blood | Crimson | Drops, fangs | A drop |

- **Fused runes** have a **braided ring** in both elements' colours and an emblem **split down the middle**. See
  [Fused Runes]({{ '/runes/fused/' | relative_url }}).
- **Knots** show a plain link ring, hiding what's inside. See [Knots]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}).
- **Secret spells** have their own centrepiece instead of a star. See
  [Secret Spells]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}).
- **Circle Disciplines** add their own moving design, such as petals or an iris. See
  [Circle Disciplines]({{ '/spellcraft/circle-disciplines/' | relative_url }}).

### Read one fast

1. **Colour** is the element.
2. **The seal** is the shape: where it's going.
3. **Count the roundels**: a long spell usually does more than one thing.
4. **Violet chain rings** are links: something more happens later.

The circle at the top of this page has seven points, a teal Nova seal and orange fire bands. Its roundels read
`Nova · Flashfire · Widen · Shock · Amplify · On Hit · Burst`. The last Burst has no effect after it, so it does
nothing: circles show weak spots too.

## Tips and counterplay

**Telegraphs.** A [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}) about to cast holds its circle in its
right hand for about a second, its nameplate turns bold between **» «** marks, and its rune marks flare. Bosses
and the Archivist do the same for about a second and a half, and their boss bar names the spell. For Zone, Rain,
Mine or Domain, the ground where it lands is marked.

When you see one:

- **Step out** of the marked ground.
- **Shoot it down.** Your bolt meeting theirs in the air bursts both. See
  [Playing Together]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}).
- **Parry** with a Shield at the last moment. See [Shields and Parrying]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}).
- **Read it.** A frost circle means keep fire ready for a Shatter. A violet chain means more is coming.
