# Casting gear

**Casting gear** works alongside your Cord. A **staff** favours one element, a **greater staff** is a stronger staff from a boss, the **Tome of the Fifth Page** opens a fifth spell, and a **focus** bends every spell you cast. You wear each piece in its own inventory slot, so you don't have to hold it.

## The gear slots

Your inventory (`E`) has a small tray on top of the window, above your armour. In Creative it's in the Survival Inventory tab, beside the Cord slot.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-gear-slots-inventory.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The survival inventory with a tray on top of the window holding a staff, a focus and the tome, and the player in the paper doll wearing them" class="shot">

| Slot | Takes |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | Any staff or greater staff |
| **Focus** | Any focus |
| **Tome** | The Tome of the Fifth Page, or the [Breath Sash]({{ '/progression/aura/' | relative_url }}#aura-forged-gear) |
| **Backpack** | A [backpack]({{ '/items/backpacks/' | relative_url }}) |

Click a piece in, or **shift-click** it. What counts is what's in your slots when you cast.

**Held gear still works while its slot is empty.** Staffs work in either hand. The tome and foci work in your off-hand. A piece in a slot replaces held pieces of the same kind: with a Fire Staff in the slot, a Frost Staff in your hand does nothing.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-gear-slots-worn.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A player seen from the front and the back wearing a staff across the back, a focus hovering off the left shoulder and the tome hanging from a belt at the hip" class="shot">
<span class="caption">Worn gear shows on you: a staff across your back, a focus by your shoulder, the tome at your hip.</span>

Gear in your slots **drops when you die**, like the rest of your inventory, unless the server keeps inventory.

## Staffs

There's a staff for each of the ten elements: **Fire, Frost, Storm, Wind, Earth, Life, Void, Arcane, Time** and **Blood**. Each one:

- gives effects of its element **+20% power**,
- makes any spell with an effect of its element cost **10% less mana**,
- leaves a **flourish** of its element when you release a charged spell. It's just for show.

## Greater staffs

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-gear-greater-staff.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A player holding a Greater Void Staff with a dark violet head, sparks and smoke around it" class="shot">

A greater staff works like a staff, but its element's effects get **+35% power**. It also gives the same 10% discount and a bigger flourish. You can't craft one:

| Where | Chance | Elements |
|---|---|---|
| The Archivist ([the Archive]({{ '/world/archive/' | relative_url }})'s boss) | 50% | Any |
| The Ender Dragon | 50%, every kill | Void, Arcane or Time |
| The Wither | 35% | Void or Blood |
| The Warden | 35% | Earth or Storm |
| An Elder Guardian | 35% | Frost or Life |
| Archive vault chests | See [Where gear is found](#where-gear-is-found) | Any |

## The Tome of the Fifth Page

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-gear-tome-row.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="The Cord screen with five spell rows; the fifth row is highlighted" class="shot">

The tome gives you a **fifth spell**, whatever your Cord.

- Put it in the Tome slot (or your off-hand). A fifth row appears in the Cord screen, with as many sockets as your Cord's other rows.
- Select it with `V`, or press `5` on the spell wheel. You can also bind **Cast spell 5 (the tome's)** in [Controls]({{ '/controls/' | relative_url }}).
- Take the tome out and spell 5 locks, but its runes are kept.
- The fifth spell can go on a [Spell Scroll]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}), but not in a [Knot]({{ '/fusion-altar/knots/' | relative_url }}).

The tome can't be crafted. Find it in chests (see below).

The **Breath Sash** shares this slot. It's a swordsman's choice instead of a fifth spell: see [Aura-forged gear]({{ '/progression/aura/' | relative_url }}#aura-forged-gear).

## Foci

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/d-gear-foci.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="First-person view holding a staff in one hand and a gold Focus of Thrift with a green gem in the other" class="shot">

A focus changes **every** spell you cast. Each is a plus shape around a Mana Crystal.

| Focus | What it does | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus of Haste** | Charges fill **40% faster** | Feather on top; Sugar, Mana Crystal, Sugar across; Gold Ingot below |
| **Focus of Thrift** | Spells cost **15% less**, but effects are **10% weaker** | Emerald on top; Gold Ingot, Mana Crystal, Gold Ingot across; Gold Ingot below |
| **Focus of the Deep Well** | **+50 max mana** | Block of Lapis Lazuli on top; Polished Deepslate, Mana Crystal, Polished Deepslate across; Polished Deepslate below |
| **Focus of Echoes** | **10% chance** a spell goes off again for free half a second later | Echo Shard on top; Amethyst Shard, Mana Crystal, Amethyst Shard across; Amethyst Shard below |
| **Focus of Resolve** | Spells hurt you **20% less**, but your effects are **15% weaker** | Iron Ingot on top and below; Amethyst Shard, Mana Crystal, Amethyst Shard across |

## Where gear is found

A chest that can hold gear rolls once for **one piece**, chosen evenly from its list:

| Chest | Chance | Possible pieces |
|---|---|---|
| Archive vault | 45% | The tome, the five foci above, or any greater staff |
| Archive library | 20% | The tome or the five foci above |
| Stronghold library | 10% | The tome or the five foci above |
| Ancient city | 12% | The five foci above |
| Woodland mansion | 8% | The five foci above |

The server can change these chances. Plain staffs are never found: craft them.

## How gear stacks

- **The same piece twice counts once.**
- **Two staffs never discount one spell twice.** A Fire and Frost spell with both staffs costs 10% less, not 20%. Each staff still boosts its own element.
- **The better staff counts.** A Fire Staff and a Greater Fire Staff give +35%, not more.
- **A staff and a focus both work.** A Fire Staff with Thrift gives Fire effects +20% then 10% weaker, and the spell costs 10% less then 15% less.
- **Gear applies last**, after Heart Circles, Cord enchantments, charge and [ranks]({{ '/fusion-altar/ranks/' | relative_url }}).

The Cord screen's readout and mana badge show what your gear does to the spell you're editing. A [Spell Scroll]({{ '/social/playing-together/' | relative_url }}#spell-scrolls) ignores gear.

All gear recipes are also in [Items and Crafting]({{ '/items/' | relative_url }}#casting-gear).
