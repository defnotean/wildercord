# The expedition field journal

> **Development build.** These twelve signatures have focused native altar, paid casting, counterplay, support, exploration and Full/Minimal presentation acceptance. Use the expedition-signatures review JAR for these additions; the public alpha.1 JAR predates them. Full lifecycle and real multiplayer acceptance remain in the roadmap.

An empty pocket that catches one arrow. Two chimes that give a foe time to move. A lantern whose owner chooses when to return. These signatures turn familiar runes into small decisions you can read during a fight or an expedition.

## Make the rune at the altar

Use the two **exact runes** in the table below, one amethyst shard, and **three experience levels**. Put the runes in two outer sockets, leave the third rune socket empty, put the shard in the catalyst socket, check the preview, and click **Fuse**. Take the result from its socket before the next recipe. Input order does not matter.

The named pair selects its signature. Other pairs of the same elements keep their ordinary elemental fusion. All twelve results are **tier III**. Learn the result and use a Cord/heart-circle setup that can cast that tier. Each is an effect rune: add a delivery shape in the spell editor.

| Signature | Exact Fusion Altar pair | Base effect mana | First useful setup |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Nullcatch** | Reflect + Collect | 20 | Self + Nullcatch, facing the incoming shot |
| **Second Bell** | Shock + Countdown | 16 | Beam + Second Bell, on a foe with space to move |

| **Quietus** | Manaburn + Stasis | 20 | Beam + Quietus, shortly before an enemy cast |
| **Blood Escrow** | Leech + Barrier | 14 | Beam + Blood Escrow, aimed at a willing ally |
| **Frost Molt** | Frostward + Cleanse | 14 | Self + Frost Molt while actually cold and grounded |
| **Pulse Ferry** | Regrowth + Time Skip | 18 | Self + Pulse Ferry beside two wounded allies |
| **Last Lantern** | Light + Rewind | 18 | Beam + Last Lantern, recording an ally's safe ground |
| **Pocket Current** | Bubble + Collect | 16 | Touch + Pocket Current, aimed at your tracked chest |

**Mana in this table is the effect's base price.** Shapes, links, gear, modifiers and the normal casting rules determine the full price shown by the editor. A paid spell can reach an unsuitable target and produce no result. A refused transfer preserves its extra health/items; it does not promise to refund the cast's mana.

Damage values below use Minecraft health points: **two health points are one heart**. These effects have fixed limits. Amplify, longer duration and repeating links do not refill their payment's finite resources. Normal defenses still apply, so admitted damage can produce less actual health loss.

## Counterplay: read the opening

### Nullcatch — keep the empty pocket facing danger

Void folds a hollow collecting pocket behind fractured Arcane mirror ribs. The travelling body has a mouth and loose cloth fragments; a successful capture folds the ribs inward with a short intake and dry closure.

The screen opens after a short **six-tick preparation** and lasts at most **three seconds**. It catches **one** approaching hostile owned projectile from the recipient's front, at close range with a clear route. One payment has one shared capture, even when several allies receive screens.

**Use it:** turn toward a visible incoming arrow, then open the pocket. **Counter it:** approach from behind, send a second shot, or wait out the screen. Melee, explosions and beams are not a general promise of protection. Friendly, ownerless and reflected projectiles are refused, including a reflected bolt whose original quarry has died. The attack's living source must remain eligible, loaded and unprotected; an invalid source cannot be captured for a reward. No arrow, stored spell, mana or damage is returned.

### Second Bell — a threat with time to answer

Two unequal copper chimes exchange a travelling spark. Their impact plants a short electrode mark. The first beat arrives **one second** after admission; the second at **two seconds**. Moving more than **one block horizontally from the original spot** cancels the second beat, provided ordinary target and sight checks still permit delivery.

One payment admits at most **two targets**. Each beat contributes at most **two damage**, each target at most **four**, and the entire payment at most **eight**, after bonuses. An absorbed contribution still spends its allowance; linked copies cannot mint another pair of beats.

**Use it:** make standing still costly while leaving an escape. **Counter it:** leave the marked spot before beat two, break sight, or use ordinary defense. It does not pin the target. Invalid targets, a departed caster or a blocked/unloaded route cancel remaining delivery. Listen for separated strikes rather than a continuous damage loop.

### Red Ledger — count the torn threads

An Arcane measuring needle holds three blood knots around a foe. During a **three-second** record, a qualifying ordinary horizontal movement of at least **one block** can tear one gate, with at least **eight ticks** between cuts. Each cut contributes at most **two damage**. Three gates, **six per target and six across the entire payment**, are the absolute ceiling after bonuses.

**Use it:** mark a foe before they choose a reposition. **Counter it:** hold position, break the valid route, or use a genuine teleport. A teleport releases the record, including a small same-world teleport; later walking cannot revive it. Very large displacement, mounted motion, anchoring and invalid mobile targets also stop it. Bosses and permanently immune targets cannot enter this movement record. There is no passive ticking damage, spreading, percentage-health scaling or resource refund.

### Quietus — seal one new cast slot

Three Arcane stamps hold an empty slot while Time sand flows backward through it. The lock opens after **six ticks**, expires after **two seconds**, and accepts only **one newly cast RuneBolt** owned by the marked hostile source. It must be young, leaving its owner, visible and in the close capture neighborhood. Existing projectiles and reflected bolts are excluded.

Only a successful catch taxes a player source's **current mana by at most eight**; it never drives mana below zero or transfers that mana to you. One payment has one catch and admits one marked target. The admission rest is **eight seconds**.

**Use it:** time the open slot against a visible spell preparation. **Counter it:** delay, choose a non-projectile action, or wait until the catch has been spent. Old bolts, arrows, melee and an empty timeout do not pay the tax. Boss, immune, protected or invalid targets are refused. Quietus grants no continuing Silence and stores no free ammunition.

## Cooperation: let the recipient choose

### Blood Escrow — fill the chamber with real health

Thick blood capillaries feed a small Arcane chamber. Give **up to three of your own health** to one eligible ally as a **six-second absorption ward**. Your remaining health cannot fall below **four**. At six health, for example, the maximum gift is two.

The ally must not already have absorption or an absorption effect. Self-targeting, a crouching player, an invalid ally or the recipient's **eight-second admission rest** refuses the gift. One payment buys one transfer. Crouching is refusal before admission; an admitted absorption ward follows its normal short expiry.

**Tradeoff:** the health really leaves you. Unused absorption expires without a refund, and its damage consumption does not heal you or earn mana. Keep a separate recovery option rather than spending down to your survival floor before a fight.

### Frost Molt — peel cold into one plate

Actual freeze exposure or Wildercord's **Frozen reaction mark** peels into a frost plate knitted by Life stitches. Generic Slowness and Weakness are not frost payments and keep their own duration.

The recipient must be willing, eligible and **on the ground**. The plate lasts at most **three seconds**, stops **one qualifying hostile projectile damage event of six damage or less**, and then shatters. Crouching or leaving valid grounded conditions sheds it. One payment makes one plate; the recipient has an **eight-second admission rest**.

**Tradeoff:** a clean target cannot conjure a plate. Larger hits, melee, explosions, ownerless/invalid-source damage and attacks that bypass the relevant ordinary protections pass. This is a specific one-hit projectile guard; it is not global invulnerability. Remove the cold first through another spell and you may remove Molt's payment too.

### Pulse Ferry — one parcel, two wounded allies

A sap parcel moves between two empty Time cradles on separate beats. The start area must contain **two distinct wounded eligible recipients** within **four blocks**, with loaded clear sight. At each one-second beat the ferry chooses a currently wounded eligible recipient it has not visited.

Each recipient gains at most **three actual healing**, with **six total** and one ferry per payment. Missing health bounds the delivery. A crouching player refuses a visit; the caster may be a recipient only if actually wounded and otherwise eligible.

**Use it:** group wounded teammates briefly around the impact area. **Counter or decline it:** crouch or leave the area. Team changes, blocked sight, death and departure can cancel later delivery. It supplies no overheal, absorption conversion, teleport or continuing regeneration. A repeated copy shares the same parcel budget.

### Last Lantern — a rendezvous, requested by its owner

An asymmetric Arcane lantern holds reversed Time sand on a willing ally player's current **safe grounded position**. It lasts **six seconds**. Their **fresh crouch edge** requests one return while they remain within **eight blocks**, in the same dimension, with loaded clear sight and a safe permitted destination body and floor.

The normal movement before that request remains free. If already crouching when recorded, release crouch and press it again to request return. One payment records one rendezvous; its recipient has an **eight-second admission rest**.

**Tradeoff:** the return is not guaranteed after the world changes. A new wall, claim, unsafe floor, ward, anchor, vehicle or invalid recipient can refuse it, spending the lantern request. It does not rewind health, mana or status, or move a hostile target. Do not treat a drawn lantern as permission to cross a sealed route.

## Expedition tools: keep the world honest

### Pocket Current — send loose supplies into your chest

Water forms a lopsided envelope around an item parcel; a dark fold pours it through the chest hinge. Aim at an ordinary **chest you placed after tracking was introduced**. Its owner record lives on that actual chest block entity. Both halves of a double chest need your matching recorded ownership and permission.

One payment moves at most **sixteen item units**, from at most sixteen ready visible loose drops within **three blocks** of the chest. The transfer simulates component-aware slot merging before committing. A full container keeps rejected goods on the ground; overflow is never discarded or redirected to player inventory.

**No effect:** a non-chest, lock, loot table, obstructed lid, foreign/untracked half, protected route, pickup delay, foreign pickup target or foreign thrower refuses the affected transaction. A matching pickup target does not override a foreign thrower reservation. Replacement or slot changes during permission checks cannot commit stale contents.

Existing untracked chests remain usable normally. To migrate one, empty it through ordinary inventory use, break it, and place it again yourself; the spell never clears old storage for you. It is a nearby intake tool, not remote access to an enemy chest.

### Wayline — take the line yourself

Three articulated Arcane glass links pull a dark grapple rope toward safe ground. Aim at a permitted support with a clear route to an eligible landing space within **six blocks**. The caster is its only rider. **Hold forward** to receive bounded ordinary motion toward the anchor; **crouch** to release. The whole tow lasts at most **two seconds** per payment.

Normal collision and lateral movement remain. A new obstruction, unsafe body space, changed permission, vehicle, anchor, death, dimension change or external Levitation ends the line. Existing faster motion makes it yield instead of multiplying your velocity. It grants no flight ability or continuing fall immunity; a short release descent effect can apply where needed.

**Use it:** bridge a small permitted reposition. **Tradeoff:** it does not teleport through a wall, pull another player or renew forever from a linked repeat.

### Night Seam — ask one thin wall a useful question

Flat Void stitches carry small Arcane inspection beads through at most **three contiguous eligible cells** along the struck face. Eligible stone is **stone, deepslate, cobblestone, stone bricks or tuff**. Beyond them must be a loaded, permitted open body space on safe ground.

For about **four seconds**, one accepted probe supplies a short passage clue and whether nearby eligible hostile movement was present at inspection. It does not promise live tracking, a full entity scan or an ore/chest map. The stitches recheck the route while visible.

**No effect:** four solid cells, another material, protected cells, block entities, special temporary terrain, dungeon wards, unloaded cells or unsafe space beyond. Blocks never change, and nobody moves. Aim squarely at the wall; an angled impact can inspect a different line of cells from the one you intended.

### Shard Compass — offer a mineral sample

Carry **raw iron, raw copper or raw gold**. The mineral needle searches for the matching ordinary or deepslate ore, choosing eligible nearby candidates around the struck cell. It inspects the **closest 512 candidates** from its bounded eight-block search neighborhood, at **32 cells per tick**. This is a deliberately bounded scan, not coverage of every block in that neighborhood.

A matching deposit must be loaded and permitted, with an accessible adjacent air cell visible within the short route limit. **Only a successful find spends one carried sample.** The result is a directional graded pebble breadcrumb route for at most **six seconds**, not an ore reward or an automatic mine.

No sample, no matching eligible deposit, a blocked route or an invalid location leaves the sample intact. A new locator replaces the caster's previous crumbs; a failed replacement cannot leave old directions pretending to be a fresh success. Following the route still requires ordinary exploration and mining permission.

## A first outing

Take Pocket Current and Shard Compass to a small permitted camp: place your own chest, leave a few ready supplies nearby, and carry one raw sample. Add Wayline for short clear-ground movement and Night Seam for a thin ordinary stone partition. Bring a teammate to try a requested Lantern return and a Ferry between two wounded allies.

For combat practice, give the marked player room to demonstrate both Bell escape and Ledger stillness. Test a single visible arrow against Nullcatch, then send a second. Each exercise makes the refusal or counter visible before you rely on it in a dungeon.

### Crowded counter windows

Nullcatch and Quietus refuse to open when more than 64 projectiles occupy their nearby three-block query area. An existing screen releases if that area becomes overcrowded. This refusal leaves all projectiles intact; opening refusal consumes no target allowance or rest, though the spell itself still costs mana. Quietus records every projectile in a complete nearby pool of up to 64, while each ordinary beat handles the nearest 12. A hostile hidden in a crowded projectile farm therefore receives no implied interception guarantee. Native crowding and provenance checks cover 65 and 256 arrows, thirteen pre-existing bolts and a genuinely later paid enemy cast. These are structural work limits, not a performance benchmark.
