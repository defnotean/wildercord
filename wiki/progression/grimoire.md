## What it is

Your **Grimoire** records everything you've discovered: reactions, secret spells, fusions, runes of the
land, creatures, affinities, your world's harmonies and quirks, and **feats**. Every first discovery pops
up as a toast and condenses mana toward your next [Heart Circle]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}).
It belongs to you, not your Cord.

The Grimoire is not your [Lore Journal]({{ '/progression/lore-journal/' | relative_url }}) (**H**). The
journal keeps the story of your travels with the sword: leads, places, people and what they said.

## How to use it

Open the Cord screen and pick the **Grimoire** tab (after Spells and Passives). Scroll to see it all and
hover a line for details. From top to bottom:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Master studies** | The Master lessons you can read or study: Relay Circle, Reweave, Excise and the lesson pack (Tollgate, Lifeline, Conduit). See [Heart Circles]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}#master-lessons). |
| **Reading Life magic** | Notes on how to read Life spells, for the Life runes you know. See [Life Outcomes]({{ '/spellcraft/life-outcomes/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Your heart** | Your [innate rune]({{ '/runes/innate/' | relative_url }}) and your leaning (your deepest affinity). |
| **Affinities** | Each element's level and progress. See [Your Affinities]({{ '/progression/affinity/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Reactions** (of 11) | Found reactions by name, the rest as **???**. See [Element Reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Secret spells** (of 10) | Found secrets, riddles you've read, and **???**. See [Secret Spells]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Sword arts** | The breathing-method arts you've played. See [Sword Arts]({{ '/progression/sword-arts/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Master forms** | The Master form books you've learned. See [Master Forms]({{ '/masters/master-forms/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Harmonies and quirks** | Your world's harmonies and the small ways it bends runes. See [Harmonies]({{ '/spellcraft/harmonies/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Runes you're still reading** | Learned runes you don't fully understand yet. |
| **Duels** | Duels won and lost. See [Duels]({{ '/social/duels/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Fusions** and **Signature fusions** | Fusions you've made, with hints for the rest. See [Combining]({{ '/fusion-altar/combining/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Woven pairs** | Exact woven pairs you've learned. Fuse any two exact effects with an Amethyst Block and 3 levels; they share one socket. |
| **Attunements** | Runes of the land you've attuned. See [Runes of the World]({{ '/world/runes-of-the-world/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Runes of the world** | Each place with its own runes, and which you know. |
| **Where you stand** and **Bestiary** | The [elemental climate]({{ '/spellcraft/affinities/' | relative_url }}#elemental-climate) where you are, and the weaknesses and resistances you've found. |
| **Creatures** | Wildlife, monsters and wanderers you've met. |
| **Feats** | Every feat, checked once earned. Hover one for how to earn it. |

## How to get it: discoveries

| First discovery | Condenses |
|---|---|
| A creature's weakness | 25 |
| A creature met | 40 |
| A sword art played | 60 |
| An affinity's first level | 100 |
| A quirk of your world | 100 |
| A reaction, fusion or signature fusion | 150 |
| An Aura milestone (method learned, breakthrough, first perfect guard...) | 150 |
| A feat, or a rune of the land attuned | 250 |
| A secret spell or a harmony | 400 |
| Tempered, Starbreaker, Low Tide, Heartwood or Grounded | 1,500 |
| The Last Page | 2,000 |
| A riddle from a Torn Page | nothing |

Each discovery counts once. Many also give an [advancement]({{ '/progression/advancements/' | relative_url }}).

## Every feat

There are **40 feats**. Each condenses **250 mana** the first time, unless noted.

### Casting

| Feat | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| **Full Charge** | Fully charge a spell, then release it. See [Casting]({{ '/spellcraft/casting/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Long Incantation** | Kill a monster with a spell of **six runes or more** (it dies within 5 seconds). A Knot counts as one rune. |
| **In Rhythm** | Chain **three casts on the beat**, each as the last comes off cooldown. |
| **Overcast** | Crack a Heart Circle to pay for a spell. See [Overcasting]({{ '/spellcraft/overcasting/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Wild Magic** | Overcast a spell and have it surge. |
| **Spell Collision** | Shoot an enemy's bolt out of the air with your own. |
| **Leaning** | Grow one affinity to level I and at least a quarter more points than any other. |
| **Scribe** | Inscribe a spell onto a scroll from the Cord screen. |

### Shields and imbuing

| Feat | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| **Spellguard** | Stop a spell with your Shield. See [Shields and Parrying]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Shieldbreaker** | Shatter someone's Shield with a stronger spell. |
| **Parry** | Raise a Shield at the last moment and turn a spell back. |
| **Imbuer** | Imbue a spell into an item or block. See [Imbuing and Glyphs]({{ '/spellcraft/imbuing/' | relative_url }}). |

### Your heart

| Feat | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| **Awakening** | Wake your innate rune at the 1st Circle. |
| **Mirrorfrost** | Turn an enemy's spell back on them. Only the innate rune Mirrorfrost can do it. |

## A full Grimoire

The **Every Page Filled** challenge wants all **11 reactions**, all **10 secret spells** and **36 of the
40 feats**. You're let off **Mirrorfrost** (only its innate rune earns it) and **Unison**, **Domain Clash**
and **Chorus** (they need other casters). The reward is 500 experience, 3 Mana Crystals and 8 Blank Runes.

## Tips and counterplay

- Check the Grimoire when you're stuck on a circle: every **???** is mana waiting.
- Meet creatures and test elements on them. Each new creature and weakness adds a little.
- The boss feats are the biggest single lumps, and several Master lessons need them.
