# The Marchkeeper Battlefields

## What it is

Rare outdoor ruins from the Marchkeepers' wars. Each holds a stone memorial that teaches one **technique intent** and gives
an illustrated field book.

## How to get it

Search **plains, sunflower plains, meadows, savannas and snowy plains**. There are three kinds:

| Tradition | What you'll see | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **The Line That Broke** | Staggered barricades with a breach | **Sunder**: break a foe's footing |
| **The Last Shelter** | A ruined shelter wall and its garden | **Bind**: hold a foe instead of chasing |
| **The Returned Step** | Paired cairns and a broken watchtower | **Echo**: leave an answering stroke |

## How to use it

1. Find the weathered stone with crossed blades in the middle.
2. Hold your blade, **sneak and stay still** until you enter the breathing stance.
3. **Use the memorial** and hold the stance next to it for **eight seconds**.

Moving away, standing up, losing your blade or losing sight of the memorial interrupts it.

Your first time at each tradition gives you its **field book** and teaches its **technique intent**. You can learn it before
Edge, but writing your own techniques still needs the usual stage. See [Techniques]({{ '/progression/techniques/' | relative_url }}).

## Tips

- Each tradition teaches you once, at any of its sites. Other players can still learn from the same memorial.
- Using a memorial again replays its three pages in chat, with no new reward.
- Supply chests hold modest materials. These ruins are not protected and have no boss.

## Server settings

`aura_world.battlefields` turns new battlefields and memorials on or off. Existing ruins stay.
