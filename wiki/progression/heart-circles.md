# Heart Circles
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What it is

Casters build rings of condensed mana around their heart, from the **1st Circle** to the **20th**, the
Master Heart. Each circle gives more mana, faster regeneration and more spell power. Four of them bring
a perk, and from the 8th Circle on they open Master lessons. Nothing is crafted or farmed: circles come
from casting, discovering and doing things with magic.

## How to get it

A circle forms in three steps:

1. **Condense mana.** Every point of mana you pay to cast a spell condenses in your heart.
2. **Earn the breakthrough.** Every circle after the 1st also asks for milestones (see the table below).
3. **Meditate** to form it once your heart is ready.

Hover the **heart badge** in the Cord screen to see your circles, perks and what the next circle still
needs, each line checked or crossed.

### Condensing mana

- The mana you actually paid counts, after discounts.
- A **Blood Price** spell counts 5 mana per point of health it costs.
- An **overcast** counts the mana you had before the circle cracked.
- **First discoveries** condense extra mana (table below).
- Passive upkeep, imbued items and glyphs letting go, a wild surge's Free Recast, inscribing a scroll and
  Creative mode condense nothing.

The amounts are running totals. Nothing is used up when a circle forms.

| First discovery | Condenses |
|---|---|
| A creature's weakness | 25 |
| A creature met (field guide) | 40 |
| A sword art played for the first time | 60 |
| An [affinity]({{ '/progression/affinity/' | relative_url }})'s first level | 100 |
| A quirk of your world | 100 |
| A reaction or a fusion | 150 |
| An Aura milestone (a method learned, a breakthrough, a first perfect guard...) | 150 |
| A feat, or a rune of the land attuned | 250 |
| A secret spell or a harmony | 400 |
| Beating the Cinder Warden, Star-Eater, Tide Scribe, Root Guardian or Storm Conductor | 1,500 |
| Beating the Archivist | 2,000 |

See [The Grimoire and Feats]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}).

### Meditating to form it

When your heart is ready, chat tells you, and the heart badge blinks.

1. Wear a Cord, stand on the ground, and **sneak without moving** or using an item.
2. Hold it for **10 seconds**, or **5 seconds** on a [ley line]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}).
3. **Any damage** breaks it, and moving or standing up starts it over.

When it forms, the circle's title fills your screen and your **mana refills completely**.

Circles 9 to 20 give the same per-circle bonuses but no new perks or passive slots. Seven of them ask you
to choose a **Circle Vow** (see below). Every breakthrough can be earned alone.

### What every circle gives

Each working circle adds **+15 max mana**, **+0.5 mana a second** and **+3% spell power**.

| Circles | Max mana | Regeneration | Spell power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +15 | +0.5/s | +3% |
| 4 | +60 | +2.0/s | +12% |
| 8 | +120 | +4.0/s | +24% |
| 12 | +180 | +6.0/s | +36% |
| 16 | +240 | +8.0/s | +48% |
| 20 | +300 | +10.0/s | +60% |

### The perks

| Perk | Circle | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Mana Skin** | 3rd | A fifth of the damage you take is paid with mana instead, at 2 mana per point of health, while you wear a Cord and have the mana. |
| **Flow** | 5th | Cooldowns are **15% shorter**. |
| **Overflow** | 7th | A spell cast at **full mana** hits **30% harder**. |
| **Archmage** | 8th | Spells and passives cost **15% less**. |

### Circle Vows

Circles 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19 and 20 each ask you to make one **vow**: you pick one of two sides. When the
circle forms, chat shows both sides as buttons. Hover over one to read it, and click it to choose it. Type `/vow`
at any time to see every vow, what you picked and what is still open.

| Circle | One side | The other side |
|---|---|---|
| 9th | **Wellspring**: +30 max mana | **Quickening**: +1.5 mana a second |
| 11th | **Keen Edge**: spells 6% stronger | **Spare Hand**: spells cost 6% less |
| 13th | **Swift Hand**: cooldowns 7% shorter | **Long Echo**: spell effects last 15% longer |
| 15th | **Overcharge**: spells 10% stronger but cost 5% more | **Austerity**: spells cost 10% less but are 3% weaker |
| 17th | **Torrent**: cooldowns 10% shorter, effects 10% shorter | **Vigil**: effects 25% longer, cooldowns 5% longer |
| 19th | **Reservoir**: +60 max mana, 0.5 less mana a second | **Spring**: +2.5 mana a second, 20 less max mana |
| 20th | **Crown of Power**: spells 10% stronger | **Crown of Ease**: spells cost 8% less, cooldowns 5% shorter |

Choosing is free. To change your mind, `/vow release <circle>` costs **5 experience levels**, and then you choose
again for free. A vow only works while its circle is whole: a cracked circle silences its vow until you mend it.

### Passive slots and your innate rune

- **Passive slots** open at the 1st and 5th Circle. See [Passives]({{ '/spellcraft/passives/' | relative_url }}).
- At the 1st Circle one of ten **innate runes** wakes in your heart, at random. It fits any Cord and
  grows **+6% stronger per working circle** (+120% at twenty). See [Innate Runes]({{ '/runes/innate/' | relative_url }}).

## Master lessons

From the 8th Circle, your circles open advanced magic. Each lesson needs that many **working** circles
(cracked ones don't count) and a boss feat. You study them in the **Master studies** section of your
[Grimoire]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}).

| Circle | Lesson | Also needs | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIII | Relay Circle | The Last Page | [Relay Circle]({{ '/spellcraft/relay-circle/' | relative_url }}) |
| X | Tollgate | Tempered | [Lesson Pack]({{ '/spellcraft/lesson-pack/' | relative_url }}) |
| XII | Ebb Ledger and Reweave | Low Tide | [Reweave]({{ '/spellcraft/reweave/' | relative_url }}) |
| XIV | Lifeline | Starbreaker | [Lesson Pack]({{ '/spellcraft/lesson-pack/' | relative_url }}) |
| XVI | Excise | Heartwood | [Excise]({{ '/spellcraft/excise/' | relative_url }}) |
| XVIII | Conduit | Grounded | [Lesson Pack]({{ '/spellcraft/lesson-pack/' | relative_url }}) |

Eight working circles also let you challenge the [Sword Masters]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}), even
without Aura.

## The breakthroughs in detail

| Milestone | What counts |
|---|---|
| **Know N runes** | Runes learned into your Codex. Knots don't count. |
| **Wear a Cord** | The Cord you wear now, or a better one. |
| **Reactions** | Different reactions in your Grimoire. See [Element Reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Defeat monsters with spells** | A hostile monster that dies within **5 seconds** of your spell hurting it. |
| **Slay Runebound** | A Runebound you kill by any means. See [Runebound]({{ '/world/runebound/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Help slay a boss** | Be alive within **96 blocks** when a boss dies: the Wither, the Warden, an Elder Guardian, the Ender Dragon, the Archivist, any dungeon boss, the Buried Keeper or a Sword Master. Once is enough. |
| **Secret spells** | Secret spells cast at least once. See [Secret Spells]({{ '/spellcraft/secret-spells/' | relative_url }}). |
| **Feats** | See [Every feat]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}#every-feat). |

Milestones you reached early still count later.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Cast a lot.** Spending mana is the fastest way to condense. A ley line doubles your regeneration.
- **Discover things.** Each first reaction, feat and secret is free mana. The Grimoire shows what's left.
- **Form circles on a ley line, somewhere safe.** Half the time, and a single hit starts it over.
- **Cracked circles.** [Overcasting]({{ '/spellcraft/overcasting/' | relative_url }}) cracks your
  outermost working circle for **3 minutes**. It gives nothing until it mends, and it can drop you below
  a lesson's circle. Don't overcast before a fight that needs your perks.
- Your rings show around you when you meditate or cast, tinted toward your deepest affinity.
