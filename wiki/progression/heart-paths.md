# Heart Paths
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What it is

When your heart forms the **Tenth Circle**, it asks which way it will grow. You choose one of three
**Heart Paths**. Each one changes your numbers a little, like a [vow]({{ '/progression/circle-vows/' | relative_url }}), and adds one thing only
that path does. You walk one path at a time. The Tenth Circle still opens its Master lesson as before.

## How to choose

1. Form the Tenth Circle by meditating, as usual.
2. Chat shows *"The Tenth Circle asks which way your heart will grow"* with the three paths as
   buttons. Hover a button to read it, and click it to choose.
3. Or type `/path` at any time to see the paths and pick one.

If you put the choice off, meditating reminds you once per session, after any open vows.

## The paths

| Path | Numbers | Only this path |
|---|---|---|
| **Path of the Storm** | Spells 8% stronger, cooldowns 5% shorter, 20 less max mana | Overflow (Circle VII) wakes at three-quarters mana instead of full |
| **Path of the Well** | +40 max mana, +1 mana regeneration a second | Meditation fills your mana twice as fast |
| **Path of the Ward** | Spells cost 6% less, effects last 15% longer | Mana Skin (Circle III) turns back 35% of a wound instead of 20% |

## Changing your path

`/path leave` steps off your path for **10 experience levels** (free in creative). You can then
choose any path again for free.

A path is silent while your Tenth Circle is unformed or cracked, just like a vow. It comes back
when the circle does. You can still leave a silent path.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/path` | Shows the three paths and the one you walk |
| `/path walk <storm, well or ward>` | Walks a path, once you hold the Tenth Circle |
| `/path leave` | Leaves your path for 10 levels |
