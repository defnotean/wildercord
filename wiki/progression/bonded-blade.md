## What it is

From **Edge** you can bond one weapon. A bonded blade is kept through death, never breaks, and nobody else can use it. It grows
with every real fight, takes your aura's colour, earns a **name** and a **trait** drawn from how you fight, and remembers its
story.

## How to get it

Any **sword, axe, spear or the mace** can be bonded, including the
[Bulwark Maul, Skyrend Glaive and Oathkeeper]({{ '/progression/weapons/' | relative_url }}). The trident can't.

1. Hold the blade and go to a **ley crossing**, where two [ley lines]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}) meet.
2. **Sneak and stand still** to enter the breathing stance.
3. Stay still for **ten seconds** while the ceremony runs: motes rise into the blade (3 seconds), the ley lines light up and
   run to you (4 seconds), a ring closes in (3 seconds), then a column of light and a bell seal the bond.

Moving, taking a blow or letting go of the blade breaks the ceremony. You can have **one bonded blade at a time**.

[Oathkeeper]({{ '/progression/sleeping-blade/' | relative_url }}) is the exception: it comes out of its stone already bonded.

## How it grows

A bonded blade gains **resonance** from fights with it **in your hand**. Each tier also waits on your Aura stage. Resonance
keeps building while it waits.

| Tier | Resonance | Needs | What it gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bonded** | the ceremony | Edge | Kept through death, never breaks, only yours |
| **Named** | 300 | Edge | A name, and blows with it draw 10% more aura |
| **Awakened** | 1,200 | Form | Choose one of three traits it offers |
| **Soulforged** | 3,600 | Sovereign, and a boss felled with it | Trait 50% stronger, 20% more aura from blows, its fullest look, and a free trait re-pick |

Where resonance comes from:

| Deed | Resonance |
|---|---|
| A foe felled | about 1 (more for a strong foe, less for the same kind over and over; a player counts once a day) |
| A boss felled | 30 more |
| An art landing | 0.6 to 1 (the Final Art 3), a bit more for extra foes |
| A finisher | 2 (5 on a boss) |
| A stance broken / a perfect guard | 0.4 / 0.5 |
| An awakening | 4 |
| A duelist beaten | 15 |
| A breakthrough to Form / to Sovereign | 120 / 240 |
| A technique reaching Peerless | 25 |

Each foe gives only so much through arts, finishers, stances and guards, so you can't farm one tough foe. Training dummies,
the practice arena and foes that can't fight back give nothing.

## Naming it

At **Named** the blade picks a name from its story (your element, your Way, where it was bonded, what it fights). On the
Blade tab you can type your own (up to 24 characters), or press **Suggest** for another, then **Name it**.

## Traits

At **Awakened** the blade offers **three traits**, based on the habits it sees most. Pick one on the Blade tab. Your first
pick is free. Changing it costs **10 experience levels**, except one free re-pick at Soulforged. A trait works only with the
blade in your hand.

| Trait | Drawn from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Well-Worn Verse** | one art used far more than others | That art costs 15% less aura and recovers 15% faster |
| **Closing Stroke** | finishers | Finishers return 50% more aura and build 4 more momentum |
| **Sundering Steel** | stances broken | Blows, arts and slashes wear stance 12% harder (6% on players) |
| **Riposte** | perfect guards | For 2 seconds after a perfect guard, your next coated blow hits 15% harder (half on players) |
| **Wind Step** | Aura Steps | Aura Step costs 25% less and recovers 15% faster |
| **Long Crescent** | slashes | Aura Slash costs 20% less and flies 20% further |
| **Inkbound Steel** | your own techniques | Techniques cost 10% less and rank up 20% faster |
| **Second Blaze** | awakenings | Awakening returns 25% sooner and you stay spent 25% shorter |
| **Mountainfeller** | bosses and strong foes | Against bosses and Runebound foes: 10% more damage, 15% more stance wear |
| **Gravewarden** | undead | Coated blows hit the undead 12% harder |
| **Last Light** | kills at low health | Below a third of your health, take 8% less damage from foes |
| **Moonwake** | kills at night | At night, blows draw 25% more aura |
| **Rallying Steel** | kills beside allies | Your finishers give allied swordsmen within 10 blocks 5 momentum |

At Soulforged each trait is 50% stronger. Against players, Mountainfeller and Gravewarden do nothing, Riposte and Sundering
Steel are halved, and damage bonuses share the normal player cap.

## Its story

The tooltip shows the tier, owner, resonance and trait. **Hold Shift** to read its story: when and where it was bonded, its
past owners, its deeds (foes, bosses, arts, finishers, techniques, perfect guards, duels won), its favourite art, and notable
moments by day.

## Only ever yours

- **Death:** it returns to its slot when you respawn, even with Curse of Vanishing.
- **Wear:** it never breaks. At its last point it stays notched until you repair it.
- **On the ground:** it never despawns, and fire, lava, cactus and explosions can't destroy it. Only you can pick it up. If it
  falls into the void, it returns to your inventory.
- **In a chest:** only you can take it out, and hoppers won't move it.
- **In someone else's hands:** it has no aura for them and returns to you. If you're offline, you get it when you join.
- **Crafting:** anvils, grindstones and smithing upgrades keep the bond. It can never be used up as an ingredient or smelted.

If it's away from you, the Blade tab tells you where it was last.

## Passing it on

You can pass your bonded blade to one of your **disciples** (see [Sparring and Lineage]({{ '/progression/lineage/' | relative_url }})).
Hold it in the breathing stance while your disciple kneels close in front of you, facing you. After **eight seconds** the blade
passes to them with its tier, name, trait and story. The disciple must have started on Aura and have no bonded blade. If they
are below the blade's tier, the extra tiers wake as they grow.

## Releasing the bond

On the Blade tab, click **Release the bond** twice. The blade becomes ordinary steel again and loses its growth. You can then
bond it, or another blade, at a ley crossing.

## The Blade tab

The fifth tab of the Aura page. Before you bond, it explains the ceremony and checks the blade in your hand. After, it shows
the blade, its tier and resonance, naming, traits (glowing gold when a choice is waiting), its story and the release button.

## For server owners

`aura` section: `bonded_blades` (switch bonding off; blades already bonded keep their protections), `resonance_gain`,
`bond_at_power` (allow the ceremony anywhere), `blade_traits`. The weapons that can bond are the `wildercord:bondable_blades`
item tag. Operator commands live under `/wildercord aura blade` (`bond`, `resonance`, `tier`, `name`, `trait`, `release`,
`pass <player>`).
