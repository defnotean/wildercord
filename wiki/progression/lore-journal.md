# Lore Journal
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What it is

The lore journal is your own record of the sword side of the world. It writes itself as you
explore: ruins you found, duelists and Sword Masters you met, what you learned, and what people
said to you. It also gives you **leads**: short discovery quests that point you to the next thing
worth finding.

The journal only watches. It never changes a fight, and nothing in it can be lost. It is kept when
you die.

## How to get it

You always have it. Press **H** to open it (you can change the key under *Wildercord: Lore* in
**Options > Controls > Key Binds**). It only opens when no other screen is open.

When something new is written, you see **Journal updated (H)** above your hotbar and hear a page
turn.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Leads** | The discovery quests you have found, open or done, with their goals and rewards |
| **Places** | Ruins and sites you found |
| **People** | Duelists and Sword Masters you met, duels you won and Master trials you passed |
| **Learned** | Your breathing method, Aura stages, field forms and Grimoire milestones |
| **Talk** | What duelists and Masters said to you, so you can read it again |

Scroll with the mouse wheel. **Home** and **End** jump to the top and bottom.

## What gets written

**Places**

- A Marchkeeper memorial, a [Sword Tomb]({{ '/progression/sword-tombs/' | relative_url }}), a
  [village tournament]({{ '/progression/village-tournaments/' | relative_url }}) board or a
  [Sleeping Blade]({{ '/progression/sleeping-blade/' | relative_url }}), when you use it or make its
  Grimoire discovery.
- The Crossroads of the [Ways]({{ '/progression/ways/' | relative_url }}), and the training grounds
  where you breathe in stance.
- Every [roadside place]({{ '/world/travel-sites/' | relative_url }}) and every
  [mine]({{ '/world/mine-sites/' | relative_url }}) you walk into.

**People**

- Each kind of duelist you meet, and each duelist you beat.
- Each [Sword Master]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}) who greets you. A Master greets you once, the
  first time you come within about 20 blocks.
- Each Master trial you win.

**Learned**

- Your [breathing method]({{ '/progression/breathing-methods/' | relative_url }}).
- Each [Aura]({{ '/progression/aura/' | relative_url }}) stage you reach, from Glow to Sovereign.
- Field forms you learn, such as Wall Turn, Stone Hinge, Cinder Lunge and Reed Slip.
- Grimoire milestones: choosing a Way, joining a lineage, your first perfect guard, your own
  technique, your first element reaction and your first field guide entry.

**Talk**

Greetings, hints and victory lines from duelists and Masters. Each of the sixteen breathing methods
has its own voice. Use a duelist you already know again and it gives you a hint, or, every other
time, a nudge toward your open lead.

## Leads

Each lead starts when you find something, and finishes when you do what it asks. Rewards are given
straight away.

| Lead | Starts when you | Then | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Scout's Wall | find a Marchkeeper memorial | speak with a Gale duelist | 30 XP, 2 Aura Shards, 40 Aura XP |
| Hessa's Gate | meet an Ember duelist | breathe in stance at a training ground (a waterfall or mountain summit) | 30 XP, 2 Aura Shards, 60 Aura XP |
| The Keeper's Openings | find a Sword Tomb | land a perfect Aura guard | 40 XP, 3 Aura Shards, 60 Aura XP |
| The Warden's Threshold | start a field guide entry | set off any element reaction | 30 XP, 2 Blank Runes |
| Different Hands | see a village tournament board | win a duel against any duelist | 30 XP, 2 Aura Shards, 1 Blank Rune, 40 Aura XP |
| The Masters' Ledger | meet a Stone duelist | meet a Sword Master | 50 XP, 3 Aura Shards, 1 Blank Rune, 80 Aura XP |

Leads you have not found yet show as hidden, with the hint *"Explore ruins, talk to duelists, read
your field guide."*

A lead can never get stuck. If you already did what it asks before you found it, it completes as
soon as it starts.

## Tips

- Lost for what to do next? Open the Leads tab, or talk to a duelist you already know.
- The Talk tab keeps Master hints. Read them again before a
  [Master trial]({{ '/masters/' | relative_url }}).
- The journal and the [Grimoire]({{ '/progression/grimoire/' | relative_url }}) are different. The
  Grimoire tracks feats and discoveries across all of Wildercord; the journal is the story of your
  travels with the sword.
