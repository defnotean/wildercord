# Cord enchantments
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What it is

Cords take **seven enchantments** of their own: three for your mana and four for your spells. They only
go on Cords and only work on the one you wear. Any combination fits on one Cord. For protection against
spells, see **Warding** on [Defending Against Magic]({{ '/progression/defence/' | relative_url }}#warding).

| Enchantment | Levels | Per level | At max | How common |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reservoir** | I-III | +25 max mana | +75 | common |
| **Wellspring** | I-III | +25% mana regeneration | +75% | common |
| **Siphon** | I-II | +2 mana back per creature your spell hits (up to 16 a cast) | +4 a creature | rarest |
| **Potency** | I-III | Spells 8% stronger | +24% | uncommon |
| **Celerity** | I-III | Cooldowns 8% shorter | 24% shorter | uncommon |
| **Thrift** | I-III | Spells and passives cost 7% less | 21% less | uncommon |
| **Persistence** | I-II | Spell effects last 20% longer | 40% longer | uncommon |

## How to get it

- **Enchanting table**, like a tool.
- **Anvil**: an enchanted book on your Cord, or two Cords combined.
- **Librarian villagers** sell them as books.
- **Enchanted books** in chests.

Upgrading a Cord at a crafting table **keeps its enchantments**, name and anvil history.

## How to use it

- **Reservoir** and **Wellspring** add to your [mana]({{ '/progression/mana/' | relative_url }}) pool
  and regeneration. Wellspring adds to the other boosts (meditating, Clarity, ley lines).
- **Siphon** counts each creature you could harm that your spell hits. One cast shares one limit of 16,
  even if it echoes or goes off twice. Imbued items and glyphs never Siphon.
- **Potency** strengthens everything a spell does: damage, healing, pushes. It multiplies with your
  [Heart Circles]({{ '/progression/heart-circles/' | relative_url }}): Potency III with twenty circles
  makes spells about twice as strong (1.24 × 1.60).
- **Celerity** multiplies with **Flow** (5th Circle, 15%): together about 35% shorter.
- **Thrift** multiplies with **Archmage** (8th Circle, 15%): together about 33% cheaper. A discount
  always saves at least 1 mana, and it never weakens a [Shield]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}).
- **Persistence** lengthens buffs, slows, burns and Shields: anything with a duration.

The HUD and Cord screen show each spell's cost and cooldown after every discount.

## Tips and counterplay

- Early on, **Reservoir** and **Wellspring** are cheapest and help most.
- **Siphon** shines with spells that hit crowds: a Burst that hits eight enemies with Siphon II gives
  back the full 16.
- **Thrift** also cuts passive upkeep.
