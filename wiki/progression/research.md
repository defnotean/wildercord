# Research Notebook
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What it is

Your **Research Notebook** tracks three one-time experiments and keeps a library of saved spell builds.

## How to get it

Use a **Book** on a [Runic Hearth]({{ '/social/runic-hearth/' | relative_url }}), or type
`/runelab notebook`. The screen shows your experiments, your saved builds, save/load/delete buttons and
a fusion hint. `/runelab research` lists your experiments in chat.

## How to use it

### Experiments

| Experiment | Reward (once) |
|---|---|
| Cast 5 different spell shapes | 1 Torn Page |
| Cast 3 different fusions (named or woven) | 2 Blank Runes |
| Cleanse a Living Greenhouse ([expeditions]({{ '/world/expeditions/' | relative_url }})) | 1 Mana Crystal |

Casts in Creative or Spectator don't count. Progress survives death and logging out. The fusion hint
suggests one fusion you can make from effects you already know.

### Saved builds

A build saves a spell's **rune sequence** and name, not gear or mana. You can keep up to **24**.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/runelab builds` | List your builds |
| `/runelab builds save "Warm breeze" 1` | Save the spell in slot 1 as "Warm breeze" |
| `/runelab builds load "Warm breeze" 2` | Load it into slot 2 |
| `/runelab builds delete "Warm breeze"` | Delete it |

- Saving under an existing name replaces it, even when the library is full.
- Loading only works if the slot is open, you know and carry every rune, and the build fits your Cord.

## Tips and counterplay

- Save a build before you experiment, so you can load it back.
- Keep builds for different Cords: a build too big for your current Cord won't load.
