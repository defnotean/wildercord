# Ways: where the path forks
{: .no_toc }

Up to **Edge**, every swordsman walks the same road: a method of their own, but the same techniques. At the Edge breakthrough the
road forks. A **crossroads** rises round you, a standard of light for each **Way**, and you strike the one you mean to walk. Your Way
gives you a **node** at Edge, at Form and at Sovereign, and each node is two things: a **passive**, and a **change to a technique you
already know** (the slash, the guard, the step, Intent, finishers, awakening, Dominion). A Way doesn't make you stronger so much as
different: a Blade cuts through what stops other swordsmen, a Bulwark stands where others give ground, a Shadowstep is never where
the blow lands, and a Banner makes everyone beside them better.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | At the **Edge breakthrough** the crossroads rises a moment after the breakthrough's title. Already past Edge? Hold the **breathing stance** a few seconds and it rises for you |
| **How** | **Strike a standard** with your blade to lean toward its Way (it flares and its name shows); **strike it again** to walk it |
| **The Ways** | **Blade** (offence), **Bulwark** (defence), **Shadowstep** (movement), **Banner** (together) |
| **Nodes** | One at **Edge**, **Form** and **Sovereign**: a passive and a change to a technique. A first choice gives you every node you've reached at once |
| **Changing** | Burn a **Crossroads Incense** at a place of power. The new Way's Form and Sovereign nodes wake as you earn experience walking it |
| **Kept** | Your Way is saved with your path and kept through death |
| **See it** | The **Way** tab on the Aura page |

## The crossroads

### At the Edge breakthrough

Break through to Edge and, a couple of seconds after the title, four standards of light rise out of the ground in an arc in front of
you, one for each Way: the **Blade**'s sword planted point-down in red, the **Bulwark**'s shield on its pole in blue, the
**Shadowstep**'s pillar of shadow trailing afterimages under a crescent in violet, the **Banner**'s pennant stirring on its pole in
gold. Everyone near sees them too. They stand for a minute; walk more than a dozen blocks away and they fade (so does a death, or a
change of world), and you can call them again (below).

### Choosing

Look at a standard and its name shows above your hotbar, with what a strike will do and its three nodes by name. **Strike it** with
your blade (an ordinary swing) and it **answers**: it flares, rings rise round its foot, the others dim, and the line over your hotbar
says to strike again. A strike at another standard moves your lean to it. **Strike the leaned standard again** within eight seconds
and you **walk its Way**: its light pours into you, the other three break apart, its name shows in your banner, and your chat lists its
nodes and which are yours already. The choice goes into your Grimoire.

Take your time: the Aura page's **Way** tab (below) shows every node in full, and you can open it while the crossroads stands.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/ways-moments.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Four night scenes of a player choosing a Way: beams of light pour from the chosen standard into the player, the other standards burst apart in sparks, and rings of the chosen Way's colour circle the player: red for the Blade, blue for the Bulwark, pale violet for the Shadowstep, gold for the Banner" class="shot">
<span class="caption">Each Way chosen: the Blade, the Bulwark, the Shadowstep and the Banner.</span>

### Already past Edge?

If you were at Edge or above before Ways came in (or you've unbound yours, or let the crossroads fade), you walk no Way yet, and a
line in your chat says so when you join. **Hold the breathing stance** (sneak and stand still with your blade in hand) for about three
seconds after it settles, and the crossroads rises round you. It needs open ground: in a cramped tunnel it tells you to find room.
A first choice is free whenever you make it: a Form or Sovereign swordsman gets **every node they've reached at once**, with nothing to
earn. The stance won't call it while a stillness trial is under way, so a breakthrough at a ley crossing is never interrupted.

## The four Ways

Each table lists a Way's nodes: the stage that opens each, its passive, and the technique it changes.

### Way of the Blade
*Strike first, strike through, and finish what you start.* Offence: cut through what stops others, and turn openings into a cascade.

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Keen Edge** | Clean hits build **momentum a third faster** | **Aura Slash pierces**: it cuts on through a held guard instead of stopping there, through **ten** foes instead of six, and when it meets another crescent it cuts it apart and flies on at half its strength |
| **Form** | **Cascade** | **Finishers add two fifths more** (against a player, their cap still holds) | **Finishers cascade**: a finisher opens the nearest creature within five blocks whose stance is half worn or more (never a player or a boss), so a worn-down crowd falls one after another |
| **Sovereign** | **Storm of Edges** | Each finisher feeds your awakening **two seconds** instead of one, up to eight | **Awakening**: while you're awakened, Aura Slash **costs nothing** and is **ready again in a second** |

Play it by wearing a crowd down evenly (sweeps, arts) and then finishing one: the cascade carries on from foe to foe. Awakened at
Sovereign, a Blade throws piercing crescents every second.

Right: a finisher landing on a husk in a ring of light, and a sweep of aura carrying on to open a second husk beside it" class="shot">

### Way of the Bulwark
*Stand where you are, and let nothing through.* Defence: a guard that turns everything, a body that won't fall, a Dominion nothing crosses.

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Wide Guard** | Your **stance takes two fifths less wear**, and a hit knocks **a third less momentum** off you | **Aura Guard covers every side**, its perfect moment lasts **half again as long** (half a second), a held guard **throws a third of each blow it catches back** at the striker (as aura off the blade), and it **turns shots back** at whoever loosed them (a little aura each) |
| **Form** | **Living Wall** | **Aura armour takes a third** of each blow instead of a quarter, and costs a fifth less | **Intent challenges**: creatures within its reach that hunt your allies (your teammates, your pets) turn on you, and a creature that strikes your raised guard **staggers** |
| **Sovereign** | **Unbroken** | While you're awakened **nothing breaks your stance** and everything a foe deals you is **a fifth weaker** (less against a player's blows); spent, **you aren't slowed** | **Dominion is a bastion**: a foe's shots crossing into it from outside are **turned back at its edge** (a faint wall of blue light stands at its rim), and the creatures inside are slowed a step more |

Play it by holding the guard more than you would: it covers your back, pays you back for every blow, and staggers what hits it. In a
group, your Intent keeps the creatures on you and off your friends.

<span class="caption">A bastion: shots turned back at its edge.</span>

### Way of the Shadowstep
*Be where the blow isn't, and strike where the eye isn't.* Movement: get behind, and let your afterimage do the rest.

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Slip** | Blows and arts that land on a foe **from behind** (a third of a turn or more off its front) wear its stance **half again as fast** | **Aura Guard**: a perfect guard against a blow **slips you behind** whoever struck, facing its back, and it loses you for a moment: your counter falls there |
| **Form** | **Afterimage** | For **two seconds after an Aura Step** every blow you land counts as from behind | **Aura Step leaves an afterimage** where you set off that **strikes** a moment later, cutting every foe beside it (a little over half a blow each) |
| **Sovereign** | **Thousand Shadows** | A **finisher landed from behind readies your Aura Step** at once | **Awakening**: while you're awakened, Aura Step **costs nothing**, is **ready again in a second**, and its afterimage **strikes twice** |

Play it as a loop: step through a pack (the afterimage cuts where you were), every blow after it from behind, the stance breaks, the
finisher lands from behind and the step is ready again. A perfect guard is a way out from in front of anything.

<span class="caption">A slip behind its striker, and an afterimage striking where the swordsman stepped from.</span>

### Way of the Banner
*No one fights alone while your banner stands.* Together: share what you build, rally your friends, and shelter them.

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Battle Cry** | **A third of the momentum you build** is built by your allied swordsmen near you too | **Finishers rally**: every finisher lets out a **rallying cry**: you and your allies near you (pets too) take **a sixth less** from foes for five seconds, and allied swordsmen build momentum and share half the aura it gave back |
| **Form** | **Rallying Presence** | **A quarter of the aura you gather** flows to your allied swordsmen near you | **Intent steadies**: while it presses on a foe, you and your allies within its reach take **a tenth less** from foes |
| **Sovereign** | **Shelter** | Allied swordsmen near you lose **a third less momentum** to hits, and **your awakening holds their momentum** at its second tier while it burns | **Dominion shelters the party**: you and your allies inside take **a fifth less** from foes, your allied swordsmen gather aura there as you do, and their momentum doesn't ebb |

"Near" is **twelve blocks**. Alone, the Banner is the quietest Way (its cries and presence still steady you, and its Dominion
shelters you); with friends, it's the strongest thing on the field.

## Allies and the Banner

Everything the Banner gives reaches only your **allies**, by the same rule chorus casting uses:

- **teammates** (players on your scoreboard team) are allies;
- with no teams, two players who **couldn't harm each other either way** (PvP off) are allies;
- two players who could fight are **never** allies, and **two duelling each other never are**;
- your **pets**, and your team's, share in cries, presence and the shelter (they don't have momentum or aura to share).

So a Banner never helps an enemy, even standing in the middle of a fight. Living Wall's challenge draws creatures off the same
allies.

## Changing your Way

A Way is meant to be lived with, so changing it costs something, three ways at once:

- **A Crossroads Incense**: two **Aura Shards** (a fallen knight's), an **amethyst shard** and **blaze powder**, crafted together.
- **A place of power**: hold it in use for two seconds (it burns, smoking in your Way's colour) at the heart of a **ley crossing**,
  where breakthroughs are earned. Anywhere else it won't catch, and isn't used up.
- **Settling**: your Way is burned out of you (its nodes go dark at once), and the crossroads rises where you stand to choose again.
  The new Way's **Edge node is yours at once**, its **Form node wakes after 120 experience** and its **Sovereign node after 240**,
  earned by fighting as you always do (about three quarters of an hour of steady fighting; a swordsman waiting at a breakthrough
  still settles). The Way tab shows each waking node filling.

Why all three: the shards make it a fight, the ley crossing makes it a journey, and the settling means a new Way is walked into rather
than bought, even by a Sovereign with shards to spare. Nothing is lost for good: you can walk any Way again, at the same price.

## The Way tab

The Aura page (the Aura badge on the Cord screen, `K`) has a **Way** tab beside Techniques and Sword strings. Before Edge it shows what
each Way gives; past Edge with no Way it breathes gold and says how to call the crossroads; once you walk one, its name and creed head
it. Under that is the **tree**: a column for each Way (its emblem and name), a row for each node stage, each node drawn as it stands for
you:

- **in force**: your Way's, reached and awake, framed in gold, its column lit behind it;
- **waking**: your Way's, after a change of Way, half lit, a thread under it filling as you earn experience;
- **upcoming**: your Way's, at a stage you haven't reached, outlined in its colour;
- **another Way's**: dark;
- **open**: no Way chosen yet, every node in its own colour.

Click a node (or hover it) to read it in full under the tree: its name and stage, where it stands, its passive and what it changes.
At the foot, how to change your Way.

## Ways and other players

- **The Blade's** finishers against a player stay under the cap every finisher keeps (a quarter of their health, and eight under the
  PvP scale); Cascade never opens a player.
- **The Shadowstep's** stance from behind never wears more of a player's stance than one blow may (a third of it).
- **The Bulwark's** throw-back and **the Blade's** slash (through a guard too, which still takes its share) are aura off the blade, so a
  player's spell defences meet them; the Bulwark's stagger and the bastion's extra slow touch creatures only.
- **Steadying** (the Banner's cries, presence and shelter, an unbroken Bulwark's awakening) never takes off more than three tenths of
  anything, all of it together; against another player's blows, only the PvP scale's share of that.
- The Banner reaches allies only (see above).

## For server owners

Ways are switched with `aura.ways` in `config/wildercord.json`. `way_settle_xp` is what a new Way asks before its later nodes wake (240;
Form at half), `way_change_at_power` whether the incense needs a place of power, and the Banner's reach and shares are `banner_range`
(12), `banner_share` (a third of momentum) and `banner_aura_share` (a quarter of aura). Operators can set a Way outright with
`/wildercord aura way <way>` (`none` to clear it), or raise the crossroads with `/wildercord aura way crossroads`.
