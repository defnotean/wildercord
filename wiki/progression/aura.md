# Aura: the swordsman's path
{: .no_toc }

The Cord is the mage's path. **Aura** is the swordsman's: mana drawn into your body and out along your blade. Learn a
**breathing method**, gather aura from real blows and a steady breath, and climb five stages, from a haze on your weapon
to a presence that claims the ground you stand on. Anyone can walk it, with or without a Cord, and it mixes freely with
spells.

## Breathing methods

### What it is

A method gives your aura its **colour**, its **element** and a small **gift** that grows with every stage. Your coated
blows, slash and arts carry that element: they meet a creature's weakness or resistance, and set off
[reactions]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}) (a frozen foe shatters under an Ember blade). There are 16
methods; add-ons can bring more. Every method's arts, finisher and look are listed on
[Breathing Methods]({{ '/progression/breathing-methods/' | relative_url }}).

| Method | Element | Gift |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | Fire | Coated blows may set foes alight (always from Edge) |
| **Rime** | Frost | Coated blows slow foes |
| **Thunder** | Storm | Coated blows may spark to the nearest other foe |
| **Gale** | Wind | A little more speed in a fight |
| **Stone** | Earth | Blows push you back less |
| **Verdant** | Life | Coated blows mend you a little |
| **Hollow** | Void | Foes near the one you strike are drawn toward it (never a boss) |
| **Starlit** | Arcane | Aura gathers faster |
| **Hourglass** | Time | A little more attack speed |
| **Crimson** | Blood | Coated blows drink a little, for a little more aura |
| **Tide** | Brine | Coated blows push foes back and soak them (fire out, a moment slowed) |
| **Iron** | Metal | Coated blows crack a foe's armour for 3 seconds |
| **Dune** | Sand | Coated blows may throw grit in a foe's eyes and blind it for a moment |
| **Echo** | Wind | Coated blows may ring again a moment later; from Edge the foe reels |
| **Dawn** | Arcane | Coated blows light foes up and burn the undead; from Flow they mend you a little |
| **Venom** | Life | Coated blows may leave a toxin that stacks; from Edge every blow does, and weakens |

### Where to find manuals

Breathing Manuals turn up where fighting is old:

- **The Archive** library and vault; **every expedition's vault** (its own element); **trial chambers** (the vaults);
  **stronghold libraries** and **ancient cities**.
- **Master weaponsmiths** and **master clerics** sell one for emeralds and a book.
- **Wandering duelists** teach theirs to anyone who beats them (see [below](#duels-with-a-duelist)).
- **Fallen knights** drop **Manual Pages**: four pages of one method and a book make its manual.

{% include recipe.html id="breathing_manual_from_pages" alt="Four Manual Pages and a book in a crafting grid, making a Breathing Manual" caption="Four pages of one method and a book: that method's manual." %}

**Changing method:** read another manual, then read it again to confirm. You keep your stage, but lose the aura you hold
and your progress toward the next breakthrough.

## Gathering aura

Aura is a pool that **never fades**. It holds 20 at Glow, 40 at Flow, 70 at Edge, 110 at Form and 160 at Sovereign.

- **Full swings fill it.** Land a full-strength swing on a real foe (a monster or an enemy player). Half-charged spam gives
  nothing, farming one spot gives less and less, and a Training Dummy gives only a quarter.
- **The breathing stance fills it.** Sneak and stand still with your blade in hand. A ring closes on the aura bar with each
  breath: **release sneak and press it again as the ring closes** for a bonus burst. Moving or being hurt breaks it. With a
  Cord you meditate for mana at the same time.
- **A waterfall bank or exposed mountain summit** makes the stance recover faster, and each full breath there teaches a
  little (inside the practice limit, see [Breakthroughs](#breakthroughs)). Stand on dry ground within three blocks of a
  falling column of water at least six blocks tall, or on open-sky mountain ground well above sea level with the land
  dropping away. A message tells you when the place counts.
- **Running dry brings backlash.** A technique you can't afford spends what you have, then leaves you slowed and weakened for
  a few seconds. It never hurts you.

## The stages

Each stage keeps everything below it. Your weapon and body show your stage for everyone to see: a haze at Glow, rippling
light at Flow, a crystal blade from Edge, a mantle at Form and a burning corona at Sovereign.

### Glow

- **Aura Coat**: while you hold aura, every blow of an aura weapon lands **10% harder** in your element, for half a point of aura.
- **Aura Sense**: each breath in the stance outlines hostile creatures within 16 blocks, through walls, for you alone.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/aura-sense.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A husk outlined in orange light, seen from the breathing stance" class="shot">

### Flow

- **Flowing Cut**: every aura weapon sweeps, wider and further than a normal sword.
- **Aura Guard**: **sneak and press the Aura key**. It holds while you keep sneaking (two seconds at most) and halves blows and
  arrows from in front, paying aura for what it stops. Empty, it breaks with backlash.
- **Perfect guard**: raise it just as a blow lands. The blow is turned aside whole and the attacker staggers (a boss is only
  slowed), an arrow flies back, and a spell is parried, the same timing as a
  [Shield parry]({{ '/spellcraft/shields/' | relative_url }}).

### Edge

- **Crystal Edge**: **a block more reach**, and a quarter of every coated blow goes through armour.
- **Aura Slash**: **tap the Aura key** to loose a crescent that cuts every foe in its path (up to 6, about 14 blocks) for a
  little more than your weapon's damage. 12 aura, ready again in 2 seconds.
- **Spellcut** (`U`), your first [Master's Art](#the-masters-arts).
- **Awakening** opens (see [Awakening](#awakening)).
- The Edge breakthrough raises the [Ways]({{ '/progression/ways/' | relative_url }}) crossroads, and opens your first
  [technique slot]({{ '/progression/techniques/' | relative_url }}) and the
  [bonded blade]({{ '/progression/bonded-blade/' | relative_url }}).

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/aura-slash.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A crescent of green aura flying away from a player toward a line of husks" class="shot">

### Form

- **Aura Step**: **double-tap the Aura key** to dash about **six blocks** the way you're moving (ahead if still). For its first
  moments nothing can touch you. It never passes through walls or into lava or fire. 12 aura, ready again in 2 seconds.
  From here a single tap waits a moment in case it's a double-tap.
- **Aura Armour**: while you hold **20 aura or more**, a shell takes **a quarter** of incoming harm (blows, arrows, spells, fire)
  for half a point of aura per point taken. It never spends you below 20. Falls, drowning and hunger aren't covered.
- **Intent**: hostile creatures within 8 blocks that are weaker than you (lower health, or a lower aura stage) are slowed and
  sometimes falter. Bosses never feel it.
- **Aura Sense** reaches 24 blocks and pulses on its own in a fight.
- **Rising Break** (`Y`) and **Driving Cut** (`J`).

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/aura-step.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A player at night a few blocks down a stone platform, a row of fading cyan afterimages of them behind, and streaks of light along the way they came" class="shot">
<span class="caption">Aura Step with Rime Breath: afterimages along the way.</span>

### Sovereign

- **Dominion**: **hold the Aura key** to claim ground about six blocks across for **8 seconds**. Foes inside are slowed and
  **hit 30% weaker**, each blow you land inside **chains** to another foe there for half its damage, and your aura flows back
  **twice as fast**. 40 aura, rests **a minute and a half**.
- The **Final Art** opens (see [Sword strings](#sword-strings)).

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/aura-dominion.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Seen from above at night: a wide orange magic circle on a stone platform round a player, husks standing inside it" class="shot">
<span class="caption">Dominion with Ember Breath.</span>

## Sword strings

Your ordinary swings are a language. Play a short **string** in time (a low swing, a leaping one, a counter after a perfect
guard, a cut after a step) and your blade answers with an **art**. Every method plays the same strings, but each method's arts
do their own thing. The full list of strings, arts and their numbers is on
[Sword Arts]({{ '/progression/sword-arts/' | relative_url }}).

| Art | Opens at | String | Aura | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | Glow | swing, swing, **low** swing | 6 | 3 s |
| **Second** | Flow | **leaping** swing, **low** swing | 8 | 4 s |
| **Third** | Edge | a **counter** | 8 | 4 s |
| **Fourth** | Form | a **step cut** | 10 | 5 s |
| **Final** | Sovereign | **full**, **full**, **full**, **low**, at the peak of momentum | 40 | 30 s |

Each art has a short **windup**: its price is paid when it starts, and if you take damage before it lands the strike is lost
(the price and rest stay spent). How arts look and move is on
[Combat presentation]({{ '/masters/combat-presentation/' | relative_url }}).

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/aura-string-first-art.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Seen from above and behind: a player wreathed in orange light, an arc of flame cut low in front of them, a burning husk ahead with a ring of light round it" class="shot">
<span class="caption">Kindling Draw, Ember Breath's First Art.</span>

## The Master's Arts

Three committed sword techniques on their own keys, shared by every method. Rebind them under
**Options > Controls > Key Binds > Wildercord: Master's Arts**.

| Art | Key | Opens at | Aura | Rest | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Spellcut** | `U` | Edge | 14 | 3 s | Severs up to 3 hostile bolts flying at you from in front, and slashes nearby foes |
| **Rising Break** | `Y` | Form | 20 | 5 s | Wears extra stance and lifts ordinary foes |
| **Driving Cut** | `J` | Form | 18 | 4 s | A committed 5-block thrust. If it damages a player holding a spell (absorption counts), it breaks the spell and seals their casting for 1 second. Held spells then can't be interrupted again for 8 seconds |

Spellcut (the `U` art) is not the same as **Spell Cut**, the timed sword swing an Ember teacher can teach you after an
Ember Master clear. See [Spell Cut]({{ '/masters/spell-cut/' | relative_url }}).

You need an aura weapon in hand. Each has a short windup and a recovery: the aura is paid at the start, and taking damage
during the windup cancels the strike. You can't stack another swing, Aura action or spell on top of it.

## Momentum and openings

### Momentum

Momentum is the thin line under your aura bar. Clean play fills it: full swings that land, arts that land, perfect guards,
stepping through an attack, breaking a stance and landing a finisher. Hits you take knock some off, and it drains a few seconds
after the fight goes quiet.

| Tier | At | Arts cost | Arts strike | Stance worn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 | 10% less | 5% harder | 10% faster |
| 2 | 50 | 15% less | 10% harder | 20% faster |
| 3 | 75 | 20% less | 15% harder | 30% faster |
| **Peak** | 95 | 25% less | 20% harder | 45% faster |

At the **peak** the line turns gold and your **Final Art** opens. A Final Art waits for the peak, and playing it spends 40
momentum. (If a server switches momentum off, a Final Art waits for a full aura pool instead.) Most methods build fastest on
foes in the state their own arts leave them in (Ember on burning foes, Rime on frozen ones, Tide on soaked ones, and so on);
Echo, Dawn and Venom have no favoured state and build the same on any foe. It can't be farmed: foes that can't fight back give
nothing, and a training dummy gives less and stops just short of tier 3 (74). Only the
[practice room]({{ '/progression/practice/' | relative_url }}) lets dummies carry you to the peak.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/momentum-bar.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Two views of the aura bar: at the second tier a thin pale orange line part way along under it, and at the peak the same line full and pale gold with a bright spark on it" class="shot">

### Stance and finishers

Every foe has a **stance**: a thin bar over its head that fills as you wear it down. Blows wear it, arts twice as fast, a
perfect guard a third at once, and Stone most of all. It recovers if you back off.

When it breaks, the foe is **opened** for a few seconds: it staggers, and a cracked gold seal beats over its head. Your next
**full swing** on it is your method's **finisher**: the blow plus about a third of what the foe has already lost (at most four
times your blade's damage), aura back, and momentum. Then the foe stands steady for a moment.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/stance-opened.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Seen from the side: a swordsman wreathed in orange flames facing a burning husk; over the husk's head a cracked gold diamond seal with a gold line under it" class="shot">

- **Bosses** have far more stance and recover fast. Opened, they are only slowed, take a smaller finisher, and grow steadier
  each time they break.
- **Players** have a stance too. No blow wears more than a third of it, an opened player is only slowed and loses their guard
  for a moment, and finishers on players are capped. Never a one-shot.

## Awakening

### What it is

From **Edge**, with a full pool and a good fight going, you can burn everything at once: **12 seconds at Edge, 16 at Form,
20 at Sovereign** at your strongest, then a price.

### How to use it

**Tap the Aura key, then press and hold it** until the ring round your crosshair closes. You need:

- your aura pool **full** (95% is enough),
- momentum at **half or more**,
- an aura weapon in hand, and the last awakening's rest over.

A small flame after the stage diamonds glows when you can. While awakened:

- your **arts cost nothing**, and momentum is held at its **peak** (so the Final Art is open at Sovereign);
- coated blows land **15% harder**, and you move and swing **10% faster**;
- each finisher adds **a second** (four at most);
- at Sovereign, **Dominion** becomes your method's own: half again as wide and as long, foes hit **40% weaker**, chains to
  **two** foes.

When it ends, your aura burns away, momentum empties and you are **spent for 30 seconds**: slowed and gathering **no aura at all**.
With an empty pool you have no coat, guard, slash, step, armour or arts. The next awakening waits **three minutes** from the end
of the last. Dying ends it.

<span class="caption">The moment of awakening, Ember Breath at Form.</span>

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/awakening-hud.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Four views of the aura bar: a pale flame after the stage diamonds when ready; the bar edged in pink with Awakened: 19 s above it; the bar empty and grey with Spent: 29 s above it; and the flame dark with a thin thread under it while it rests" class="shot">

### Tips and counterplay

- Awaken to finish a fight, not to start one. A spent swordsman is easy prey for half a minute.
- Against players an awakening is modest: its extra damage is capped like aura's other bonuses, and its holds are as short
  as any art's.

## The spellblade

From **Edge**, **cast a spell while sneaking** with your blade in hand and the spell flows into the blade instead of leaving.
Your next **Aura Slash** within **5 seconds** carries it: the spell lands on the first few foes the slash cuts, or bursts
where the slash breaks. You pay both the spell's mana and the slash's aura. If you don't slash in time, the spell leaves as
normal. Self spells, secret spells and overchannelled spells never ride a blade.

## Blade and spell together

### Aura marks

A coated blow or slash sometimes leaves its element's [reaction mark]({{ '/spellcraft/reactions/' | relative_url }}) on a foe
for a mage to set off (15% at Glow, 5% more each stage). A method never sets off its own mark.

### Resonant Strikes

Land elemental spell damage and a full coated swing, slash or art on one foe within about a second for a
[Resonant Strike]({{ '/progression/resonant-strikes/' | relative_url }}).

## Breakthroughs

### How to get one

You grow by landing full swings on real foes, more when you're in danger and against bosses or stronger foes. At a stage's
limit the next diamond on the aura bar pulses gold and a **breakthrough** waits. Reach the total experience, then pass one
trial:

| Next stage | Total experience | Trials (any one) |
|---|---:|---|
| Flow | 450 | **Stillness**: 30 seconds of breathing stance where ley lines cross, or at a qualifying waterfall or summit. **Stronger foe**: fell a boss, Runebound or a foe with twice your health with blade and aura only, within 60 seconds |
| Edge | 1,800 | The same as Flow |
| Form | 5,400 | **Tempest**: 45 seconds of stance at a ley crossing in an open-sky thunderstorm. **Guardian**: fell a boss with blade and aura only within 3 minutes. **Duel**: beat a wandering duelist without magic |
| Sovereign | 13,500 | Tempest for 60 seconds, the guardian trial, or the duel |

Any spell of yours touching a trial foe spoils the trial. See [Ley Lines]({{ '/progression/ley-lines/' | relative_url }}).
A formal disciple can also break through by knocking out their own master in a spar, up to one stage below the master (see
[Sparring and Lineage]({{ '/progression/lineage/' | relative_url }})).

### Tips: if your rank seems stuck

- The experience bar **waits for the trial**. More fighting won't push past it.
- Training Dummies, the practice arena and waterfall or summit breathing share a **40 experience lifetime practice limit**. If
  you stall around 40, fight real hostile foes. Animals and pets teach nothing.
- Only **full** swings with your own aura weapon count. The breathing stance restores aura but gives no experience.
- Open the Cord screen (`K`) and choose **Aura**: hover the experience line for the rules.

## The Aura page

Open the Cord screen (`K`) and click the **Aura badge** (it's there even without a Cord). Tabs:

- **Aura**: your method, stage, pool, next breakthrough and its trials, and every technique.
- **Sword strings**: your arts, their strings, prices at your momentum, and your finisher. Click a method's colour to browse it.
- **Way**: the [Way tree]({{ '/progression/ways/' | relative_url }}).
- **Writing**: [techniques of your own]({{ '/progression/techniques/' | relative_url }}).

## Duels with a duelist

[Wandering duelists]({{ '/world/creatures/' | relative_url }}#wandering-duelist) teach their method to anyone who beats them.
Use one with a blade in hand, then use it again within ten seconds to accept.

- **It meets you at your stage** and has a tell for every move: a raised guard (from Flow), a blade held high before a slash
  (from Edge), a low crouch before a dash (from Form).
- **Nobody dies.** A beaten duelist yields; if you fall you're knocked out on one heart and restored. Leaving forfeits; after
  five minutes it's a draw.
- **Winning teaches** its method (or gives its manual if you already have one), a little experience and a Grimoire entry.
  Winning without magic counts as a Form or Sovereign trial.
- **Lose, and it waits** half a minute before you can try again.

## Aura and other players

- Aura blows are ordinary blows, so armour counts. What aura adds against a player is capped and scaled like a spell's bonuses,
  and the slash meets [spell defences]({{ '/progression/defence/' | relative_url }}) in full.
- Intent only presses on a player with a lower stage. A player inside a foe's Dominion is weakened and slowed less than a creature.
- **A held guard halves a slash** from in front; **a perfect guard sends it back**.
- **Two slashes meeting** break together, or lock into a timed clash (see
  [Sparring and Lineage]({{ '/progression/lineage/' | relative_url }})).
- Teams and the server's PvP rule are respected.

## Looks, sound and settings

Your blade leaves trails cut the way you swung, your body shows your stage and flares in a fight, full swings land with a short
hit-stop, and techniques name themselves in a banner. Each method has its own colour and sound. In first person, nothing fills
the middle of your screen.

All of it can be tuned in **Magic visual settings** (assign it a key in Minecraft's Controls): blade trails, body aura, impact,
banners, camera motion and reduced flash. See [Combat presentation]({{ '/masters/combat-presentation/' | relative_url }}).
