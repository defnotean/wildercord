# The Arena

## What it is

An **Arena Stone** turns a duel into a ranked bout. Two casters step up to the same stone and fight round it on the
duel's own rules: nobody dies, nothing is dropped, and both are put back as they began. What changes is your
**rating**, and with it your rank on the season's ladder.

## How to get it

Craft it from two Gold Ingots, an Amethyst Shard, an Ender Pearl and five Polished Deepslate:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Gold Ingot | Amethyst Shard | Gold Ingot |
| Polished Deepslate | Ender Pearl | Polished Deepslate |
| Polished Deepslate | Polished Deepslate | Polished Deepslate |

## How to use it

- **Use** the stone to step up. You wait there for up to a minute, and a restart doesn't cut the wait short.
- When a second caster uses the same stone while you're still within a few blocks of it, a three-second countdown
  begins, then the fight.
- Stay within **16 blocks** of the stone. A ring of glyphs marks the edge. Stepping out, logging off or being brought
  down loses the bout. After three minutes it's a draw.
- **Sneak-use** the stone to read the ladder: the season's top five, and where you stand.

You can't step up if you were hurt or fought another player in the last few seconds, the same as a duel.

## Rating and ranks

Everyone starts at **1000**. A bout moves both ratings by Elo: beating a stronger caster gains you more than beating
a weaker one, and a draw pulls the two ratings together. One bout moves a rating by at most 32.

| Rank | From |
|---|---:|
| Apprentice | 0 |
| Adept | 1100 |
| Duellist | 1200 |
| Archmage | 1350 |
| Grandmaster | 1500 |

## Seasons

A season lasts **28 days**. When a new one begins, every rating is pulled halfway back to 1000, and wins and losses
start again from nothing. Only casters who have fought this season show on the ladder.

## Tips

- Ranked bouts don't count towards your plain duel record, and plain duels don't touch your rating.
- Put the stone somewhere open. The fight uses a circle 32 blocks across.
