# Parties


## What it is

A **party** is a group of up to **8 players** who play together. Party members can't hurt each
other by accident, and your helpful magic treats them as friends. Joining a party is always your
choice: nobody can add you without your say.

## How to get it

1. Type `/party invite <player>`. If you aren't in a party yet, this makes one with you as leader.
2. The other player sees your invitation in chat with **[Accept]** and **[Decline]** buttons. They
   can click one, or type `/party accept <your name>` or `/party decline <your name>`.
3. The invitation lasts **60 seconds**.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/party` or `/party list` | Shows your party and its leader |
| `/party invite <player>` | Invites a player (leader only, once you have a party) |
| `/party accept <player>` | Joins the party of the player who invited you |
| `/party decline <player>` | Turns down that player's invitation |
| `/party leave` | Leaves your party |
| `/party kick <member>` | Removes a member, even one who is offline (leader only) |
| `/party disband` | Ends the party for everyone (leader only) |

## How it works

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Most members | 8 |
| An invitation lasts | 60 seconds |
| Wait between invitations | 1 second |
| Invitations waiting at once | up to 8 |

- **The leader** is the only one who can invite, kick and disband. If the leader leaves, the member
  who joined earliest becomes leader.
- **One party at a time.** You can't invite someone who is already in a party, and you must leave
  your party before you accept another. Accepting one invitation cancels any others you had.
- **Your party lasts** through death and logging out, but every party ends when the server restarts.
- **Logging out** cancels any invitations you had waiting.

## What a party does

- **No harm between members.** Your spells, Aura, arrows and other projectiles, pets and summons
  can't hurt party members. That covers effects that land later, too: fire, freezing, harmful
  statuses and knockback are all held back.
- **Helpful magic reaches them.** Heals, shields and buffs land on party members.
- **Allies everywhere.** Party members count as allies for [chorus casting](chorus.md),
  [Runic Hearth](runic-hearth.md) links, the Way Banner of the
  [Ways](../progression/ways.md), Aura clashes, and wards a party member set.
- **Duels still work.** Two party members can agree to a [duel](duels.md),
  and the duel lets them hit each other.

Some things a party doesn't change: the cost of your own spells, healing, and the world's own
dangers like lava or falling.

## Tips

- Making a party before a [Sword Master](../masters/index.md) trial keeps your spells
  and Aura arts from catching each other. Each challenger still joins the trial on their own.
- A party also works on a server with PvP on, so friends can fight side by side without worrying
  about stray hits.
- See [Playing Together](playing-together.md) for the full rules on
  who your spells can harm and help.
