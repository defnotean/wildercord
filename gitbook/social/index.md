# Friends and Rivals

Magic is better with other people. Form a party, trade runes with a villager, take on daily contracts, duel a friend
without anyone dying, cast one great spell together, and teleport to each other.

## The short version

- **Form a party** with `/party invite <player>`. Up to 8 players. Party members can't hurt each other, and your
  helpful spells land on them. See [Parties](playing-together.md#parties).
- **Harmful spells never touch you, your pets, your party or your team.** Other players are only hit when PvP is on, or
  in a duel. By default a spell hits a player for 60% of what it does to a monster.
- **Fight a Sword Master together.** Up to 8 challengers can join one trial. See
  [Sword Masters](../masters/index.md).
- **Share a spell** with a spell code, a [Spell Scroll](playing-together.md#spell-scrolls),
  a [Knot](../fusion-altar/knots.md) or the runes themselves.
- **Hit the same foe together** with different elements for a
  [Unison](playing-together.md#unison) (+50%), or with the same shape for a
  [chorus](chorus.md) (up to twice as strong).
