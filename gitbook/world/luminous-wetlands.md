# Lights Along the Bank

## What it is

**Lantern Newts** live in open, shallow water in swamps and mangrove swamps. They have wide heads, frilled gills and
three nodules that glow at night. Feed one gently and it gives you a **Dusk Pearl**, used for Marshlights and field
notes.

![A Lantern Newt swimming beneath the surface of a shallow pool](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/wetland-newt-day.png)
<span>A Lantern Newt in shallow water.</span>

## How to get it

- **Watch first.** Newts swim to nearby seagrass and browse without eating it up. Seeing one adds it to your Bestiary.
- **Offer Seagrass** while the newt is wet (in water or rain) and calm. It gives **one Dusk Pearl**, then rests for
  **2 minutes**.
- Hurt newts flee and refuse food for a while. Dry newts give nothing.
- Killing a newt gives no pearl. Newts don't breed.

Groups are one or two newts, and no more spawn where three already live.

## How to use it

| Recipe (shapeless) | Result |
|---|---|
| Dusk Pearl + Glass + String + Seagrass | **Marshlight**: a reed lantern with light level 12. Works above or under water |
| Dusk Pearl + Book | **Lights Along the Bank**: Iona's three-page Tideward field notes |

![A submerged Marshlight in a pool at night, with a newt and a dropped Dusk Pearl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/wetland-marshlight-night.png)
<span>A Marshlight under water at night.</span>

**Magic:** **Tidebreath** and **Life** spells make a wet newt's lanterns brighten and trill. It can answer again after
10 seconds. This doesn't heal it or give a pearl.

## Tips and counterplay

- Keep newts calm and wet; rain counts.
- Marshlights draw [Glimmerwings](creatures.md#glimmerwing), which pollinate
  [Moonreed](moonreed-gardens.md).
- Give newts a place to rest with a [Reed Refuge](reed-refuges.md).
- Watch for [Reedback Crabs](reedback-crabs.md) on the same banks.
- Server owners can switch natural newt spawns off.
