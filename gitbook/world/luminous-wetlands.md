# Lights Along the Bank

Look for **Lantern Newts** in open, shallow water in swamps and mangrove swamps. Their wide heads, tiny splayed feet, frilled gills and paddle tails sit low in the water. Three nodules brighten at night and answer certain spells.

![A Lantern Newt swimming beneath the surface of a shallow pool](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/wetland-newt-day.png)
<span>A real in-game newt in the shallow-water test habitat.</span>

## Watch first, gather gently

Newts travel to nearby seagrass and pause to browse. They leave living plants standing. Observing one unlocks its bestiary entry.

Offer **Seagrass** while the newt is wet or in rain and undisturbed. It offers **one Dusk Pearl**, then rests for **two minutes** before another gathering. Hurt newts flee and temporarily refuse feeding. Dry newts cannot offer pearls. Reloading the world preserves the gathering rest.

Newts cannot breed. Killing them gives no pearl: this material comes from a peaceful interaction.

## A small answer to magic

**Tidebreath** and **Life** magic brighten a wet newt's lanterns and provoke a short answering trill. The response has a ten-second rest. It does not heal the animal, produce a pearl or reset gathering. Other spells do not automatically trigger it.

## Weave a Marshlight

| Ingredients | Result | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dusk Pearl + Glass + String + Seagrass | Marshlight | A reed cage around a living pearl; light level 12 |
| Dusk Pearl + Book | Lights Along the Bank | Iona's three-page Tideward field notes |

These are shapeless recipes. Marshlights can be placed above or below water and preserve the water inside them. Their woven ribs and stepped pearl use a custom block model. They use static block lighting with no ticking block entity.

![A submerged Marshlight on the right of a pool at night, with a newt and a dropped Dusk Pearl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/wetland-marshlight-night.png)
<span>Underwater placement, pearl gathering and night lighting in the test habitat.</span>

Natural groups contain one or two newts. Spawning requires a shallow, open-water site and refuses another newt when three already live nearby. The Lantern Newt option and wildlife master switch can disable natural spawning.

[Moonreed Gardens](moonreed-gardens.md) extends the bank with moth pollination, perennial flowers and a Dewglass Lens for inspecting gathering conditions. Larger ecological relationships and exploration sites remain in development.
