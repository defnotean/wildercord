# Rare Meals

Four camp meals are rarer than the rest. Each one needs something you can only get from the wilds' strangest corners,
plus a pinch of **Wayfarer's Saffron** from a caravan. Cook one in a Camp Pot like any other meal. It feeds like a feast and
gives stronger buffs that last longer.

| Meal | Needs | Feeds | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Titan's Roast | Giant's Heart, beef, potato, saffron | 16, saturation 1.4 | Health Boost II 10 min, Resistance I 5 min, Nourished III 10 min |
| Everfrost Sorbet | Everfrost Shard, sweet berries, sugar, saffron | 8, saturation 1.0 | Fire Resistance 10 min, Clarity II 10 min |
| Wildbloom Salad | Wildbloom Petal, golden carrot, sweet berries, saffron | 10, saturation 1.1 | Speed II 8 min, Jump Boost II 8 min, Regeneration I 1 min |
| Starfall Bisque | Star Dust, salmon, glow berries, saffron | 12, saturation 1.2 | Focused II 10 min, Night Vision 10 min, Slow Falling 5 min |

## Getting the ingredients

- **Giant's Heart.** It drops when a [roaming giant](roaming-giants.md) falls, and one time in four you get two.
- **Everfrost Shard, Wildbloom Petal and Star Dust.** You harvest these from the [residues](residues.md) that big spells leave behind.
- **Wayfarer's Saffron.** A caravaneer sells two at a time for 4 emeralds.

## Cooking one

A rare meal is only ever cooked when you choose it. Cycle the pot's cookbook to it. The pot never spends a Giant's Heart or a
shard on its own, even if it's the richest meal you can afford. Rare meals have a purple name and say "A rare meal:" above
their buffs.
