# The Astral Observatory

![The Star-Eater, a floating knot of void ringed with glowing star shards, high under a dome of purple glass, its golden shield circles turning a spell back](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/world-astral-reflect.jpg)
<span>The Star-Eater's shield turns a spell back on its caster.</span>

An observatory on the End's outer islands, with a glass dome over a star map and a telescope aimed at the void. Its
keeper is the **Star-Eater**, a floating knot of void with one great eye, whose shield of star shards turns every
spell back on whoever cast it. To beat it you need spells heavy enough to break the shield, or the nerve to knock its
own shards back into it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dimension** | The End |
| **Biomes** | End highlands and end midlands (the outer islands) |
| **How often** | Roughly one in every square 512 blocks across |
| **Doors** | Void and Arcane, then Time and Wind; the vault's is Void and Life |
| **Boss** | The Star-Eater, 320 health |
| **Its trick** | A shield that turns spells back |
| **Boss's own rune** | [Starmaw](../runes/world.md#starmaw) (Tier IV) |
| **Trophy** | The Astral Lens |
| **Runes found only here** | [Constellation](../runes/world.md#constellation), [Eclipse](../runes/world.md#eclipse) |

## Finding it

It stands on an island's surface, never over the void: its dome needs ground beneath it, and end stone foundations reach
down to the island wherever the ground falls away under the rest. You'll need to reach the outer islands first,
past the dragon's island.

Look for **the dome**: a bowl of purple and black glass rising over the island, with an old copper telescope poking out
through a slit on the far side. At the front, a **terrace** of end stone bricks with a purpur path and obsidian edges
leads between six purpur pillars crowned with end rods. The whole Observatory is about 42 blocks wide and 76 deep.

## The Star-Eater

### Waking it

It opens its eye the first time a player comes within **14 blocks** of the star-altar: *"The stars in the floor go dark.
The Star-Eater opens its eye."* It rises 3 blocks over the altar with its shield already up. Its first spell comes
about 3 seconds later.

### What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health** | 320 |
| **Armour** | 6 |
| **Knockback** | None: it can't be moved |
| **Immune to** | Fire (fire spells' burning damage does nothing), falls, and other monsters |
| **Moves** | It floats in a slow circle about 4.5 blocks out from the altar and 5 blocks up. With its shield broken it sags to about 2 blocks up and slows. It never strays more than 20 blocks from the altar |
| **Casts from** | Up to 32 blocks away |
| **Boss bar** | Purple, darkens the sky. Between spells it shows its shield: *shield up (20 mana)*, *loosing star shards*, or *shield broken!* |
| **Spell power** | 10% more than a Runebound's: 66% of normal on Easy, 88% on Normal, 110% on Hard |
| **Affinities** | Weak to **life** (+50%), resists **void** (half). In the End your void spells hit 20% harder, which doesn't make up for its resistance. See [Creature Affinities](../spellcraft/affinities.md) |

### Its trick: the shard shield

The Star-Eater's shield works exactly like your own Shield spell, turned against you (see
[Shields and Parrying](../spellcraft/shields.md)):

| Phase | Shield strength |
|---|---|
| 1 | 20 mana |
| 2 | 28 mana |
| 3 | 36 mana |

- **A spell that costs more than the shield's strength breaks it**, and goes on through for its full damage.
- **A spell that costs that much or less is stopped, and turned back.** A moment later the Star-Eater casts the harmful
  part of your spell back at you (its shape, modifiers, links and harmful effects; a Self or Touch spell comes back as a
  Bolt, and a spell with nothing harmful left comes back as a plain arcane bolt), at its own power. It tells you why:
  *"Turned back! Its shield stops any spell of 20 mana or less: cast one that costs more, or knock its star shards back
  into it"*.
- **What counts is the spell's full price**, the whole spell, links and all, before any discount. Thrift, your Heart
  Circles and a mana storm lower what you pay, not what the spell weighs against the shield. Since the Cord screen shows
  the cost after your discounts, a spell that shows more than the shield's number always breaks it.
- **Arrows and blades glance off** while the shield is up.
- A spell it turns back doesn't make the shield any stronger or weaker.

**Its star shards break it too.** While its shield is up and it isn't casting, it opens a small circle toward you
(with an amethyst chime; the boss bar says *loosing star shards*) and a second later looses a volley of **star
shards**: 1 in the first phase, 2 in the second, 3 in the third. A volley comes every 6.5 to 8.5 seconds in the first
phase, 5.5 to 7.5 in the second and 4.5 to 6.5 in the third. They home in gently at about 9 blocks a second,
break on walls, and fade after 8 seconds. A shard that hits you does 6 damage on Hard (5 on Normal, 4 on Easy).

**Knock a shard back** and it turns with a bright ring and flies home fast. Three ways:

- **Swing at it.** Swing your weapon (or your fist) as it arrives: it has to be within about 3 blocks of you and in
  front of you while your swing is still going.
- **Shoot it.** A bolt of your own that passes right by it knocks it back (your bolt is spent).
- **Let your Shield take it.** If you have a Shield up when a shard hits you, the Shield turns it back, and your Shield
  isn't used up.

A knocked-back shard takes **16** off the shield. So one shard leaves the first phase's shield at 4 (any spell over 4
mana then breaks it); two break the second phase's outright; three break the third's. If the shield is already down,
a shard that reaches it strikes the Star-Eater for 10 damage instead.

**Broken**, the shield bursts into shards (*"The Star-Eater's shield shatters. Strike now!"*) and the Star-Eater sags,
open, for **9 seconds** in the first phase, **8** in the second and **6.5** in the third. While it's open:

- it takes **25% more damage** from everything, arrows and blades included,
- it **can't cast**, and any spell it was telegraphing is broken off,
- it **throws no shards**.

Then its shield grows back at the phase's full strength.

### Between phases: the eclipse

At two thirds and one third of its health it **eclipses**. For **3 seconds** it can't be hurt; a black core swells at
its eye and everything within 12 blocks is drawn slowly in toward it. Just before the end, everyone within 6 blocks
takes 5 damage on Hard (4 on Normal, 3 on Easy) and is flung away as a new, stronger shield bursts open. The boss bar
says *eclipsing*, and you read:

- the first time: *"The Star-Eater swallows the light. Its shield grows back stronger."*
- the second time: *"The Star-Eater eclipses. Its shield is harder than ever."*

Two Runebound answer it, about 7 blocks from the altar: a **stray** with **Arcane Spark** and a **skeleton** with **Blind
Bolt**. The second time they're Adepts. They vanish when the Star-Eater falls.

No blow carries it past the start of its next phase.

### Strategy

- **Life magic once its shield is down.** It's weak to life (Vinelash, Moonpetal, Venom's hit, Rootsnare) and resists
  void, whatever the End's climate adds to it. A spell heavy enough to break its shield goes through: make its effect
  one of life's. See [Creature Affinities](../spellcraft/affinities.md).
- **Bring a heavy spell for each phase.** You need a spell costing more than 20, then 28, then 36 mana:

  | Spell | Full cost | Breaks the shield in |
  |---|---|---|
  | `Bolt · Explode` | about 23 | phase 1 |
  | `Comet · Explode` | about 34 | phases 1 and 2 |
  | `Bolt · Lightning · Amplify` | about 38 | all three phases |

  Explode and Lightning are Tier III (an Amethyst Cord or better). Longer spells weigh more: everything after a link
  counts.
- **Or break it with its own shards.** In the first phase, knock back one shard and any spell over 4 mana finishes the
  shield. In the second, knock back both shards of a volley; in the third, all three.
- **Keep a Shield up during volleys.** Shards that hit your Shield fly back on their own and don't use it up, and a
  strong enough Shield also stops its spells.
- **Hit hardest while it's open.** For 9, 8 or 6.5 seconds it takes 25% more from everything and can't fight back.
  That's the time for your biggest spells, and for arrows and blades too.
- **Don't feed the shield small spells.** Every spell it stops comes straight back at you.
- **Leave fire at home.** It's immune to burning.
- **Get clear of the eclipse.** Be more than 6 blocks away when it ends, or you're flung across the dome.
- **Kill the keepers.** The skeleton's Blind Bolt leaves you in darkness, which makes shards hard to see.

### When it falls

It dies slowly, over 3 seconds, its darkness imploding into a last burst of starlight. Then:

- **Everyone who fought it** and is still within 96 blocks of the altar gets a **Tier IV rune** they don't know yet,
  straight into their pack.
- **Its killer** gets, the same way:

  | Loot | |
  |---|---|
  | **The Astral Lens** | Its trophy. Hold it up: see in the dark for 3 minutes and fall like starlight for 1 (then it rests for 5) |
  | **[Starmaw](../runes/world.md#starmaw)** | Its own Tier IV rune: 14 damage, and it swallows each of the target's good effects for 3 more damage apiece |
  | **3 Mana Crystals** | |
  | **A Torn Page** | |
  | **A void or arcane rune** | One Tier II or III rune from the gallery chest's list |
  | **A rune of the Observatory** | 50% chance: Constellation or Eclipse |

- **220 experience** on the altar.
- Anything that doesn't fit, or has nobody to take it, is left on the altar, glowing and safe.
- **Starbreaker**: everyone within 64 blocks earns this feat (*"Shattered the Star-Eater's shield and brought it
  down"*), worth **1,500 mana** toward the next Heart Circle, and the advancement *Starbreaker*.
- It counts as a boss for the **7th Heart Circle** for everyone within 96 blocks.

## The Vault

![The Observatory vault: a crying obsidian block with an end rod on it, between two chests on an end stone floor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/world-astral-vault.jpg)

Behind the Void and Life door off the Dome. Each of its **two chests** holds:

| Pool | What |
|---|---|
| **1 Tier IV rune** | One of Domain, Sonic Boom, Wither, Dragon Breath, Summon, Starfall, Hollow, Infinity, Reversal, Stasis or Rewind |
| **1 rune of the Observatory** | [Constellation](../runes/world.md#constellation) or [Eclipse](../runes/world.md#eclipse), equally likely |
| **Sometimes another** | 35% chance of a second rune of the Observatory |
| **2 to 4 of** | 1 to 2 Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, 1 to 4 ender pearls, 2 to 6 gold ingots, 2 to 6 chorus fruit, 2 to 6 amethyst shards, 1 to 3 diamonds, an echo shard, or a shulker shell |

The runes of the Observatory:

- **Constellation** (Tier III shape): joins up to 5 enemies within 12 blocks of you (that you can see: never through a
  wall) in a constellation of light and strikes them all at once.
- **Eclipse** (Tier III void): a dark disc eclipses the point for 5 seconds: enemies beneath it (4 blocks) are blinded,
  take 2 damage a second, and your spells hit them 20% harder.
