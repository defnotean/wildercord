# The Astral Observatory

![The Star-Eater, a floating knot of void ringed with glowing star shards, high under a dome of purple glass, its golden shield circles turning a spell back](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/world-astral-reflect.jpg)

## What it is

An observatory on the End's outer islands, kept by the **Star-Eater**: a floating knot of void with one great eye.
Its shield of star shards turns spells back on whoever cast them. To beat it, cast spells heavy enough to break the
shield, or knock its own shards back into it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biomes** | End highlands and end midlands (the outer islands) |
| **How often** | About one in every square 512 blocks across |
| **Doors** | Void and Arcane, then Time and Wind. The vault's is Void and Life |
| **Boss** | The Star-Eater, 320 health |
| **Its own rune** | [Starmaw](../runes/world.md#starmaw) (Tier IV) |
| **Trophy** | The Astral Lens |
| **Runes found only here** | [Constellation](../runes/world.md#constellation), [Eclipse](../runes/world.md#eclipse) |

## How to find it

Reach the outer islands, past the dragon's island. Look for **a dome** of purple and black glass with a copper
telescope poking out of it, and a terrace of end stone bricks lined with purpur pillars and end rods at the front.

## The Star-Eater

It opens its eye the first time a player comes within **14 blocks** of the star-altar, and rises with its shield
already up. It floats in a slow circle over the altar and never strays more than 20 blocks from it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health** | 320 (phases change at about 213 and 107) |
| **Armour** | 6, and it can't be knocked back |
| **Immune to** | Burning, falls and other monsters |
| **Weak to** | Life (+50%). Resists void (half). See [Creature Affinities](../spellcraft/affinities.md) |

### Its trick: the shard shield

Its shield works like your own Shield spell (see [Shields and Parrying](../spellcraft/shields.md)).
Its strength is **20 mana** in phase 1, **28** in phase 2 and **36** in phase 3.

- **A spell that costs more than the shield breaks it** and hits in full.
- **A spell that costs that much or less is turned back.** A moment later it casts the harmful part of your spell back
  at you.
- **What counts is the spell's full cost** before any discounts. If the Cord screen shows more than the shield's
  number, the spell breaks it.
- **Arrows and blades glance off** while the shield is up.

**Its star shards.** While shielded, it looses volleys of homing star shards: 1 in phase 1, 2 in phase 2, 3 in phase 3.
Knock one back and it flies home, taking **16** off the shield. Three ways to knock one back:

- **Swing at it** as it arrives, within about 3 blocks and in front of you.
- **Shoot it** with a bolt of your own that passes right by it.
- **Let your Shield take it.** The shard turns back, and your Shield isn't used up.

If the shield is already down, a returned shard strikes the Star-Eater instead.

**Broken**, the shield shatters and the Star-Eater sags, open, for **9 seconds** (phase 1), **8** (phase 2) or
**6.5** (phase 3). While open it takes **25% more damage** from everything, including arrows and blades, can't cast,
and throws no shards. Then its shield grows back.

### Its spells

Each spell is named on the boss bar and lands 1.4 seconds after its circle opens.

| Phase | Its spells |
|---|---|
| **1** | Volleying Arcane Spark, Drawing Orb, Blind Bolt |
| **2** | Blackflame Comet, Wide Arcane Rain, Banish Beam, Hex Bolt › Arcane Lance |
| **3** | Starfall Zone, Greater Arcane Lance, Drawing Bolt › Arcane Comet, Hex-Arcane Domain |

**The eclipse.** At each phase change it can't be hurt for **3 seconds**. Everything within 12 blocks is drawn in, then
everyone within 6 blocks is hurt and flung away as a stronger shield bursts open. Two Runebound answer it (a stray with
Arcane Spark and a skeleton with Blind Bolt; Adepts the second time). No single blow skips a phase.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Bring a heavy spell for each phase**: one costing more than 20, then 28, then 36 mana. For example, `Bolt ·
  Lightning · Amplify` breaks the shield in all three phases (Lightning is Tier III, so you need an Amethyst Cord or
  better). Longer spells cost more.
- **Or use its own shards.** In phase 1, knock back one shard and any spell over 4 mana finishes the shield. In phase
  2, knock back both shards of a volley; in phase 3, all three.
- **Keep a Shield up during volleys.** Shards bounce off it for free.
- **Hit hardest while it's open**, with life magic if you can (Vinelash, Moonpetal, Rootsnare).
- **Don't feed it small spells.** Every one comes back at you.
- **Leave fire at home.** It's immune to burning.
- **Be more than 6 blocks away** when the eclipse ends.
- **Kill its keepers.** Blind Bolt leaves you in darkness, which makes shards hard to see.

## When it falls

- **Everyone who fought** and is within 96 blocks of the altar gets a **Tier IV rune** they don't know yet, straight
  into their pack.
- **Its killer** also gets the **Astral Lens** trophy, the **[Starmaw](../runes/world.md#starmaw)** rune,
  3 Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, a void or arcane rune, and a 50% chance of a rune of the Observatory.
- 220 experience drops on the altar. Anything that doesn't fit is left there, safe.
- Everyone within 64 blocks earns the **Starbreaker** feat (1,500 mana toward your next Heart Circle) and advancement.
  It counts as a boss for the 7th Heart Circle.

**The Astral Lens** is kept, not spent: hold it up for 3 minutes of Night Vision and 1 minute of Slow Falling, then it
rests for 5 minutes.

## The vault

![The Observatory vault: a crying obsidian block with an end rod on it, between two chests on an end stone floor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/world-astral-vault.jpg)
<span>The vault, behind the Void and Life door.</span>

Each of its two chests holds a **Tier IV rune**, a **rune of the Observatory** (35% chance of a second), and 2 to 4 of:
Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, ender pearls, gold, chorus fruit, amethyst shards, diamonds, an echo shard, or a shulker
shell.

The runes of the Observatory:

- **Constellation** (Tier III shape): links up to 5 enemies you can see near you with lines of light and strikes them
  all at once.
- **Eclipse** (Tier III void): a dark disc blinds and hurts enemies beneath it, and your spells hit them harder.

Full details are on [Runes of the World](../runes/world.md).
