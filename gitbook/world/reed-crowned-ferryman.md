# Reed-Crowned Ferryman

An old ferry basin in the [Luminous Reedlands](luminous-reedlands.md)
keeps a guardian of woven reeds, wet timber and a hanging lantern. The Tideward call
it the **Reed-Crowned Ferryman**, after the boatman remembered in their crossing
stories. A title shared by many villages does not settle whose name the first
boatman carried.

## The Last Crossing

![The Last Crossing's four pools, open dry paths and green-roofed bell shelter spanning a wooded Reedlands channel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/last-crossing-reedlands.png)

Look for a square basin with four shallow pools, lantern posts and broad dry paths
leading to all four shores. Its entrance shelter holds a **Crossing Bell** and a
two-page instruction book. The supply chest can hold Tide Marker, Shore Sense or
Mooring Call runes. Finding the basin earns **The Last Crossing** and adds a Places
entry to your lore journal.

Use the bell in Survival to begin the optional challenge. The guardian gives you
three seconds before its first attack. Reading the book, visiting the basin or
finishing the peaceful **Drowned Road** story does not begin combat. Guardians
must be enabled, and the world must be above Peaceful difficulty.

The Ferryman stays at the central mooring. Its raft, crown, long oar, suspended
anchor and opening mantle each help announce what it is about to do. The basin's
crossing paths give sword users dry footing close to the raft.

## Read the crossing

| Move | Warning | How to answer | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oar sweep | The oar draws back for 1.6 seconds. A marked arc shows its locked direction and 6.5-block reach. | Move to the sides, behind the locked sweep, or beyond its reach. | 1.2 seconds |
| Anchor cast | A circle marks the landing for 2.2 seconds. | Leave the 2.25-block-radius circle. The anchor keeps its marked place when you move; a hit pulls you a short distance toward the raft. | 1.6 seconds |
| Lantern wake | The mantle opens for 2.8 seconds. Crosses mark danger, and two clear lines bound the safe crossing. | Stand in the empty strip between the lines. The safe crossing stays 3.5 blocks wide through the impact. | 2 seconds |

The markings remain visible with **Minimal** particles. Each attack can hit you
once, and there is time to answer between attacks. The base damage is 6 for the
oar, 5 for the anchor and 7 for the wake, before the world's normal difficulty,
tempering and your defences. Base health is 180 with 4 armour; progression and
nearby challengers can increase its health through normal boss tempering.

At half health the Ferryman changes its move order and rotates later safe
crossings. A tell already in progress keeps the direction and safe area it showed
you. Read each new wake instead of assuming the last crossing will remain safe.

## Leaving and returning

The four paths stay open. Leaving the arena cancels an attack aimed at you. After
ten seconds without an eligible challenger, the guardian heals and returns to its
mooring. Ring the bell again when you are ready. Reloading a saved encounter also
returns it to rest, so no unfinished attack lands as the world reopens.

The bell remembers its guardian. Repeated clicks do not create extra bosses, and
a decorative bell placed elsewhere has no encounter to summon. The basin appears
only in newly generated chunks; world upgrades do not build it over an existing
shore or settlement.

## The second count

When the Ferryman falls, each surviving player still in the arena who dealt an
accepted hit during that attempt receives **80 XP**, a permanent field note, and
one **Compass of the Last Crossing**. The compass points back to that exact basin
in its dimension. It works without placing a lodestone, giving you a landmark to
return to on later river journeys.

Return to a Tideward Reedkeeper after the victory. Once you have heard the
keeper's original four stories, they acknowledge the crossing you completed and
the compass you brought home. Their response remains in your journal's Talk tab.

The bell stays complete after victory and cannot repeat the reward. Leaving an
attempt or reloading it clears that attempt's participation. The peaceful coastal
journal route keeps its own clues and rewards.
