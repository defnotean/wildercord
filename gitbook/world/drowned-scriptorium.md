# The Drowned Scriptorium

![Lightning arcing across the flooded pit of the Drowned Scriptorium, striking a creature wading in it, with sea-lantern pedestals standing out of the water](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/drowned-scriptorium.jpg)
<span>A storm spell in the flood shocks everything wading in it.</span>

## What it is

A dry library on the deep ocean floor. You enter from below, through a pool in the floor. Its keeper, the **Tide
Scribe**, floods its pit and drains it again, over and over. Water conducts: storm magic in the flood shocks everything
wading in it, the Scribe hardest of all.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biomes** | Deep oceans (deep, deep cold, deep lukewarm, deep frozen) |
| **How often** | About one in every square 640 blocks across |
| **Doors** | Frost and Storm, then Life and Wind. The vault's is Storm and Arcane |
| **Boss** | The Tide Scribe, 340 health |
| **Its own rune** | [Tidewrit](../runes/world.md#tidewrit) (Tier IV) |
| **Trophy** | The Drowned Quill |
| **Runes found only here** | [Drowning Word](../runes/world.md#drowning_word), [If Wet](../runes/world.md#if_wet) |

## How to find it

Look for **a lantern-lit path** of prismarine over the sea floor, with sea-lantern pillars either side and a glass
dome behind. Bring a way to breathe: the Tidebreath rune, a Water Breathing potion or a Respiration helmet.

**The hall's guards:** two drowned (Shock Bolt, Frost Wave), a witch (Bubble Bolt) and a drowned **Adept** (Shock
Ring). The witch's Bubble leaves you soaked, and storm spells hit a soaked target with Conduct. Kill the witch first.

**The hall chest** holds 2 to 3 Tier II or III storm and frost runes, plus 2 to 3 of: a Torn Page, Blank Runes, lapis,
prismarine crystals, a Mana Crystal, ink sacs, nautilus shells, or (rarely) a heart of the sea.

## The Tide Scribe

![The Scriptorium's arena from the balcony with the tide out: a dry pit with rings of brick and sea lanterns round the core, six pedestals, and a glass dome above](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/world-tide-pit.jpg)
<span>The pit with the tide out. The pedestals and the balcony stay dry when it floods.</span>

It rises over its core the first time a player comes within **14 blocks**. It floats, can't be knocked back, breathes
under water and never strays more than 16 blocks from the core. It's weak to **life** (+50%) and resists **frost**
(half), though frost still freezes its flood. See [Creature Affinities](../spellcraft/affinities.md).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health** | 340 (phases change at about 227 and 113) |
| **Armour** | 6 |
| **Immune to** | Falls and other monsters |
| **Boss bar** | Blue. Between spells it shows the tide: rising, flooded, going out, or *stranded in the ice!* |

### Its trick: the tide

![The Tide Scribe swimming in its flooded pit, the water two blocks deep, the six pedestals standing dry above it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/world-tide-flood.jpg)
<span>The tide is in.</span>

The pit (within 12 blocks of the core) floods **two blocks deep**, then drains, over and over:

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dry** | 12 seconds | 10 seconds | 8 seconds |
| **Rising** | 2 seconds | 2 seconds | 2 seconds |
| **Flooded** | 15 seconds | 13 seconds | 11 seconds |
| **Draining** | 1.5 seconds | 1.5 seconds | 1.5 seconds |

The balcony and the tops of the six pedestals stay dry. Each drain clears all the water and ice from the pit.

While the pit is flooded:

- **Storm shocks the flood.** A storm spell landing in the water shocks everything in it that your spells can harm,
  including you if you're wading (friends and pets are spared). The Scribe takes **five times** the shock, straight
  through its water armour, and **staggers for 2 seconds**: it can't cast and takes 50% more from other hits.
- **Frost freezes the surface** into ice you can stand on. If the Scribe is swimming within 3.5 blocks of where it
  lands, it's **stranded for 3.5 seconds**: held still, can't cast, and takes **50% more damage**. It can't be
  stranded again for 5 seconds after it breaks free.
- **The water wraps it.** While swimming it takes **half damage** from everything except a shock through the water.
  When the pit is dry, it takes full damage.
- **Its own storm spells shock you** through the flood too, if you're in it.

### Its spells

Each spell is named on the boss bar and lands 1.4 seconds after its circle opens.

| Phase | Its spells |
|---|---|
| **1** | Shock Bolt, Frost Wave, Bubble Bolt |
| **2** | Shock Rain, Coldsnap Ring, Jolt Orb, Bubble Bolt › Shock Bolt |
| **3** | Shock Domain, Lightning Bolt, Freeze Wave, Wide Jolt Rain |

If you're wet (in water, rain, or soaked by a bubble), its storm spells set off **Conduct** on you for extra damage.

**Calling the tide.** At each phase change it can't be hurt for **3 seconds**, and if the pit was dry the tide starts
rising at once. Two Runebound drowned rise in the pit (Shock Bolt and Frost Touch; Adepts the second time). No single
blow skips a phase.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Get out of the water when the tide comes in.** Stand on the balcony or a pedestal. From there, shock the flood
  freely.
- **Shock the flood.** Even a plain `Bolt · Shock` does big damage to it through the water. Pile other damage into the
  2-second stagger.
- **Freeze it where it swims** to strand it, then hit hard. The ice also makes a floor, until the next drain.
- **Never cast storm while wading.** The shock hits you too.
- **Use life magic while it's dry** (Vinelash, Moonpetal). Frost's own hit does only half, so use frost for freezing.
- **Watch the boss bar.** *The tide is rising* gives you about 2 seconds to climb out.
- **Kill the drowned it calls.** Their storm reaches you through the flood.
- **Beware of being soaked** after a Bubble Bolt.

## When it falls

The tide goes out for good. Then:

- **Everyone who fought** and is within 96 blocks of the core gets a **Tier IV rune** they don't know yet, straight into
  their pack.
- **Its killer** also gets the **Drowned Quill** trophy, the **[Tidewrit](../runes/world.md#tidewrit)**
  rune, 3 Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, a storm or frost rune, and a 50% chance of a rune of the Scriptorium.
- 220 experience drops on the core. Anything that doesn't fit is left there, safe.
- Everyone within 64 blocks earns the **Low Tide** feat (1,500 mana toward your next Heart Circle) and advancement. It
  counts as a boss for the 7th Heart Circle, and the feat itself is one of the things the 12th Heart Circle asks for.

**The Drowned Quill** is kept, not spent: hold it up for 3 minutes of Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace, then it
rests for 5 minutes.

## The vault

Each of its two chests holds a **Tier IV rune**, a **rune of the Scriptorium** (35% chance of a second), and 2 to 4 of:
Mana Crystals, a Torn Page, prismarine crystals, gold, ink sacs, diamonds, nautilus shells, an echo shard, or a heart
of the sea.

The runes of the Scriptorium:

- **If Wet** (Tier II link, the more common): the rest of the spell fires only if you're in water or rain.
- **Drowning Word** (Tier III frost): fills the target's lungs with water for a few seconds, and soaks it.

Full details are on [Runes of the World](../runes/world.md).
