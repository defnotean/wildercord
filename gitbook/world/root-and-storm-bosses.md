# Root Guardian and Storm Conductor

Newly generated Rootbound Mazes and Storm Spires now contain an altar for their own boss. Previously generated structures retain their existing blocks.

The **Root Guardian** has a leafy crown, branching arms and an emissive heart behind three visible bindings. Right-click with shears to prune a binding; hits while it burns can also prune. Pruning has a 12-tick cooldown. Removing all bindings opens the heart for six seconds and interrupts its current cast. Bound damage is reduced to 35%, so it cannot become permanently invulnerable. Bindings regrow and reset at phase transitions. Three spell phases use roots, mire, venom and earth attacks.

The **Storm Conductor** is a floating copper gyroscope with rotating terminals and a blue core. Stand within two blocks of the sparking arena rod to ground its charge. This interrupts its cast, grants five seconds of full damage and rotates the active rod. Recharging takes eight seconds. While charged it takes half damage. Rod lookup follows actual positions, including rotated structures. Its phases use arcs, wind waves, prism cuts, rain, thunderclaps, barrages and lances.

Both have existing three-phase safeguards, saved encounter state, boss bars, defeat discoveries, advancements, Tier IV rewards and protected loot. Their existing dungeon relics are in boss loot. Localization, textures, altar variants and loot are generated by the asset pipeline.

## Storm Spire: the copper mountain observatory

Newly generated Storm Spires have an octagonal stone foundation, dark buttresses, aged copper bands and inset cyan glass. A three-flight switchback staircase joins the lower archive, induction-coil room and wind-instrument gallery. You can walk the stairs in Survival; the climb does not require a scaffold or flight spell.

The central glass-insulated conductor joins the floors. Above the sealed chamber, eight ribs hold nested copper rings and a tall lightning crown. The windows and lamps give the tower a recognizable outline at night.

Two guarded hall caches lead to the Storm/Wind rune-seal door. The upper vault retains its two reward chests, Storm Conductor altar and three grounding rods. Its walls, floor and roof remain warded. The crown is architectural decoration; it grants no passive combat bonus.

These layouts appear in newly generated structures. Existing towers keep their placed blocks and rewards. Peaks too close to the world's build ceiling are skipped so the crown is not cut off.
