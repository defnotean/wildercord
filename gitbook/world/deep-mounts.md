# Water and Burrowing Mounts

Two new mounts, tamed and saddled like horses. The **Reefback Turtle** swims you through the sea. The **Delver
Mole** digs you a tunnel through soft ground.

## Taming and riding

Both are tamed the way you tame a horse. Climb on with an empty hand until it stops throwing you off. Feeding a wild
one its food calms it, so it takes fewer tries. Once it's tame, put a saddle on it (sneak and use it to open its
inventory) and ride. Two tame ones fed golden apples or golden carrots raise a young one.

## Reefback Turtle

A sea turtle big enough to carry you, with coral growing on its shell. Look for it on **beaches** and **mangrove
shores**, on sand, mud or gravel.

- **On land** it's slow, a little over half a horse's pace.
- **In water** it swims the way you look. Hold forward and look down to dive or up to climb, up to 75 degrees. Strafe
  to slip sideways at half speed. Hold back to back off slowly. Let go and it holds still in the water.
- It never throws you off under water, and while you ride it in water you have **Conduit Power**, so you can breathe
  and see clearly.
- It eats **kelp**, **dried kelp** and **seagrass**. Each bite heals it a heart and calms a wild one.
- It has 30 to 38 health and a tough shell (6 armour). It drops a turtle scute now and then.

## Delver Mole

A mole the size of a pony, with spade claws and a pink star for a nose. Look for it on **plains**, **meadows** and in
**forests**.

- Saddled and ridden, it **digs as it goes**. Hold forward and it takes a bite out of the ground ahead every fifth of
  a second, a tunnel tall enough for it and you.
- Look straight ahead to tunnel level. Look **well down** (more than 35 degrees) to dig down, and **well up** to dig
  up.
- It digs anything a **shovel** takes that is **no harder than grass**: dirt, grass, sand, gravel, clay, mud, snow,
  soul sand. It stops at stone, ores and anything that holds items.
- What it digs drops, just as if you'd dug it yourself.
- It never digs where you couldn't dig yourself: claims, spawn protection, adventure mode and other mods' protection
  all hold.
- It eats **carrots**, **potatoes** and **beetroot**. Each heals it a heart and calms a wild one.
- It has 26 to 34 health and walks a little slower than a horse.

## Tips

- A reefback is the quickest way across an ocean, and the only way to explore a reef without coming up for air.
- To tunnel down safely with a delver, dig down a few blocks, then look ahead to level out.
- A delver can't dig through stone, so in the hills it tunnels until it meets rock.
