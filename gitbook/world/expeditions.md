# Expeditions and Relics

## What it is

Three small expeditions in the Overworld, each built around one puzzle. Each has hall chests, a few Runebound guards,
a warded vault, and a **relic** in the vault: an off-hand charm that trades one spell bonus for a drawback. They only
appear in newly generated land.

| Expedition | Where | About one per | The puzzle | Vault relic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clockwork Crypt** | Deserts and badlands | 544 blocks | Store your spells in a clock, then release them | Keeper's Hourglass |
| **Living Greenhouse** | Flower, birch and dark forests | 512 blocks | Cleanse its heart, or harvest it | Living Seedpod |
| **Moving Sky Ruin** | Windswept hills, windswept forests, meadows | 608 blocks | Ride shifting stepping stones | Sky Feather |

Each layout comes in a few versions, some with extra side passages or outer paths. The main route is always there.

## How to use them

### Clockwork Crypt

A half-buried clock gallery. Its **Clockwork Control** sets off a trap: a warning circle glows on the floor for 2
seconds, then anyone standing in it takes 4 damage.

- **Cast a harmful spell at the control** to store its damage (up to 12) and pause the trap.
- **Use the control** (or hit it with a time spell) to release the stored damage. After a 1-second warning, it strikes
  monsters around the circle.
- Using the control also pauses the trap for a few seconds.

### Living Greenhouse

A glass conservatory with a broken path over water. Its **Greenhouse Heart** gives you one choice:

- **Cleanse it** with three life spells or bone meal uses. A moss path grows across the gap, and you get glow berries,
  a Torn Page and Regeneration. It also counts toward your [research notebook](../progression/research.md).
- **Harvest it** with shears for 2 Mana Crystals and a Torn Page instead. The path stays broken.

You can only choose once. A rim route lets you cross without life magic.

### Moving Sky Ruin

A quartz ruin floating above the hills, reached by scaffolding. Its **Sky Anchor** shifts three stepping stones
together.

- **Use the anchor** or hit it with a wind spell to shift the stones. They also shift on their own every few seconds
  while someone is within 24 blocks, and carry riders with them.
- If something blocks a stone's next spot, none of them move. Clear the way.
- **Fall below the ruin** and you're caught and set back on the anchor, with no fall damage.

## Relics

Hold a relic in your **off hand** and it changes your spells. It takes up the hand you might use for another casting
item. Relics don't change your Cord's tier or slots.

| Relic | Bonus | Drawback |
|---|---|---|
| **Keeper's Hourglass** | Spells last 30% longer | Spell power -15% |
| **Living Seedpod** | Mana cost -10% | Spell power -15%, cooldowns +10% |
| **Sky Feather** | Cooldowns -15% | Mana cost +15% |

## Tips and counterplay

- **Crypt:** cheap harmful spells fill the clock fast; release it when monsters gather in the gallery.
- **Greenhouse:** cleanse if you want the path and research credit; harvest if you need Mana Crystals.
- **Sky Ruin:** wind spells shift the stones from a distance. Don't fear the fall.
- Pick a relic for your build: the Hourglass suits long-lasting zones and wards, the Seedpod suits mana-hungry casters,
  the Feather suits fast, spammy spells.

For the two bigger expedition bosses, see [Root Guardian and Storm Conductor](root-and-storm-bosses.md).
