# Roadside Sites

![A spruce wayfarer inn with a steep roof and a stone chimney on a hillside, a gravel road leading to its door](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/sites/wayfarer-inn.jpg)

Eight quiet places along the overworld's roads. None of them is guarded, and they turn up a little more often than other sites, so a traveller keeps meeting them. They only appear in newly generated land, on fairly flat dry ground.

Standing in one adds a Places entry to your [lore journal](../progression/lore-journal.md) (H), and finding each one gives an advancement named after the site. The Wayfarer Inn's advancement sits under Ley Line, and the other seven sit under Wayfarer Inn.

## The sites

**Wayfarer Inn.** A spruce inn at a crossroads, in plains, meadows, forests, taiga, savanna, snow and cherry groves. It has beds, a lit hearth, tables and a resident cat. A map and a note hang by the door. The Guest Ledger on the lectern tells you about the other roadside sites. The pantry chest holds food, a map and travel runes.

**Town life (0.12).** Inns generated on 0.12 also have a **bounty board** by the door and three keepers who stay home, can’t be hurt and never leave:

- **The bounty board** offers you one hunt a day: 3 to 8 of one creature, slain within 256 blocks of the board. Click it to read the bounty, click again to take it, and click once the hunt is done to be paid up to 8 emeralds and 5 to 10 reputation. Sneak-click to give a bounty up.
- **Standing** grows with reputation: Stranger, Known (15), Friend (40), Honoured (80). It is yours across every inn and kept through death.
- **The Inn Cook** buys crops and sells the Camp Pot and meals.
- **The Stablemaster** sells leads, hay and saddles. As you rise, they add golden carrots and name tags; then, at Friend, the **Ridgeback Deed** (use it and a tame, saddled Ridgeback Stag of yours is led up) and iron horse armour; then diamond horse armour and golden apples.
- **The Master’s Emissary** sells blank runes and Mana Crystals, then aura shards and experience bottles, and at Honoured one technique scroll a day.

**Watchtower.** A stone-brick tower on windswept hills, savanna plateaus, meadows, plains and taigas. Climb the ladder to the top for the view. The signal fire sits on hay, unlit: light it with flint and steel and its smoke rises high. The watch chest up top holds runes that read the sky, sun, moon and land, plus a spyglass and flint and steel.

**Broken Bridge.** A stone bridge over a creek, with its middle fallen in. Jump the gap, or fall into the deep water and climb out by the ladders on either bank. The toll booth on the far bank keeps the takings and bridge runes such as Icepath, Wind Steps and Surefoot.

**Rune Library.** A small stone library, lined with bookshelves, with a red carpet on one side and a blue one on the other. The Reader's Riddle on the first lectern names a carpet and a shelf column; which one changes from library to library. Count the columns from the door and break the lowest book there to find the reading nook and its chest. The second lectern explains the [Rune Catalog](../spellcraft/rune-catalog.md).

**Standing Stones.** Nine stones in a ring, the tallest at the far side. The book on the dais tells of the first roads, and where the road-makers left a gift for the next walker. Dig under the coarse dirt before the tallest stone.

**Caravan Camp.** Three covered wagons around a campfire, with a wandering trader who stays with the camp and two pack llamas. Two wagons carry chests with emeralds, leads, sometimes a saddle, and riding runes. It is also found in deserts.

**Cartographer's Hut.** A birch hut with a flag on a tall pole. Maps and a compass hang on the walls. The desk chest holds a map that marks another roadside site (or a blank map when none is near), plus sense and bearing runes.

**Vow Circle.** An open shrine with one quartz pillar for each [Circle Vow](../progression/circle-vows.md). The book on the centre lectern lists both sides of every vow, with the `/vow take` command for each, and how to release a vow with `/vow release`, which costs levels.

## Rewards

Every chest holds runes that the Rune Catalog lists under Use: Travel or Use: Exploring, along with maps, books, paper and food. Open the Catalog with Ctrl+B or the Catalog link on the Cord screen. The newer travel runes are also described in the [everyday rune packs](../runes/everyday-packs.md).
