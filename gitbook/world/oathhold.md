# Oathhold, Sovereign Citadel City

**Oathhold** is a colossal walled medieval-fantasy capital city spanning **2,048 × 2,048 blocks** across temperate Overworld lowlands (`/locate structure wildercord:oathhold`).

## Layout & Major Districts

- **Outer Imperial Walls & Moat**: An 8-block-thick crenellated curtain wall (`1,920 × 1,920` blocks inside) ringed by a 28-block water moat, four cardinal twin-tower barbican gatehouses, thirty-two outer bastion fortresses, and eastern harbor sea-gates.
- **Outer Metropolitan Wards**: Four 22-block Imperial Causeways with triumphal arches link cardinal outer districts:
  - **The Great Oathbound Tournament Colosseum** (West Ward)
  - **Grand Imperial Harbor, Lighthouse & Six Sailing Galleons** (East Ward)
  - **Grand King's Square & Imperial Exchange Hall** (South Ward)
  - **High Sovereign Abbey & Cloister Gardens** (North Ward)
  - Dense 48 × 48 urban blocks of half-timbered townhouses, outer canals with arched stone bridges, and courtyard gardens.
- **Lower Sovereign City**: Twin-tower inner curtain walls, arched stone moat bridges, the **Circular Statue Fountain Plaza**, **Central Grand Canal** with three footbridges, open-air **Grand Market Hall** and colorful bazaar stalls, **Great Forge Hall** with twin smoking chimneys, arched **Stone Aqueduct**, **Copper-Roofed Guildhall**, and **The Gilded Lantern Grand Inn**.
- **The Upper Acropolis**: Ascend the grand central staircase to the elevated fortress terrace housing:
  - **The Sovereign Great Hall**: Vaulted gothic stone nave with second-story viewing galleries, amber-gold solar rose window, roaring hearth fireplaces, war-council tables, hanging chandeliers, and gilded Throne Dais guarded by **Oathbound High Knights**.
  - **Hall of the Oathbound**: Fortified knight barracks keep and open sparring courtyard.
  - **Arcanum Grand Library & Observatory**: White calcite hall crowned by a ribbed weathered-copper dome, multi-colored stained glass, and terraced cherry-blossom gardens.
  - **Sovereign Cathedral & Grand Bell-Spire**: Cruciform gothic cathedral rising 98 blocks above the Acropolis.
