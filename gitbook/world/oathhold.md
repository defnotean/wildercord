# Oathhold, Sovereign Citadel City

**Oathhold** is a colossal walled medieval-fantasy capital city spanning **2,048 × 2,048 blocks** across temperate Overworld lowlands (`/locate structure wildercord:oathhold`).

## Layout & Major Districts

- **Outer Imperial Walls & Moat**: An 8-block-thick crenellated curtain wall (`1,920 × 1,920` blocks inside) ringed by a 28-block water moat, four cardinal twin-tower barbican gatehouses, thirty-two outer bastion fortresses, and eastern harbor sea-gates.
- **Outer Metropolitan Wards**: Four 22-block Imperial Causeways with triumphal arches link cardinal outer districts:
  - **The Great Oathbound Tournament Colosseum** (West Ward)
  - **Grand Imperial Harbor, Lighthouse & Six Sailing Galleons** (East Ward)
  - **Grand King's Square & Imperial Exchange Hall** (South Ward)
  - **High Sovereign Abbey & Cloister Gardens** (North Ward)
  - Dense 48 × 48 urban blocks of half-timbered townhouses, outer canals with arched stone bridges, and courtyard gardens.
- **Lower Sovereign City**: Twin-tower inner curtain walls, arched stone moat bridges, the **Circular Statue Fountain Plaza**, **Central Grand Canal** with three footbridges, open-air **Grand Market Hall** and colorful bazaar stalls, **Great Forge Hall** with twin smoking chimneys, arched **Stone Aqueduct**, **Copper-Roofed Guildhall**, and **The Gilded Lantern Grand Inn**.
- **The Upper Acropolis**: Ascend the grand central staircase to the elevated fortress terrace housing:
  - **The Sovereign Great Hall**: Vaulted gothic stone nave with second-story viewing galleries, amber-gold solar rose window, roaring hearth fireplaces, war-council tables, hanging chandeliers, and gilded Throne Dais guarded by **Oathbound High Knights**.
  - **Hall of the Oathbound**: Fortified knight barracks keep and open sparring courtyard.
  - **Arcanum Grand Library & Observatory**: White calcite hall crowned by a ribbed weathered-copper dome, multi-colored stained glass, and terraced cherry-blossom gardens.
  - **Sovereign Cathedral & Grand Bell-Spire**: Cruciform gothic cathedral rising 98 blocks above the Acropolis.

## Citizens, City Law & Neutral Oathbound Knights

- **Over 750 Named Citizens & Merchants**: Oathhold is populated across all districts, guildhalls, boulevards, canal promenades, plazas, and outer residential blocks by articulated humanoid inhabitants spanning 16 professions (Guild Receptionists, Guild Master, Blacksmiths, Herbalists, Bakers, Clothiers, Fishmongers, Scholars, Bullionists, Colosseum Promoters, Builders, Prelates, Watchmen, Street Sweepers, Townsfolk, and Court Patricians). Merchant trades scale with your **Adventurer Guild Rank** (`F–S`).
- **Neutral High Knights & City Law**: Patrolling **Oathbound High Knights** do **not** attack peaceful players — they protect the capital from monsters and maintain order. Committing crimes inside Oathhold walls (**Assault**, **Murder**, **Theft** from district chests/barrels, or **Vandalism**) marks you **Wanted** with an Emerald bounty and alerts nearby Knights. Sneak-speak with a Sanctuary Prelate or Watchman carrying Emeralds — or run `/adventurer payfine` — to pay restitution and restore peaceful standing.

## Adventurer's Guild Requests (Ranks F–S)

Click any **Adventurer's Guild Request Board** (in the Copper Guildhall HQ, Fountain Plaza, Colosseum, Harbor Quay, King's Square, or Abbey) or speak with a **Guild Receptionist** to open the 6-row Request Board Menu (`30` rotating requests across **Copper Rank F** up to **Orichalcum Rank S**):

- **Street & Hall Cleaning**: Brush away spawned cobwebs and overgrown moss patches on city squares and walkways (cleaning quests never trigger vandalism).
- **Herb & Reagent Foraging**: Gather dandelions, poppies, pink petals, sweet berries, glow berries, or wetland moonreed floss and turn them in at the board.
- **Artisan Work Shifts**: Tend anvils, tavern hearths, smithing tables, or Arcanum lecterns to earn honest wages and guild points.
- **District Courier Deliveries**: Carry Guild ledgers and manifests across cardinal wards to any citizen stationed at the destination district.
- **Monster Subjugation**: Cull cellar silverfish, canal drowned, outpost zombies/skeletons, or slumbering dungeon guardians.
- **Sanctioned Colosseum Arena Bouts**: Accept an Arena request (`F/E` up through `S`), step onto the West Colosseum sand or speak to the **Colosseum Promoter**, and defeat summoned Knight challengers without triggering criminal Wanted status.
- Run `/adventurer` anytime to check your Adventurer License Rank, Guild Points, active request, or Law standing (`/adventurer abandon` forfeits a current request).
