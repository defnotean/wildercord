# The bank that raises its claws

A reed crown does not always mark a plant. Reedback Crabs keep the shallow edges of swamps and mangrove swamps: mudstone shells, six walking legs, eyestalks and large bronze claws make them easy to recognize once they move.

![A Reedback Crab raising its hinged claws on a shallow bank](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/reedback-warning.png)

## Read the warning

Unsafe approaches within five blocks attract the crab's attention. It stays near its home bank. Sneak outside its two-block personal space to pass quietly; Creative and Spectator players do not provoke it.

| Phase | Read it | Respond |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | Claws rise and the reed crown trembles for two seconds | Step sideways before it sweeps |
| Sweep | Both claws commit through the original forward lane | Remain outside that lane; it does not turn the sweep after you |
| Recovery | The claws settle for three seconds | Reposition or answer with an attack |

Staying in the lane takes five damage before ordinary damage protection. Walls block a sweep's line of sight. Retreating beyond its territory ends pursuit. The crab harms neither plants nor terrain during its attack.

## Give it a different answer

**Tidebreath** calms a crab for ten seconds. **Wind** impacts interrupt its warning or sweep into recovery. These responses share a separate ten-second rest, so repeated impacts cannot renew the same response immediately. An ordinary attack breaks its calm.

![A Reedback Crab with lowered claws after Tidebreath](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/reedback-water-calm.png)

The spell responses are creature behavior, independent of the spell's other effects. A damaging wind spell still deals its ordinary damage. The crab remains a creature rather than a boss, with ordinary support/control behavior.

## Habitat and useful materials

Natural groups contain one crab. A shallow wet bank, solid footing and open sky are required. Inland dry ground and deep water are refused; two nearby crabs prevent another natural spawn. Peaceful suppresses spawning and aggression. The wildlife master switch and the individual `reedback_crab` setting control natural spawning.

A defeated crab leaves **one to three Clay Balls**. Clay already has uses in fieldcraft, including a [Bastion Poultice](../progression/aura-beasts.md). Observation records the crab in the existing bestiary. There is no automatic breeding or resource production.

Build your [Moonreed garden](moonreed-gardens.md) away from its personal space, or use a careful approach to share the bank.
