# Coffer Mimics

## What it is

A **Coffer Mimic** is an iron-bound oak coffer that is really a monster. It waits at wayside shrines, shut and perfectly
still. Stand near it a while and its lid lifts a little and creaks, and drool runs out from under it. Open it, hit it or
stay right beside it and it wakes up biting. It has 36 health and bites for 5.

Feed one books and it may take to you. A tamed mimic follows you around and works as a chest you can carry.

## Fighting one

- Up close it bites. From further off it shoots its tongue out to pull you in, or crouches with its lid chattering and
  leaps at you.
- If the leap misses, it lands on its back with its legs kicking. Hit it then, while it's soft.
- Hurt it hard and fast and it clamps shut for a moment.
- Left alone, it walks back to its spot and shuts again. Its belly is full of gold.

## Taming one

Use a **book** on it, shut or awake. Any book that goes on a bookshelf works: a book, a book and quill, a written book
or an enchanted book.

| Action | Cost | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Feed an untamed mimic | 1 book | **1 in 3** chance it takes to you (hearts). Otherwise it puffs smoke and eats the book anyway |
| Feed your own mimic | 1 book | Heals 4 health. It refuses books at full health |
| Open it | Nothing | Use it with any item or an empty hand: a 27-slot chest, for its owner only |
| Pet it | Nothing | Sneak and use it with an **empty hand**: its lid lifts and it purrs |
| Stay or follow | Nothing | Sneak and use it while **holding something**: it stays put, or starts following again |

A tamed mimic:

- never bites its owner, and drops whatever it was chasing when it's tamed;
- follows you, and shuts up as a coffer beside you a couple of seconds after you stop;
- turns up beside you if you get more than 16 blocks ahead;
- won't despawn, even in Peaceful;
- stays shut and locked for everyone else. Only its owner can open it, feed it or give it orders.

If your mimic dies, everything inside it spills on the ground where it fell. Its belly stays with it through relogs and
restarts.
