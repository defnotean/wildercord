# Items and crafting

Every item and block Wildercord adds, apart from the runes themselves: what it's for, its recipe, and where to find
it. For the recipe of every rune, see **[Rune Recipes](rune-recipes.md)**.

![The Wildercord tab of the creative menu: four Cords, a Blank Rune, a Mana Crystal, a Torn Page, a Training Dummy, a Scribing Desk, the Tome of the Fifth Page, four foci, twenty staffs and several blocks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-creative-tab.jpg)
<span>The Wildercord tab in the creative menu</span>

Recipes below are for a crafting table. A grid shows a **shaped** recipe (the layout matters; an empty cell is an
empty slot). A list is a **shapeless** recipe (put the items anywhere in the grid). All of them show up in the recipe
book.

## Runes and blanks

### Blank Rune

The plain tablet every rune is made from.

![Crafting grid: top row empty · Cobblestone · empty; middle row Cobblestone · Lapis Lazuli · Cobblestone; bottom row empty · Cobblestone · empty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/blank_rune.png)

4 Cobblestone around 1 Lapis Lazuli makes **4 Blank Runes**.

**Used for:**

- **Every rune recipe:** a Blank Rune plus a few items. See [Rune Recipes](rune-recipes.md).
  Once you've held a Blank Rune, all the rune recipes appear in your recipe book.
- **The Twine Cord** and the **Scribing Desk** (below).
- **Tying Knots** at the [Fusion Altar](../fusion-altar/knots.md).
- **Attunement:** some lands hold a rune of their own. Meditate there with a Blank Rune in hand and it becomes that
  rune. Its tooltip counts how many you've attuned. See [Runes of the World](../world/runes-of-the-world.md).
- **Lightning runes:** drop Blank Runes on the ground within two and a half blocks of a **lightning rod** during a
  thunderstorm. When lightning strikes the rod, every blank lying there becomes a Lightning rune, a whole stack at a
  time. See the Lightning rune on [Storm effects](../runes/effects/storm.md).

**Also from:** a Novice [Runesmith](../social/runesmith.md) (4 for 1 emerald), reaction and
element-cast [contracts](../social/contracts.md) (6 or 4), Archive library chests and the hall
chests of the other dungeons, a closed [rift siege](../world/world-events.md), and some
[advancements](../progression/advancements.md) (8 each).

### Runes

Each rune is its own item, named after its rune (*Fire Rune*). Hold one and right-click to learn it for good; after
that the item is a spare. Runes stack to 16.

- **Recipes:** [Rune Recipes](rune-recipes.md) lists every craftable rune.
- **Fishing:** a treasure catch in open water is often a rune, and magic waters can tangle one in your line on top of
  the catch. See [Fishing](../world/runes-of-the-world.md#fishing).
- **Everything about them:** [Runes](../runes/index.md).
- **Ranked runes** (*Fire Rune II*) shimmer and come from the [Fusion Altar](../fusion-altar/ranks.md).
- **Spares** can be ranked up or combined at the Fusion Altar, or sold and swapped at a
  [Runesmith](../social/runesmith.md).
- A rune from an add-on mod that's no longer installed shows as a **Silent Rune**. It stays safe and wakes up again
  when the add-on comes back.

### Knot

A whole spell tied into one rune at the [Fusion Altar](../fusion-altar/knots.md), from a Blank
Rune, a piece of String and one of your spells. Named after the spell (*Knot: Ember Fan*), it takes one socket, costs
10% less mana than the runes inside, and anyone can learn it with a right-click. Knots stack to 16.

## Cords

A Cord is worn in the **Cord slot above your off-hand** (open your inventory with E). Each Cord is crafted from the one
before it, so you build them up in order. Cords don't stack. See [Cords](../spellcraft/cords.md)
for everything about them.

| Cord | Sockets per spell | Spells | Runes up to | Mana | Regenerates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Twine Cord** | 3 | 1 | Tier I | 100 | 5 a second |
| **Copper Cord** | 5 | 2 | Tier II | 150 | 6 a second |
| **Amethyst Cord** | 8 | 3 | Tier III | 225 | 7 a second |
| **Echo Cord** | 12 | 4 | Tier IV | 300 | 8 a second |

## Mana

### Mana Crystal

![Crafting grid: top row Lapis Lazuli · Amethyst Shard · Lapis Lazuli; middle row Amethyst Shard · Diamond · Amethyst Shard; bottom row Lapis Lazuli · Amethyst Shard · Lapis Lazuli](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/mana_crystal.png)

4 Lapis Lazuli, 4 Amethyst Shards and a Diamond make **1 Mana Crystal**. Crystals stack to 16.

**Use it:** right-click to absorb it: **+10 max mana, forever**. Up to **10** crystals count (+100 max mana); after
that the game tells you your mana can't grow further with crystals, and the crystal isn't used. See
[Mana](../progression/mana.md).

**Used for:** every Tier III rune recipe (with a Diamond), the Wellstone, every staff and every focus.

**Also from:**

|---|---|
| Ancient city chests | 30% |
| Stronghold library chests | 30% |
| End city treasure chests | 25% |
| Trial chamber vaults | 25% |
| Bastion treasure chests | 25% |
| Buried treasure | 20% |
| Woodland mansion chests | 20% |
| Archive library and vault chests, and the halls and vaults of the other dungeons | sometimes |
| The Archivist, the Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater, the Tide Scribe | 3 each |
| The Riftcaller | 1 to 3 |
| A Fallen Star | 1 |
| A closed rift siege | sometimes 1 |

| Some [advancements](../progression/advancements.md) | 1 or 3 |

## Paper magic

### Spell Scroll

One of your spells, written on paper, that **anyone** can cast **once**, with or without a Cord, whether or not they
know its runes.

- **Made:** on the Cord screen, press **Inscribe a scroll** with the spell selected. It takes a sheet of **Paper**, an
  **Ink Sac** (or Glow Ink Sac) and **twice what the spell costs you to cast**.
- **Named** after its spell (*Scroll of Splitting Frost Bolt*); it shimmers, and its tooltip shows the spell's
  readout and who inscribed it.
- **Used:** right-click to cast it; the scroll is used up.
- Scrolls stack to 16.

Everything about scrolls: [Spell Scrolls](../social/playing-together.md#spell-scrolls).

### Torn Page

A page torn from an old grimoire. **Right-click to read it:** you learn the riddle of a
[secret spell](../spellcraft/secret-spells.md) you haven't found yet, and the riddle goes into your
Grimoire. In the Overworld, the page's margin also sketches the way to the nearest **Archive**: *an Archive lies about
650 blocks to the northwest*.

If there's no riddle left you haven't found or been given, the page says so and isn't used up. Pages stack to 16.

**Found:**

| Where | Chance or amount |
|---|---|
| Stronghold library chests | 45% |
| Ancient city chests | 25% |
| Woodland mansion chests | 25% |
| Trial chamber vaults | 15% |
| Dungeon (monster spawner) chests | 12% |
| Desert pyramid chests | 12% |
| Fishing in open water | about 1 treasure catch in 11 (see [Fishing](../world/runes-of-the-world.md#fishing)) |
| Archive library and vault chests, and the halls and vaults of the other dungeons | often |
| The Archivist | 2 |
| The Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater, the Tide Scribe | 1 each |
| A [Runebound](../world/runebound.md) | 6% (a Runebound Adept: 20%) |
| A Runesmith | Journeyman: 14 emeralds and a Book. Expert: 16 emeralds. |

## Blocks you can craft

### Fusion Altar

![Crafting grid: top row Deepslate Tiles · Block of Amethyst · Deepslate Tiles; middle row Block of Amethyst · Lodestone · Block of Amethyst; bottom row Deepslate Tiles · Block of Amethyst · Deepslate Tiles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/fusion_altar.png)

Ranks runes up, fuses two effects into a new one, and ties spells into Knots. Mine it with a pickaxe. See
[The Fusion Altar](../fusion-altar/index.md).

### Scribing Desk

![Crafting grid: top row Wooden Slab · Wooden Slab · Wooden Slab; middle row Blank Rune · Bookshelf · Blank Rune; bottom row empty · Wooden Slab · empty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/scribing_desk.png)

Any wooden slabs. A villager without a job becomes a [Runesmith](../social/runesmith.md) at it, and
right-clicking it opens your [daily contracts](../social/contracts.md). Best mined with an axe.

### Wellstone

![Crafting grid: top row Polished Deepslate · Block of Amethyst · Polished Deepslate; middle row Block of Amethyst · Mana Crystal · Block of Amethyst; bottom row Deepslate Tiles · Deepslate Tiles · Deepslate Tiles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/wellstone.png)

A mana well. Set it on a **ley line** in the Overworld and it **wakes**: circles turn on the ground around it, and
everyone within **12 blocks** regenerates mana **50% faster**. Off a ley line it only glows faintly. Wild wisps rise
out of its mana at any hour, and a familiar told to stay near it waits there. Mine it with a pickaxe. See
[Ley Lines and the Wellstone](../progression/ley-lines.md).

### Training Dummy

![Crafting grid: top row empty · White Wool · empty; middle row Stick · Hay Bale · Stick; bottom row empty · Smooth Stone Slab · empty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/training_dummy.png)

A straw dummy for trying your spells. Use it on a block to set it up facing you. It never dies: every hit floats up as
a number, and its name shows your damage per second. **Sneak and punch it** to pick it back up. Dummies stack to 16.
Hits on a dummy don't count for contracts. See [Training Dummy](../progression/training-dummy.md).

the **Backpack slot** on the gear tray and press `B`. Whatever is inside stays inside, wherever the backpack goes.

## Familiars

### Wisp Lantern

![Crafting grid: top row empty · Amethyst Shard · empty; middle row Glass Pane · Lantern · Glass Pane; bottom row empty · Amethyst Shard · empty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/wisp_lantern.png)

Where your familiars rest. **Use** it to send the familiar that's out home, or to call it out again; **sneak and use**

so any lantern works. See [Familiars](../companions/familiars.md#the-wisp-lantern).

### Greater staffs

A **Greater Fire Staff** and so on, one for each element. Can't be crafted: they drop from the Archivist (50%, any
element), the Ender Dragon (50%: Void, Arcane or Time), the Wither (35%: Void or Blood), the Warden (35%: Earth or
Storm) and Elder Guardians (35%: Frost or Life), and are found in Archive vaults. Effects of its element +35% power.

### Tome of the Fifth Page

Tome slot (or off-hand): a fifth spell to thread and cast while it counts. Can't be crafted: found in Archive vaults and libraries
and in stronghold libraries.

## Trophies

Each of the three dimension dungeons' bosses always drops its trophy. A trophy is kept, not spent: **hold it up**
(right-click) for a boon, then it rests for **5 minutes** before it works again. Trophies don't stack, and fire and
lava can't destroy them.

| Trophy | From | Hold it up for |
|---|---|---|
| **Cinder Heart** | The Cinder Warden, in the [Ember Sanctum](../world/ember-sanctum.md) | Fire Resistance for 3 minutes |
| **Astral Lens** | The Star-Eater, in the [Astral Observatory](../world/astral-observatory.md) | Night Vision for 3 minutes and Slow Falling for 1 minute |
| **Drowned Quill** | The Tide Scribe, in the [Drowned Scriptorium](../world/drowned-scriptorium.md) | Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace for 3 minutes |

## Blocks found only in the world

These blocks are part of Wildercord's dungeons and events. They can't be broken or collected in survival. The first
three appear in the creative menu for builders.

| Block | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Rune Seal** | The Archive's doors | Each is carved with an element's glyph. Strike it with a spell of that element and every block of that element in the door lights up. Light every element in the door within ten seconds and the door dissolves. See [The Archive](../world/archive.md). |
| **Archive Lectern** | The heart of the Archive | The Archivist rises from it the first time a player comes near. It goes quiet once the Archivist falls (if its Archivist is lost without being killed, it wakes a new one a few minutes later). |
| **Dungeon Altar** | The heart of each dimension dungeon's arena | The dungeon's boss rises from it the first time a player comes near: the Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater or the Tide Scribe. It goes quiet once the boss falls. |
| **Fallen Star** | Where a star comes down in a [starfall](../world/world-events.md) | A glowing lump of starstone that can't be mined, and that even the Wither can't break. Once its guards are beaten, use it to break it open for a rune and a Mana Crystal. It crumbles once looted, or fades after twenty minutes. It has no item at all. |
