# Backpacks


A **backpack** is extra room you carry with you. Everything in it stays in it wherever the backpack goes: in your
hand, on your back, in a chest, on the ground, or dropped when you die.


---

## The three backpacks

| Backpack | Holds | Made from |
|---|---|---|
| **Backpack** | 2 rows, 18 slots | leather, string and a chest |
| **Reinforced Backpack** | 3 rows, 27 slots (a chest's worth) | a Backpack, a chest, iron and leather |
| **Runewoven Backpack** | 4 rows, 36 slots (as much as your whole inventory) | a Reinforced Backpack, a Mana Crystal, an Echo Shard, amethyst and Blank Runes |

Each one holds a single backpack per slot (they don't stack). Its tooltip says how full it is, lists the first few
things in it, and reminds you how to open it.

## Opening a backpack

- **In your hand:** use it (right-click), from either hand.
- **Worn:** press `B` (**Open backpack**, under Wildercord in the game's controls). This works in the world and
  in your inventory.

The backpack opens like a chest of its size, above your inventory. Move things in and out as you would with a chest:
click, drag, or shift-click to send a stack across. `B`, `E` or `Esc` closes it again. It opens and closes with a
rustle of leather (and a chime of amethyst for the Runewoven one).

**An open backpack stays where it is.** If you opened it from your hotbar, its slot is shaded and locked until you
close it: you can't pick it up, throw it, or swap it out with a number key or `F`. Hover it to see a reminder.

## The Backpack slot

![The survival inventory with a tray on top holding a staff, a focus, the tome and, in the fourth slot, a backpack](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-backpack-slot.jpg)
<span>The Backpack slot, the fourth on the gear tray</span>

Your inventory (`E`) has a **Backpack slot**, the fourth slot on the small tray on top of the window, after the
[gear slots](../gear.md). In creative it's in the Survival Inventory tab, beside the gear slots.
It holds one backpack. Click one into it, or **shift-click** a backpack and it goes there by itself (shift-click it
again to take it off). Hover the empty slot to see what it's for.

A backpack in the slot is **worn**: `B` opens it, and it shows on your back.

## Wearing it

![A player seen from behind wearing a red Reinforced Backpack with a Fire Staff strapped across it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-backpack-worn.jpg)
<span>A dyed Reinforced Backpack, with a staff strapped over it</span>

Everyone around you sees the backpack on your back, with its straps over your shoulders, in its own colour or the one
you dyed it:

- a **staff** in your Staff slot is strapped across the outside of the pack;
- it sits over a **chestplate** or a **cape**;
- with **elytra** it's worn over the folded wings, and while you glide, with the wings spread, it tucks in against
  your back between them;
- it isn't shown while you're **invisible** (or to anyone, while you're a spectator).

Only which backpack you wear shows to others: what's inside is yours alone.

## What can't go inside

Nothing that holds items goes in a backpack, so storage can't be nested inside storage:

- no other backpack;
- no shulker box, empty or full;
- no bundle;
- no chest (or other block) picked up with its contents still in it. An empty chest or furnace is fine.

A backpack doesn't go inside a shulker box or a bundle either, and a hopper won't feed one into a shulker box. It can
go in a chest, a barrel, an ender chest, or anywhere else.

## Keeping what's inside

- **Dropped** (with `Q`), a backpack lies on the ground with everything in it. Pick it up and it's all still there.
- **In a chest or a hopper,** it's moved whole. A dispenser throws it out whole.
- **When you die,** a backpack in your inventory or your Backpack slot drops with everything in it (unless the server
  keeps inventory, when it stays with you, as the rest of your inventory does).
- **Burnt up** (in lava or fire, or blown up), a Backpack or Reinforced Backpack spills what it held on the ground,
  as a shulker box does. The Runewoven Backpack doesn't burn.
- **If you leave the game** with a backpack open, what you'd moved into it is still there when you come back.

## Dyeing

Every backpack dyes like leather armour: put it in a crafting grid with one or more dyes to mix a colour. What's inside
stays inside. To wash the dye out, use the backpack on a cauldron with water in it.
