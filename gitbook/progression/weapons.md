# Swordsman's Weapons


Three weapons are made for the swordsman's path. Each is a full 3D weapon in your hand and gives one gift to your aura.

| Weapon | What it is | How to get it | Its gift |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Oathkeeper**](#oathkeeper) | A longsword | Draw it from the Sleeping Blade's stone | Cheaper guard, stronger perfect guards |
| [**Bulwark Maul**](#bulwark-maul) | An axe | Forge a diamond or netherite axe | Aura Guard costs 40% less |
| [**Skyrend Glaive**](#skyrend-glaive) | A spear | Forge a diamond or netherite spear | Aura Slash 60% stronger, longer and wider |


## Oathkeeper

### What it is

A fullered steel longsword with a dark wrapped grip, gilt quillons and green oath-stones in the guard and pommel. It hits like a
diamond sword: **7 attack damage** and **1.6 attacks per second**. It is fire resistant, so lava won't destroy it.

![Oathkeeper drawn and held in first person beside the cleared socket](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/oathkeeper-in-hand.png)

### How to get it

There is no recipe. Oathkeeper rests in the stone of a [Sleeping Blade](sleeping-blade.md)
landmark. Reach **Form**, have no bonded blade, empty your main hand, kneel by the stone and use it. Hold still for six
seconds. The blade comes out already [bonded](bonded-blade.md) to you.

### How it fights

Its gifts work only while it is **bonded to you** and **in your main hand**:

| When you... | Oathkeeper... |
|---|---|
| Raise or hold Aura Guard | Makes it cost **15% less** aura |
| Land a perfect guard | Builds **30% more** momentum, with its own sound and a cut in the ground |
| Land ordinary hits | Builds **20% less** momentum |

It adds no flat damage and does not widen the perfect-guard window. It rewards waiting and answering, not chasing.

## Bulwark Maul

### What it is

An iron-bound maul with a head of riven stone and glowing geodes. It is still the axe it was forged from, with the same damage,
speed, enchantments and durability.

![A player holding the Bulwark Maul, aura glowing round it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/bulwark-maul-in-hand.jpg)

### How to get it

Forge it at a **smithing table**: an **Aura Shard** (dropped by
[fallen knights](../world/monsters.md#fallen-knight)), a **diamond or netherite axe**, and **Geode Grit**
(a [reagent](../world/residues.md#reagent-uses) from riven stone and geode crawlers).

<div>
![Bulwark Maul (diamond)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/bulwark_maul_from_diamond.png)
![Bulwark Maul (netherite)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/bulwark_maul_from_netherite.png)
</div>

### How it fights

While it is in your main hand, your **Aura Guard** (sneak and press the Aura key, **Z** by default) costs **40% less** aura,
raised and held. Like any axe it can also break a raised guard.

## Skyrend Glaive

### What it is

A storm-lacquered glaive with a crescent blade and a lightning-lit edge. It is still the spear it was forged from, with the same
attacks, enchantments and durability.

![A duelist holding the Skyrend Glaive side on, its edge lit with storm aura](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/skyrend-glaive-in-hand.jpg)

### How to get it

Forge it at a **smithing table**: an **Aura Shard**, a **diamond or netherite spear**, and a **Fulgurite Shard** (the storm
[reagent](../world/residues.md#reagent-uses)).

<div>
![Skyrend Glaive (diamond)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/skyrend_glaive_from_diamond.png)
![Skyrend Glaive (netherite)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/skyrend_glaive_from_netherite.png)
</div>

### How it fights

While it is in your main hand, your **Aura Slash** (tap the Aura key, from Edge) hits **60% harder**, flies **30% further
and wider**, and cuts **two more foes**. Against players the extra damage is held to the same cap as other aura bonuses.

## Tips

- All three can be [bonded](bonded-blade.md). Oathkeeper comes bonded; the maul and glaive
  bond at a ley crossing like any axe or spear.
- A forging keeps everything the weapon had. Enchant and repair it as normal. The tooltip shows the gift and the base weapon.
- Pick by how you fight. The maul suits players who guard a lot. The glaive suits players who open with slashes. Oathkeeper suits
  players who wait for the perfect guard.
- A server can tune or switch off the maul and glaive gifts (`aura_world` settings `forged_gear`, `skyrend_slash`,
  `bulwark_guard_cost`). The weapons stay either way. The numbers above are the defaults.
- The third forging, the Lumenedge sword, is on the [Aura page](aura.md#aura-forged-gear).
