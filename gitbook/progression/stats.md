# Stats

Type `/stats` to see how far you've come, or `/stats <player>` to see someone else. It lists:

- **Time in this world.**
- **Heart Circles** formed, and any ascensions past the 20th.
- Your **breathing method** and Aura stage.
- **Duelists beaten** and **Sword Master trials won**.
- How much of the Grimoire's **field guide** you've filled.
- What your **[codex](../world/codex.md)** has tallied: creatures slain and kinds mastered.
- **Feats** earned, and **deaths**.

A road you haven't started isn't listed. A swordsman with no Heart Circle won't see a Heart Circles line, and a mage with no
breathing method won't see an Aura line. Your [lore journal](lore-journal.md) keeps the story behind the numbers.
