# The Marchkeeper Battlefields

Old barricades, shelter gardens and retreat cairns occasionally interrupt the open country. They preserve the history of the Marchkeepers: soldiers who became protectors, then guides. Search plains, sunflower plains, meadows, savannas and snowy plains. Placement is rare and rejects flooded or steep ground.

## Listen to a memorial

Find the weathered stone with crossed blades at the centre. Hold your blade, sneak and stay still until you enter the **breathing stance**, then **use the memorial**. Keep the stance beside it for eight seconds. Standing, moving away, losing your blade, a blocked view, or losing the memorial interrupts the memory.

The first discovery of each tradition gives **its illustrated field book** and teaches **a technique intent**. You can learn the part before Edge; writing and using your own techniques still require their usual stage. Each tradition teaches once per player, across every memorial of that tradition. Another player can recover their own lesson from the same site. Ordinary placed markers have no old-ground memory.

| Tradition | Landmark | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Line That Broke | Staggered barricades with an open breach | **Sunder**: break a foe's footing |
| The Last Shelter | A ruined shelter wall and its recovering garden | **Bind**: hold a foe instead of pursuing them |
| The Returned Step | Paired cairns and a broken watchtower | **Echo**: leave an answering stroke |

The three field books connect the histories and point toward the other traditions. Supply chests contain modest surviving materials. These are outdoor landmarks, not sealed boss vaults; their ruins can be altered.

## Return visits

Use a remembered memorial to reread all three pages in chat. This does not produce another book, technique lesson or discovery reward. The actual book uses the normal reading screen and remains a portable record. Its cover matches the tradition's memorial crest.

Servers can disable new battlefield generation and memory interactions with `aura_world.battlefields`. Existing ruins remain in the world. Biomes, spacing and separation are data-driven under the `old_battlefield` structure and `old_battlefields` structure set.
