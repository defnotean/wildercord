# Ways: where the path forks


## What it is

At the [Aura](aura.md) Edge breakthrough your path forks into four **Ways**. Your Way gives
you a **node** at Edge, Form and Sovereign. Each node is a **passive** plus a **change to a technique you already know** (the
slash, guard, step, Intent, finishers, awakening or Dominion). A Way makes you different rather than simply stronger.

![The same scene by day and by night: four rippling cloth standards on bronze poles, with red sword, blue shield, violet crescent and gold rallying crests](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-crossroads.jpg)
<span>The crossroads, just after the Edge breakthrough, by day and by night.</span>


## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | At the **Edge breakthrough**. Already past Edge with no Way? Hold the **breathing stance** a few seconds |
| **How** | **Strike a standard** to lean toward its Way; **strike it again** to walk it |
| **The Ways** | **Blade** (offence), **Bulwark** (defence), **Shadowstep** (movement), **Banner** (together) |
| **Nodes** | One at **Edge**, **Form** and **Sovereign**. A first choice gives you every node you've reached at once |
| **Changing** | Burn a **Crossroads Incense** at a ley crossing |
| **Kept** | Through death |
| **See it** | The **Way** tab on the Aura page |

## How to get it

### The crossroads

A couple of seconds after the Edge breakthrough, four cloth standards rise in an arc in front of you: the **Blade** (a sword on
red), the **Bulwark** (a shield on blue), the **Shadowstep** (a crescent on violet) and the **Banner** (a rallying crest on gold).
They stand for a minute. Walk more than a dozen blocks away, die or change world and they fade, but you can call them again
(below).

1. Look at a standard: its name and three nodes show above your hotbar.
2. **Strike it** with your blade to lean toward it. It flares and the others dim. Striking another moves your lean.
3. **Strike the leaned standard again** within eight seconds to **walk its Way**. Your chat lists its nodes.

Take your time: the **Way** tab on the Aura page shows every node in full, even while the crossroads stands.

![Through the player's own eyes: the four crested cloth standards in a row ahead, the blue shield standard under the crosshair, and above the hotbar the words Way of the Bulwark, Strike to lean toward it, and the names of its three nodes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-first-person.jpg)
<span>Looking at a standard names its Way and its three nodes.</span>

### Already past Edge?

If you're at Edge or above with no Way, **hold the breathing stance** (sneak and stand still, blade in hand) for about three
seconds after it settles and the crossroads rises round you. It needs open ground. A first choice is free whenever you make it,
and gives you **every node you've reached at once**. It won't rise during a stillness trial.

## How to use it

Your nodes work on their own once reached. Each table lists the stage, the passive and the technique it changes.

### Way of the Blade
*Strike first, strike through, and finish what you start.*

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Keen Edge** | Clean hits build **momentum a third faster** | **Aura Slash pierces**: it cuts through a held guard, reaches **ten** foes instead of six, and cuts through another crescent, flying on at half strength |
| **Form** | **Cascade** | **Finishers add two fifths more** (a player's cap still holds) | **Finishers cascade**: a finisher opens the nearest creature within five blocks whose stance is half worn or more (never a player or a boss) |
| **Sovereign** | **Storm of Edges** | Each finisher feeds your awakening **two seconds** instead of one, up to eight | While awakened, Aura Slash **costs nothing** and is **ready again in a second** |

Wear a crowd down evenly with sweeps and arts, then finish one: the cascade carries on from foe to foe.

![Left: a player's crescent of orange aura cutting through one husk and flying on to the husk behind it. Right: a finisher landing on a husk in a ring of light, and a sweep of aura carrying on to open a second husk beside it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-blade.jpg)
<span>Keen Edge's slash cutting through, and a Cascade.</span>

### Way of the Bulwark
*Stand where you are, and let nothing through.*

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Wide Guard** | Your **stance takes two fifths less wear**, and hits knock **a third less momentum** off you | **Aura Guard covers every side**, its perfect moment lasts **half again as long**, a held guard **throws a third of each caught blow back**, and it **turns shots back** (for a little aura each) |
| **Form** | **Living Wall** | **Aura armour takes a third** of each blow instead of a quarter, and costs a fifth less | **Intent challenges**: creatures near you hunting your allies turn on you, and a creature that strikes your raised guard **staggers** |
| **Sovereign** | **Unbroken** | While awakened, **nothing breaks your stance** and foes deal you **a fifth less** (less against players); spent, **you aren't slowed** | **Dominion is a bastion**: shots from outside are **turned back at its edge**, and creatures inside are slowed more |

Hold the guard more than you normally would: it covers your back, pays you back for every blow and staggers what hits it.

![A player standing in a Dominion's circle of runes with a ring of pale blue light at its rim, a skeleton beyond it, and three of its arrows turned back in flashes of light at the Dominion's edge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-bulwark.jpg)
<span>A bastion: shots turned back at its edge.</span>

### Way of the Shadowstep
*Be where the blow isn't, and strike where the eye isn't.*

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Slip** | Blows and arts **from behind** wear stance **half again as fast** | A perfect guard against a blow **slips you behind** the striker, facing its back |
| **Form** | **Afterimage** | For **two seconds after an Aura Step** every blow counts as from behind | **Aura Step leaves an afterimage** that **strikes** foes beside it a moment later (a little over half a blow each) |
| **Sovereign** | **Thousand Shadows** | A **finisher from behind readies your Aura Step** at once | While awakened, Aura Step **costs nothing**, is **ready again in a second**, and its afterimage **strikes twice** |

Play it as a loop: step through a pack, strike from behind, break the stance, finish from behind, and step again.

![Left: a player who has just slipped round a husk's blow to stand at its back, a violet arc on the ground tracing the way they went and a flash where the blow met their guard. Right: a violet afterimage of the player left standing between two husks, striking them as the player stands clear](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-shadowstep.jpg)
<span>A slip behind its striker, and an afterimage striking where the swordsman stepped from.</span>

### Way of the Banner
*No one fights alone while your banner stands.*

| Stage | Node | Passive | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | **Battle Cry** | **A third of the momentum you build** is built by allied swordsmen near you too | **Finishers rally**: you and allies near you (pets too) take **a sixth less** from foes for five seconds; allied swordsmen build momentum and share half the aura it gave back |
| **Form** | **Rallying Presence** | **A quarter of the aura you gather** flows to allied swordsmen near you | **Intent steadies**: while it presses on a foe, you and allies in its reach take **a tenth less** |
| **Sovereign** | **Shelter** | Allied swordsmen near you lose **a third less momentum** to hits, and **your awakening holds their momentum** at its second tier | **Dominion shelters**: you and allies inside take **a fifth less**, allied swordsmen gather aura there, and their momentum doesn't ebb |

"Near" is **twelve blocks**. Alone, the Banner is the quietest Way; with friends, it's the strongest.

![Left: a player landing a finisher on a rival, a gold pennant of light over their head, a ring of gold at their feet and a thread of gold running to an ally ringed in gold. Right: the player's Dominion, the ally inside it in a ring of gold while the rival stands in the same circle without one](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-banner.jpg)
<span>A rallying cry, and a sheltering Dominion.</span>

### A Way's part

Each Way also **lends** you a part for [techniques of your own](techniques.md): the Blade
**Pierce**, the Bulwark **Ward**, the Shadowstep **Afterimage** and the Banner **Rally**. Ward and Rally are found nowhere else.
Leave the Way and a technique written with its part rests until you walk the Way again.

### Who counts as an ally

The Banner (and Living Wall's challenge) only reaches **allies**: your scoreboard teammates, or, with no teams, players who
couldn't harm each other. Players who could fight, or who are duelling each other, are never allies. Your pets, and your
team's, share in cries, presence and shelter.

### Changing your Way

Changing costs three things at once:

- **A Crossroads Incense**: two **Aura Shards** (a fallen knight's), an **amethyst shard** and **blaze powder**, crafted together.
- **A place of power**: hold it in use for two seconds at the heart of a **ley crossing**. Anywhere else it won't catch, and
  isn't used up.
- **Settling**: your old Way goes dark and the crossroads rises to choose again. The new Way's **Edge node is yours at once**,
  its **Form node wakes after 120 experience** and its **Sovereign node after 240**, earned by fighting as usual.

You can walk any Way again later, at the same price.

## The Way tab

The Aura page (the Aura badge on the Cord screen, `K`) has a **Way** tab. It shows a tree with a column per Way and a row per
stage. Your nodes are framed in gold when **in force**, half lit while **waking** after a change, and outlined while still
**upcoming**; other Ways' nodes are dark. Click or hover a node to read it in full.

![The Aura page's Way tab: Your Way: Way of the Banner and its creed, then four columns of emblems (a sword, a shield, a crescent and a pennant) over three rows, Edge, Form and Sovereign, the Banner's column framed in gold and lit, and under it Shelter, Sovereign, in force, with its passive and what it changes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/ways-page.jpg)

## Tips and counterplay

- **The Blade's** finishers against a player stay under every finisher's cap, and Cascade never opens a player.
- **The Shadowstep's** strikes from behind never wear more of a player's stance than one blow may (a third).
- **The Bulwark's** throw-back and **the Blade's** piercing slash are aura off the blade, so a player's spell defences meet them.
  The Bulwark's stagger and the bastion's extra slow touch creatures only.
- **Steadying** from all sources together never takes off more than three tenths of anything, and less against players.
- The Banner never helps an enemy.

Server owners can switch Ways off, change how much a new Way asks before it settles, and tune the Banner's reach and shares.
