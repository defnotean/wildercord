# Sword arts


Every breathing method answers the five [sword strings](aura.md#sword-strings) its own
way. The strings are the same for everyone, so you learn them once; what your blade does when you play one depends on your
method. Ember sets the field alight, Rime slows, freezes and shatters, Thunder leaps from foe to foe faster than the eye,
Gale reaches far and takes the ground from under your foes, and Stone hits hardest of all and stands unmoved.

## The five strings

| Art | Opens at | String | Aura | Rests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First Art** | Glow | swing, swing, **low** swing | 6 | 3 s |
| **Second Art** | Flow | **leaping** swing, **low** swing | 8 | 4 s |
| **Third Art** | Edge | a **counter** (your first swing after a perfect Aura Guard) | 8 | 4 s |
| **Fourth Art** | Form | a **step cut** (your first swing after an Aura Step) | 10 | 5 s |
| **Final Art** | Sovereign | **full**, **full**, **full**, **low**, with your aura full | 40 | 30 s |

Every method's art in a slot costs and rests the same and is worth about as much: a method is a different answer, never a
better one. (Thunder's Crackle rests a little less, 2.5 s, for being lighter.)

## How arts work

- **Damage is a share of your weapon's own**, written below as "×": an art at 1× strikes as hard as your blade does (an iron
  sword's 6, a netherite one's 8), so a better blade carries every art with it. Arts are aura off the blade in your method's
  element, like the slash: they meet weaknesses and resistances, set off reactions, and can leave aura marks.
- **Each art names itself** in a banner as it goes off, makes your aura surge, cuts its own trail and plays its own sound over
  your method's.
- **Your first time playing an art** writes it into your [Grimoire](grimoire.md) (and
  condenses a little mana toward your next Heart Circle). The Grimoire page lists every art you've played, by method, and your
  own method's still to play.
- **The Aura page** shows your arts on its **Sword strings** tab, and a row of every method's colour above them: click one to
  read another method's arts (a method still playing the common arts is dimmed).
- **Your own view stays clear.** The big shapes (a lance of lightning, a mirror of ice, a gout of flame) are drawn for
  everyone watching you and for you in third person; in first person you see a thin, low version, never a wall of light across
  the middle of your view.
- **An art with nowhere to go** (a rush against a wall, a blink with nobody near) doesn't go and costs nothing; you're told
  why above the hotbar.

## Ember Breath

Fire that spreads and lingers: damage now and burning after, and the least control of any method.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kindling Draw** (First) | A fast low draw-cut across the front (3.5 blocks, up to 4 foes, 0.55×) that sets its foes alight, and a **line of fire** running 5 blocks on ahead along the ground, burning 3 s and setting alight whatever walks into it. |
| **Rising Cinders** (Second) | A rising slash (0.7×, up to 4 foes) that throws the foes in front into the air in a shower of embers and sets them alight. A moment later **cinders rain off each one** onto whoever stands within 2 blocks of it (0.25×). |
| **Backdraft** (Third) | The counter: **the blow you caught comes back as a gout of flame.** Whoever struck takes it whole (0.75× and their own blow, up to a whole weapon's worth of it); the rest of the cone, 4.5 blocks long, takes half. All set alight and thrown back. |
| **Wildfire Rush** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a second rush** 7 blocks on, cutting through every foe in the way (0.85×, up to 5) and setting them alight. The ground behind you burns 4 s. |
| **Sunfall** (Final) | You leap, a sun gathers on your blade, and you **come down in a blazing arc**: everything within 4.5 blocks of where you land is struck (2.2× at the heart, 1.5× at the edge), set alight 5 s and thrown back, and **a ring of fire** 5 blocks out stands 5 s, searing whoever is in it twice a second. Your fall never hurts you. |

The fire of a line, a trail or a ring is only light: nothing in the world catches.

## Rime Breath

Slow, then freeze, then shatter. A Rime swordsman's foes grow heavier with frost the longer the fight goes, and every frozen
foe is something to shatter. A foe frozen by an art is frozen for a mage's Shatter too.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Frostbite** (First) | A cut (3.3 blocks, up to 4 foes, 0.6×) that **crusts and slows** for 3 s. **A third crust** within 8 seconds freezes a foe solid for 1.5 s. |
| **Hailfall** (Second) | A cut in front (0.5×), and **a cloud** over the foe you struck (or 3.6 blocks ahead) that rains seven hailstones for a second over a 2.6-block circle: every stone that lands on a foe strikes it (0.28×) and slows it, up to three on one. |
| **Glacier Mirror** (Third) | The counter: whoever struck takes 0.9× and is **frozen solid** for 2 s, the foes beside it chilled, and **a mirror of ice** stands before you for 2.5 s, turning projectiles from in front back at whoever loosed them. |
| **Skate** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a glide** 8 blocks on along a path of ice you lay as you go. Foes in the way are cut (0.7×), chilled and crusted; **a frozen foe shatters** (1.5×, its ice cutting those within 2 blocks for 0.3×). For 5 s the path speeds you and your allies and slows foes. |
| **Winter's Hush** (Final) | A hush falls over a wide cone in front (7.5 blocks, up to 10 foes): everything in it takes 0.8× and is **frozen solid**, and a moment and a half later everything still frozen **shatters**, hard (1.7×), its ice cutting those within 2 blocks. |

Skate's path is a shape of light over the ground. Over **water** it freezes for real (vanilla's frosted ice) so you can glide
across, only where you're allowed to build, and it thaws in time.

![Seen from behind a player wreathed in pale blue light: three husks ahead encased in ice, white rings and a frost seal spreading over the stone round them](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-winters-hush.jpg)

## Thunder Breath

Faster than the eye, from foe to foe: many foes, a little each, and interrupts. A foe a Thunder art strikes is left
**ionised** for 5 s, for a storm spell to conduct through, and every hold below breaks what the foe was winding up.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Crackle** (First) | **Three cuts on one foe** faster than the eye (0.27× each), each throwing a spark of lightning to another foe within 4 blocks (0.15×). The first breaks whatever it was winding up: a charge, a bow, a fuse. Rests 2.5 s. |
| **Skyfall** (Second) | **A bolt called down** out of the sky a moment later on the foe you struck (or the ground 4 blocks ahead): everything under it takes 0.95× and is held still a moment, and it **arcs on** to two more foes within 4.5 blocks (0.35×). |
| **Static Riposte** (Third) | The counter: lightning through whoever struck (0.8×), then **leaping on** to the nearest foe, and on again, up to four more within 5 blocks, each a little weaker than the last, each held still a moment. |
| **Bolt Step** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **you blink from foe to foe**, up to four within 8 blocks and in sight (those ahead first), with a cut on each (0.75×) that holds it still a moment. |
| **Heaven's Spear** (Final) | You stand and **gather lightning** on your blade for a moment, then drive **a lance of it** 20 blocks down your line of sight through everything in it (2×, up to 8 foes), and **the sky strikes** each one it ran through. The lance runs level unless you clearly aim up or down (at most 30 degrees), and stops at a wall. |

## Gale Breath

Reach, the ground taken from under them, and the wind at your back.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Cutting Breeze** (First) | **A blade of wind** loosed off your cut, flying 10 blocks on past your sword's reach (2 blocks wide), cutting up to four foes (0.5×) and pushing them back. |
| **Updraft** (Second) | The foes in front **thrown high** (0.65×, up to 4), and you rising after them, hanging a moment to follow. While they're up, **your blade lands a quarter harder** on them, and spells bite them harder too. |
| **Eye of the Storm** (Third) | The counter: **a spinning cut all round you** (3.2 blocks, up to 6 foes, 0.8×) that throws every foe near back. For 3 s after you're the eye of a storm: **projectiles turn aside** before they reach you, and foes are blown back off you. |
| **Tailwind** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a long dash** 11 blocks on, the foes in the way cut (0.6×) and shoved aside, and the wind at your back: you run faster for 5 s, and your allies near the way faster still. |
| **Hundred Winds** (Final) | For 3 s **you're a whirlwind of cuts** 5 blocks round: every quarter of a second each foe in it is cut (0.22×) and **drawn in**, and at its end they're all thrown into the air (0.5×). |

## Stone Breath

Weight, footing taken, and standing firm: Stone hits hardest of all on one foe, takes the ground from under it, and stands
unmoved itself.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rockbreaker** (First) | **A heavy cut** (0.8×) that shakes your foe's footing: it can't act for a moment, goes heavy-footed for 1.5 s, and is **cracked** for 3 s so spells bite it harder. The shock spills onto those within 2 blocks of it (0.3×). |
| **Avalanche** (Second) | **A slam where you come down** (still in the air, you're driven down first), and **a shockwave** rolling out 4 blocks over the ground: everything it reaches is struck (0.9× near you, 0.5× at the edge), thrown back and slowed. |
| **Unmoved** (Third) | The counter: whoever struck is **hurled back by their own blow** (0.9×) and stunned as they land, and **you harden like stone** for 4 s: blows land a fifth lighter and nothing knocks you back. |
| **Landslide** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a heavy charge** 8 blocks on that catches up to four foes in front and **carries them along**, throwing them on at its end (0.9×). A foe driven into a wall is **crushed** against it (half as hard again) and held a second. |
| **Mountain Splitter** (Final) | **An overhead strike that splits the ground** in a line 15 blocks ahead, stone bursting up along it in turn and **throwing up** whatever it reaches, hard (2.1×, up to 10 foes), stunned as they come down. |

The stone that rises is only a shape: the ground is never broken.

## The other methods

Verdant, Hollow, Starlit, Hourglass and Crimson Breath play the five **common arts** on the same strings for now, until their
own arrive: an arc of aura in front of you (First), a rising arc that throws foes up (Second), a cut that staggers the foe you
countered (Third), a line of aura five blocks ahead (Fourth), and a ring of aura that throws every foe near back (Final). See
[the arts](aura.md#the-arts).

## Arts, other players and the world

- **Never a one-shot.** An art deals one player at most 8 damage in all (after the PvP scale, before their armour and spell
  defences), and every art meets armour, Warding and the spellguard like a spell. A totem still saves.
- **Holds are short.** An art freezes or stuns a player for at most three quarters of a second, and no art can hold the same
  player again for 4 seconds; it sets a player alight for at most 3 s and throws a player only so hard. Bosses are only ever
  slowed: never frozen, held, lifted, pulled or thrown.
- **Teams and the server's PvP rule are respected**: a player you can't harm is never touched, and allies are only ever helped
  (Skate's path and Tailwind speed them).
- **Nothing is griefed.** Fire, ice and stone left by an art are light and shapes; only Skate's ice over water is real, it
  thaws, and it never forms where you couldn't build (claims, warded ground, or with world-changing magic switched off).

Server owners can scale every art's damage (`aura.art_damage`), stop Skate freezing water (`aura.art_terrain`), or switch
strings off altogether (`aura.strings`).
