# Sword arts


Every breathing method answers the five [sword strings](aura.md#sword-strings) its own
way. The strings are the same for everyone, so you learn them once; what your blade does when you play one depends on your
method. Ember sets the field alight, Rime slows, freezes and shatters, Thunder leaps from foe to foe faster than the eye,
Gale reaches far and takes the ground from under your foes, Stone hits hardest of all and stands unmoved, Verdant roots its
foes and mends its allies, Hollow draws foes in and silences them, Starlit sets stars that burst and gets its aura back,
Hourglass echoes, rewinds and holds foes still in time, and Crimson bleeds its foes and drinks from them.

<span>Sunfall, Ember Breath's Final Art.</span>


## The five strings

| Art | Opens at | String | Aura | Rests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First Art** | Glow | swing, swing, **low** swing | 6 | 3 s |
| **Second Art** | Flow | **leaping** swing, **low** swing | 8 | 4 s |
| **Third Art** | Edge | a **counter** (your first swing after a perfect Aura Guard) | 8 | 4 s |
| **Fourth Art** | Form | a **step cut** (your first swing after an Aura Step) | 10 | 5 s |
| **Final Art** | Sovereign | **full**, **full**, **full**, **low**, at the peak of your momentum | 40 | 30 s |

Every method's art in a slot costs and rests the same and is worth about as much: a method is a different answer, never a
better one. (Thunder's Crackle rests a little less, 2.5 s, for being lighter.) All fifty were weighed against each other on one
scale, damage, the foes they reach, how long they hold, how far they go, what they mend and the aura they give back, so no
method's five outweigh another's.

## How arts work

- **Damage is a share of your weapon's own**, written below as "×": an art at 1× strikes as hard as your blade does (an iron
  sword's 6, a netherite one's 8), so a better blade carries every art with it. Arts are aura off the blade in your method's
  element, like the slash: they meet weaknesses and resistances, set off reactions, and can leave aura marks.
- **Each art names itself** in a banner as it goes off, makes your aura surge, cuts its own trail and plays its own sound over
  your method's.
- **Your first time playing an art** writes it into your [Grimoire](grimoire.md) (and
  condenses a little mana toward your next Heart Circle). The Grimoire page lists every art you've played, by method, and your
  own method's still to play.
- **The Aura page** shows your arts on its **Sword strings** tab, and a row of every method's colour above them: click one to
  read another method's arts (an add-on's method playing the common arts is dimmed).
- **Your own view stays clear.** The big shapes (a lance of lightning, a mirror of ice, a gout of flame) are drawn for
  everyone watching you and for you in third person; in first person you see a thin, low version, never a wall of light across
  the middle of your view.
- **An art with nowhere to go** (a rush against a wall, a blink with nobody near) doesn't go and costs nothing; you're told
  why above the hotbar.
- **Momentum makes every art cheaper and stronger**: up to a quarter off its price and a fifth harder at the peak, where the
  Final Art opens (see [Momentum](aura.md#momentum)). An art that lands builds momentum, and
  its strikes wear a foe's stance twice as fast as a blow (Stone's quakes faster still), toward an opening and your method's
  [finisher](aura.md#finishers).

## Ember Breath

Fire that spreads and lingers: damage now and burning after, and the least control of any method.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kindling Draw** (First) | A fast low draw-cut across the front (3.5 blocks, up to 4 foes, 0.55×) that sets its foes alight, and a **line of fire** running 5 blocks on ahead along the ground, burning 3 s and setting alight whatever walks into it. |
| **Rising Cinders** (Second) | A rising slash (0.7×, up to 4 foes) that throws the foes in front into the air in a shower of embers and sets them alight. A moment later **cinders rain off each one** onto whoever stands within 2 blocks of it (0.25×). |
| **Backdraft** (Third) | The counter: **the blow you caught comes back as a gout of flame.** Whoever struck takes it whole (0.75× and their own blow, up to a whole weapon's worth of it); the rest of the cone, 4.5 blocks long, takes half. All set alight and thrown back. |
| **Wildfire Rush** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a second rush** 7 blocks on, cutting through every foe in the way (0.85×, up to 5) and setting them alight. The ground behind you burns 4 s. |
| **Sunfall** (Final) | You leap, a sun gathers on your blade, and you **come down in a blazing arc**: everything within 4.5 blocks of where you land is struck (2.2× at the heart, 1.5× at the edge), set alight 5 s and thrown back, and **a ring of fire** 5 blocks out stands 5 s, searing whoever is in it twice a second. Your fall never hurts you. |

The fire of a line, a trail or a ring is only light: nothing in the world catches.

## Rime Breath

Slow, then freeze, then shatter. A Rime swordsman's foes grow heavier with frost the longer the fight goes, and every frozen
foe is something to shatter. A foe frozen by an art is frozen for a mage's Shatter too.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Frostbite** (First) | A cut (3.3 blocks, up to 4 foes, 0.6×) that **crusts and slows** for 3 s. **A third crust** within 8 seconds freezes a foe solid for 1.5 s. |
| **Hailfall** (Second) | A cut in front (0.5×), and **a cloud** over the foe you struck (or 3.6 blocks ahead) that rains seven hailstones for a second over a 2.6-block circle: every stone that lands on a foe strikes it (0.28×) and slows it, up to three on one. |
| **Glacier Mirror** (Third) | The counter: whoever struck takes 0.9× and is **frozen solid** for 2.5 s, the foes beside it chilled, and **a mirror of ice** stands before you for 2.5 s, turning projectiles from in front back at whoever loosed them. |
| **Skate** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a glide** 8 blocks on along a path of ice you lay as you go. Foes in the way are cut (0.7×), chilled and crusted; **a frozen foe shatters** (1.5×, its ice cutting those within 2 blocks for 0.3×). For 5 s the path speeds you and your allies and slows foes. |
| **Winter's Hush** (Final) | A hush falls over a wide cone in front (7.5 blocks, up to 10 foes): everything in it takes 0.8× and is **frozen solid**, and a moment and a half later everything still frozen **shatters**, hard (1.7×), its ice cutting those within 2 blocks (each foe is cut by flying ice once, however many shatter beside it). |

Skate's path is a shape of light over the ground. Over **water** it freezes for real (vanilla's frosted ice) so you can glide
across, only where you're allowed to build, and it thaws in time.

![Seen from behind a player wreathed in pale blue light: three husks ahead encased in ice, white rings and a frost seal spreading over the stone round them](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-winters-hush.jpg)

## Thunder Breath

Faster than the eye, from foe to foe: many foes, a little each, and interrupts. A foe a Thunder art strikes is left
**ionised** for 5 s, for a storm spell to conduct through, and every hold below breaks what the foe was winding up.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Crackle** (First) | **Three cuts on one foe** faster than the eye (0.27× each), each throwing a spark of lightning to another foe within 4 blocks (0.15×). The first breaks whatever it was winding up: a charge, a bow, a fuse. Rests 2.5 s. |
| **Skyfall** (Second) | **A bolt called down** out of the sky a moment later on the foe you struck (or the ground 4 blocks ahead): everything under it takes 0.95× and is held still a moment, and it **arcs on** to two more foes within 4.5 blocks (0.4×). |
| **Static Riposte** (Third) | The counter: lightning through whoever struck (0.8×), then **leaping on** to the nearest foe, and on again, up to four more within 5 blocks, each a little weaker than the last, each held still a moment. |
| **Bolt Step** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **you blink from foe to foe**, up to four within 8 blocks and in sight (those ahead first), with a cut on each (0.75×) that holds it still a moment. |
| **Heaven's Spear** (Final) | You stand and **gather lightning** on your blade for a moment, then drive **a lance of it** 18 blocks down your line of sight through everything in it (2×, up to 8 foes), and **the sky strikes** each one it ran through. The lance runs level unless you clearly aim up or down (at most 30 degrees), and stops at a wall. |

![From above and to one side: a player wreathed in yellow light thrusting a sword, a broad white-hot lance of lightning running from the blade through four husks in a line, rings of light round each and bolts striking down on them](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-heavens-spear.jpg)

## Gale Breath

Reach, the ground taken from under them, and the wind at your back.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Cutting Breeze** (First) | **A blade of wind** loosed off your cut, flying 12 blocks on past your sword's reach (2 blocks wide), cutting up to four foes (0.45×) and pushing them back. |
| **Updraft** (Second) | The foes in front **thrown high** (0.65×, up to 4), and you rising after them, hanging a moment to follow. While they're up, **your blade lands a quarter harder** on them, and spells bite them harder too. |
| **Eye of the Storm** (Third) | The counter: **a spinning cut all round you** (3.5 blocks, up to 6 foes, 0.8×) that throws every foe near back. For 3 s after you're the eye of a storm: **projectiles turn aside** before they reach you, and foes are blown back off you. |
| **Tailwind** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a long dash** 13 blocks on, the foes in the way cut (0.6×) and shoved aside, and the wind at your back: you run faster for 5 s, and your allies near the way faster still. |
| **Hundred Winds** (Final) | For 3 s **you're a whirlwind of cuts** 5.5 blocks round: every quarter of a second each foe in it is cut (0.24×) and **drawn in**, and at its end they're all thrown into the air (0.5×). |

![Seen from above and behind a player wreathed in mint-green light: rings of wind racing out over the stone round them, white whorls round them, three husks thrown outward](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-eye-of-the-storm.jpg)

## Stone Breath

Weight, footing taken, and standing firm: Stone hits hardest of all on one foe, takes the ground from under it, and stands
unmoved itself.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rockbreaker** (First) | **A heavy cut** (0.8×) that shakes your foe's footing: it can't act for a moment, goes heavy-footed for 1.5 s, and is **cracked** for 3 s so spells bite it harder. The shock spills onto those within 2 blocks of it (0.3×). |
| **Avalanche** (Second) | **A slam where you come down** (still in the air, you're driven down first), and **a shockwave** rolling out 4 blocks over the ground: everything it reaches is struck (0.9× near you, 0.5× at the edge), thrown back and slowed. |
| **Unmoved** (Third) | The counter: whoever struck is **hurled back by their own blow** (1×) and stunned as they land, and **you harden like stone** for 4 s: blows land a fifth lighter and nothing knocks you back. |
| **Landslide** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a heavy charge** 8 blocks on that catches up to four foes in front and **carries them along**, throwing them on at its end (0.9×). A foe driven into a wall is **crushed** against it (half as hard again) and held a second. |
| **Mountain Splitter** (Final) | **An overhead strike that splits the ground** in a line 15 blocks ahead, stone bursting up along it in turn and **throwing up** whatever it reaches, hard (2.1×, up to 10 foes), stunned as they come down. |

The stone that rises is only a shape: the ground is never broken.

![Seen from behind a player wreathed in gold light: a line of grey stone bursting up out of the platform ahead, rubble flying, husks thrown high at its far end](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-mountain-splitter.jpg)

## Verdant Breath

Mending and binding: Verdant hurts the least of any method and mends the most. Its cuts **root** foes where they stand (they
can still turn and strike you, but not walk away), and blossom, brambles and a grove mend you and your allies while you fight.
The roots, brambles, flowers and trees are only shapes: nothing grows in the world.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Thorn Lash** (First) | **A lash of thorned vine** flicked out 4.5 blocks, past your sword's reach, across a narrow cone in front (up to 3 foes, 0.45×). The first it catches is **rooted** for 1.5 s and pricked by the thorns three times while it's held (0.07× each). |
| **Blossom Fall** (Second) | A falling cut (0.75×, up to 4 foes), and where it lands **a carpet of blossom** 7 blocks across opens for 4 s: you and your allies on it are **mended** 2 health at once and 1 more each second, and foes on it are slowed. |
| **Rooted Parry** (Third) | The counter: **roots seize whoever struck** (0.95×, rooted 1.5 s), thorns burst up round you and prick the foes within 2.5 blocks (0.3×), and **you mend by what your guard caught**: three quarters of the blow, at least 3 health, at most 6. |
| **Wild Growth** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a second rush** 7 blocks on, the foes in the way cut (0.65×, up to 5) and snagged by roots a moment, and **brambles** spring up behind you for 5 s: foes in them are slowed and pricked, your allies in them mended a health a second. |
| **Grove's Heart** (Final) | You **plant your blade in the ground**: roots burst up under every foe within 6 blocks (1.5×, up to 10), binding them for 2.5 s, and **a grove rises** round you for 8 s: you and your allies in it are mended a health a second, and foes still in it are slowed and pricked. |

**Mending is held.** Whatever arts mend one body, Verdant's or anyone else's, comes to at most 10 health at once and a health a
second after that: arts buy you time in a fight, but they never outpace it. (Verdant's own passive, a little health back from
each coated blow, is separate.)

![Seen from above and behind a player wreathed in green light: a ring of young trees, cherry pink and flowering green, standing round a stone platform, roots twisting up round four husks, rings of green light on the ground and a tamed wolf beside the player](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-groves-heart.jpg)

## Hollow Breath

Drawing in, silencing, stepping through: Hollow takes away the space between you and your foes. It draws foes to your blade,
gathers a crowd into one point for your next cut, swallows a blow and **silences** whoever struck, and steps through a rift to
the far side of a foe. What it strikes is left **shadowed** (a life spell on it sets off Blight).

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Void Cut** (First) | A cut that tears a hole in the air at your blade's end and **draws up to three foes** in front, from as far as 5.5 blocks, **in to your feet** (0.6×), slowed a moment as they come. |
| **Collapse** (Second) | A falling cut that **opens a well** in the ground 2.5 blocks ahead: for most of a second it drags every foe within 4.5 blocks in toward it, then **collapses** on everything it gathered (0.8× at its heart, 0.55× at its edge). |
| **Null Parry** (Third) | The counter: the blow you caught is **swallowed by the void**, and whoever struck is cut (1×), stunned a moment and **silenced**: a creature can't cast, draw a bow or light a fuse for 3 s; a player can't cast, play an art or use the Aura key (but to guard) for 1.5 s. The foes beside you are shoved off (0.25×). |
| **Rift Step** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, you go into **a rift** and come out of another just past the nearest foe ahead (up to 8 blocks, in sight), **cutting it as you pass** (1×). Foes within 2.5 blocks of either rift are dragged in and cut (0.35×). |
| **Event Horizon** (Final) | You cut **a black sphere** into the air 4.5 blocks ahead. For 2 s it **drags in** every foe within 7 blocks and grinds whatever it holds (0.12× every quarter second), then it **crushes** everything near it (1.4× at its heart, 0.9× at its edge). |

A boss is never drawn, dragged or silenced; it's only slowed, and what it was winding up broken. Another player is drawn only
gently, and any steady pull is slower than a sprint, so a player can always run out of it.

![Seen from behind a player wreathed in violet light: a black sphere hanging over the stone ahead, a bright violet core inside it and rings of light tilted round it, a dark magic circle on the ground beneath, a husk caught against it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/arts-event-horizon.jpg)
<span>Event Horizon.</span>

## Starlit Breath

Stars that burst, and aura given back: a Starlit swordsman fights from a little further off with darts and falling stars, sets
**stars** on the foes it strikes for its next art to burst, and **gets aura back** for every foe its stars find (given straight
back after the art's own price is paid).

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Star Needle** (First) | A thrust that looses **three darts of starlight** 9 blocks on, each seeking a little toward a foe (0.22× each). Each that strikes **sets a star** on its foe and gives you half an aura back. |
| **Meteor Shower** (Second) | **Stars brought down** out of the sky over a circle ahead (round the foe you struck): five small ones (0.25×, at most three on one foe) and **a great one** on its heart (0.4×) that sets stars on what it strikes. Each foe struck gives you an aura back (up to four). |
| **Constellation Guard** (Third) | The counter: whoever struck is cut (0.6×) and set with **a constellation of four stars** that burst one by one a moment and a half later (0.2× each, or on whoever stands where it fell), each giving you 0.75 aura back. **Every foe near already carrying a star bursts at once** (0.35×). |
| **Comet Dash** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a second rush** 8 blocks on trailing stars, the foes in the way cut (0.6×) and starred. A moment later **the trail bursts** star by star along its length (0.45× to everything within 1.5 blocks), an aura back for each foe it catches (up to five). |
| **Nova** (Final) | You gather starlight on your blade for a moment, then **it bursts out in a ring** over everything within 7 blocks (1.9× at the heart, 1.3× at the edge, up to 12 foes), throwing it back; starred foes burst with it (0.4× more). You get **2.5 aura back for every foe struck**, up to 20, half of what it cost. |

## Hourglass Breath

Echoes, rewinds and moments held still: an Hourglass swordsman fights a moment ahead and a moment behind.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Echo Cut** (First) | A cut in front (0.5×, up to 4 foes), and **a golden afterimage** left where you stood that **strikes it again** a moment later (0.5×), the way you faced, at whatever stands there then. |
| **Rewind Leap** (Second) | A falling cut (0.95×, up to 4 foes) that **drags its foes in time** (slowed hard for 2 s), then **time snaps you back** to where you leapt from, if you may still stand there and it's no more than 10 blocks. |
| **Stopped Moment** (Third) | The counter: whoever struck is cut (0.8×) and **held still in time** for 2.5 s, then flung back and struck again by **the moment's snap** as time starts once more (0.25×). The foes beside you are dragged a moment. |
| **Blur** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a rush faster than any other**, 7 blocks on, the foes in the way cut (0.7×) and slowed. For 3 s **time drags round you**: foes within 5 blocks are slowed, projectiles crossing in slow to a third, and you're quickened. |
| **Thousand Moments** (Final) | **Time stops round you**: every foe within 7 blocks is held still for 2.5 s (up to 10) while **your cuts gather** on it, golden crescents hanging in the air, and when time moves again **they all land at once** (2.2×), with half of every blow you struck it meanwhile (up to 1× more). |

Holding another player still in time is a hold like any other art's: three quarters of a second at most, and not again for 4
seconds. What was gathered on them still lands when time moves.

## Crimson Breath

Bleeding, drinking, frenzy, and the price of it: Crimson hurts the most of any method but Ember. Its cuts open **wounds** that
bleed on (harder while their bearer runs; a wind spell on a bleeding foe sets off Rupture), and it **drinks** a share of what it
deals back as health, held like any mending: at most 10 health at once and a health a second after, so a Crimson swordsman lasts
longer in a fight, but can always be killed.

| Art | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Bloodletting** (First) | A deep cut (0.55×) that **opens a wound** in up to three foes in front: it bleeds for 3 s (0.08× every half second, half again while they run), and you **drink** a quarter of what it bleeds. |
| **Red Rain** (Second) | A falling cut that bursts 2 blocks ahead, striking everything within 3.5 blocks (0.7×), and **a red rain** falls there for 2 s: every foe under it bleeds (0.08× every half second), and you drink back three tenths of all of it (up to 4 health). |
| **Sanguine Parry** (Third) | The counter: the blow you caught becomes **a wound in whoever struck it**: cut (0.7×), stunned a moment, and bleeding the blow's worth back out over 3 s (up to a whole weapon's worth). You **drink half** of all of it (up to 6 health), and blood off the parry nicks the foes beside you. |
| **Frenzy** (Fourth) | Straight after your step, **a second rush** 7 blocks on through the foes in the way (0.7×, up to 5). Each one cut, and each blow of yours that lands after, **quickens your blade** a twentieth, up to a fifth faster, until five seconds from the rush. |
| **Crimson Moon** (Final) | **A gamble.** It takes **a quarter of your health** (5 of 20), then a great arc of blood-light strikes every foe before you, 6 blocks out (2.5×, up to 10), opens wounds in them (0.1× every half second for 3 s), and **drinks back half** of everything it deals, up to 10 health. Against a crowd you come out ahead; against nothing you've only paid. |

**Crimson Moon never kills you.** Its price is taken straight from your health (not dealt as damage, so armour and absorption
don't change it and a totem never needs to save you), and it never leaves you with less than **a heart** (2 health). If you're
already down to a heart, it costs nothing more.

## The common arts

A breathing method without arts of its own (an add-on's) plays the five **common arts** on the same strings: an arc of aura in
front of you (First), a rising arc that throws foes up (Second), a cut that staggers the foe you countered (Third), a line of
aura five blocks ahead (Fourth), and a ring of aura that throws every foe near back (Final). See
[the arts](aura.md#the-arts).

## Arts, other players and the world

- **Never a one-shot.** An art deals one player at most 8 damage in all (after the PvP scale, before their armour and spell
  defences), even at the peak of momentum, and every art meets armour, Warding and the spellguard like a spell. A totem still
  saves. An art wears at most half of a player's stance (see [Duels](aura.md#duels)).
- **Holds are short.** An art freezes, stuns, roots or holds a player still in time for at most three quarters of a second,
  and no art can hold the same player again for 4 seconds; it sets a player alight for at most 3 s and throws a player only so
  hard. A steady pull (Collapse's well, Event Horizon) drags a player slower than they can sprint. Bosses are only ever slowed:
  never frozen, held, rooted, lifted, pulled, thrown or silenced.
- **Silence is brief.** Null Parry silences a player for a second and a half: no spells, no arts, no Aura key but the guard (so
  they can still defend). No art can silence the same player again for 5 seconds.
- **Mending and drinking are held.** All arts together mend one body at most 10 health at once and a health a second after.
- **Teams and the server's PvP rule are respected**: a player you can't harm is never touched, and allies are only ever helped
  (Skate's path and Tailwind speed them, Verdant's arts mend them).
- **Nothing is griefed.** Fire, ice, stone, roots, brambles, flowers and trees left by an art are light and shapes; only Skate's
  ice over water is real, it thaws, and it never forms where you couldn't build (claims, warded ground, or with world-changing
  magic switched off).

Server owners can scale every art's damage (`aura.art_damage`), stop Skate freezing water (`aura.art_terrain`), or switch
strings off altogether (`aura.strings`).
