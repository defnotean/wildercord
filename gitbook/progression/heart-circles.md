# Heart Circles


Casters build rings of condensed mana around their heart, from the **1st Circle** to the **20th**, the
Master Heart. **Archmage** remains the 8th Circle milestone. Each circle deepens your mana and sharpens your spells, and some bring a perk. There's
nothing to craft and nothing to farm: circles come from casting, from discovering, and from the things
you do with magic.


## How a circle forms

Three things, in order:

1. **Condense mana.** Every point of mana you spend casting spells condenses in your heart.
2. **Earn the breakthrough.** Every circle after the 1st also asks for milestones: runes known, a
   better Cord, reactions, monsters, secrets, feats.
3. **Meditate** to form it, once your heart is ready.

Hover the **heart badge** in the Cord screen (left of the mana badge) at any time. It shows your
circles, what they give, your perks, and what the next circle needs, each line ticked ✔ or crossed ✘.

### Condensing mana

- Every point of mana you **pay to cast a spell** condenses toward your next circle: the price you
  actually paid, after discounts.
- A **Blood Price** spell counts 5 mana for every point of health it costs.
- An **overcast** counts the mana you had before the circle cracked.
- **Discoveries** condense extra mana the first time: see the table below.
- These don't condense anything: a passive's upkeep, an imbued item or glyph letting go (it was paid for
  when you imbued it), a wild surge's Free Recast, inscribing a scroll, and casting in Creative mode.

The amounts are running totals: you need 600 condensed for the 1st Circle, 2,000 in all for the 2nd,
and so on. Nothing is used up when a circle forms.

| First discovery | Condenses |
|---|---|
| A creature's weakness, found | 25 |
| An [affinity](affinity.md)'s first level (I) with an element | 100 |
| A reaction | 150 |
| A fusion at the Fusion Altar | 150 |
| A feat | 250 |
| A rune of the land, attuned | 250 |
| A secret spell | 400 |
| The Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater or the Tide Scribe | 1,500 |
| The Archivist | 2,000 |

A riddle from a Torn Page condenses nothing. See [The Grimoire and Feats](grimoire.md).

### Meditating to form it

When enough mana has condensed and the breakthrough is earned, you're told in chat: *"Your heart is
ready to form the 3rd Circle. Meditate (sneak and stand still) for 10 seconds without getting hurt to
form it."* The heart badge in the Cord screen blinks until you do.

1. Wear a Cord, stand on the ground, and **sneak without moving** (and without using an item). After a
   second you're meditating: soft lights circle your feet and glyphs drift in toward you.
2. Hold it for **10 seconds** (**5 seconds on a [ley line](ley-lines.md)**).
   Your rings spin faster and faster, mana streams in toward your heart, the new ring draws itself in
   light a little further round each second, and a chime rises in pitch.
3. **Taking any damage** breaks your concentration (*"Your concentration broke: the circle unravels"*),
   and standing up or moving starts it over too.

When it forms: the circle's title fills your screen, light breaks out of your heart, a circle opens on
the ground, and your **mana refills completely**. Chat tells you what you gained: *"3rd Circle formed:
+15 max mana, +0.5 mana/s and +3% spell power."*, plus any perk or passive slot.

### The perks

| Perk | Circle | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Mana Skin** | 3rd | A fifth of the damage you take is paid with mana instead, at 2 mana per point of health. It only works while you wear a Cord and have the mana to pay. |
| **Flow** | 5th | Your cooldowns are **15% shorter**. |
| **Overflow** | 7th | A spell cast while your mana is **full** hits **30% harder**. |
| **Archmage** | 8th | Your spells and passives cost **15% less** mana. |

At 20 working circles, your innate rune receives +120% power from circles, and the circle factor in
Domain clashes is ×3. Overcasting still removes the outermost working circle, one at a time.

Flow and Archmage stack with the Celerity and Thrift [Cord enchantments](enchantments.md).

### Passive slots and your innate rune

- **Passive slots** open at the 1st and 5th Circle: see [Passives](../spellcraft/passives.md).
- At the **1st Circle**, a few seconds after the title, one of ten **innate runes** wakes in your heart,
  chosen at random: *"Your innate rune awakens: ..."*. It's yours alone, it's in your Codex straight
  away, it fits any Cord, and it grows **+6% stronger for every circle** you've formed (a cracked one adds
  nothing until it mends). See
  [Innate Runes](../runes/innate.md). Your first earns the **Awakening** feat.

## The breakthroughs in detail

| Milestone | What counts |
|---|---|
| **Know N runes** | Runes learned into your Codex. A Knot is a spell, not a rune, so it doesn't count. |
| **Wear a Cord** | The Cord you're wearing now. A better Cord counts too. |
| **Set off reactions** | Different element reactions in your Grimoire, any of the eleven: Shatter, Conduct, Wildfire, Implode, Collapse, Overload, Fracture, Blight, Unweave, Rupture, Elapse. The 6th Circle wants any five of them, not all. See [Element Reactions](../spellcraft/reactions.md). |
| **Defeat monsters with spells** | A hostile monster that dies within **5 seconds** of one of your spells hurting it, whatever finished it off. |
| **Slay Runebound** | A Runebound killed by you (spell, blade or arrow). See [Runebound](../world/runebound.md). |
| **Help slay a boss** | Be alive, and not spectating, within **96 blocks** when a boss dies: the Wither, the Warden, an Elder Guardian, the Ender Dragon, the Archivist, the Cinder Warden, the Star-Eater or the Tide Scribe. You're told *"A boss has fallen. Your heart can now break through to the 7th Circle."* You only need one, ever. |
| **Find secret spells** | Secret spells cast at least once. See [Secret Spells](../spellcraft/secret-spells.md). |
| **Long Incantation** | Slay a monster with a spell of **six runes or more** (it dies within 5 seconds of that spell hurting it). |
| **In Rhythm** | Chain **three casts on the beat**: each one just as the last comes off cooldown (see [Casting](../spellcraft/casting.md)). |
| **The Last Page** | Be within 64 blocks when the Archivist falls. See [The Archive](../world/archive.md). |

Milestones you reached before you needed them still count: a Runebound slain on day one counts toward
the 6th Circle or later ones.

## The rings

Your circles show as rings of light around your heart, one per circle, each on its own tilt and
turning its own way like a gyroscope. The inner rings are deep blue, burning out to white gold at the
8th, then passing through opal colours to white gold at the 20th. The later rings nest more closely
so the whole heart stays compact. Once your magic leans toward an element (your deepest
[affinity](affinity.md)) they're tinted halfway toward its colour.

- While you **meditate**, other players see them turning; your own view stays clear, with light at your feet.
- Whenever you **cast**, they spin up round you for a moment, for everyone around you to see. You
  don't see it yourself: it happens on every cast, and it would fill the bottom of your view.

## Cracked circles

**Overcasting** (casting a spell you can't afford, with a second press) cracks your outermost working
circle to pay for it. A cracked circle gives nothing (its mana, regeneration, power, perk or passive
slot, or its +6% on your innate rune) until it mends **3 minutes** later. It never costs you the circle
itself or your condensed mana. The HUD shows **✦** and how many are cracked. Everything about it is on
[Overcasting and Wild Magic](../spellcraft/overcasting.md).

## Tips

- **Cast a lot.** Nothing condenses faster than spending mana. A ley line doubles your regeneration, so
  you can spend twice as much.
- **Discover things.** Each first reaction, feat and secret is a lump of mana for free. The Grimoire
  lists what's left to find.
- **Form circles on a ley line**: 5 seconds instead of 10, so there's half as long for something to
  hit you. Somewhere safe helps too: a single hit starts it over.
- **Don't overcast before a big fight** at the 7th or 8th Circle: your outermost perk goes first.
