# Echo Master

## What it is

Every blow from the Echo Master rings twice. It fights at the same steady pace as the Ember Master: wind-ups of about 0.25 to 0.7 seconds, and an opening of about 0.75 seconds after a chain. It has 23 named [techniques](techniques.md) and raises its guard after every third attack.

At the start of the trial it tells you its lesson: its blows ring twice, so leave the lane before the second toll, then counter.

## How to get it

No wandering duelist breathes Echo. To call this Master, breathe Echo yourself. Then sneak and use a [wandering duelist](../world/creatures.md#wandering-duelist) of any method except Starlit, Hourglass, Crimson, Tide, Iron or Dune, since those always call their own Master. You can also type `/master challenge echo`. You need Aura Form or Heart Circle VIII. See [Sword Masters](index.md) for the full trial rules.

To train for it, look for a **Resonance Chamber** under deep dark, dripstone or lush caves. A bell frame marks it on the surface, and a ladder leads down. Its Echo vault is sealed with wind: hit every seal block with a wind spell within 10 seconds. See [Masters' sites](../world/masters-sites.md). On the deep dark floor, an **Echo Post** holds Echo manual pages. See [wild places](../world/wilds-sites.md).

## Tolling Bell

Tolling Bell is one of the Master's named techniques: a thrust, then a reply cut. It comes up three times as often as any other single technique. The banner reads "Leave the lane! It tolls twice."

- The lane is **9 blocks** long and **2.2 blocks** wide, with pale lines along its sides. It starts half a block behind the Master.
- The second toll rings down the same lane from the same spot, even if the Master has turned since.
- Jumping does not clear it. Step out sideways.
- Each toll that hits you also makes you dizzy for 2.5 seconds.
- Together the two tolls deal about 37 damage before armor. The second one lands at about **1 second**, and the Master is open for about **0.75 seconds** after.

It needs a clear line to you, so solid cover blocks it.

## Other moves

- **Techniques:** chains of two to four strikes. Read each strike's shape. See [Reading a Master](index.md#reading-a-master).
- **Pursuit dash:** if you hold a spell too long, it may dash up to 4.8 blocks at you, at most once every 7 seconds.
- **Crescent volley:** flying blades if you stay far away or high up.

## Rewards for a clear

- Your first Echo clear teaches the **Wave** [technique part](../progression/techniques.md).
- The win goes into your [lore journal](../progression/lore-journal.md). Type `/master victories` to see which Masters you have beaten.

## The Echo breathing method

Echo is a Wind method. Its passive gives your blows a chance to ring again, and from Edge onward the foe also reels. Its arts unlock as your aura grows:

| Art | Opens at | Input |
|---|---|---|
| Ringing Cut | Glow | swing, swing, low swing |
| Resonant Chord | Flow | leaping swing, then low swing |
| Counterpoint | Edge | first swing after a perfect Aura Guard |
| Reverb Step | Form | first swing after an Aura Step |
| Grand Resonance | Sovereign | three full swings, then low swing |

See [Breathing Methods](../progression/breathing-methods.md) for the full numbers. A Resonance Chamber's Echo chest can hold Echolocate, Resonant Shriek and Push runes.

## Tips and counterplay

- When you see the pale side lines, step sideways out of the lane. Don't jump, and don't back straight up.
- If the first toll hits you, there is a short gap before the second. Use it to leave the lane, not to swing.
- The Master may turn, but the second toll won't. Stand beside the old lane and counter from there.
- The Echo Master guards after every third attack. Flank it or use an axe.
