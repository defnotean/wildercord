# Gale Master

## What it is

The Gale Master is the fastest school. Its techniques land about 15% sooner than Ember's, and it closes distance quickly. Its trick is footwork: it slips sideways, then strikes the spot where you were standing. It has 33 named [techniques](techniques.md). It raises its guard after every third attack.

At the start of the trial it tells you its lesson: it steps aside, then strikes where you stood. Leave the lane or parry, then counter.

## How to get it

Sneak and use a [wandering duelist](../world/creatures.md#wandering-duelist) who breathes Gale, or type `/master challenge gale`. You need Aura Form or Heart Circle VIII. See [Sword Masters](index.md) for the full trial rules.

## Crosswind Reprise

The boss bar shows "Crosswind Reprise", then "Crosswind reply".

1. **Gather.** The Master leans its blade high for **0.4 seconds**.
2. **Step.** It moves **1.8 blocks** sideways in **0.2 seconds**.
3. **Reply.** It plants and marks a narrow lane, aimed at where you were before it moved. The lane is 1.5 blocks wide and 5.25 blocks long. It strikes **0.5 seconds** later for 26 damage.

The reply never turns to follow you. To answer it:
- Step out of the lane.
- Back away beyond its reach.
- Stand behind solid cover.
- Dodge, or face the blade and parry.

It only hits the player it chose. Afterwards the Master is open for **1.2 seconds**.

The Master uses it when you're 2.5 to 4.75 blocks away: after its first attack, then about once every four attacks, and never more than once every 5 seconds. It can't step through walls, gaps, fluids or past the arena edge.

## Other moves

- **Techniques:** chains of two to four strikes. Read each strike's shape. See [Reading a Master](index.md#reading-a-master).
- **Pursuit dash:** holding a spell too long invites a dash of up to 6.4 blocks, at most once every 5 seconds. Gale's dash is the longest and comes back the soonest.
- **Crescent volley:** flying blades if you stay far away or high up. Gale's blades fly faster than the other schools'.

## Rewards for a clear

- Your first Gale clear teaches the **Afterimage** [technique part](../progression/techniques.md).
- A Gale duelist can then teach you **Reed Slip** (at Form) and **Wall Turn** (at Sovereign). See [Master Forms](master-forms.md).

## Tips and counterplay

- When the Master steps sideways, move. The reply aims at where you were, not where you are.
- Don't hold spells around a Gale Master. Its dash reaches farthest and returns soonest.
- Its fast tempo leaves short recoveries. Take one or two hits on it, then reset.
