# Dune Master

## What it is

The Dune Master fights like a sandstorm. First a lane of sand runs at you, then a storm hits everywhere except where the sand has already passed. Its strikes are slow and deliberate. Each one winds up for about 0.6 to 0.7 seconds, and it stays open for 0.7 to 0.8 seconds after a chain. It has 22 named [techniques](techniques.md) and raises its guard after every third attack.

At the start of the trial it tells you its lesson: sidestep its sand lane, then step back into it before the storm.

## How to get it

Sneak and use a [wandering duelist](../world/creatures.md#wandering-duelist) who breathes Dune, or type `/master challenge dune`. A Dune duelist always calls the Dune Master. You need Aura Form or Heart Circle VIII. See [Sword Masters](index.md) for the full trial rules.

To train for it, look for a **Sundial Court** in the desert. Its Dune shrine is sealed with earth: hit every seal block with an earth spell within 10 seconds. See [Masters' sites](../world/masters-sites.md). Deserts also hide a **Dune Temple** with Dune manual pages. See [wild places](../world/wilds-sites.md).

## Shifting Sands

The banner reads "Sidestep the lane, then step back into the pale sand!"

1. **The lane.** The Master aims a lane at you, **7 blocks** long and **2.2 blocks** wide. The aim follows you until 0.3 seconds before the sand runs. At **0.65 seconds** the sand runs, dealing 26 damage and blinding you for 1 second. Sidestep it.
2. **The storm.** The used lane turns pale. At **1.35 seconds** a storm hits everywhere within **5.5 blocks** of the Master except the pale lane, dealing 26 damage. Step back into the pale sand, or get more than 5.5 blocks away.

Solid cover blocks it. After the storm the Master is open for about **0.8 seconds**.

The Master uses Shifting Sands when you are 1.5 to 6 blocks away and roughly level with it. It comes as the second move of a four-move phrase, at most once every 7.5 seconds.

## Other moves

- **Techniques:** chains of two to four strikes. Read each strike's shape. See [Reading a Master](index.md#reading-a-master).
- **Pursuit dash:** if you hold a spell too long, it may dash up to 4.8 blocks at you, at most once every 7 seconds.
- **Crescent volley:** flying blades if you stay far away or high up.

## Rewards for a clear

- Your first Dune clear teaches the **Bind** [technique part](../progression/techniques.md).
- The win goes into your [lore journal](../progression/lore-journal.md). Type `/master victories` to see which Masters you have beaten.

## The Dune breathing method

Dune is the Sand method. Its passive gives your blows a chance to blind. Its arts unlock as your aura grows:

| Art | Opens at | Input |
|---|---|---|
| Grit Flick | Glow | swing, swing, low swing |
| Quicksand | Flow | leaping swing, then low swing |
| Sandveil | Edge | first swing after a perfect Aura Guard |
| Dune Runner | Form | first swing after an Aura Step |
| Sea of Sand | Sovereign | three full swings, then low swing |

Sandveil hits the foe whose blow you guarded first, then the others nearby. See [Breathing Methods](../progression/breathing-methods.md) for the full numbers. A Sundial Court's Dune chest can hold Dust Devil, Geode and Brace runes.

## Tips and counterplay

- Sidestep just far enough to clear the lane, because you need to step straight back into it.
- The pale lane is the only safe ground inside the storm. Check where it lies before you move.
- If the lane blinds you, remember where the Master was and step back the way you came.
- The Dune Master guards after every third attack. Flank it or use an axe.
