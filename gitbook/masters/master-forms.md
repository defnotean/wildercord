# Master Forms

## What it is

Master forms are movement skills taught by wandering teachers after you beat a Sword Master. There are four: **Wall Turn**, **Cinder Lunge**, **Reed Slip** and **Stone Hinge**. You have **one Master-form slot**, so only one form is ready at a time. Written techniques keep their own slots; a form never takes one of them.

Every form costs real Aura, ends in a short exposed recovery, and gives you no invulnerability.

| Form | Teacher | You need | Cost | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wall Turn | Gale teacher | Sovereign (Aura Stage V) and a Gale Master clear | 20 Aura | 6 s |
| Cinder Lunge | Ember teacher | Sovereign and an Ember Master clear | 25 Aura | 5 s |
| Reed Slip | Gale teacher | Form (Aura Stage IV) and a Gale Master clear | 12 Aura | 2.5 s |
| Stone Hinge | Stone teacher | Sovereign and a Stone Master clear | 20 Aura | 6 s |

The cost is the same while you are awakened.

## How to get it

1. Reach the Aura stage in the table. See [Aura](../progression/aura.md).
2. Clear that school's Master trial. Clears from before the form existed still count, and any breathing method works. See [Sword Masters](index.md).
3. Find a wandering teacher of that school. Talk to them for a hint, then **sneak and use them with an empty main hand** while they can see you (within 6 blocks).
4. Read the illustrated lesson and accept it. You learn the form for good and get the lesson as a written book if you have a free slot. The lesson also stays in your Grimoire and on the Master-form page.

A Gale teacher offers Wall Turn first. Reed Slip is offered once you know Wall Turn, or when you cannot learn Wall Turn yet (for example, at Form stage before Sovereign).

## How to use it

### Equipping and switching

Open the Cord screen (**K**), click the **Aura** badge, then **Master form**.

- **Equip** puts the form in your slot. **Empty Master-form slot** clears it.
- Once you know both Wall Turn and Stone Hinge, **Show Wall Turn / Show Stone Hinge** flips between their pages.
- Cinder Lunge and Reed Slip are on the **Field forms** page, reached from the same screen.
- Equipping one form removes whichever form was in the slot.

You can only equip or switch on safe ground, out of combat, when nothing else is in progress, and after the shared rest is over. Switching never skips a rest you still owe.

All forms use the **Equipped Master form** key, **C** by default. You can rebind it under **Wildercord: Master forms** in Minecraft's Controls. A small line above the hotbar shows the form's state and rest.

Every form needs a held Aura weapon and free movement. You cannot start one while flying, riding, swimming, climbing, guarding, frozen, pulled, or during another art. **Sneak** cancels a form. A hit that lands interrupts it. Nothing is refunded.

### Wall Turn

An air kick off a wall.

1. Jump so you are in the air beside a solid wall with room for your body.
2. Press **C** to brace. You stop against the wall for up to half a second.
3. Let go, then press **C** again to kick away. Hold a movement key to steer. The kick always leaves the wall, up to 4 blocks across and 1.25 blocks up.
4. Land on safe, solid ground. You get one Wall Turn per jump.

The rest is 6 seconds, then 0.5 seconds of landing recovery. Other Aura arts wait until you land. Falling still hurts.

### Cinder Lunge

A short charge that ends in one cut.

1. On solid ground, look where you want to go and press **C**.
2. You plant for a quarter second, then dash up to 5 blocks.
3. The dash stops when it reaches a foe. You then cut the nearest foe ahead, for more than an ordinary swing.

If a wall, ledge, hazard or ward stops the dash early, there is no cut. Recovery is 0.8 seconds, or 1.2 seconds if stopped.

### Reed Slip

A quick side step that keeps you facing your foe.

1. On solid ground, hold a strafe or back key and press **C**.
2. You slide 2.5 blocks that way. With no key held, you step straight back. It never moves you forward.

For 0.7 seconds afterwards you cannot guard, step or use arts.

### Stone Hinge

> Stone Hinge is still in testing. Your server operator may need to switch it on.

A planted stance that turns a frontal shove to one side.

1. Stand on firm ground (not sneaking). Hold **exactly one** strafe key, left or right.
2. Press **C**. You plant for 0.3 seconds, then catch for 0.6 seconds. You can barely move while planted.
3. The first melee blow that comes from in front of where you planted (within about 60 degrees) pushes you toward the side you held, at the same strength, instead of backwards.

The damage still lands. Only the shove turns. Turning your camera after planting does not change which way counts as "in front". Recovery is 0.5 seconds. The 6-second rest starts at the plant, even if nothing hits you.

The plant is refused before you pay if that side is unsafe: a drop, hazard, ward edge or the world border.

## Tips and counterplay

- Practice on safe ground. Each form costs real Aura and gives no XP.
- **Wall Turn:** the brace is a pause. An archer can hit you there. Plan your landing before you kick.
- **Cinder Lunge:** the crouch is the warning. Step aside and the lunge passes your old spot, leaving the user exposed at the end. Standing at a ledge or behind a block stops it short with no cut.
- **Reed Slip:** it is not a dodge. Nothing passes through the user; they just leave the lane. Punish the heel recovery.
- **Stone Hinge:** hit from the side or behind, use arrows or blasts, or simply wait out the 0.6-second catch. Those all push as normal. Mobs with extra knockback and attacks that skip the normal melee hit are not turned either.
