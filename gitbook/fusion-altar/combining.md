# Combining

## What it is

On the [Fusion Altar](index.md) you can fuse two effect runes into a new **fused rune**.
Every pair of the ten elements has a rune of its own, and so does every element with itself: **55 in all**. On top of
those, **40 signature fusions** come from particular pairs of runes.

## How to get it

1. Put an effect rune in each side socket.
2. Put an **Amethyst Shard** in the middle.
3. Take the result. It costs **3 XP levels** (free in Creative).

- **Any effect of an element counts.** Ember works as well as Fire.
- **Fused runes count too**, as the element their tooltip shows. Firestorm is a Fire effect, so Firestorm with any
  Frost effect makes Steam.
- **Ranks carry over.** The new rune takes the lower rank of the two you put in.

## How to use it

- Fused runes are **Tier III** and need an Amethyst Cord or better. **Doomclock**, **Cometfall** and **Dust Devil**
  are **Tier IV** and need an Echo Cord.
- They can't be crafted or found. Fusing is the only way.
- They take modifiers like any effect, can be [ranked up](ranks.md), and can be
  fused again as their own element.
- The first one of each you make goes into your Grimoire. Ones you haven't found show as `??? + ???` with a hint of
  their elements.

### Exact weaving

Use an **Amethyst Block** in the middle instead of a shard to keep the exact effects you put in, instead of getting the
grid's fusion.

- A weave holds **2 to 8 effects**. You can add to an existing weave.
- 3 or 4 effects make it at least **Tier III**; 5 to 8 make it **Tier IV**.
- It costs **3 XP levels for each extra effect**, and its mana is the full cost of everything inside.

### Imprinting your innate

Sneak and use a **Blank Rune** on the altar to imprint your awakened innate onto it. It costs the Blank Rune and
**3 XP levels**. A weave holding your innate is **Tier IV**, and only you can use it. Two different innates can't be
woven together.

See [Physical magic](../spellcraft/physical-magic.md) for more.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Fuse what you have spare.** Any effect of the right element works, so use the cheap ones.
- **Mind the lower rank.** Fusing a rank III rune with a rank I rune gives rank I.
- **Hunting signatures?** Your Grimoire shows the two elements of each one you're missing.
- **Signature** (your first) and **Hallmarks** (five) are advancements for signature fusions.
- To fit a whole spell into one socket, tie a [Knot](knots.md).
