# Ranks

![The tooltip of a Fire Rune II: Effect, Tier II, Fire, and a gold line reading Rank II: +25% power, at the same mana](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-tooltip-rank.jpg)
<span>A ranked rune shimmers, and its name and tooltip give its rank</span>

## What it is

Every rune you learn starts at **rank I**. A higher rank hits harder **at the same mana cost**, in every spell the rune
is threaded in.

| Rank | Power | Mana |
|---|---|---|
| I | as crafted | as crafted |
| II | **+25%** | the same |
| III | **+50%** | the same |

Rank III is the top.

## How to get it

At the [Fusion Altar](index.md):

1. Put three copies of the same rune, **all at the same rank**, in the three rune sockets.
2. Leave the middle socket **empty**.
3. The panel reads **Upgrade**. Press **Fuse**.

| To make | Put in | XP levels | Rank I copies in all |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank II | 3 rank I runes | 2 | 3 |
| Rank III | 3 rank II runes | 5 | 9 |

From nothing to rank III takes **9 copies** and **11 XP levels**. You can't mix ranks.

### Where to get copies

- **Craft them.** See [Rune Recipes](../items/rune-recipes.md).
- **Buy them** from a [Runesmith](../social/runesmith.md), or earn them from
  [daily contracts](../social/contracts.md).
- **Find them** in chests and on monsters. Tier IV runes and runes of the world can only be found.

## How to use it

Right-click a ranked rune to learn it.

- **If you know the rune at a lower rank,** it goes up, in every spell and passive it's in, including
  [Knots](knots.md).
- **If you don't know it yet,** you learn it at that rank.
- **A rank never goes down.** A copy at your rank or lower can't be learned and isn't used up, so keep it for fusing.

How ranks work:

- **Power** means damage, healing, force and blasts: the things Amplify strengthens.
- **Ranks stack** with Amplify, Heart Circles, enchantments, charging and gear.
- **Effects measured in levels** (like Swift's Speed or Haste) and **mining tiers** only gain at rank III, by one level
  or one tier. Damage or healing from the same rune still gets the full bonus.
- **Ranks belong to the caster.** Whoever casts a spell uses their own ranks. Monsters always cast at rank I.

### Which runes can rank

Only **effects with power**. Shapes, modifiers, links, Knots and [innate runes](../runes/innate.md)
can't. If **Amplify** works on a rune, it has ranks.

Of the 55 [fused runes](../runes/fused.md), 49 can be ranked. The six that can't are Glacier,
Nullify, Second Wind, Soulbond, Timesteal and Warp.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Combining keeps the lower rank** of the two runes. See [Combining](combining.md).
- **The Runesmith** only takes plain rank I runes for buybacks and rerolls.
- **Heart Circle** breakthroughs count runes you know, not ranks.
- A rank III Tier IV rune is a real prize: those can only be found.
