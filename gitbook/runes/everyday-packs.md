# Everyday Rune Packs

## What they are

Not every rune is for fighting. Six families of everyday runes help with the rest of your life in the world: camping
and travel, fishing, farming, mining and building, exploring, and looking after your friends.

| Pack | What it's for | Runes |
|---|---|---|
| [Hearth](#hearth-runes) | Gentle, long-lasting comforts for camp and road | 48 |
| [Tide](#tide-runes) | Fishing, the shore and life on the water | 48 |
| [Farmstead](#farmstead-runes) | Fields, animals, the kitchen, bees and trees | 48 |
| [Delving](#delving-runes) | Mining, masonry, light, levers and storage | 48 |
| [Wayfarer's](#wayfarers-runes) | Finding your way, reading the world, villagers | 46 |
| [Support](#support-runes) | Healing, guarding and calming foes without harm | 46 |

On top of these, the hearth pack brings [17 everyday links and 36 modifiers](#everyday-links-and-modifiers) that fit
these runes to the job.

**None of these runes deals damage.** The support pack can hold, push or calm an enemy, but never hurts it.

Every rune here is an ordinary rune: learn it, thread it on your Cord, and cast it like any other. Its family and
element pages list the full numbers, recipe and modifiers, and the [Rune Codex](codex.md)
lists every rune in the game. In the [Rune Catalog](../spellcraft/rune-catalog.md), the **Use**
filter (Farming, Fishing, Mining, Building, Exploring, Travel, Support, Passive) finds them fast.

## How to get them

- **Crafting.** Every Tier I to III rune in these packs has a recipe, like any other crafted rune. See
  [Tiers](index.md#tiers).
- **Chests.** Each pack turns up in the chests of the places that would use it. Village chests are the main source,
  listed per pack below.
- **New sites.** The chests of the [farm sites](../world/farm-sites.md) hold farmstead and hearth
  runes, [water sites](../world/water-sites.md) hold Tide runes,
  [mine sites](../world/mine-sites.md) hold delving runes, and
  [roadside sites](../world/travel-sites.md) hold hearth and Wayfarer's runes.
- **Tier IV runes are never crafted.** Homeward, Ocean's Favor, Cloche, Feast Day, Motherlode, Delvemark, Grace,
  Aegis, Accord and Citadel only come from loot, as noted below.

## Hearth runes

Comforts for camp and the long road. Their timers are long (often 5 or 10 minutes), and casting one again only
refreshes the time, it never stacks. 14 of them can sit in a [passive slot](../spellcraft/passives.md).

**Where to find them:** plains, taiga and snowy village houses (home comforts), cartographer chests and shipwreck
map chests (road runes, including Homeward), and toolsmith and temple chests (craft runes).

| Rune | Tier | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Slowburn](effects/life.md#slowburn) | I | Hunger drains half as fast for 10 minutes. Can be a passive. |
| [Warm Cloak](effects/fire.md#warm_cloak) | I | You can't freeze for 10 minutes, even in powder snow. Can be a passive. |
| [Waymark](effects/arcane.md#waymark) | I | A pillar of light only you can see, from 160 blocks, for 10 minutes. Up to 3 at once. |
| [Gravefinder](effects/arcane.md#gravefinder) | I | Pale motes point the way to where you last died, for a minute. |
| [Orbcall](effects/arcane.md#orbcall) | I | Experience orbs within 10 blocks drift to you for 5 minutes. Can be a passive. |
| [Whistle](effects/wind.md#whistle) | I | Your pets within 48 blocks that aren't sitting or leashed come to you. |
| [Deepwarn](effects/earth.md#deepwarn) | I | Warns you of lava below or a long drop ahead, for 5 minutes. Can be a passive. |
| [Camp Ward](effects/arcane.md#camp_ward) | II | Wards an 8-block camp for 5 minutes: monsters that appear inside fade at once. One per caster. |
| [Hollow Pocket](effects/void.md#hollow_pocket) | II | Opens your own 9-slot pocket. What you keep there stays yours, even through death. |
| [Nightwatch](effects/arcane.md#nightwatch) | II | A bell warns you when a monster within 16 blocks starts hunting you, for 10 minutes. Can be a passive. |

| [Lodestar](effects/arcane.md#lodestar) | II | Sets your lodestar where it lands, or at your feet. |

## Tide runes

Runes for anglers and sailors. They change how long you wait for a bite and how lucky you are, never what the
fishing loot itself is.

**Where to find them:** village fisher chests, small and big underwater ruins, and shipwreck supply chests. Some of
them (Angler's Lure, Bait Blessing, Reeling Tide, Bobber Bell, Water Reading, Tackle Mend, School Sight, Shoal Herd,
Storm Glass, Fathom, Ice Auger and Drift Net) can also come up on a fishing line. See
[Fishing for runes](index.md#fishing-for-runes).

| Rune | Tier | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Angler's Lure](effects/frost.md#angler_lure) | I | Halves the wait for a bite on your bobber, leaving at least 1 second. |
| [Bait Blessing](effects/arcane.md#bait_blessing) | I | Luck for 60 seconds (Luck II with Amplify), so better catches come a little more often. |
| [Bobber Bell](effects/arcane.md#bobber_bell) | I | For 60 seconds your bobber rings and splashes when a fish bites. |
| [Water Reading](effects/arcane.md#water_reading) | I | Tells whether your bobber sits in open water (needed for treasure) and whether rain is speeding up bites. |
| [Reeling Tide](effects/frost.md#reeling_tide) | I | Reels in your bobber. A biting fish is caught as usual. |
| [Skimstep](effects/frost.md#skimstep) | II | Run across water for 15 seconds. Crouch to sink. |
| [Fair Wind](effects/wind.md#fair_wind) | II | In a boat, sail the way you look for 10 seconds. |
| [Wreck Sense](effects/arcane.md#wreck_sense) | II | Underwater chests and barrels, and suspicious sand and gravel within 20 blocks, shine for 15 seconds. |
| [Diving Bell](effects/frost.md#diving_bell) | III | Under water, a glass-walled pocket of air for 15 seconds. |
| [Ocean's Favor](effects/frost.md#oceans_favor) | IV | Water Breathing, Dolphin's Grace, Conduit Power and Night Vision for 120 seconds. Found only in big underwater ruins. |

## Farmstead runes

Runes for fields, herds, the kitchen, beehives and woodland. Every block they change respects land claims, and the
runes that use items (seeds, saplings, food) take them from your own inventory.

**Where to find them:** plains, savanna and taiga village houses, and shepherd and butcher chests.

| Rune | Tier | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Tillage](effects/earth.md#tillage) | I | Tills the dirt and grass in a 5-by-5 patch into farmland, as a hoe would. |
| [Sow](effects/earth.md#sow) | I | Plants seeds from your inventory into the empty farmland of a 5-by-5 patch. |
| [Fleece](effects/wind.md#fleece) | I | Shears every woolly sheep within 5 blocks. |
| [Ripen](effects/time.md#ripen) | II | Moves each crop, stem, berry bush and nether wart in a 5-by-5 patch one stage on. |
| [Dewkeep](effects/storm.md#dewkeep) | II | A mist keeps a 5-by-5 patch of farmland wet for 60 seconds, even far from water. |
| [Scarecrow](effects/wind.md#scarecrow) | III | For 20 seconds, shoos rabbits, foxes and monsters out of a 5-block spot. Pets and farm animals stay. |
| [Courtship](effects/arcane.md#courtship) | III | Up to 6 grown farm animals within 5 blocks fall in love at once. |
| [Hearthcook](effects/fire.md#hearthcook) | III | Cooks up to 8 raw food items in your inventory, as a smoker would. |
| [Coppice](effects/earth.md#coppice) | III | Fells the tree you strike (up to 32 logs) and replants the stump with a sapling from your inventory. |

Runes for miners and builders: digging, masonry, lighting, flipping levers and sorting chests. They follow land
claims and protection, and what they mine drops as a pickaxe would.

**Where to find them:** village toolsmith, mason and temple chests. Motherlode and Delvemark come only from the
vault chests of the great dungeons, such as the [Archive](../world/archive.md) and the
[Ember Sanctum](../world/ember-sanctum.md).

| [Plumb Line](effects/earth.md#plumbline) | I | Digs a shaft up to 8 deep. Stops a block above lava, water or a drop. |

| [Plankway](effects/earth.md#plankway) | I | Lays a lasting bridge up to 12 long, from planks or stone in your pack. |
| [Stair Delve](effects/earth.md#stairdelve) | II | Carves a stair 5 steps down the way you face. Stops short of lava, water or a drop. |

**Where to find them:** village cartographer, temple and shepherd chests, shipwreck map chests, ruined portals,

## Support runes

Runes that heal, guard, help a party, and calm or hold enemies without hurting them. Bosses shrug off most of the
calming ones.

**Where to find them:** village temple, plains house, weaponsmith and armorer chests, pillager outposts and igloos.
The Tier IV wards also drop from raiders: Ravagers can drop Citadel or Aegis, and Pillagers rarely drop Accord or
Grace. Grace also turns up in temple chests.

| Rune | Tier | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Worst First](effects/arcane.md#worst_first) | I | Heals the most hurt ally within 8 blocks (you too) for 6. |
| [Mending Mist](effects/frost.md#mending_mist) | II | A 4-block mist for 8 seconds: allies inside heal 1 each second. |
| [Managift](effects/arcane.md#managift) | II | Gives up to 20 of your mana to each ally player hit; they get three quarters of it. |
| [Stillbind](effects/void.md#stillbind) | II | Holds the target in place for 3 seconds without harm. Bosses are only slowed. |
| [Pacify](effects/arcane.md#pacify) | II | The creature forgets its target for 6 seconds; harming it ends this. |
| [Keepsafe](effects/earth.md#keepsafe) | II | For 10 minutes the block hit can't be broken by anyone outside your party, nor by explosions. |
| [Guardlink](effects/earth.md#guardlink) | III | For 15 seconds, 40% of an ally's damage comes to you instead. |
| [Hexguard](effects/void.md#hexguard) | III | Refuses the next harmful effect that would land within 20 seconds. |
| [Sanctuary](effects/arcane.md#sanctuary) | III | A 6-block circle for 30 seconds where no monster spawns, and monsters inside are nudged out. |

## Everyday links and modifiers

The hearth pack also has 17 links and 36 modifiers made for these runes. All of them can be crafted, and they
turn up in plains, taiga, savanna, snowy and desert village house chests.

**Conditions** (10 links): the rest of the spell fires only if something is true. If Night, If Day, If Raining,
If Underground, If Alone, If Near Ally, If Unhurt, If Holding Tool, If Brimming (more than half your mana) and
If In Fields (farmland within 4 blocks). If the condition isn't met, the rest doesn't fire and you get back the mana that part cost.

**Watchers** (7 links): the rest of the spell waits for something you do, then fires where it happened.

| Watcher | Waits up to | For |
|---|---|---|
| [On Mine](links.md#on_mine) | 30 seconds | The next block you mine |
| On Harvest | 1 minute | The next ripe crop you pick |
| [On Catch](links.md#on_catch) | 2 minutes | Your next catch with a rod |
| On Sprint | 15 seconds | You starting to sprint |
| On Splash | 30 seconds | You entering water |
| On Mount | 30 seconds | You riding something |
| On Wake | 10 minutes | You waking from a bed |

You can have up to 8 watchers waiting at once. Setting another one replaces the oldest.

**Modifiers** (36) change a rule rather than a number. Each one can go on an effect only once, and one that can't
work on the effect, or clashes with another, is refused with a message saying why. They come in three groups:

- **Gathering:** what happens to the blocks an effect breaks. [Tidy](modifiers.md#tidy)
  sends drops into your pack, Replanting replants crops, [Veinfollow](modifiers.md#veinfollow)
  follows an ore vein (up to 8 more blocks), [Timbering](modifiers.md#timbering) fells the
  logs above (up to 24 more), and Magnetic pulls nearby items to you. Kilned (smelted drops), Silken (Silk Touch) and
  Windfall (Fortune III) each decide what a block drops, so an effect can only take one of them.
- **Tending:** extra care where the spell lands, like Sowing, Furrowing, Fertile,
  [Mending](modifiers.md#mending), Nourishing, Purifying and Matchmaking.
- **Targeting:** who the effect lands on and how hard, like Culling (monsters only), Hallowed (undead only),
  [Triage](modifiers.md#triage) (the most hurt only), Gentle (skips farm animals, pets and
  villagers), Sparing (skips other players and their pets), and Inward or Selfless (only one of the two per effect).
  Steady stops an effect breaking any block and makes it 10% cheaper.

Full details: [Links](links.md) and [Modifiers](modifiers.md).

## Tips

- Pair a world rune with a **field shape** such as Furrow, Seedbed or Shaft to work a whole row, bed or shaft at
  once. See [Shapes](shapes.md).
- A watcher such as On Mine or On Harvest lets a spell wait and fire right where you're working.
- Hearth runes marked "Can be a passive" are worth a passive slot on long trips.
