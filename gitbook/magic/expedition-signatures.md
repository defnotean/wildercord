## What it is

Twelve [signature fusions](../fusion-altar/combining.md#signature-fusions) built around small,
readable choices: catching a shot, giving a foe time to dodge, helping an ally who agrees to it, and tools for
exploring.

## How to get it

Put the exact pair of runes in a [Fusion Altar](../fusion-altar/index.md) with an **Amethyst Shard** and
pay **3 XP levels**. Other runes of the same elements still make the usual fusion. All twelve are **Tier III** effect
runes: give them a shape.

| Signature | Runes | Counts as | Mana | Try it with |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| **Nullcatch** | Reflect + Collect | Void | 20 | Self, facing an incoming shot |
| **Second Bell** | Shock + Countdown | Time | 16 | Beam, on a foe |
| **Red Ledger** | Bleed + Reveal | Blood | 18 | Beam, on a foe about to move |
| **Quietus** | Manaburn + Stasis | Arcane | 20 | Beam, just before an enemy casts |
| **Blood Escrow** | Leech + Barrier | Blood | 14 | Beam, on a friend |
| **Frost Molt** | Frostward + Cleanse | Frost | 14 | Self, while frozen and on the ground |
| **Pulse Ferry** | Regrowth + Time Skip | Life | 18 | Self, beside two wounded friends |
| **Last Lantern** | Light + Rewind | Arcane | 18 | Beam, on a friend |
| **Pocket Current** | Bubble + Collect | Frost | 16 | Touch, on your own chest |
| **Wayline** | Span + Grapple | Arcane | 18 | Touch or Beam, on nearby ground |
| **Night Seam** | Light + Zipper | Void | 12 | Beam, straight at a thin stone wall |
| **Shard Compass** | Prospect + Treasure Sense | Arcane | 12 | Beam, carrying a raw ore |

The mana shown is the effect's own cost; shapes, modifiers and gear change the full price. Amplify, longer duration
and repeats don't raise these spells' limits. A spell that finds nothing to work on still costs its mana.

## How to use it

### Counters

**Nullcatch.** After a moment (0.3 seconds) a pocket opens in front of the target for up to **3 seconds** and swallows
**one** hostile projectile coming at their front. One catch per cast, however many allies it covers. Nothing is
returned to you.
*Counter:* shoot from behind, fire a second shot, or wait it out.

**Second Bell.** Two chimes strike a foe, **1** and **2 seconds** later, for up to **2 damage** each. If the foe moves
more than **1 block** from where it stood, the second chime misses. Up to **2** foes, **8 damage** in all.
*Counter:* step away before the second chime.

**Red Ledger.** For **3 seconds**, each time the foe moves a block or more, a cut deals up to **2 damage** (at most
every 0.4 seconds, **3 cuts**, **6 damage** in all). A teleport ends it.
*Counter:* stand still, or teleport.

**Quietus.** After 0.3 seconds, for **2 seconds**, it catches **one** spell the marked foe casts. If the foe is a player,
the catch drains up to **8 mana** from them. Old bolts, arrows and melee aren't caught. A foe can't be marked again for
**8 seconds**.
*Counter:* wait, or do something other than cast a spell.

Nullcatch and Quietus won't open where more than 64 projectiles are crowded together. Bosses can't be targeted.

### Helping friends

Sneaking refuses these spells.

**Blood Escrow.** Gives a friend up to **3 of your own health** as absorption for **6 seconds**. You never go below
**4 health**. Not on yourself, or on someone who already has absorption. Each friend can receive it once every
**8 seconds**. Unused absorption is lost.

**Frost Molt.** Turns a friend's frozen state into a frost plate for up to **3 seconds**. It blocks **one** projectile
hit of **6 damage or less**, then breaks. They must be frozen and on the ground. Once every **8 seconds**.

**Pulse Ferry.** Needs **two wounded** friends within **4 blocks**. Over two beats a second apart it heals the most
hurt one, then the other: up to **3 health** each, **6** in all. You can be one of them if you're hurt.

**Last Lantern.** Marks a friend's spot for **6 seconds**. If they sneak while within **8 blocks** of it, they're sent
back there, if the spot is still safe and the way is clear. Already sneaking? Let go and sneak again. Once every
**8 seconds**.

### Tools

**Pocket Current.** Aim at a chest you placed yourself. Up to **16** items lying within **3 blocks** go into it. What
doesn't fit stays on the ground. It won't touch other players' chests or drops, or locked or loot chests. Chests placed
before this rune existed don't count: break it and place it again.

**Wayline.** Aim at safe ground within **6 blocks**. **Hold forward** to be pulled toward it for up to
**2 seconds**; **sneak** to let go. It only moves you, and won't pull you through walls.

**Night Seam.** Aim straight at a wall up to **3 blocks** thick of stone, deepslate, cobblestone, stone bricks or tuff.
For **4 seconds** it shows whether there's open space behind it and whether a hostile creature is there. No blocks
change.

**Shard Compass.** Carry **raw iron, raw copper or raw gold**. It searches up to **8 blocks** around the hit for
matching ore and, if it finds some, lays a trail of pebbles toward it for **6 seconds**. Only a find uses up one raw
sample.

## Tips and counterplay

- Take Pocket Current and Shard Compass to camp: place your own chest and carry a raw ore.
- Add Wayline for short climbs and Night Seam to check a stone wall before you dig.
- Practise with a friend: dodge a Second Bell, stand still under a Red Ledger, and test Nullcatch with one arrow, then
  two.
- See also [Field Signatures](field-signatures.md).
