# Familiars

![Eight small glowing wisps of different colours drifting over a dark meadow at night](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/wisps.jpg)
<span>Wild wisps drifting over a ley line at night</span>

## What it is

**Wisps** are small creatures of light that drift along ley lines at night. Tame one and it becomes your
**familiar**: it floats at your shoulder, speeds up your mana and casts a small spell to help you.

Wisps come in eight elements. There are no Time or Blood wisps.

| Wisp | Food | Its little spell |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Blaze Powder | Throws an ember bolt at what you're fighting and sets it alight |
| **Frost** | Snowball | Chills what you're fighting: a little damage and Slowness II |
| **Storm** | Glowstone Dust | Zaps what you're fighting |
| **Wind** | Feather | Jump Boost in a fight, and catches you when you fall |
| **Earth** | Clay Ball | Resistance while you're under attack |
| **Life** | Glow Berries | Heals you when you're hurt |
| **Void** | Ender Pearl | Pulls loose drops and experience to you |
| **Arcane** | Amethyst Shard | Makes nearby monsters glow, so you can see them through walls |

## How to get it

### Finding wisps

Wild wisps are rare and only appear near players in the **Overworld**, and only when mob spawning is on.

- **On ley lines at night**, 16 to 40 blocks from you. Look along the violet ribbons of a
  [ley line](../progression/ley-lines.md) after sunset.
- **Near an awake Wellstone, day or night**, 6 to 16 blocks from you.
- The world tries about every ten seconds, with at most three wild wisps within 64 blocks of you.
- A wild wisp fades after five minutes at most, sooner once day comes.
- Its element is random.

Wild wisps keep about six blocks away. **Sneak** and they let you within two and a half blocks. Nothing can hurt a
wisp.

### Taming

![Two wisps close up at night: a pale blue one with sparkling motes and a small orange one with a flame](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-wisp-close.jpg)
<span>Wild wisps up close. Sneak and they'll let you near.</span>

A wisp answers only to **its own element**. Make **three offerings** to it, each within a minute of the last. An
offering is either:

- **a spell of its element** that hits it (any effect of that element counts), or
- **its food**: right-click it while holding the food.

On the third offering it bonds with you and comes out at your shoulder.

- **One spell counts once.** Offerings less than half a second apart are ignored.
- **The wrong element scares it.** Another element's magic or food makes it forget everything. Start over.
- **One tamer at a time.** If someone else makes an offering, it starts over with them.
- **Food is never wasted** on an offering that doesn't count.
- **Twelve at most.** With 12 familiars already, a wild wisp won't accept anything from you.

Your first familiar earns the **Kindred** feat. A familiar of all eight elements earns **Menagerie**.

## How to use it

![A player in daylight with a small glowing Fire Wisp floating above their right shoulder, its nameplate reading Player0's Fire Wisp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-familiar-shoulder.jpg)
<span>A Fire Wisp at its owner's shoulder</span>

- It floats behind your right shoulder with a nameplate such as *Alex's Fire Wisp*.
- If it falls more than 14 blocks behind, or gets stuck for three seconds, it teleports to you.
- It follows you between dimensions, comes back after you die, and returns to its lantern when you log off.
- It can't be hurt, projectiles pass through it, and it never breaks blocks.
- It only harms what you're fighting. It never hurts another player unless you two are allowed to fight
  (PvP on, or a [duel](../social/duels.md)). See
  [friendly fire](../social/playing-together.md#friendly-fire-and-pvp).
- It only helps while it's within **24 blocks** of you.

### Levels

Your familiar gains experience when you, your spells or your pets kill a monster while it's out and within 24 blocks:
**1** per monster, **20** per boss. Only the familiar that's out gains experience.

| Level | Experience | Spell every | Spell strength | Extra mana regeneration (with a Cord) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 12 seconds | ×1 | +10% |
| 2 | 40 | 10 seconds | ×1.3 | +15% |
| 3 (top) | 150 | 8 seconds | ×1.6 | +20% |

It only casts when there's a reason to. **What you're fighting** means whatever you hit, or hit you, in the last five
seconds, or else the nearest monster within 12 blocks that's hunting you. It must be within 16 blocks of the familiar
and in sight.

| Familiar | When | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | You're fighting | 3 damage, on fire 2 s | 3.9 damage | 4.8 damage |
| **Frost** | You're fighting | 1.5 damage, Slowness II 4 s | 1.95 damage, 5.2 s | 2.4 damage, 6.4 s |
| **Storm** | You're fighting | 4 damage | 5.2 damage | 6.4 damage |
| **Life** | You're missing at least 1 heart | Heals 3 | Heals 3.9 | Heals 4.8 |
| **Wind** | You're fighting | Jump Boost I 8 s, Slow Falling 3 s | 10.4 s, 3.9 s | **Jump Boost II** 12.8 s, 4.8 s |
| **Earth** | A creature hit you in the last 5 s | Resistance I 4 s | 5.2 s | 6.4 s |
| **Arcane** | Monsters nearby aren't glowing | Monsters within 16 blocks glow 6 s | 20 blocks, 7.8 s | 24 blocks, 9.6 s |
| **Void** | Always | Pulls drops within 7 blocks | 9 blocks | 11 blocks |

Two familiars also watch over you all the time:

- **Wind** gives you Slow Falling for 3 seconds once you've fallen more than 4 blocks.
- **Void** pulls loose items and experience to you. It skips items a player dropped and anything within 8 blocks of
  another player.

Want a scout, guardian or gardener instead? See
[Familiar jobs](jobs-and-echoes.md).

### Commands

| Do this | Result |
|---|---|
| **Sneak and use** your familiar | It **stays** put. Do it again and it **follows**. |
| Tell it to stay within 12 blocks of an **awake Wellstone** | It **waits at the Wellstone** and stops being your active familiar, so you can call another. |
| **Sneak and use** a familiar waiting at a Wellstone | Collect it. Your active familiar goes back to the lantern. |
| Use a **name tag** on it | Names it (up to 32 characters). Only you can name it. |

A familiar told to stay still helps while you're within 24 blocks. One waiting at a Wellstone stays even while you're
offline, but doesn't help until you collect it.

### The Wisp Lantern

The **Wisp Lantern** calls out and puts away your familiars. Craft it from a lantern, two glass panes and two
amethyst shards.

![Crafting grid: top row empty · Amethyst Shard · empty; middle row Glass Pane · Lantern · Glass Pane; bottom row empty · Amethyst Shard · empty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/wisp_lantern.png)

| Do this | Result |
|---|---|
| **Use** with a familiar out | Sends it into the lantern |
| **Use** with none out | Calls out the last one you had out |
| **Sneak and use** | Calls out the next familiar in your list |

Its tooltip lists every familiar you keep, with name, element, level, experience and where it is. Your familiars
belong to **you**, not the lantern: any Wisp Lantern works, and losing one loses nothing.

## Tips and counterplay

- **Limits:** 12 familiars in total, one out at a time, top level 3.
- Wild wisps only appear in the Overworld, but your familiar goes anywhere with you.
- Bosses that give 20 experience: the Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden, Elder Guardian, the Archivist, dungeon bosses
  (Cinder Warden, Root Guardian, Star-Eater, Storm Conductor, Tide Scribe), the Buried Keeper and
  [Sword Masters](../masters/index.md).
