# Familiars

**Wisps** are small creatures of light: a glowing orb with a soft trailing tail and two tiny eyes, tinted by its
element. Wild ones drift along ley lines at night. Win one over and it becomes your **familiar**, floating at your
shoulder, quickening your mana and casting a small spell of its own to help you.

![Eight small glowing wisps of different colours drifting over a dark meadow at night](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/wisps.jpg)
<span>Wild wisps drifting over a ley line at night</span>

## The eight wisps

Wisps come in eight elements. There are no Time or Blood wisps.

| Wisp | Food | Its little spell |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Blaze Powder | Throws an ember bolt at what you're fighting and sets it alight |
| **Frost** | Snowball | Chills what you're fighting: a little damage and Slowness II |
| **Storm** | Glowstone Dust | Zaps what you're fighting |
| **Wind** | Feather | Jump Boost in a fight, and catches you when you fall |
| **Earth** | Clay Ball | Resistance while you're under attack |
| **Life** | Glow Berries | Heals you when you're hurt |
| **Void** | Ender Pearl | Pulls loose drops and experience to you |
| **Arcane** | Amethyst Shard | Makes monsters nearby glow, so you can see them through walls |

## Where and when wisps appear

Wild wisps are rare, and only ever near a player in the **Overworld**.

- **On ley lines, at night.** Once it's dark, a wisp can drift up out of a ley line somewhere between 16 and 40 blocks
  from you. Look along the violet ribbons of a [ley line](../progression/ley-lines.md) after sunset.
- **Around an awake Wellstone, at any hour.** While you're within 12 blocks of a [Wellstone](../progression/ley-lines.md)
  that's awake on a ley line, wisps can rise out of its mana 6 to 16 blocks from you, day or night.
- **Now and then.** The world tries about every ten seconds near each player, and there are never more than three wild
  wisps within 64 blocks of you. A wisp never rises over water.
- **Only when mobs spawn.** With mob spawning turned off, no wisps rise.
- **They don't stay.** A wild wisp fades after five minutes at most, and sooner once day comes.

A wisp's element is random, so a Menagerie takes patience.

### How wild wisps behave

- **They keep their distance.** Walk up to one and it drifts away, keeping about six blocks from you.
- **Sneak to get close.** A wisp lets you within two and a half blocks while you're sneaking, or once you've started
  taming it.
- **They drift slowly, low over the ground**, following wherever the ley line runs strongest.
- **Nothing can hurt a wisp.** They're mana given a shape: swords, arrows, fire and falls do nothing. Only falling out
  of the world ends one.

## Taming a wisp

![Two wisps close up at night: a pale blue one with sparkling motes and a small orange one with a flame](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-wisp-close.jpg)
<span>Wild wisps up close. Sneak and they'll let you near.</span>

A wild wisp answers only to **its own element**.

1. **Offer it its element three times**, within a minute of each other. An offering is either:
   - **a spell** of its element that lands on it (any effect of that element counts, so a Bolt of Fire works on a Fire
     Wisp), or
   - **its food**: right-click the wisp holding its food, and one is eaten.
2. Each offering makes it chime a little higher, and you see *The Fire Wisp drinks your magic (1 of 3)*.
3. On the third it **bonds** with you: a bright chord, a ring of light, and it's your familiar. It comes out at your
   shoulder straight away (any familiar you had out goes back into your lantern).

Things to know:

- **One spell counts once.** However many effects of its element a spell has, it's one offering. Offerings closer
  together than half a second are ignored, so a Zone's pulses or a volley won't rush it.
- **The wrong element spooks it.** Magic of any other element, or another element's food, makes it shy away for a
  few seconds and forget everything: start again from nothing. *The wisp shies from Frost magic: it answers only to Fire.*
- **A minute's patience.** Leave more than a minute between offerings and it forgets.
- **One tamer at a time.** If someone else offers it something, it starts over with them.
- **Food only goes when it counts.** A wisp only eats when the offering counts, so food is never wasted on a spooked
  or full-up attempt.
- **Twelve at most.** If you already keep 12 familiars, a wild wisp won't take your magic or your food (and you're told
  so).

Your first familiar earns the **Kindred** feat. Bond with a wisp of every one of the eight elements for **Menagerie**.
Both are also advancements.

## Your familiar

![A player in daylight with a small glowing Fire Wisp floating above their right shoulder, its nameplate reading Player0's Fire Wisp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/images/d-familiar-shoulder.jpg)
<span>A Fire Wisp at its owner's shoulder</span>

- **It floats at your shoulder**, just above and behind your right shoulder, with a nameplate such as
  *Alex's Fire Wisp*. It flies round things in the way.
- **It blinks back to you.** If it falls more than 14 blocks behind, or can't get any closer for three seconds, it
  teleports to your side.
- **It goes where you go.** Log off and it goes back into its lantern, and comes out again when you return. It
  follows you between dimensions and comes back after you die.
- **It can't be hurt**, and arrows and other projectiles pass straight through it, so it never catches a shot meant
  for you.
- **It never breaks blocks.**
- **It only harms what you're fighting**, and never another player unless the two of you are fighting each other and
  that's allowed at all (PvP on, or a [duel](../social/duels.md)). The same
  [friendly fire rules](../social/playing-together.md#friendly-fire-and-pvp) apply as for your spells.
- **It only helps while it's near you**, within 24 blocks.

### Faster mana

While your familiar is out and within 24 blocks, and you're wearing a Cord, your mana regenerates faster:

| Level | Extra mana regeneration |
|---|---|
| 1 | +10% |
| 2 | +15% |
| 3 | +20% |

### What each familiar does, by level

Every familiar casts its little spell every **12 seconds at level 1, 10 at level 2 and 8 at level 3**, whenever
there's a reason to (if there isn't, it looks again each second). Its spells also get stronger with each level:
level 2 is 1.3 times as strong as level 1, and level 3 is 1.6 times.

**What you're fighting** means: whatever you struck, or whatever struck you, in the last five seconds, or failing
that the nearest monster within 12 blocks that's hunting you. It has to be within 16 blocks of the familiar and in its
sight.

| Familiar | When it casts | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | You're fighting something | 3 damage, set alight for 2 seconds | 3.9 damage | 4.8 damage |
| **Frost** | You're fighting something | 1.5 damage and Slowness II for 4 seconds | 1.95 damage, 5.2 seconds | 2.4 damage, 6.4 seconds |
| **Storm** | You're fighting something | 4 damage | 5.2 damage | 6.4 damage |
| **Life** | You're missing at least 1 heart | Heals you 3 (1.5 hearts) | Heals 3.9 | Heals 4.8 |
| **Wind** | You struck or were struck in the last 5 seconds | Jump Boost I for 8 seconds and Slow Falling for 3 | Jump Boost I for 10.4 s, Slow Falling 3.9 s | **Jump Boost II** for 12.8 s, Slow Falling 4.8 s |
| **Earth** | A creature struck you in the last 5 seconds | Resistance I for 4 seconds | 5.2 seconds | 6.4 seconds |
| **Arcane** | There are monsters nearby that aren't glowing | Monsters within 16 blocks glow for 6 seconds | within 20 blocks, 7.8 seconds | within 24 blocks, 9.6 seconds |
| **Void** | All the time (see below) | Pulls drops within 7 blocks | within 9 blocks | within 11 blocks |

Two familiars also watch over you all the time, once a second:

- **Wind** catches you when you fall: if you've fallen more than 4 blocks (and aren't flying or already slow-falling),
  it gives you Slow Falling for 3 seconds.
- **Void** pulls loose items and experience orbs toward you. It only takes items nobody dropped (a monster's loot, a
  broken block) and never anything within 8 blocks of another player, so it never takes someone else's things.

### Levels and experience

Your familiar gains experience from every **monster** slain by you, your spells or your pets, as long as it's out
and within 24 blocks:

- **1 experience** for each monster.
- **20 experience** for a boss: the Ender Dragon, the Wither, the Warden, an Elder Guardian, the Archivist, the Cinder
  Warden, the Star-Eater or the Tide Scribe.

| Level | Experience needed | Spell every | Mana regeneration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | from the start | 12 seconds | +10% |
| 2 | 40 | 10 seconds | +15% |
| 3 (the top) | 150 | 8 seconds | +20% |

When it levels up it flares, chimes, and tells you what it gained: *Ember grows to level 2: +15% mana regeneration, a
spell every 10 seconds.* A familiar grows a little bigger and brighter with each level.

Only the familiar that's out gains experience, so the others in your lantern wait at their own levels.

## Commands to your familiar

| Do this | And it... |
|---|---|
| **Sneak and use** (right-click) your familiar | stops and **stays** where it is. Do it again and it **follows** you. |
| Tell it to stay **within 12 blocks of an awake Wellstone** | **waits at the Wellstone** instead. It stops being your familiar that's out, so you can call out another. |
| **Sneak and use** a familiar waiting at a Wellstone | collects it: it follows you again, and whichever familiar was out goes back into the lantern. |
| Use a **name tag** on it | names it. Its nameplate shows its name and yours (*Ember · Alex*). Names can be up to 32 characters. |

- A familiar told to **stay** still helps you (mana, spells) as long as you're within 24 blocks of it.
- A familiar **waiting at a Wellstone** stays there even while you're away or offline, and doesn't help you until
  you collect it.
- **Only you can name your familiar.** Anyone else's name tag is refused and they keep it.

## The Wisp Lantern

The **Wisp Lantern** keeps the familiars you aren't using. Craft it from a lantern, two glass panes and two amethyst
shards (see [Items and Crafting](../items/index.md#wisp-lantern)).

![Crafting grid: top row empty · Amethyst Shard · empty; middle row Glass Pane · Lantern · Glass Pane; bottom row empty · Amethyst Shard · empty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/defnotean/wildercord/main/wiki/assets/recipes/wisp_lantern.png)

| Do this | And it... |
|---|---|
| **Use** the lantern while a familiar is out | sends it home into the lantern |
| **Use** the lantern with none out | calls out the last familiar you had out |
| **Sneak and use** the lantern | calls out the next familiar in your list (the one out goes home first). A familiar waiting at a Wellstone leaves it and comes. |

- **Its tooltip lists every familiar you keep**, each with its name, element, level and experience toward the next
  level, and where it is: *out with you*, *waiting at a Wellstone* or *in the lantern*.
- **Your familiars belong to you, not to the lantern.** Any Wisp Lantern works as yours, and losing one loses no
  familiars. You don't even need a lantern to keep a familiar: it's only for calling them out and sending them home.
- With no familiars yet, it says so: *strike a wild wisp with magic of its own element to bond with it*.

## Limits

- **12 familiars** at most, out, in the lantern and waiting at Wellstones together.
- **One out at a time.** Familiars waiting at a Wellstone don't count as out.
- **Eight elements.** There are no Time or Blood wisps.
- **Overworld only** for wild wisps, though your familiar follows you anywhere.
- **Level 3** is the highest.
